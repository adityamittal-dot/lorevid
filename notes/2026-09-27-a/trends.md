## Takeaways
1. Keep the "for sleep" audience's core preference: one character, one job, whole-life arc, slow/calm/sensory delivery, no shocks late in the video.
2. Titles should promise a concrete daily-life texture (a role + a striking noun/stake), not a generic era label — avoid "History of the Salt Trade" in favor of a personal framing.
3. A 10-minute, hand-crafted script is a differentiator against the glut of hours-long generic AI "for sleep" compilations — lean into craft (real sensory detail, a genuine arc) rather than length.
4. West Africa / the trans-Saharan trade is unrepresented across both this channel's history and the competing channels surfaced today — good freshness angle, and research sources are rich enough to support it.
5. Keep the hook personal and stakes-based rather than event-based, since the audience is here to fall asleep, not to be shocked awake by a dramatic historical event.
