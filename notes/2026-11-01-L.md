## Angle
Most "Void Century explained" videos either recap Imu's reveal or treat the whole topic as one big vague mystery. This deep dive structures it as a chronological inventory: everything Joy Boy specifically left behind, in the order a viewer could verify it chapter by chapter — the war, the Iron Giant, the three Ancient Weapons, the thirty Poneglyphs (including the Ohara incident and every arc's Poneglyph beat), the broken promise to Fish-Man Island, the Empty Throne/Imu, Lili's disappearance, Laugh Tale and the One Piece, and Joy Boy's literal return through Luffy. The connecting argument (section 11, the verdict) is that every major unsolved mystery in the franchise — Imu, the Will of D., the Ancient Weapons, the One Piece itself — traces back to this one century, which is the "why do all these separate mysteries keep connecting" angle data/community.md flags as under-covered. Opening archetype used: a bold claim stated flat before any evidence ("The Void Century is a hundred years the World Government erased from history, and Joy Boy is the reason it ended"). This differs from 2026-10-31-L's identical-archetype cold open and 2026-10-27-L's identical archetype too, so the next long session should pick a different opening (cold-open-on-the-moment, a quote, "everyone believes X," a countdown of clues, or a second-person scene).
