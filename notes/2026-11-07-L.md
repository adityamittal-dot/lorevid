## Fact check
Spawned a haiku subagent as a hostile expert fan. It flagged seven items. Five were false positives, directly contradicted by the actual pack text: Carmel being "Mountain Witch" is stated as fact, not speculation, in both the Elbaph.md pack (she "used the newly built Sheep's House to sell orphans to the World Government... directly responsible for giving the Marines John Giant, the first giant ever to be enlisted" [Ch. 867]) and the Whole_Cake_Island_Arc.md pack ("Working as an Underworld trafficker named 'Mountain Witch'... sold Linlin for a hefty price" [Ch. 868]); Brook disguised as Luffy destroying the portrait is explicit pack text ("Brook, who had disguised himself as Luffy, went up to the Carmel portrait and destroyed it" [Ch. 864]); Bege's plan explicitly targets the portrait as her one weakness because her skin resists his KX Launchers ("Big Mom had skin as hard as iron, but Bege knew how she could become weak... Bege gave Luffy the task of destroying the portrait" [Ch. 859]); the "twelve-day fast, seventh day" detail is not contradictory (snapping partway through a twelve-day fast, on day seven, is consistent, not a mismatch); and John being "the first giant Marines ever enlisted" is explicit pack text. Two flags were fair and led to real fixes: "Pudding raised from birth" for the assassination plot overstated what's confirmed, so it was softened to "groomed for years"; and "Chiffon abused for years" overstated the pack's actual wording ("abused by Big Mom after the marriage's failure," no specific duration given), so it was changed to "abused afterward for resembling Lola."
