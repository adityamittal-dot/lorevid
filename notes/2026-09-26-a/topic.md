**Chosen:** Your Life as a Byzantine Silk Weaver in Constantinople
**Angle:** Not a battle or a court intrigue, but the quiet, prestigious craft that clothed an empire — you are
born into a family silk-weaving workshop in Constantinople not long after Justinian's monks first smuggled
silkworm eggs out of China, learn the loom as a girl in a trade unusually open to women, rise through the
guild's strict rules on thread count and dye colour (imperial purple reserved for the palace alone), weave a
bolt destined for a foreign king as a diplomatic gift, survive a lean year when the guild's privileges are
tightened, and grow old still at the loom, passing the craft to your own daughter. Evergreen (no current-events
hook), no violence, and distinct in region, craft and gender frame from every topic already in `scripts/done/`
and `scripts/reserve/`.
