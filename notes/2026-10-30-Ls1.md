## Angle

This is the long's sharpest single claim distilled to one idea: Yamato's whole identity depends on a page in a journal that, in the What If, was never written — distinct from the long's ending (that's Ls2's territory), giving each Short its own payoff. Hook pattern: contrarian claim, not reused from the previous Short.

## Self-review
1. One idea, argued: erase one logbook entry, and Yamato's defiance of Kaidou loses its national cause but not its personal one — a claim a fan could push back on.
2. Canon evidence: chapter 968 (Oden's decision) and chapter 985 (Kaidou's offer, Yamato's refusal) are both named with numbers.
3. Theory honesty: framed explicitly as "what if," never claiming this is confirmed.
4. What-If rule: one event changed (the logbook's missing page), consequences follow Yamato's established character logic, no power or outcome changes.
5. Payoff before the loop: line 8 ("What he loses...") delivers the answer before the final two lines.
6. Loop: last line ends mid-sentence on "his whole war with Kaidou..." which finishes directly into line 1's own phrase "Yamato's whole war with Kaidou is built on one page in a logbook."
