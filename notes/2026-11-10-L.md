## Angle
Blackbeard is almost always covered as a Devil Fruit matchup character (Blackbeard vs Luffy, his "three souls" theory). The Rocks_D._Xebec.md and Marshall_D._Teach.md packs support a sharper, chapter-confirmed claim instead: the wiki states directly that Teach "apparently respects and admires his late father... having the same goal of world domination," named his flagship after him, and now rules Hachinosu — the exact island Rocks once conquered and ruled until his own fall. The theory layer this video adds is connecting those confirmed facts into a single reading: Blackbeard's Impel Down recruiting, his former-Warlord and former-Admiral hires, and his multi-Devil-Fruit hunting all mirror his father's "quality over quantity" Davy Back Fight crew-building, down to the wiki's own near-identical description of both crews as having "no true camaraderie, simply united by aligned interests." Opening archetype used: "Everyone believes X, chapter N says otherwise" — rotating off the "bold claim, flat" archetype that had run six times in a row in the backlog.
