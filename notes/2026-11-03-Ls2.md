## Angle
Hook pattern used: contrarian claim ("Flampe loved Katakuri... until his legend ended."). Flampe is the family member most devoted to Katakuri on the surface, making her betrayal the sharpest evidence that the family's love was conditional on the record. When respect survives a loss (Luffy continues fighting, covers the secret, wishes him luck) but family devotion *ends* at the loss (Flampe threatens exposure, shows no concern), the difference becomes undeniable. The angle is distinct from 2026-11-03-Ls1 (which shows Luffy's earned respect) by centering the failure of conditional love rather than the triumph of genuine respect.
