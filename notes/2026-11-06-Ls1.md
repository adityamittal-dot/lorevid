## Angle
This Short extracts the single most pivotal moment from the "What If Usopp never joined" long: the flag burning at Enies Lobby. It shows why Usopp's specific skill (sniping) became irreplaceable, not because he was the strongest, but because nobody else could make that exact shot in that exact moment. The angle resonates because it's about a crew missing one irreplaceable specialist.

The hook works because the first two lines set the actual moment, then the Short pivots to "but what if not" which frames the whole argument. Viewers understand the specialization that makes one person irreplaceable.

## Fact check
Sogeking burned the World Government flag at Enies Lobby (chapter 419) and convinced Oimo and Kashii to defect through a friendship built in Little Garden. His sniper specialization enabled both feats. If Usopp never joined the crew, neither moment could happen. All facts verified in long video sources.
