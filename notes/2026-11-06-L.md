## Fact check
Spawned a haiku subagent as a hostile expert fan, specifically instructed to check the real canon events the script says Usopp was responsible for (not the fictional premise itself). It confirmed as accurate: Usopp/Johnny spotting the Fish-Men and leaving Zoro to be captured, Usopp saving Genzo, Nami's hand injury covering for a faked betrayal, Usopp defeating Chew, Sogeking shooting down the flag, Sogeking convincing Oimo and Kashii to defect via the Little Garden friendship, Zoro being handcuffed to Sogeking against Jabra and Kaku, Sogeking sniping the Seastone keys to Franky, Sogeking rallying Luffy against Lucci, and the Franky/Fukurou/cola-refill/Number 4 key sequence. It flagged two things: (1) that Yubashiri "rusted" rather than "broke" — checked against the actual wiki pack text, which explicitly says "Zoro's sword Yubashiri was broken during this fight," so the script's wording matches the cited source exactly; the subagent's correction was from outside general knowledge, not the pack, so no change was made. (2) That saying Nami's hand-stab trick worked "without anyone actually getting hurt" was imprecise since Nami hurts her own hand — this was a fair catch, so the line was reworded to "without actually betraying anyone else at all," which is accurate to what the deception protects against.
