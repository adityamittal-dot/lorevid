## Angle
Rayleigh is a fan favourite from the wider cast, and the timeskip is evergreen. The common reading is that Rayleigh simply trained Luffy; the wiki itself says he left him alone for the last six months. This video argues that exit was the method: Rayleigh's own rules (two years cannot cover the basics, ch. 597; Haki grows against stronger opponents, ch. 886) make him unable to be both teacher and the stronger opponent. A steelmanned counterargument (he just ran out of things to teach) is answered with his own words. Verdict is labelled as my read, not canon.
Opening archetype: a bold claim stated flat, before any evidence (previous long, 2026-11-06-L, was a What If cold open).
