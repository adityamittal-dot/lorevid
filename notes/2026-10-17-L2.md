## Angle
Zoro and Luffy both spent two years getting stronger, and both now wield Conqueror's Haki, but the routes were different. Luffy's Haki came through a structured course with Rayleigh on Rusukaina. Zoro's came through a sword standard set by Mihawk, then a forced awakening when Enma demanded he stop holding back. The community file already has active debate on how Zoro got Conqueror's Haki and whether Sanji will match it. The existing script 2026-09-28-b1 ("Zoro's Conqueror's Haki Finally Makes Sense (1194)") covers the 1194 control angle, so this video differs by making the Luffy comparison the spine. The existing script 2026-10-03-a2 ("Rayleigh Never Gave Luffy Conqueror's Haki") argues the opposite on the source of Luffy's power; this video is consistent with it, since Luffy already had Conqueror's Haki and Rayleigh taught control.

Opening archetype: a second-person-free cold open built on a question: "Luffy spent two years learning Haki... Zoro spent two years with Mihawk". The core question lands at line 3.
