## Angle
Kizaru is covered as a power-scaling and emotional figure elsewhere in the queue (his calm cracking at Egghead). This is a different lens: narrative function. Across Sabaody, Marineford and Egghead he arrives to set a clock, is interrupted by whoever Oda needs, and leaves the plot on track. Searchable by "Kizaru explained"; the claim is arguable and every beat carries a chapter number. Opening archetype: a bold claim stated flat, before any evidence (the previous long used the same archetype, so the next long should pick a different one).
