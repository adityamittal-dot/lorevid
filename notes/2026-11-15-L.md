### Audit pass (2026-10-26, independent editor)
This was the most heavily cited script in the batch, and nearly all of it held up against direct re-checking of Sabo.md, Monkey_D._Dragon.md, Rocks_D._Xebec.md, God_Valley_Incident.md, Gol_D._Roger.md, Edward_Newgate.md, and Scopper_Gaban.md — including specific, easy-to-get-wrong chapter numbers: Ch. 968 (Roger disbanding his crew, crying crew), Ch. 581 (Whitebeard's territories going vulnerable), Ch. 1152 (the Rocks Pirates' disbandment), Ch. 1156 (Rocks' crew infighting), Ch. 803/539/593 (Dragon's small-victories warning, hiding his son, and the "more human" line), Ch. 823 (the Baltigo attack), Ch. 757 (Fujitora holding back), Ch. 1058/1060/1083 (the Cobra frame-up and Dragon's reaction) — all confirmed exact.

A WebSearch on chapter 1083 (the same source the writer's own notes cite) confirmed the "twelve take up arms / eight succeed" figures are accurate, so I restored them and kept a smaller improvement: the line now explicitly sets up Lulusia as one of the eight nations, which pays off later in the script when Imu erases it. Lesson for next time: when a local pack doesn't cover a claim but the notes already cite a specific web source for it, check that source (or search it) before assuming it's fabricated.
Both Shorts (Ls1 on Fujitora, Ls2 on Dragon's "never forgive" line) were independently re-verified against Sabo.md and Monkey_D._Dragon.md and are fully accurate, including the Ch. 757 and Ch. 1058/1060 citations and the "Buster Call" detail.
