## Angle
Sunday deep dive on the evergreen search term "Sabo Explained". The fresh argument: a crew is a promise to one captain's dream, so every great crew (Roger, Rocks, Whitebeard, Donquixote, Kaido, Big Mom) ended when that dream did; Sabo's oath is rooted in a wound (Gray Terminal's fire, Jalmack's cannon, Cobra's death) that belongs to no captain. Proof points: he stayed in the Army with every memory back (ch. 794), the Army outlived Baltigo (ch. 823), and Sabo accepts the Cobra frame (ch. 1054-1060, 1085-1086). Steelman: Dragon is a single point of failure, and the Blackbeard Pirates and Straw Hats show crews can last. Chapters 1159-1166 tie Dragon's God Valley wound to Sabo's.
Opening archetype: a bold claim stated flat, before any evidence. The siblings Shorts (Fujitora held back; Dragon would never forgive Sabo) each take one side point; the long only touches both briefly.
