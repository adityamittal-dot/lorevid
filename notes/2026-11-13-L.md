## Fact check
Self-checked every claim directly against the Monkey_D._Luffy.md, Smoker.md, Monkey_D._Garp.md, and Monkey_D._Dragon.md packs (no subagent spawned, per this session's instructions). Confirmed accurate: Smoker's pack states directly that he would have succeeded arresting Luffy at Loguetown had Dragon not interfered, and that this moment is the origin of his career-long fixation on Luffy; Dragon's pack confirms he caused a gust of wind that freed Luffy from Smoker's grip and that Smoker never learned why Dragon helped until Sengoku's public reveal at Marineford; Buggy's near-execution of Luffy (foiled by lightning) is a separate, earlier event in the same arc that this What If does not touch, since the single changed event is specifically Dragon's non-appearance during the later Smoker confrontation; Zoro, Nami, Usopp, and Sanji are all confirmed recruited before Loguetown in the actual story timeline; Garp's pack confirms he "initially refused to capture Luffy in Water 7 until explicitly ordered to," that he "bends the rules" for family, and that he turned down every offer of promotion to Admiral specifically because he refuses to answer to the Celestial Dragons (not because of any Loguetown-specific reason — this session deliberately did not invent a fabricated causal link between Loguetown and that real, separately-documented refusal); Garp's pack also confirms his mutual, respectful decades-long rivalry with Gol D. Roger; Smoker's established grudge-holding personality and his real Arabasta and Punk Hazard alliances with Luffy are both confirmed in his pack. Coby's presence at Loguetown and his later role pleading for peace at Marineford are both established canon used here only to show a plausible, modest shift in which lesson he draws from the same day, not a rewrite of his later actions.
One deliberate correction made during this pass: an early draft line implied Garp's refusal of the Admiral promotion was caused by this Loguetown incident — that is not supported by the pack (the real cause is his refusal to serve under the Celestial Dragons) and was rewritten to keep that detail factually separate from the hypothetical.
