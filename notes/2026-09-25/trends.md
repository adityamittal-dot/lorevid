## Takeaways
- The channel's exact format ("Your Life as a ___", second person, calm bedtime pace) is already a proven, actively-running pattern on competing channels — lean into it, but never copy their specific titles (Ragnar, Musashi, Shieldmaiden, Geisha are all taken).
- Roman gladiators, Vikings and generic "medieval" life are the most saturated eras in this niche right now; an under-covered, still-visually-rich era (Mughal India, Aztec Tenochtitlan) will stand out more in search and suggested-video rows.
- The audience explicitly wants undramatic, sensory, low-stakes narration — so any violence or danger (war, punishment, disease) should be mentioned soberly and briefly, never lingered on, exactly as WRITER.md's safety rules already require.
- A warm human-animal bond (a Mughal elephant keeper and his elephant) offers a gentler emotional spine than a soldier or raider topic while still delivering the "exotic imperial court" spectacle this audience responds to.
- scripts/done/ only contains one prior video (2026-09-24, "Your Life as a Samurai's Wife in Edo Japan", Edo Japan setting) — today's pick just needs to avoid Edo Japan/Japan specifically, which a Mughal India or Aztec topic already does.
