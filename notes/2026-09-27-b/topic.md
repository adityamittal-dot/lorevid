**Chosen:** Your Life as a Royal Physician in the Joseon Court
**Angle:** Told through one night-and-day cycle inside the palace medical bureau (Naeuiwon) — diagnosing the queen
by pulse and voice alone, mixing herbal remedies by lamplight, the constant tension of a wrong diagnosis meaning
your own life, and the quiet reward of a fever finally breaking before dawn. Leans into candlelight, medicine
smells, hushed palace corridors — genuinely soothing rather than clinical, and distinct from the generic
"royal court" videos already out there because it's told through one specific, high-stakes job.
