## Angle
Eustass Kid is usually covered as a side character in Luffy/Law's Wano story, or as a quick "what happened to Kid at Elbaph" news beat. The Eustass_Kid.md pack supports a sharper character throughline instead: his single defining trait, an absolute refusal to ever kneel or back down, is explicitly what let him survive Kaidou's attempt to break his spirit in Udon's Prisoner Mine and help finish off two Emperors at Onigashima — and the pack states just as directly that the identical trait is what made him "overestimate his own strength" against Shanks at Elbaph, costing him his ship, his crew's safety, and every Road Poneglyph rubbing he'd collected. This is a Sharp Theory Claim format built on a single consistent character trait across two opposite outcomes, not a What If. Opening archetype used: a second-person cold open ("You're chained in Wano's prison mines...") — rotating away from the "bold claim, flat" archetype used across the recent backlog run.

### Audit pass (2026-10-26, independent editor)
Re-checked every concrete claim in the long and both Shorts directly against Eustass_Kid.md, Kaidou.md, and Shanks.md. The writer's fact-check list above holds up almost entirely — this was the most accurate script of the batch so far. Found and fixed:
- **Overreach:** a line claimed Kid "dislikes using weapons of mass destruction" as a general character trait. The pack only supports one specific moment — Kid declined to buy Caesar Clown's doomsday weapon out of curiosity about the black market, not a stated general principle. Rewrote the line to stick to that one confirmed moment instead of generalizing it into a trait.
- **Unsupported certainty:** a line asserted Kid "still has his whole crew" after Elbaph. The pack says Shanks spared the surrendering Kid Pirates, but Dorry and Brogy sank their ship immediately after regardless — the crew's ultimate fate past that point isn't stated either way in the pack, only that Kid and Killer were "knocked out," not killed. Softened the claim to what's actually confirmed: Kid and Killer both survived.
- **Ls1 fix:** "Big Mom's strongest homie" (Zeus) is a superlative the pack never makes — fixed to name Zeus directly instead of an unverified ranking claim. Also credited Killer by name for knocking out Napoleon (the pack splits that job between Zoro/Prometheus and Killer/Napoleon; the draft's passive phrasing dropped Killer's credit).
- Ls2 checked out completely clean against the pack (the arm being rebuilt during Onigashima, severed by Hawkins' link, and only repaired much later at Ch. 1076/1109 all match exactly).
