## Angle
Most Nanami content either recaps his death for shock value or treats it as a sad surprise. This video argues the opposite: the manga spent roughly 90 chapters (23 through 120) proving Nanami's one documented edge over Mahito — surviving a direct hit from Idle Transfiguration, something no one else managed — and then systematically removed every piece of that edge during Shibuya (an eye to Dagon, half his body to Jogo, his whole team incapacitated, a horde of transfigured humans fought solo) before Mahito ever got a second shot at him. It's a theory/analysis breakdown, not a What If — nothing in the story is changed, only re-sequenced into an argument. Opening archetype used: a bold claim stated flat before any evidence ("Nanami Kento was never supposed to survive Shibuya a second time"), paired with the first-encounter detail as the hook for why that claim is true. No prior long in this backlog batch used this archetype (the others in the batch are Shanks/Marineford, Minato/Fox seal, Kuma/Bonney, Doflamingo/Law, Oden What If, Void Century).
