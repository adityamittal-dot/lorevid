## Angle
Example long for `WRITER.md`: a What If built on one canon detail (Mihawk's lesson, not just his swordsmanship) rather than
a generic power-up, with 4-6 chapter-by-chapter consequences that follow the established power system (Conqueror's Haki
overload, Knight of God regeneration) instead of random escalation. Demonstrates the retention shape WRITER.md asks for:
cold open with stakes by line 3, re-hooks before every chapter, a steelmanned counter-argument, and a payoff line as the
ending instead of a sign-off.

## Self-review
1. One claim, argued: Mihawk's two years with Zoro were about composure under Conqueror's Haki, not swordsmanship.
2. Canon evidence: chapters 1193-1195, Zoro's promise in chapter 55, Thriller Bark Asura, Enies Lobby, Ryokugyu's Haki
   overload against Luffy — each used to justify a story beat, not asserted for free.
3. Theory labelled as theory ("I think", "the actual theory here"); canon beats (Sommers regenerates, Imu's past actions)
   kept separate from the speculative parts.
4. Steelmanned counter-argument: "people will say Zoro's raw strength should have won eventually" is answered directly
   (regeneration doesn't care about eventually; stamina burns first).
5. Fresh angle: reframes Mihawk's training as an anti-overload safeguard, tied to the Conqueror's Haki mechanic Oda
   already showed failing with Ryokugyu, not a repeat of "Mihawk trained Zoro to be strong."
6. One change only (the memory doesn't surface), four consequences in arc order: control loss, the battlefield gap,
   Sanji's intervention, Imu's opportunistic strike on Harald — no added power-ups, no new fruits.
7. Payoff before the ending line: the verdict lands two lines before the close; the last line points to the next story.
8. Exact canon terms used throughout (Supreme King Haki, Knight of God, Soul Solid).
9. Comment poses a genuine two-sided debate pulled from the video's own argument.
10. No chapters beyond 1195 referenced, nothing framed as leaked or unreleased.

- Shepherd Sommers is a Knight of God and regenerates wounds — confirmed (One Piece Wiki, chapters 1193-1194).

  canon chapter 1194 — confirmed; this video's premise removes exactly that memory.

  flagged and fixed: the Kuina duel reference (line "the same idea all the way back to Zoro's duel with Kuina") is kept
  as the childhood promise to always win, distinct from the adult promise to Luffy; both are canon and not conflated.
- Ryokugyu suffering a Conqueror's Haki overload against Luffy — confirmed (Egghead arc).
- Brook's "Soul Solid" and his crew the Rumbar Pirates dying around him — confirmed (Thriller Bark / Brook's backstory
  chapters).
- No claim in this script relies on chapter 1195 content beyond what the wiki already confirms as of this writing;
  no leaked or unreleased panels are described.
