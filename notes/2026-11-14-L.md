### Audit pass (2026-10-26, independent editor)
Re-checked every claim the writer flagged as unverified (the Imu fight, arm loss, Kizaru draw, Edd War, God Valley, Shanks scenes) directly against Scopper_Gaban.md, plus everything else. All of it checked out — chapter 1151's exact "second, not third" line, the Imu fight details, the Kizaru-draw comparison, Edd War, and the God Valley rescue are all accurate.
The bigger problem was padding: this script sat right at the word floor (2312 words) and had roughly 14 lines that just restated the line right before them with no new fact ("That's my read, and the rest of the evidence leans the same way" after a claim already made; "Notice who Roger needed in that moment: Gaban and Rayleigh, side by side" restating the line before it; "Rayleigh's best feats are real, but the stakes behind them were smaller" repeating the previous line's exact claim; "I'd say that matters, because most of the cast never gets anywhere near that level" restating the line before it; two near-identical "it's a small moment/scene" lines about Shanks and Luffy; "Zoro didn't even know about it himself" repeating a clause from the line immediately before it; two generic closing lines, "I keep coming back to that" and "that's the line I keep thinking about," that said nothing new back to back). Rewrote all of these into new, pack-verified facts instead of cutting them, so the floor is now cleared with real content, not restated commentary: Gaban leading the charge alongside Rayleigh against the Whitebeard Pirates [Ch. 966]; the crew meeting Dorry and Brogy at Little Garden after Edd War; Gaban's role covering the truth of Harald's death and Loki's patricide for Hajrudin; Gaban going into the Imu fight already wounded by the Knight of God Gunko; his one-stroke defeat of Knight of God Shepherd Sommers [Ch. 1149]; his fourteen-year suspicion of Elbaph's official story about Loki [Ch. 1152]; a specific, human beat from his reunion with Shanks (Ripley's tart, the threatened gossip) [Ch. 1169]; Shanks having already tried to get Gaban to trust Luffy before Gaban warmed to him; a direct callback to his Yachitakeru-vs-Honebami clash with Imu; and Gloriosa, a Rocks Pirates defector, asking Gaban's ship for a ride off God Valley [Ch. 1165-1166]. Also fixed one clarity issue: a line about "Garp told Gaban that Roger had fathered a son" was technically accurate (it's about Ace, not Shanks, per Ch. 1169) but never named Ace, risking confusion with Luffy; named Ace explicitly.
Both Shorts needed real fixes: Ls1 claimed Imu "had to block with two weapons," which isn't supported anywhere in the pack (Imu's weapon is a single shapeshifting polearm) — reworded to drop the invented detail. Ls2 invented a beat where "Sanji assumed it was him" before Gaban named Zoro — no such moment exists in the pack — and separately claimed "Luffy had already sensed it," which also isn't supported; both were rewritten to stick to what the pack actually shows (Gaban naming Zoro directly, with no guessing game and no claim about what Luffy already knew).
