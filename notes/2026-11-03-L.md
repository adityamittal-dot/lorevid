## Angle
Most Katakuri content is pure power-scaling (Supreme King Haki, Future Sight, the Luffy duel's feats). The pack's own "Relationships" section hands over a sharper, less-covered angle: track who actually sees Katakuri as a person versus who only loves the unbeaten legend, and the family comes up short on almost every count (Big Mom calling him "a lowly child," his siblings' total disbelief/rage rather than concern when he loses, Oven trying to attack Luffy to erase the "stain" rather than checking on his brother, Flampe's devotion flipping the instant the image cracks) — while Luffy, an enemy, is the one who sees the hidden mouth, the gluttony, and still respects him, covers the secret with his hat, and never gives him a mocking nickname. That's a specific, disagreeable claim with dense evidence on both sides. Opening archetype used: a bold claim stated flat, before any evidence — same as 2026-11-02-L, so the *next* long in the backlog should use a different archetype (e.g. cold-open-on-the-moment or a second-person scene) per WRITER.md's rotation rule.

## Fact check
Spawned a haiku subagent as a hostile expert fan. It flagged four claims as unverified from its own knowledge: Big Mom calling Katakuri "a lowly child," Flampe threatening to expose his secret, the earplugs given to his siblings during Big Mom's rampages, and Katakuri watching Vegapunk's Egghead broadcast with his siblings. All four are directly present in the data/wiki/onepiece/Charlotte_Katakuri.md pack: "a lowly child of hers" and "threatened to expose his secret" are the pack's own prose (Relationships section, sourced from the wiki's cited passages); the earplugs beat carries an explicit [Ch. 868, Ep. 838] citation; the Egghead broadcast beat carries [Ch. 1119, Ep. 1151]. The subagent doesn't have pack access, so these are false positives, not errors. Fix made anyway: added an explicit "chapter 868" mention into the earplugs line so the citation is visible in the narration itself, matching how 2026-11-02-L handled the same kind of false-positive flag. Everything else the subagent checked (the eel-mouth secret and silenced kitchen staff, the Mirro-World duel, Katakuri's "no longer beneath my level" admission, the post-fight hat-covering and no-nickname beats, Oven's rage and failed attack on Luffy blocked by Germa 66, the Galette rescue and Perospero concern) it confirmed as accurate.
