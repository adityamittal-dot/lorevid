## Angle
Kizaru is usually covered only as a power-scaling matchup (Kizaru vs Luffy, Kizaru's Devil Fruit explained). The Borsalino.md pack supports a sharper, character-driven claim instead: his entire "laziest Admiral" reputation — the wrong Den Den Mushi calls, the shrugged-off mangrove tree, circling real fights at Marineford instead of finishing them — is a deliberate emotional distancing method, and it is explicitly shown breaking at Egghead when his mission forces him to end Dr. Vegapunk's life. The pack states this directly: Kizaru "wished that he had brought darker sunglasses to hide his emotions," and the killing "caused Kizaru immense amount of emotional pain," ending with him snapping at Sakazuki and Sakazuki sincerely apologizing and calling him "brother" — all explicitly unusual behavior for him. This is a Sharp Theory Claim format (WRITER.md's best-performing format), not a What If. Opening archetype used: cold-open on the moment itself (dropping straight into Kizaru right after the act, no setup) — a deliberate break from the "bold claim, flat" archetype used in the last 6 longs in the backlog.

### Audit pass (2026-10-26, independent editor)
Re-checked every concrete claim against Borsalino.md and Vegapunk.md directly (not against the writer's own self-review above). Found and fixed:
- **Wrong speaker/wrong timing (factual error):** the script had Vegapunk's satellite "Lilith" call Kizaru "a sad, cold-hearted man" *after* the killing. The pack says it was **Vegapunk himself**, in person, moments *before* Saturn's fatal strike and Kizaru's finishing blow — not a stranger speaking posthumously. Fixed the two lines that stated this and rewrote the closing "strangest part of the theory" beat, which had been built entirely on the wrong attribution (it claimed only a stranger ever called him cold-hearted and that "the people closest to him never stopped trusting him" — false once the speaker is corrected; the truer, sadder beat is that his oldest friend of 26 years died believing the mask was all there was).
- **Double-counted fact (padding dressed as evidence):** Z's "you rely too much on your Devil Fruit" / "still hard like a rock" exchange is a single scene at Firs Island (Borsalino.md), but the script presented it as two separate incidents ("years earlier" and then "said it again" at Firs Island) to manufacture a false sense of a repeating pattern. Replaced the first occurrence with a different, verified detail (Kizaru deferring to a trusted subordinate's orders — also pack-supported) instead of just cutting it, to hold the word count.
- **Invented specific:** "aim a laser beam through a held coin" — the pack only supports "destroy a small object held in someone's hand... despite the great distance"; no coin is named anywhere. Reworded to match exactly what's supported.
- **Loose paraphrase:** "orphaned boy" for Sentomaru — the pack says he was "cast out from his village" / "kicked out of his village," not that he was an orphan. Fixed to match the pack.
- Everything else in the self-review's claim list (Sentomaru training, wiretap mistake, mangrove tree, Rayleigh/Zoro, Marineford stalemate with Marco incl. the seastone cuffs, Bonney/Rayleigh contrast, feeding Luffy, the Sentomaru fight, the Sakazuki/"brother" outburst, Elegia/Uta) checked out against the packs and was left as is.
- Unverified but low-risk: the Marineford beat order is paraphrased from the pack's summary paragraph rather than pinned chapter-by-chapter (already flagged by the writer); treated as acceptable since no specific chapter number is misattributed.
- **Wrong fact (caught via the Ls1 Short's own notes):** the long claimed Kizaru "never once" broke off from the Marco stalemate to go after Luffy at Marineford. The pack directly contradicts this — Kizaru shot Luffy and kicked him toward Whitebeard, and "kept hounding Luffy while he was within his grasp, first at Sabaody and then at the Summit War." The Ls1 Short's fact-check notes had already flagged and avoided this claim but the long itself was never corrected; fixed the long's two affected lines to say Kizaru split his attention between Marco and directly shooting at Luffy.
