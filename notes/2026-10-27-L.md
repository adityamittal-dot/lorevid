## Angle
The common framing of this scene is "Minato had no choice, the chakra was too big for a baby, so he panicked and sealed it into Naruto." The wiki's own account contains a detail almost no retelling mentions: Kushina offers an alternative that night — reseal the Nine-Tails into herself so it ends with her — and Minato explicitly turns it down, stating he believes Naruto will need the fox's power later against Tobi. That's the spine of this video: not "he had no choice" but "he had a choice and made it on purpose," then built a multi-stage support system (Kushina's chakra, his own failsafe chakra, the key routed to Jiraiya) into the seal rather than just closing the lid on an emergency. Opening archetype used: a bold claim stated flat before any evidence ("Minato didn't seal the Nine-Tails inside Naruto because he had no other choice. He planned it."), the same archetype as 2026-10-31-L, so the next long session should pick a different one (cold-open-on-the-moment, a quote, "everyone believes X," a countdown of clues, or a second-person scene).

## Fact check
Claims checked against the wiki packs listed in Sources, with chapter citations:
- Minato was a rare prodigy, Jiraiya's apprentice, and believed by Jiraiya to be the Child of the Prophecy — supported, Minato_Namikaze.md [Ch. 498, Ch. 144, Ch. 503].
- Minato single-handedly rescued Kushina from a Kumogakure kidnapping by tracking strands of her hair, which made him known across nations; Kushina had called him "unreliable" and "girly" before falling for him — supported, Minato_Namikaze.md [Ch. 498].
- During the Third Shinobi World War, enemy shinobi were given flee-on-sight orders against him; in the anime he single-handedly defeated roughly a thousand Iwagakure ninja, forcing a peace treaty; years before his death, people already believed only he could stop Orochimaru — supported, Minato_Namikaze.md [Ch. 94] and the Minato power-profile section (anime feat noted as anime-only, stated as such in the video's narration register, not claimed as manga canon).
- Tobi (the masked man) killed the Anbu/midwives, captured newborn Naruto, and was stopped by Minato, who then protected Kushina and fought to free the Nine-Tails from Tobi's control — supported, Naruto_Uzumaki.md and Minato_Namikaze.md [Ch. 500, Ch. 501].
- Minato used Gamabunta to pin down the Nine-Tails and teleported it to Kushina's location, where she restrained it with a barrier — supported, Minato_Namikaze.md (History section, Nine-Tails' Attack).
- Kushina offered to have the Nine-Tails resealed into herself so it would end with her; Minato rejected this, believing the Nine-Tails' power would be needed later if Tobi returned, and decided — recalling Jiraiya's "Child of the Prophecy" belief — to make Naruto the new jinchuriki — supported, Minato_Namikaze.md (History section, Nine-Tails' Attack), the central claim of this video.
- The Nine-Tails' chakra was too immense to seal whole into an infant, so Minato used the Dead Demon Consuming Seal (Shiki Fujin, which consumes the user's soul) to split it, sealing the Yin half in himself and the Yang half in Naruto via the Eight Trigrams Sealing Style; Kurama was reportedly shocked by the same sealing skill it had seen from Kushina — supported, Minato_Namikaze.md [Ch. 91, Ch. 504].
- Minato wove Kushina's chakra into the seal so she could later help Naruto, and mixed in his own chakra programmed to activate and restore the seal if it weakened — supported, Minato_Namikaze.md [Ch. 504, Ch. 439].
- Minato summoned the toad Gerotora with his last strength and sent the seal's key to Jiraiya — supported, Minato_Namikaze.md [Ch. 504].
- The Third Hokage honored Minato's wish that Naruto be seen as a hero, but most of the village ostracized Naruto instead, and the Third Hokage banned mentioning the Nine-Tails; this isolation drove Naruto to pull pranks for attention — supported, Naruto_Uzumaki.md [Ch. 2, Ch. 440] and the Personality section.
- Naruto first accessed the Nine-Tails' chakra unconsciously during the Haku fight, then repeatedly against Neji, Gaara, and Orochimaru; Akatsuki's Itachi and Kisame tried to capture him at Shukuba Town; he drew on it again against Sasuke at the Valley of the End — supported, Naruto_Uzumaki.md (Part I/Part II Synopsis sections).
- Jiraiya trained Naruto to work with the fox; at Mount Myoboku, Kurama's interference kept Fukasaku from fusing with Naruto directly, so Naruto used shadow clones for senjutsu training instead; Naruto's chakra reserves were about four times a jonin's as a child — supported, Naruto_Uzumaki.md (Abilities/Chakra and Sage Mode sections).
- Killer B helped Naruto gain Nine-Tails Chakra Mode; learning Kurama's true name and earning its friendship gave him full access with no drawbacks, including surviving the Ten-Tails' Tenpenchii and Toneri's Moon-splitting explosion unharmed — supported, Naruto_Uzumaki.md (Jinchuriki Powers section).
- Minato returned as a reanimated (Edo Tensei) jinchuriki of his own Yin half in chapter 631, gaining Nine-Tails Chakra Mode instantly; Black Zetsu later stole that half from him in chapter 664 — supported, Minato_Namikaze.md [Ch. 631, Ch. 664].
- In the Boruto-era continuity, Naruto's bond with Kurama leads to Baryon Mode, which trades both their lifespans for power — supported, Naruto_Uzumaki.md (Baryon Mode section, Boruto manga/anime citations).
No claim in the script lacks a citation above; the anime-only "thousand Iwa-nin" feat is flagged in-pack as anime canon and narrated without overstating it as manga fact.
