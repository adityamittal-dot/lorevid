## Angle
Hashirama is usually covered only as a power-scaling matchup (Hashirama vs Madara feats, Wood Release explained). The Hashirama_Senju.md pack supports a sharper, relationship-driven claim instead: his entire arc with Madara, from childhood to the Valley of the End to their final reconciliation during the Fourth Great Ninja War, traces back to one specific vow the pack quotes almost directly: that he would protect the village from anyone who threatened it, "whether they are my friends, siblings or my own children." This video argues that line is the key to understanding why Hashirama both ended Madara's life and never stopped calling him a friend. This is a theory/analysis breakdown, not a What If. Opening archetype used: a paraphrased quote from the source material framing the question up front — rotating off the "bold claim, flat" archetype used across the recent backlog run, and giving this batch's one Naruto/Boruto slot a distinct feel from the five One Piece longs around it.
