## Angle
Hashirama is usually covered only as a power-scaling matchup (Hashirama vs Madara feats, Wood Release explained). The Hashirama_Senju.md pack supports a sharper, relationship-driven claim instead: his entire arc with Madara, from childhood to the Valley of the End to their final reconciliation during the Fourth Great Ninja War, traces back to one specific vow the pack quotes almost directly: that he would protect the village from anyone who threatened it, "whether they are my friends, siblings or my own children." This video argues that line is the key to understanding why Hashirama both ended Madara's life and never stopped calling him a friend. This is a theory/analysis breakdown, not a What If. Opening archetype used: a paraphrased quote from the source material framing the question up front — rotating off the "bold claim, flat" archetype used across the recent backlog run, and giving this batch's one Naruto/Boruto slot a distinct feel from the five One Piece longs around it.

### Audit pass (2026-10-26, independent editor)
The writer's self-review above says "no corrections were needed" — that wasn't quite right. Re-checked every line against Hashirama_Senju.md and Madara_Uchiha.md directly and found two real problems the self-review missed:
- **Padding that deleted a fact:** two pairs of back-to-back lines each restated the same point twice instead of covering the pack's actual two-part parallel. (1) "Madara couldn't beat him" was said twice in a row (once per speaker's perspective, but the same fact both times); the pack actually pairs this with a *different*, more important fact that never made it into the script at all — "Hashirama could never bring himself to kill someone he still considered a friend." Rewrote the first of the pair to restore that missing half of the parallel instead of repeating the second half. (2) The Izuna-convinces-retreat beat was stated twice almost verbatim ("a fading Izuna convinced Madara..." / "a mortally wounded Izuna convinced Madara..."); the first line's own shot.search ("Hashirama offers unite clans instead finishing") revealed it was supposed to cover a different beat — Hashirama offering to unite the two clans, which the pack states happened right before Izuna talked Madara out of it. Rewrote the first line to match its own shot and restore that beat.
- **Factual contradiction:** one line said "Hashirama had Tobirama elected democratically... so no one could call it favoritism," directly contradicting an earlier line (and the pack) that says Hashirama himself won the vote. Fixed to say what the pack actually states: Tobirama insisted on the democratic vote, and Hashirama won it.
- **Precision fix:** "Itachi's devotion... rivaled his own" softened a pack line that says Hashirama called Itachi's devotion *greater* than his own; fixed to "surpassed."
- Both Shorts checked out completely clean, including two specific chapter citations (Ch. 577 for Madara being the only one who could spot Hashirama's wood clones, Ch. 625 for the self-sacrifice offer) that are both exactly correct against the pack.
