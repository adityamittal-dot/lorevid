## Angle
Naruto's origin is usually told starting from his birth and the Nine-Tails sealing. The packs' own Jiraiya/Nagato history sections hand over a sharper frame: Jiraiya already had a self-declared "Child of Prophecy" — Nagato — whom he trained for three full years as a surrogate father, decades before Naruto was even born, and whose novel (inspired by Nagato's own words) is the direct reason Minato named his son Naruto in the first place. That's a specific, citable claim that reframes Jiraiya's bond with Naruto as a second attempt, not a first one. ## Fact check
Spawned a haiku subagent as a hostile expert fan. It confirmed 25+ core claims (the prophecy, Minato's training and the Rasengan link, the Sannin/orphans meeting, Nagato's Rinnegan awakening, the three-year training period, "The Tale of the Utterly Gutsy Shinobi," the Hokage offers, the Hanzo ambush and Yahiko's sacrifice, Jiraiya's spy mission and death, Naruto's forgiveness of Nagato, the two-Children-of-Prophecy reveal, and Obito crediting Jiraiya) and flagged four items. Three were false positives — directly supported by the packs with bracketed citations the subagent didn't have access to: Minato being one of Jiraiya's own genin students at the time ([Naruto Ch. 503]), Pain making it rain over Amegakure "every Sunday" ([Naruto Ch. 368]), and the Gutsy Shinobi dedication to Nagato ([Naruto Ch. 446]). The fourth was a real error worth fixing: I had written that Minato tried to talk Jiraiya out of the name "Naruto" — the pack actually says the reverse, that Jiraiya tried to talk Minato and Kushina out of it because it would make him the godfather ([Naruto Ch. 382]). Fixed that line to put the roles the right way around, and also tightened the Minato line to say "one of his own genin students" rather than a bare "genin," since the subagent read that as a rank inconsistency with later-life Minato.
