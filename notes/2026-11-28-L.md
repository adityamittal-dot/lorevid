## Angle
Fans say "Obito's gift" and stop. The wiki shows the gift is only the eye: Kakashi pays chakra for it daily, earned the Mangekyo through his own loss, trained Kamui up from barely aimable to near-instant, and his Kamui is the exact counter that unmasked Obito. The only effortless power was the spirit-borrowed dual Mangekyo, which vanished. Sharp, arguable, no recap. Opening archetype: "Everyone believes X. Chapter N says otherwise."
