## Angle
Oden never actually opened Wano's borders in canon — he died trying to become shogun before he could. The batch's assigned title only works as a sharp claim once reframed: not "what if the opening never happened" (it never did), but "what if Oden never even formed the dream of opening them at all." That single erased decision at chapter 968 is the one page Yamato finds in Oden's logbook that turns into his entire identity and his specific defiance of Kaidou's New Onigashima Project — removing it doesn't change any fight outcome (no power creep), only the motivation behind choices that are already canon, ending on the real, lesser-known chapter 1041 beat where Momonosuke himself refuses to open Wano's borders out of fear. Opening archetype used: "Everyone believes X [he opened them / tried to]. Chapter N says otherwise" — flipped into a claim-then-twist cold open, distinct from the previous long's archetype.
