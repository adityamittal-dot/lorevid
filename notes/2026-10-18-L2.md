## Angle
Naruto never met Itachi in a free fight. The one real clash (Contact) happened while Kabuto controlled Itachi through Edo Tensei. Tsukuyomi needs eye contact and is Mangekyo-only, so the restraint question splits into cost, mercy and strategy. The video argues it is a combination, and that the rule plus the Contact setup explains more than any single reason. Format: theory breakdown (not a What If), chosen because it is the second long of the day and a What If would repeat the One Piece long's format.
