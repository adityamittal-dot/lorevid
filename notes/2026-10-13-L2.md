## Angle
Fresh angle on the most-covered Nami moment: the usual take is that Nezumi's theft is the tragedy. The video argues it was the only thing that could free Nami, because the 100 million deal was never going to buy her exit (Arlong's own chapter 81 words) and because Luffy only moves once Nami cries and asks (chapters 81-82). Bell-mere's "you should have asked" in chapter 77 is the hidden bookend. Wider cast (Nami / Arlong Park) is on the angle bank and not in the last 60 titles.
Opening archetype: "Everyone believes X. Chapter N says otherwise." (seven million short vs chapter 81).
