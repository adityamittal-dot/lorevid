## Angle
Power-scaling videos on Marineford always frame it as "Akainu vs Whitebeard." The wiki packs show the opposite pattern once you track Akainu's actual target choices line by line: every time he has a free choice, he chases Ace and then Luffy, and only engages Whitebeard defensively (stopping a naginata swing about to level the island, blocking a quake aimed at the platform). That's a sharp, citable claim nobody else is making this way, and it reframes Absolute Justice as a doctrine about bloodline/lineage rather than raw strength — which also ties cleanly into current Elbaph-era Gorosei behavior for a forward-looking beat. Opening archetype used: a bold claim stated flat, before any evidence (per WRITER.md's "Opening archetypes" list) — next long should use a different one (e.g. cold-open-on-the-moment or a second-person scene).

It confirmed as accurate: the ch.550 Sengoku reveal, the ch.561 Squard manipulation, the ch.573 Akainu-aims-at-Luffy beat, Akainu's presence at the Ohara Buster Call, the Garp/Sengoku restraint moment, Crocodile's intercept, and Shanks ending the pursuit. One flag was a false positive (Rouge's pregnancy timeline) — the pack's own text supports the 20-month/15-months-after-Roger figure as written, so no change needed there.
