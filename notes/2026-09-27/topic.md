## Reasoning
- Medieval Castle Blacksmith is ruled out purely on freshness: yesterday's video was already medieval (peasant woman, Black Death). Two medieval days in a row would flatten the channel's variety.
- Viking Raider and Roman Gladiator both carry a violence-coded premise that fights the "calm bedtime" promise; either can work but only with a title and angle that route around the raid/arena cliché.
- Egyptian Pyramid Builder scores well across the board: strong demand, plenty of grounded sensory detail (rations, mudbrick, work-gang camaraderie, the Nile flood calendar), an inherently physical-labor-and-rest daily rhythm that suits a sleepy pace, and it hasn't appeared in `scripts/done/` yet.
- Gladiator's demand edge is real, but the trend check found a same-titled existing video and mostly shock-framed competitors — reusing it today without heavy reframing risks both a title collision and the wrong tone. Saving it for a day when we have more room to build a truly distinct angle is the safer call.

**Chosen:** Your Life as an Egyptian Pyramid Builder
**Angle:** Not a slave under the whip (outdated cliché) but a conscripted or paid work-gang laborer during the Nile's flood season — the physical grind of hauling stone, the food and beer rations that kept the gangs going, the camaraderie of a named crew, the ache of muscle and the awe of watching the monument rise course by course, ending on the quiet pride of having helped build something meant to outlast every one of us.
