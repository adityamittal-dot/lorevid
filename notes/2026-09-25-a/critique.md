# Critique — Your Life as an Aztec Merchant in Tenochtitlan

Re-reading `draft1.md` cold, as three separate critics.

## Bedtime viewer

- The opening line, "Before the sun rises over Lake Texcoco, your mother folds a plain maguey cloak into your arms, and tells you the road will decide your whole life," sets mood well but the "stake" is vague — WRITER.md's own example ("By sunset, you will know if he is coming home") is a *ticking, concrete* stake. "The road will decide your whole life" is a theme statement, not a stake I feel in my body in the first three seconds.
- The whole video runs to roughly 1,583 words of narration, which is about twelve minutes at bedtime pace — well past the 8.5-11.5 minute target. A sleepy viewer drifting off at minute nine should be landing on the resolution, not still mid-Chapter-Four.
- Chapter Three is tense scene after tense scene after tense scene (24 through 34, nearly the whole chapter) with no calmer beat in between — "for a long moment nobody on either side moves or speaks a single word" into "that night you cannot sleep, listening to your own heartbeat" into "every man around your fire goes still" is three jolts in a row with no breathing room, which risks actually waking a drowsy viewer up rather than lulling them.
- "Something in your chest loosens" (Chapter Three) and "you feel the same loosening in your chest" (Chapter Five) repeat the exact same image almost word for word — the second occurrence should have felt like an echo on purpose, but as written it just reads as reused material.
- Scene 51, "You still leave the city in darkness, though you no longer truly need to, because the quiet has become a kind of prayer you are not ready to give up," is lovely but at thirty words it runs long and the sentence has to be rushed to fit calm pacing.
- Too many consecutive scenes open with "You" (forty-five of sixty-one) — read aloud back to back this becomes a metronome and stops feeling like a voice telling you a story.

## Historian

- Scene 13, "let your gold ride hidden inside a hollow cane," fuses two separate research facts that were never one fact: pochteca ritually bundling their *walking canes* (research.md #10) and gold dust being carried in *hollow bird quills* as currency (research.md #16). Nothing in the sources describes a cane used to smuggle gold — this is an invented conflation and needs to be corrected or clearly softened.
- Scenes 25-27, the armed standoff at a "narrow crossing" resolved by a ritual first-gift exchange, is dramatized invention with no supporting line in research.md. It should be reframed as one of the dangers pochteca faced in general (which *is* supported), not narrated as a specific remembered event with invented blocking and dialogue-free choreography.
- Scene 31, "Word reaches your party that traders from Tenochtitlan were killed on a road not far from here," draws on research.md #25, which is explicitly marked "(debated)" for exact dates and locations. The script currently presents it as a plain, undisputed fact happening to the narrator personally — it needs to stay general enough that it doesn't overstate certainty the sources don't have.
- Scene 30's word "ledger" is an anachronistic accounting term with no basis in the Mesoamerican record cited in research.md (Aztec traders used memory, oral report, and pictorial codices, not ledgers) — should be cut or replaced.
- Nothing in the draft is factually wrong at the level of names, places, prices, or institutions (Tlatelolco, Yacatecuhtli, the sixty-thousand-buyer market, the graded measuring boxes, the sumptuary-law cotton restriction, the sixteen-year-old-turned-fourteen apprenticeship arc) — every one of those matches a numbered line in research.md. The only real accuracy problems are the two invented-detail scenes above and the one overstated "debated" fact.

## YouTube strategist

- Length is the single biggest strategy problem: at ~1,583 narration words this is a ~12-minute video when the format promises "about ten minutes." Needs a real trim, not padding cuts, to land at 1,250-1,350 words.
- Chapter Three ends on "old habits kept you alive on the road," which closes the chapter emotionally instead of opening a loop into Chapter Four — there's no pull forward. Chapter Four ends on "something in you goes quiet with pride," which is the same problem: warm, but it doesn't tease anything coming in Chapter Five.
- The two near-identical "loosening in your chest" lines will read to a viewer (and to an algorithm doing repetition/quality checks) as recycled writing rather than deliberate echo — needs either a clear callback ("that same loosening, the way it always feels") or a genuinely different image the second time.
- No banned AI-filler words ("tapestry," "testament," "delve," "little did you know," "in a world where") appear anywhere — good, that part is clean.
- Thumbnail/title promise is not yet written, but the draft itself never shows an on-screen "shocking" moment strong enough to honestly support a shock-style thumbnail option — the strongest beat is the hill-country crossing, so thumbnail option three needs to be honest about tension/danger rather than promising violence the script (correctly, for bedtime-safety reasons) never delivers.
- Sentence-opener monotony ("You... You... You...") flagged by the bedtime-viewer critic is also a pacing problem for narration: read aloud, forty-five same-word openers in a row makes the AI voice's prosody flatten out, which increases the chance of a viewer's attention sliding off mid-video rather than settling into sleep.

## Must fix
1. Trim total narration from ~1,583 words to roughly 1,250-1,350 words by cutting or merging scenes, prioritizing the most repetitive beats in Chapters Two and Four.
2. Rewrite the opening scene so the first three sentences land a concrete, ticking stake, not just a mood — per WRITER.md's own "By sunset, you will know if he is coming home" model.
3. Fix scene 13's invented "hollow cane" gold-smuggling detail — either cut the gold-hiding detail entirely or move it to the accurate, sourced image of a hollow quill of gold dust used as currency (research.md #16), kept separate from the cane-bundling ritual (research.md #10).
4. Rewrite the hill-country crossing (scenes 25-27) so it reads as a real, sourced category of danger pochteca faced generally, rather than a specific invented confrontation staged like a remembered scene with blocking and gestures no source describes.
5. Soften scene 31 ("Word reaches your party that traders from Tenochtitlan were killed on a road not far from here") so it does not overstate the certainty of a fact research.md itself marks "(debated)" — keep it general, framed as the kind of news pochteca dreaded, not a pinned-down incident.
6. Cut or replace the word "ledger" in scene 30 — it is an anachronistic accounting term not supported by any source in research.md.
7. Break up the unbroken run of tense scenes in Chapter Three with at least one calmer, reflective beat in between, so the chapter does not deliver three consecutive jolts.
8. De-duplicate the "loosening in your chest" image — keep it once as the emotional high point, and give the second homecoming a distinct sensory image instead of repeating the same phrase.
9. Give Chapter Three and Chapter Four each a genuine open-loop closing line that pulls into the next chapter, instead of the current emotionally-closed endings.
10. Vary sentence openers so significantly fewer than forty-five of sixty-plus scenes begin with the bare word "You" — mix in openers built from setting, sound, or object instead.
11. Trim the two over-length scenes (currently 30 and 29 words) down to 18-28 words each.
