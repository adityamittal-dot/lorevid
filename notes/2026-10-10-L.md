## Fact check
Checked in a single pass against the One Piece Wiki pages (Chapter 1083, Eight-Nation Revolution, Knights of God, Mary Geoise, Monkey D. Dragon) fetched via Firecrawl. WebFetch was blocked for the fandom domain. No subagent was used.

Confirmed:
- Chapter 100: Dragon's gust stops Smoker pinning Luffy at Loguetown during a storm.
- Chapter 432: Garp reveals Dragon is Luffy's father.
- Chapter 1083: Sabo's three objectives (World Nobles' symbol, freeing slaves including Kuma, destroying Mary Geoise food stocks); Dragon says the raid drew Marine attention; twelve nations rose, eight succeeded, Lulusia annihilated; supply carriers attacked; Dragon calls the Knights of God "the real battle".
- Karasu vs Fujitora, Morley vs Ryokugyu, Lindbergh freeing slaves, Sabo seeking collar keys (Ch 1083 long summary).
- Eight-Nation Revolution: eight of twelve succeeded; Lulusia destroyed.
- Imu and the Five Elders used an Ancient Weapon on Lulusia as a Mother Flame test.
- Knights of God in Elbaph: Gunko, Sommers and Killingham kidnap schoolchildren to force Jarul to let the Owl Library burn; Imu possesses Gunko to speed the takeover.
- Mary Geoise attacked by an unknown force during the Elbaph arc, set ablaze; Imu possessed Gunko; Shamrock's return stabilised the situation.
- The attackers of Mary Geoise are not identified in the wiki.

Corrected:
- Lulusia test: the wiki does not say it was approved because of its proximity to Mary Geoise. Line reworded to match the source.
- Knights "are Imu's direct enforcers": wiki describes them as an elite Celestial Dragon group. Reworded.
- Elbaph: removed "Loki" and the claim Sommers fights Zoro and holds Ito Ito no Mi (not found in fetched sources). Reworded the Gunko/Shamrock arrival sequence to what the sources support.
- Hostage plan: wiki says the children were used to force Jarul to allow the Owl Library to burn, not "burn their own schools".
- "Nobody saw it happen on screen" and "reported, not shown": softened, since the wiki cites chapter 1060 showing Lulusia.
- "Dragon's report arrives as a flashback": corrected, since the report is in-scene and the raid is the flashback.
- "Revolutionary Army is the only group in canon that has broken in once": overclaim, reworded.
- "Nobody saw his face" (Loguetown) and "watched the Going Merry leave": not supported by the sources, removed.
- "Never tried to take credit" and "said nothing": not supported, reworded as framing.

Unverified, kept as framing (flag for a human check):
- Garp raising Luffy as Dragon's entrusted child is supported ("Dragon entrusted his son to his estranged father to be raised"). The Dadan step was removed.
- Opinion lines about Dragon's method, Oda's intent and the Blackbeard motive are presented as the author's theory, not as fact.
