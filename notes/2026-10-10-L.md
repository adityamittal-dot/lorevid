Fixed in the script:
- Mr. 2: the "sees what Crocodile would do to Vivi" defection and the "Oda built it as proof" line were unsupported. Reworded to his bond with Luffy's crew and siding with them after Crocodile falls.
- Tom/Franky: "Tom's family, same lineage" was wrong. The blueprint passes from Tom to his apprentice Franky in Water Seven.
- Clock tower: the threat is a timed bomb, not a cannon. Vivi reaches it with Chopper's help, and Pell carries it away in canon. Replaced "cannon" with "bomb" throughout and fixed the "Vivi and the crew" line.
- Pluton's location: Operation Utopia wanted the location from the Tomb's Poneglyph, not Pluton itself "dug up" in Alabasta. The Poneglyph points to Wano.
- Vegapunk's "800 years" notes: no source found. Replaced with an unattributed characterization.
- Impel Down: Crocodile escapes in the breakout shortly before Marineford. Removed "fights alongside Luffy at Marineford," which was not supported.
- Crocodile's freedom after Alabasta: he was jailed in Impel Down, so "walks away keeping his freedom" was wrong. Reworded.
- Robin/Crocodile: the lie gets her stabbed. Removed the unsupported "Luffy interrupts" claim.
- Doflamingo "a decade" changed to "years"; Rio Poneglyph line reworded to match its sourced role (Void Century history; Road clues point to Laugh Tale).

Not verified and left in place (what-if framing or low stakes): Zoro vs Mr. 1, Gear Second's stamina cost, Whiskey Peak/Rainbase assassins, Robin's "twenty years" as a fugitive.
