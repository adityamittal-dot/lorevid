## Angle
Bellamy is a high-recall, low-coverage character: fans remember the Jaya punch and the Dressrosa tears but rarely connect them. The fresh angle is that the "coward" label confuses bad judgment about whom to serve with bad nerve, built on the Jaya and Dressrosa mirror (same Luffy fist, opposite meaning) and the Vivre Card refusal.
