## Angle
Thesis: the dragon form was the strongest-looking move, not the strongest move in the duel. Size, scales and flight made it the most visible choice, but it cost control and made Kaido a bigger target once Luffy's Conqueror's Haki grew and his pace changed. Fan explainers describe the hybrid form as more maneuverable, which is the evidence for the trap reading.
