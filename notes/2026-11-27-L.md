## Angle
The channel already has a Void Century lore video (what Joy Boy left behind). This one is a different argument: the century stayed hidden through three kinds of silence (the Government's ban, the heroes who chose not to speak, and the guardians who sealed the truth away), so the villain is a system rather than Imu. Opening archetype: a bold claim stated flat. Shorts take side points (the Ohara lake of books, Lili's erased record) and do not answer the headline.
