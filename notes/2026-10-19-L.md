This video argues the comedy is the delivery and the loyalty is the message, and that the whole relationship traces back to one Loguetown moment: a crime boss watching a boy refuse to look afraid. The theory is labelled as "my read" throughout. Opening archetype: cold-open on the moment itself (a feared crime boss weeping before a pirate ship, question stated by line 3). ## Fact check
Each claim below was checked against the source listed. Dropped claims are listed at the end.
- Bartolomeo was a feared crime boss in his local town. Supported by farotech and sportskeeda results (as a feared local crime boss).

- Luffy's ambition to become King of the Pirates inspired Bartolomeo to sail. Supported by farotech result.

Supported by wiki Chapter_9 result. Narration says "his story ended right there" instead of "died" for advertiser rules.
- Bartolomeo entered the Corrida Colosseum to win the Mera Mera no Mi for Luffy. Supported by designbeep and a second search result.
- Gambia was flattened by Vice Admiral Maynard before his match. Supported by search result describing the Colosseum.
