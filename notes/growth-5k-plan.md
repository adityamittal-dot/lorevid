# Growth plan: longs to 5,000 views each

Based on `data/performance.md` (updated 2026-11-01) and `data/trends.md`/`channel.json`. Read-only research; no other files edited.

## Current numbers (longs only)

- 127 subscribers, 197,880 total channel views, 156 videos.
- Best long ever: ~1,700 views ("Why Zoro Never Learned Haki Like Luffy"). Most longs land 100-1,300 views. Several "What If" evergreen longs die at 1-150 views.
- Long traffic sources (lifetime): SUBSCRIBER 14,161, RELATED_VIDEO 3,602, YT_SEARCH 532, NO_LINK_OTHER 152, YT_OTHER_PAGE 93, YT_CHANNEL 53, EXT_URL 28, **PLAYLIST 18, NOTIFICATION 6, END_SCREEN 2**.
- Retention: top longs hold 55-73% at 0:30 (the 2026-10-16 cold-open rewrite worked) but fall to 17-35% by the 25% mark and 5-18% by 50%. AV% mostly 10-20%, best 27%.
- Shorts separately pull 1,000-6,000 views each — the channel *can* get discovered, just not as longs.
- Cadence: 1 long/day (15:00 UTC) + 2 Shorts/day (14:30/17:00 UTC), per `upload.py:next_slot`. Weekly 25-36 min deep dive.

## Bottleneck: impressions/discovery, not CTR

No CTR/impressions metric is tracked anywhere in this repo (`data/performance.md` has no such column) — that itself is a gap. But the traffic-source table proves the bottleneck indirectly: **87% of long watch-minutes come from SUBSCRIBER+RELATED_VIDEO**, i.e. people who already know the channel. YT_SEARCH is 2.9% of views despite "search-first" titles being enforced since 2026-10-16. PLAYLIST and END_SCREEN are near-zero even though the channel runs Shorts that get 10-100x more raw views than the longs. That means the Shorts audience basically never reaches a long video — the funnel is broken, not just underperforming. Retention is a secondary, real problem (25%/50% marks are weak even on the best videos) but fixing it only helps videos that already get impressions; right now almost nothing outside existing subscribers *gets* impressions at all.

## Ranked changes (max 8)

1. **Fix the Shorts→long funnel (biggest lever).** END_SCREEN shows only 2 lifetime views and PLAYLIST 18, despite Shorts routinely hitting 1,000-6,000 views. `upload.py`/`pipeline.py` never call a YouTube end-screen API (none exists for Shorts), so `related_long`/`end_text` (WRITER.md §"Shorts cut from it") rely on description links that almost nobody opens. Action: on every `Ls1`/`Ls2` upload, pin a comment with a direct link + a reason to click ("full breakdown here ↓"), and make `end_text` name the long's hook, not just tease it generically. Wire the pinned-comment text in `pipeline.py` around the existing comment-posting block (`upload.py:365` `post_first_comment`). Expected impact: largest single multiplier — even a 5-15% Shorts→long CTR (industry benchmark) on a 2,000-view Short is 100-300 extra long views, more than most longs get today. [trymypost.com end-screen 2026](https://www.trymypost.com/blog/youtube-shorts-end-screen-strategy-2026), [tubebuddy 3-click strategy](https://www.tubebuddy.com/blog/use-this-3-click-youtube-shorts-strategy-to-grow-your-long-form-videos/)

2. **[NOT POSSIBLE, checked in Studio 2026-11-02: the series playlist option is gone; the menu only offers podcast/course] Turn series playlists into "Series" playlists.** `upload.py:_get_or_create_playlist` creates an ordinary playlist; YouTube's algorithmic session-boost only applies to the "Series playlist" setting (Studio-only toggle), which also unlocks auto-play-next. PLAYLIST traffic is 18 views lifetime — essentially unused. Action: manually flip each series playlist (`channel.json` → `series[].playlist`) to "Series" in Studio once; add a `studio_todo.md` reminder going forward. Expected impact: playlists tuned for binge can lift session duration up to ~40%, which is reportedly the top session-contribution signal YouTube uses for long-form ranking. [bosswallah 2026](https://bosswallah.com/blog/creator-hub/youtube-playlist-strategy-to-increase-views-watch-time-in-2026/), [frameos.studio 2026](https://frameos.studio/blog/youtube-playlists-strategy)

3. **Shorten longs from 12-15 min to ~9-11 min.** WRITER.md §"Long Video Rules" (line 283) mandates 2300-3000 words / 12-15 min. 2025-2026 benchmark data puts strong retention at 50-60% for 8-12 min videos vs only 35-45% for 15 min+ — this channel's longs sit at 10-27% AV%, well under even the worse bucket, and the 25%/50% marks (not just 0:30) are where viewers now bail. Action: edit the length rule to ~1,800-2,400 words / 9-11 min, keep the existing re-hook timer (0:30/3:00/6:30) but compress the back half instead of adding more chapters. Expected impact: per the cited case study, trimming to 10-12 min nearly doubled impressions in a month via the retention→impressions feedback loop. [prepublish.ai retention benchmarks 2026](https://prepublish.ai/blog/youtube-retention-benchmarks-2026), [ytshark ideal length 2026](https://ytshark.com/ideal-youtube-video-length/)

4. **Bias topic selection toward chapter-reaction/trending over evergreen "What If."** `data/trends.md` shows niche chapter-reaction longs (GrandLineReview etc.) pulling 20,000-100,000+ views/day within days of a new chapter, while this channel's "What If" evergreen longs (e.g. "What If Gojo Cremated Geto's Body?" 123 views, "What If Corazon…" 175 views) are the worst performers in the whole table. WRITER.md §6 already calls chapter reactions "candidates" but doesn't force priority over What-Ifs. Action: in `plan.py:day_items`, when a new chapter/episode dropped in the last 7 days (per `channel.json series[].release`), force that day's long to be the chapter-reaction/trending angle and push the queued What-If back a day. Expected impact: these topics already have 10-50x the ceiling of evergreen ones in the same niche. [trends.md internal data — no external search needed]

5. **Add search-first keyword pressure beyond the title.** YT_SEARCH is still only 2.9% of long views, unchanged in ratio since the 2026-10-16 search-first title rule (then 89/5.5k ≈ 1.6%). Titles alone aren't moving search traffic. Action: in WRITER.md §7 "Description," require the exact search phrase to open the description's first sentence (not just appear naturally) and add it as one of the first 2 tags — both are 2026 ranking-factor consensus items beyond the title. Expected impact: moderate — search is a small slice today, but it's pure incremental reach with no retention risk. [fluxnote.io YouTube SEO 2026](https://fluxnote.io/blog/youtube-channel-seo-guide-2026)

6. **Make long thumbnails face/emotion-first, not just two-character layout.** WRITER.md §7 thumbnail spec (device:"circle", two characters side-by-side, 0-3 words) doesn't require an expressive face in the dominant position. 2026 CTR data: thumbnails with an expressive face outperform faceless/graphic ones by 20-30%. Action: add a rule that `search`'s character crop must be chosen for visible facial expression (shock/anger/determination), not just a "colourful close-up," and keep text to 3-5 words max (23% CTR lift vs 0 words, per the same data) instead of the current 0-3 word allowance which sometimes ships with none. [awisee thumbnail stats 2026](https://awisee.com/blog/youtube-thumbnail-best-practices/)

7. **Post community updates tied to long releases.** `data/community.md` exists for topic research but the channel doesn't appear to use the Community tab to resurface longs to the 127 subscribers between uploads (NOTIFICATION traffic is only 6 views total — bell notifications aren't converting either). Action: add a same-day community post (poll or image) linking the day's long whenever a chapter-reaction video goes up, to double-dip the subscriber base that's already 87% of views. Low effort, low but free impact since it only reaches existing subs — rank last among content changes but costs nothing in the pipeline.

8. **Re-time the long upload slot.** Longs ship at a fixed 15:00 UTC (20:30 IST) in `upload.py:next_slot`. With only 127 subs this has negligible measurable effect yet, but once funnel/retention fixes (#1-3) raise raw numbers, A/B the slot against US evening (02:00-04:00 UTC) since English-language anime-theory audiences skew US/EU. Ranked last: sample size is too small right now to act on, revisit after #1-4 land.

## Summary

The channel has the content engine (cold opens already fixed, titles already search-first-ish) but almost no path for non-subscribers to reach a long video — Shorts, the channel's only real reach, dead-end at the long's door (#1, #2 fix that). Retention past 0:30 (#3) and topic selection (#4) are the next real levers once discovery improves; the rest are smaller, free wins.
