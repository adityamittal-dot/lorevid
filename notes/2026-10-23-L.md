## Angle
Most fans file Yuji's Shibuya defeat as a loss. This video's claim is that the loss was the mechanism: Gojo's fingers could not get past Yuji's will while he stayed in control, and Shibuya removed that control through a defeat plus eleven fingers at once. Sukuna's win was borrowed, and Shinjuku is where Yuji paid it back by separating Sukuna from Megumi's body. The Shibuya-era Choso and Nanami/Yuji material is already covered on the channel, so this piece stays on the loss-as-setup mechanism and does not retell those scenes.

Opening archetype: a bold claim stated flat before any evidence ("Yuji Itadori lost to Sukuna, and I think that loss was the setup for everything."). The previous long (2026-10-22 backlog) used a different archetype.
