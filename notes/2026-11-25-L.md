## Angle
Evergreen search topic ("Every Gear Of Luffy Explained", Gear 2 through the new 1198 form). Original thesis: every gear is what Luffy built right after a loss (Blueno, the steel door, Sabaody and Marineford, Kaido three times, Imu in 1188), and each one charges a stated price (life span warning, shrinking, Haki drain and a time limit, stamina and Imu's attention). It ends with a ranking of the prices and a steelman that the prices are not real. The 1198 form is placed on the ladder in one chapter and the Zoan/war argument is left to the 1198 video (11-18 argues what the form means for the war; this one argues the ladder). Opening archetype used: "Everyone believes X, and the chapter says otherwise" (gears are upgrades, Enies Lobby says each is a bill). It differs from A, which opens cold on the moment itself.
