## 3. Patterns
- Titles: ALL CAPS claim, "?!", named entity (Luffy, Nika, Imu, Dragon, Shanks) plus the chapter number, 1-2 emoji. Shorts use "X Is ACTUALLY Y?! 😱" plus 3 hashtags.
- Length: 8-11 min for chapter-reaction or spoiler videos. 16-18 min at the large channels. Deep dives of 27 min to 1h+ work only for Syv, who has 97K subs.
- Timing: small channels upload the spoiler video within 1-3 days of the raw scans, and 3-7 videos per chapter cycle (Updated Weeb, EneruReview, Syv). 1199 spoilers are out now.
- Live content: Syv's 1h+ "Parcast" gets 23-35K, below his normal 50-100K. Small streams are tiny (ZoroFanboy 560, Dak's Sake 2.5h 6.8K).
- Collab: Syv and Zhoniin did a joint 1199 predictions video (24K). Subs effect unverified.
- Playlists, pinned comments, community posts and Shorts-to-long links: unverified, because I could not see them.

## 4. Subscriber conversion
Only correlational evidence. Chapter-tied long videos on small channels convert at roughly 3 subs per 1,000 views, versus lorevid Shorts at 0.56. Shorts-led Rocks D is at most 1.8. Description CTAs ("share your theories", "subscribe") appear on both faceless channels, but no measured effect.

## 5. Enforcement (real cases)
- Tubefilter, 2026-07-13: YouTube reworded its inauthentic-content rules into three categories. Generic or repetitive content, with templated storylines and little commentary, can be demonetized, and adding your own "spin" avoids it. The article says anime is not mentioned, AI voice is not a category, and the rules themselves are unchanged.
- TorrentFreak, 2026-08-14: a California federal court (Judge Thompson, order 2026-07-17) denied three Korean anime-review channels' motion to quash a Kadokawa DMCA subpoena. The videos used about 15% of an episode with TTS and narration and were found "not sufficiently transformative". Creators appealed. It is an unmasking order, not a merits ruling, and it relies on Barnes v. YouTube (Feb 2026).
- vidIQ, 2026-04-15: Toei has struck recap channels. Narration that only describes the screen is the risk.
- I found NO verified mass termination of faceless anime theory channels in 2026. Claims of 18,000 channels come from unsourced blogs (unverified).
