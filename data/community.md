## One Piece

### Right now (mid-November 2026)
- **Manga**: Chapters 1196, 1197 ("Love Is Defense") and **1198 ("The Island Where Monsters Live" per one source) are out**; 1197 came out around Nov 1 and 1198 around Nov 8 (one source lists 1197 as Nov 1, another Nov 2, so don't state exact hours). The manga is now on a break: **1199 is due Sunday November 22, 2026** (JST Nov 23) and 1200 on Nov 29. Real spoilers for 1199 are expected around Nov 17 — do not write 1199 content before then, and no leaks in scripts. The reason for the break was never officially stated; Fandomwire counts it as Oda's 13th break of the year, and one leaker (Redon, via Dexerto France) guesses it is to line chapter 1200 up with a special 30th-anniversary Jump issue. Treat that as a guess.
- **Chapter 1197 recap**: Sanji's Ivankov lesson pays off — he accepts that protecting himself matters as much as protecting friends, breaks the shaft of Longinus and shatters Killingham's helmet with a direct hit. **Conqueror's Haki is still disputed**: one outlet says advanced Armament plus a Conqueror's awakening, another says the chapter has Sanji never announce one. Say "tease/implied", never "confirmed". One outlet frames it as Zoro = Luffy's sword, Sanji = Luffy's shield (an interpretation, not canon).
- **Chapter 1198 recap (spoiler-based, some details uncertain)**: Imu reaches a **third form** (elongated torso, large horns, long neck; reportedly costly and not sustainable for long). **Loki** shows a hybrid Zoan form of the Dragon-Dragon Fruit (Nidhogg) and is **beaten by Imu in one blow**. Imu says he must return to Mary Geoise or his life is in danger, and goes after Luffy. Luffy, already struggling to keep Gear 5 going, vanishes from the fight and reappears on an Elbaph rooftop in a **new dark, ape-like form with tail, fangs and a monkey-like nose**, then bites Zaza (the rain goddess) and she explodes. **The chapter does not name the form** — "Gear 6" is only a fan/YouTube label. Sanji also shatters Killingham's armor. One French outlet reports a hint that ancient technology (Uranus-type, uncertain) may be in Elbaph and tied to Imu's push to enslave giants.
- **Top theories (all unconfirmed)**: (1) a new stage or awakening of Gear 5 / the Nika fruit; (2) **Sun Wukong / Monkey King** (Luffy's "Monkey" name, Journey to the West; Ei Nerd, Fandomwire and a YouTuber lean on it); (3) Hanuman (tied by one fan theory to Dragon's wind link); (4) Luffy lost control of the fruit near death and it acted on its own — the "Nika taking over" reading; (5) Momotaro (1198 as a nod, with Imu as the oni) — a minor theory. **Dragon**: Fandomwire runs an "Oda sets up return of Dragon" piece and a YouTuber's title claims Dragon makes Luffy control the form; I could not open the article, so what exactly was set up for Dragon is **uncertain**. **"Nerona Imu"** (the Void-Century Saint Imu Nerona, per Ivankov in ch. 1086) is a long-running theory; nothing found says 1198 confirms it.
- **Anime**: Confirmed still on break. Episode 1180 aired September 27, 2026; the official account says the next episode airs **in 2027**, schedule to be announced. Toei caps the series at 26 episodes a year. **No date given** — one French outlet says spring 2027 but no official source found. Do not write "this week's episode" Shorts. Also 2027: **ONE PIECE FILM GOD VALLEY (Summer 2027)** and Netflix's Wit Studio animated remake **The One Piece** (all seven episodes worldwide, February 2027).
- **Netflix live-action**: Season 2 premiered March 10, 2026; Season 3 was renewed in August 2025 and has entered production (Alabasta arc expected, reported not official; Xolo Maridueña as Ace, Cole Escola as Bon Clay). No official release date; a mid-2027 window is only reported. Newest result found was April 2026, so there may be later news.
- **Community mood**: 1198 is the loudest chapter in weeks — "what is that form" dominates, with a "Gear 6?!" framing in video titles. Sanji Conqueror's Haki is still a split read. Imu's third form and Loki's one-hit loss pull both hype and "Loki got wasted" complaints (uncertain, from titles only). "Imu fatigue" is less of an issue when the angle is the new form or the cost of Imu's third form.

## What fans reject (avoid all of these)
From web/Reddit discussion about the state of these fandoms on YouTube:
- **AI-voice Shorts with broken grammar** over random clips. Our voice is synthetic, so the writing must be flawless and natural to compensate.
- **Clickbait that doesn't pay off**: a title that promises "confirmed" when it's a theory (e.g., stating Sanji "has" Conqueror's Haki or Zoro "killed" Sommers when the chapter only implies it).
- **Lazy What Ifs** with no cause and effect ("dumb what-ifs"). A What If must follow the story's own rules.
- **Recycled theories stated as new** (Blackbeard three souls, Shanks' twin) without a fresh piece of evidence.
- **Wrong terms or facts**: calling Supreme King Haki the wrong thing, wrong chapter numbers, wrong names. Fans correct them in the comments, and it kills trust.
- **Overdone formats fans are visibly tired of**: another generic "how strong is Imu" video with no new angle; Boruto romance-subplot content played straight instead of acknowledging the fatigue around it.
