# Black Flash

Source: https://jujutsu-kaisen.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Flash (fetched 2026-10-26). Bracketed [Ch. N] / [Ep. N] are the wiki's own citations.

Black Flash is a phenomenon in jujutsu that greatly amplifies the user's physical strike when cursed energy is applied to it within one-millionth of a second.

### Description
Black Flash is a distortion in space that occurs when cursed energy is applied within 0.000001 seconds (1 microsecond) of a physical hit. When a jujutsu sorcerer is able to achieve this, their cursed energy flashes black, and the destructive power of their strike is equal to a normal hit to the power of 2.5. [Ch. 48, Ep. 19]
Using Black Flash requires incredible concentration, and no sorcerer is capable of using it at will, not even Satoru Gojo. It does not occur randomly and requires immense skill and combat potential to pull off. While Yuji Itadori is thought to be blessed by the sparks of black due to his potential, the same goes for Mahito because the sparks do not choose who to bless. [Ch. 126, Ep. 44] Gojo speculated that more conditions must be met for Black Flash to occur, as he would be able to use it at will if applying cursed energy before a blow was the only requirement. He believed that the blend of one's cursed energy, the opponent's cursed energy, one's physical condition, and even the temperature and humidity could affect whether a Black Flash arises or not at a given moment. [Ch. 256]
According to Aoi Todo, the difference of the ability to understand the essence of cursed energy among those who have used Black Flash and those who have never used it is the difference between heaven and earth. As mentioned by Kento Nanami, it is difficult to unleash twice or on the same day, and regardless of how the user achieved it the first time, whether through skill or simple luck, they enter a state where manipulating their cursed energy becomes something as simple and natural as breathing. It can result in a feeling of omnipotence, as if everything revolves around them, which Nanami compares to being "in the zone", the state of elite focus that athletes experience. [Ch. 50, Ep. 20] This leads to a greatly increased performance in combat, allowing the user to operate at 120% of their maximum potential. [Ch. 128, Ep. 44] In this awakened state, a sorcerer can also draw out and use any dormant cursed techniques engraved within them. [Ch. 257]
After landing two consecutive Black Flashes, a skilled sorcerer can restore their reverse cursed technique output if depleted by constructing a unique reverse cursed technique circuit that distributes its function and burden to areas of the brain different from usual. [Ch. 235] [Ch. 258] [Ch. 264]

### Usage

#### Yuta Okkotsu
In the anime, while fighting Suguru Geto, Yuta Okkotsu accidentally broke his sword by imbuing it with too much cursed energy, so he landed a hard punch on the curse user and hit him with Black Flash.
Yuta's Black Flash (Anime).gif|Yuta punches Geto in the face with Black Flash.

#### Kento Nanami
During the Night Parade of a Hundred Demons, Nanami achieved the record of using Black Flash four times in a row. The first three times, he landed devastating punches that obliterated several cursed spirits at once, and the fourth time, he used his Ratio Technique in combination with Black Flash to slice a giant curse in half. Nanami held this record for over a year, though he accredits it to getting lucky, and he does not believe that using Black Flash multiple consecutive times is something to celebrate. [Ch. 50, Ep. 20]

#### Yuji Itadori
During the Kyoto Sister-School Goodwill Event, Todo taught Yuji how to harness his cursed energy by timing it with his superhuman speed and strength. By explaining how to make his cursed energy flow properly and pitting him against Hanami, Todo was able to help Yuji unleash Black Flash against the special grade cursed spirit. [Ch. 48, Ep. 19] It dealt a great deal of damage to Hanami, and while "in the zone", Yuji used it four more times to completely overpower Hanami, matching Nanami's record of four consecutive Black Flashes. [Ch. 50, Ep. 20]
While fighting two-on-two against Eso and Kechizu, Yuji and Nobara Kugisaki both used Black Flash together to defeat the Cursed Womb: Death Paintings. Yuji's punch destroyed Eso's arm entirely, and so quickly that the incarnated special grade cursed object couldn't even react. This moment proved that Yuji is the one chosen by the sparks of black. [Ch. 61, Ep. 24]
Yuji utilized Black Flash multiple times in his and Todo's final battle with Mahito. He capitalized on Todo's ability to swap places with others using Boogie Woogie to land direct hits on the special grade curse's soul. [Ch. 127, Ep. 44] [Ch. 130, Ep. 45] Although Black Flash cannot be used on command, Yuji's performance during the fight made it seem to Mahito like he could. To avoid getting killed, he partially deactivated Instant Spirit Body of Distorted Killing to trick Yuji into attacking his vulnerable area so he could counter and strike at his neck. However, Yuji's Divergent Fist and Todo's ruse disrupted Mahito's plan, leaving him open to a finishing Black Flash imbued with Yuji's maximum cursed energy that broke through his defenses. [Ch. 132, Ep. 45]
During the raid against Sukuna, Larue distracted the King of Curses with Cutie Honey. This gave Yuji the opportunity to use Black Flash against the special grade jujutsu sorcerer, which also damaged the building behind them. [Ch. 256] Yuji proceeded to use it on him six more successive times, surpassing Nanami's previous record of four. [Ch. 257] Later, using the distraction provided by Todo's Boogie Woogie, Yuji landed his eighth Black Flash on Sukuna's chest. [Ch. 260] In the duel's climax, he landed his ninth and final Black Flash after the King of Curses was weakened by an unexpected Resonance from Nobara and fell victim to Yuji's Divergent Fist, ending the fight. [Ch. 267]
On January 14, 2087, Yuji used Black Flash against Osuki after blocking his axe. The amplified force behind the punch sent the Simurian pinballing through the street, ultimately crashing into the ground with enough force to create piles of debris behind him. [Ch. 20]

#### Nobara Kugisaki
While fighting Eso and Kechizu, Nobara and Yuji landed a dual blow of Black Flashes to the curse-human hybrids. Nobara used it in conjunction with her Straw Doll Technique to blast a nail through Kechizu's head before finishing him off with Hairpin. [Ch. 61, Ep. 24]

#### Mahito
After emotionally breaking Yuji by leaving Nobara on the brink of death using Idle Transfiguration, Mahito caught Yuji off-guard with Black Flash, launching him across Shibuya Station and overwhelming him with numerous attacks. [Ch. 126, Ep. 44] After using a 0.2-second Self-Embodiment of Perfection against Todo, he landed another Black Flash against the sorcerer and injured his abdomen. [Ch. 130, Ep. 45]

#### Aoi Todo
During his and Yuji's battle with Mahito, Todo succeeded in unleashing Black Flash by kicking Mahito, not wanting to be left behind by Yuji's growth. However, this was unable to do any lasting damage to the cursed spirit due to his soul being unaffected by the attack. [Ch. 128, Ep. 44]

#### Satoru Gojo
Gojo had managed to perform Black Flash for the first time sometime before the 2018 Kyoto Sister-School Goodwill Event. Despite his overwhelming power and proficiency with cursed energy manipulation, even he is unable to use it at will. While he has not beat Nanami's record of four consecutive Black Flashes, he has landed more Black Flashes in total. [Ch. 256]
While clashing with Sukuna, Gojo wounded the King of Curses with a devastating Black Flash when he least expected it. [Ch. 232] He went to on use it a second time against Merged Beast Agito and punched straight through its stomach, [Ch. 234] Gojo attacked Eight-Handled Sword Divergent Sila Divine General Mahoraga twice with Black Flash; the first time, he blasted the shikigami away, and the second time, he sent Mahoraga crashing through a building, even as it blocked the strike with the Sword of Extermination. [Ch. 235]

#### Sukuna
During the Ryomen Sukuna Raid, the King of Curses used Black Flash on Maki Zenin and launched her through a highway's railing. [Ch. 253] Later on, he unleashed it a second time on Larue, crushing the sorcerer against a wall and causing him to spit out blood. [Ch. 255] Sukuna used it once more against Maki, sending her crashing through a window and increasing his output for Dismantle, [Ch. 256] and landed a fourth Black Flash against Choso, who used his Blood Manipulation to reduce the damage. [Ch. 256] He later hit Yuji with a fifth Black Flash. [Ch. 257] While Todo was distracted by Yuta, Sukuna hit him with his sixth Black Flash, breaking his vibraslap. [Ch. 263] After Hana Kurusu's Jacob's Ladder failed to separate him from Megumi Fushiguro, Sukuna struck her with a seventh Black Flash. At the last second, Todo shielded her from the attack, though the impact incapacitated both of them. [Ch. 264]
