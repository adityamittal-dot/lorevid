# Shibuya Incident Arc

Source: https://jujutsu-kaisen.fandom.com/wiki/Shibuya_Incident_Arc (fetched 2026-10-30). Bracketed [Ch. N] / [Ep. N] are the wiki's own citations.

The Shibuya Incident Arc is the sixth arc in Gege Akutami's Jujutsu Kaisen. It follows the entirety of the Shibuya Incident on October 31, 2018, when the alliance between cursed spirits and curse users forged by Mahito and Pseudo-Geto finally makes their move to seal Satoru Gojo.

### Synopsis

#### Evening Festival
Based on suspicions and an investigation built around a mole at Kyoto Jujutsu High, Utahime Iori takes Satoru Gojo's first-years to Kokichi Muta's supposed location. She informs them that Kokichi wasn't chosen due to any specific suspicion, but by the process of elimination.
With his Puppet Manipulation cursed technique used over a wide range, Kokichi could easily steal information and feed it to the enemy curse users. Yuji breaks down the door to his hideout, but he isn't there. The fact that their group was misled is enough proof for Utahime to confirm Kokichi is Jujutsu High's mole.
Meanwhile, Kokichi meets with Mahito and Geto. They forged a Binding Vow where Kokichi agreed to give them information from Jujutsu High in exchange for Mahito using his Idle Transfiguration technique. Kokichi feels the contract was violated because it also stated Mahito's side couldn't harm Kyoto students. Mahito blames that on Hanami and claims he should just kill Kokichi regardless. Geto refuses to allow him to violate the pact and Mahito finally grants Kokichi with a healthy body capable of standing on his own two feet. With the vow fulfilled, Kokichi and Mahito both agree it's time to fight. [Ch. 79, Ep. 30]
Kokichi uses his lair full of Mechamarus to distract Mahito and escape outside. The hideout implodes under Mahito's feet, forcing him outside and pitting him face to face with Mechamaru Mode: Absolute. It's a goliath of an armored puppet that stands several meters taller than Mahito while standing in a lake. Mahito embraces the challenge and ignores the giant puppet's heat waves and physical attacks. With the special grade cursed spirit fully confident that he can't be hurt, Kokichi prepares his secret technique. [Ch. 80, Ep. 31]
Using a mysterious projectile, Kokichi manages to injure Mahito. This leaves the curse vulnerable to Mechamaru's offense. Even Geto acknowledges that his skills and strategy might just be able to exorcise Mahito. Kokichi believes he can achieve his dream of walking amongst the others but he's taken off guard when Mahito suddenly counters by activating his Domain Expansion: Self-Embodiment of Perfection. [Ch. 81, Ep. 31]
Mahito believes he's won after activating the automatic hit of his domain and applying Idle Transfiguration. However, Kokichi survives and Mechamaru stabs into Mahito using another one of Kokichi's secret tubes. He reveals the tubes contain New Shadow Style: Simple Domain, allowing Mechamaru to use the domain and neutralize Mahito's technique. The curse explodes and the domain disappears.
Kokichi believes victory is at hand and turns his attention to Geto, but Mahito suddenly breaks into Mechamaru's cockpit. He attempts to stab Mahito with another tube as the curse crawls into the area. Their hands pass by each others but Kokichi isn't fast enough at close range.
In Kokichi's last moments, Kasumi promises to come to see him one day. Following his death, Mahito reveals to Geto that he allowed Kokichi to believe he'd one in order to sneak up and go for the kill. He's happy to have gotten to have fun and see good techniques like Simple Domain before Shibuya in ten days. Geto adds that their commissioned curtains are ready as well. Just over a week later, October 31, 2018, arrives and the day in Shibuya has finally come. [Ch. 82, Ep. 31]

#### Gate Open

##### Entering Shibuya
On October 31, at 07:00 PM, a large curtain was cast around Shibuya and many sorcerers are called to the scene. Professionals and jujutsu high students are broken into four teams and briefed on the situation. Meanwhile, the civilians trapped inside Shibuya are forced to call for Satoru Gojo. Eventually, their pleads are met and the strongest sorcerer arrives to the scene.
Outside the curtain, at the Aoyama Cemetery, Yuji wants to hurry to back up his teacher, but Mei Mei does not take it that seriously. Soon she receives a call and is informed that another curtain has just appeared at Meiji-Jingumae Station. The team heads out towards their target as Gojo encounters Jogo, Choso, and Hanami at the Fukutoshin Line Platform B5F. [Ch. 83, Ep. 32]
The only viable escape route is blocked by Hanami's roots and many civilians are pushed onto the railway. The curses begin slaughtering the non-sorcerers, wanting to create a blind spot for Satoru. Jogo and Hanami get closer and activate Domain Amplification in order to get past Gojo's infinity. The strongest sorcerer dodges in time, realizing what the spirits' plan is.
Before the incident began, Geto and Jogo surmised that their best chance at beating Gojo is putting him in a crowded location. Having realized the arrogance of the curses, Satoru takes off his blindfold and promises to take Hanami down first, since the plant curse still had not learned his lesson. [Ch. 84, Ep. 32]
The strongest sorcerer approaches the cursed spirits and the battle resumes. Gojo confuses Hanami by only using cursed energy and seizes the opportunity to rip out the spirit's optical roots. Ignoring Choso, Satoru strengthens his Limitless technique and crushes Hanami, exorcising one of the unregistered special grades.
Meanwhile, Mei Mei's team is informed of the new curtain around the Fukutoshin Line Platform. They are briefed on the curtain's restrictive nature and told that there is a possibility of transfigured humans being spotted in between the two curtains. [Ch. 85, Ep. 33]
Mei Mei uses her technique to scout through the Meiji-Jingumae Station, figuring out that whoever activated the curtain is between B1F and B2F. She assumes that Yuji would prefer to battle one strong spirit rather than several transfigured humans, so she sends him to the location of the bigger threat.
As Yuji enters Platform B2F, he comes across a cursed spirit. He realizes that the curse is protecting a commissioned curtain and attacks him after he comments on the taste of humans. The locust boasts about his intelligence, only to be shocked when Yuji easily figures out he's a grasshopper curse. [Ch. 86, Ep. 32]
The cursed spirit's power originated from humanity's resentment towards the grasshopper plague. He quickly fights back against Yuji, still boasting about his intelligence. Despite its physiology, Sukuna's vessel overpowers the grasshopper curse and takes him down thanks to his raw skill. After the commissioned curtain is destroyed, Mahito heads towards Platform B5F. [Ch. 87, Ep. 32]

##### Satoru Gojo's All-or-Nothing Gamble!
Jogo tries fighting Gojo on his own, but he can't do much. Choso's attempts to help are also largely in vain. As Geto and Dagon watch on, Mei Mei's team runs through B4F. They encounter a transfigured human and come to a terrifying realization.
A train arrives at Platform B5F. Civilians rush towards it, but the vehicle is filled to the brim with transfigured humans who burst out and begin slaughtering the helpless non-sorcerer hostages. Mahito exits the train last, excited about the air full of fear. [Ch. 88, Ep. 33]
The human curse joins the battle, using Body Repel alongside Choso's Supernova to crush and kill a large number of people. Jogo feints an attack, knowing that Gojo's personal limit is to avoid killing civilians with his own hands, even if he's willing to let some die because of the curses.
Contrary to the spirits' expectations, Satoru activates his Domain Expansion: Unlimited Void. He did so for a mere 0.2 seconds, just enough to briefly incapacitate every being on the platform. Within the next 299 seconds, the strongest sorcerer alive had annihilated all the transfigured humans that were released on Platform B5F. Immediately after, Geto activates the Prison Realm. [Ch. 89, Ep. 33]
Prior to the Shibuya Incident, Geto had explained the conditions of the Prison Realm to the unregistered special grades. Among these was that Gojo needed to be within a four-meter radius of the item for one minute upon activation. The loophole was that the one minute had to last inside Satoru's brain.
The curse user made use of that fact by walking out in the open, letting Satoru see him. As memories flooded the sorcerer's mind, he is trapped in the Prison Realm. Gojo recognizes whoever is standing in front of him as an imposter. Pseudo-Geto reveals his brain and explains that he coveted Cursed Spirit Manipulation and that Suguru's body being left intact helped him. Meanwhile, one of Mechamaru's smaller puppets tries to contact Yuji. [Ch. 90, Ep. 33]
