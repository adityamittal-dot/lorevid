# Kinji Hakari

Source: https://jujutsu-kaisen.fandom.com/wiki/Kinji_Hakari (fetched 2026-10-31). Bracketed [Ch. N] / [Ep. N] are the wiki's own citations.

Kinji Hakari is a supporting character in the Jujutsu Kaisen series. He is a third-year student at Tokyo Jujutsu High who was suspended for clashing with authority. While away from the school, Kinji is running the Gachinko Fight Club, a venue for hand-to-hand combat between sorcerers for money.

### Appearance
Hakari is a tall young man with tan skin and a muscular build. He has small magenta-colored eyes with thin slitted eyebrows and a thin mustache made of stubble. While at Jujutsu High, Hakari had black hair styled into short twisted locks with a fade below his brow line. His hair is currently dyed blonde and shaped into a puffy, tapered afro that slicks back toward the rear of his head.
As the boss of Gachinko Fight Club, Hakari dressed the part of a bookie. He sported a white tank-top beneath his dark fur coat, along with gray pants and light-colored boots. After agreeing to combat the Culling Game, Hakari sports a Jujutsu High uniform jacket with the same jeans and boots. Unlike Yuji Itadori's hooded jacket, Hakari's uniform hood is entirely connected and the same color as the rest of his jacket.

### Personality
Hakari is a self-absorbed individual who cares little for the rules and regulations of Jujutsu High. Yuta Okkotsu describes him as moody, [Ch. 146, Ep. 50] and most students around his age consider him a good-for-nothing. [Ch. 153, Ep. 52]
Hakari enjoys watching people become immersed in their passions, which he describes as their "fever" burning hot. He is clever and channels his own fever into manipulating others. Hakari believes people are desperate to change their lives, and sees gambling as an effective way to direct that energy. He claims that no one is without fever, and his ultimate goal is to manipulate the collective fever of Japan through his fight club.
According to Kirara Hoshi, Hakari's fever burns hottest when he's working as a jujutsu sorcerer. However, his attitude and unconventional cursed technique do not align well with the higher-ups. He was suspended for assaulting a conservative official and chose to operate outside institutional rules. Hakari is willing to take advantage of the public revelation of curses to promote his fight club, aiming to have it officially recognized by jujutsu authorities as regulations evolve. [Ch. 155, Ep. 52]
Hakari only puts his life on the line for another sorcerer if they demonstrate a genuine fever. Upon meeting Yuji, Hakari initially sensed a weak fever, believing Yuji's presence was due to others' recommendations. Yuji explained that he considers himself a cog in the system of jujutsu sorcerers, which Hakari found uninteresting. However, Yuji ultimately impressed him, showing that even a cog could burn with passion. Hakari agreed to cooperate with Yuji and his allies, believing that the fever never lies. [Ch. 157, Ep. 53]
Despite his brash demeanor, Hakari is deeply appreciative of those who supported him. He was visibly upset upon learning that Masamichi Yaga had died and Satoru Gojo was sealed, stating he hadn't felt that down since Yakul was shot with an arrow. Yuji, Megumi Fushiguro, and Panda found the comment unusual, interpreting it as a sign that Hakari lives a relatively calm life.
Hakari agreed to assist Megumi and the others, clarifying that it was not a personal favor but a formal arrangement. When Panda asked about his motives, and Megumi interjected with a guess, Hakari was annoyed. While he felt bad about Yaga and Gojo, he was especially surprised to learn that Megumi was the head of the Zenin Clan. Almost immediately, Hakari softened his attitude and proposed becoming close allies, hoping to secure influence within the Big Three Families. [Ch. 157, Ep. 53]
Hakari often appears selfish and rude, but he respects others' passions and aspirations. When he met Charles Bernard, a potential enemy, he was willing to listen to him express frustration over the Culling Game. Hakari claimed he only joined Charles on a ferris wheel to scout the area for a date location, masking his curiosity. While Charles believed his dreams were futile, Hakari provoked him by mocking his passion, understanding that Charles needed a release for his anger. [Ch. 181] After their battle, Hakari checked on him and asked to read his manga. He encouraged Charles to keep pursuing his dreams once the Culling Game ended. [Ch. 190]
Hakari doesn't actively seek conflict, but he thrives in dangerous situations and enjoys betting on himself in combat. This was evident in his life-or-death fight against Hajime Kashimo, where both exchanged taunts and compliments mid-battle. Hakari smiled frequently, even while being pushed to the brink, and praised Kashimo's strength. [Ch. 188] Although victorious, Hakari stated he didn't feel like the winner, and ultimately made a deal with Kashimo to form an alliance, offering him what he most desired. [Ch. 190]
Hakari proves to be a reliable ally, cooperating effectively with fellow students and honoring agreements. He was deeply involved in planning the raid on Makyo Shinjuku and expressed concern for Yuta and the use of Gojo’s body. [Ch. 261] Hakari also played the role of mediator, de-escalating a potential clash between Yuta and Kashimo. He reminded Yuta to trust the plan and successfully persuaded Kashimo to wait and fight after Gojo. [Ch. 234] [Ch. 244] Staying true to his word, Hakari battled Uraume to allow Kashimo a chance at fighting Sukuna. [Ch. 237] He expressed admiration for Yuta's courage and reliability, which further ignited his own fever. When Maki Zenin criticized Yuta's actions during the raid, Hakari defended him. He maintained unwavering faith in his comrades during their clash with the strongest sorcerer in history and regretted being unable to join them in the final moments. [Ch. 245] [Ch. 252] [Ch. 269]

### Abilities and Powers

#### Overview
Overall Skill Level: Hakari is widely recognized as one of the most powerful students at Tokyo Jujutsu High. Gojo, the undisputed strongest sorcerer of the modern day, believed that Hakari and Yuta Okkotsu had the potential to rival him. [Ch. 11, Ep. 6] Before Yuji ever met Hakari, he heard from his senpai that Hakari's abilities were exceptional. Yuta himself said that Hakari was the stronger of the two of them when he's worked up. [Ch. 146, Ep. 50]
Hakari is not only an elite close-quarters combatant who possesses serrated cursed energy but also an extraordinary jujutsu specialist armed with a dynamic domain. He distinguished himself in the Culling Game by defeating Kashimo, not only one of the strongest players in the death game, but one of the most powerful sorcerers Kenjaku ever encountered in 1,000 years. [Ch. 187]
Hakari's domain is unique and his luck is reliable, making for a combination capable of opposing any threat. Charles could see briefly into Hakari's future, but was instantly bested as soon as Kinji hit the jackpot of his domain. Kashimo nearly killed Hakari on several exchanges but he was able to repeatedly open his domain and successfully hit the jackpot to enter his unkillable mode. Hakari's strategic thinking and expert battle sense ultimately complemented his techniques and led to victory over Kashimo. [Ch. 190]
Hakari was chosen to fight Sukuna's closest ally, Uraume, during the raid in Makyo Shinjuku. The Jujutsu High sorcerers planned extensively for the raid and everyone was confident enough to leave the ancient ice sorcerer to Kinji. Uraume was a powerful ancient sorcerer who was forced to go all out after realizing Kinji wasn't afraid of embracing his sorcery at the cost of his humanity. Kinji never slowed down his attacks despite having his limbs frozen and destroyed. He never hesitated to counterattack and surprise his opponent with instantly regrown limbs. While Kinji was fighting Uraume in his unkillable mode, his regeneration exceeded the speed Gojo and Sukuna displayed during their showdown. [Ch. 245] While Hakari wasn't able to overcome Uraume before the raid ended, they fought an extensive, high-level jujutsu battle, and Hakari survived in the end. [Ch. 268]

#### Physical Prowess
Master Hand-to-Hand-Combatant: Hakari is an exceptionally capable hand-to-hand combatant, able to apply life-threatening pressure to Yuji, a very proficient taijutsu specialist. Yuji stopped resisting in order to win Hakari's approval and ended up suffering several blows that dealt serious damage to the first year. [Ch. 157, Ep. 53]
While his fighting style is very direct, Hakari isn't just a brawler. He's a skillful martial artist with a wide range of physical attacks in his arsenal. He uses combinations of punches, kicks, knees, and elbows among other grappling moves and strikes to take out his opponent with maximum force.
Hakari's pure hand-to-hand combat skills were enough to put pressure on Charles even while the latter could see into his future. He was so confident in his abilities that Hakari began their fight with his hands in his pockets while only defending himself with kicks. Charles acknowledged that not for his cursed technique, Hakari would've defeated him right away with physical prowess alone. [Ch. 182]
The full extent of Hakari's close combat prowess was on display against Kashimo, an incarnated sorcerer who slayed forty Culling Game players. While Hakari's base state can't challenge Kashimo's mastery of martial arts [Ch. 187], his close combat abilities shine the most when he's amped up by his domain's jackpot. Fueled with limitless cursed energy, Hakari supercharged his physical attacks and overpowered Kashimo in several exchanges. [Ch. 186] [Ch. 188] Up against cursed spirits, Kinji effortlessly tore a swarm of them apart with his bare hands. [Ch. 189]

#### Intelligence
High Tactical Intellect: Hakari doesn't present himself as a fighter with a plan because he's fearless and straightforward. That being said, Hakari is an experienced sorcerer with a high strategic battle sense and a knack for reading his opponents. Upon learning about Charles' ability to see the future, Hakari immediately attacked to confirm how it works and then quickly came up with the countermeasure. He knew Charles needed to see his target to use this ability and decided to attack Charles's blind spots with relentless pressure. Hakari also predicted that Charles would counter by attacking his own blind spots and acted accordingly by parrying his attempts. [Ch. 182] Even inside his domain, Hakari knew Charles still had a slight advantage and kept the pressure on to prevent him from focusing on the future. [Ch. 183]
While he is a relatively cerebral fighter, Hakari tends to recklessly abandon tactics when he's in his unkillable mode. He allowed himself to be hit by Kashimo during a surge of cursed energy because the assault barely hurt his reinforced body. [Ch. 186] Despite the lack of caution, Hakari's recklessness does often work in his favor. Immediately after his arm was dismembered by Kashimo's lightning, Kinji continued his own offense because he knew the unkillable mode would regenerate his arm. This took Kashimo off guard and allowed Hakari to retake the momentum of their duel. [Ch. 186] Hakari's unkillable mode is rooted in a fully automatic reverse cursed technique but he doesn't overly rely on the involuntary healing factor and knows how to best make use of it. When Kashimo attempted to destroy his head in one shot, Hakari voluntarily ejected his opponent's cursed energy while allowing his reverse cursed technique to heal him while he breaks. [Ch. 188]
After being hit by Kashimo's electric discharge twice, Hakari figured out there must be a trick to the attack and assumed his opponent couldn't launch it again. Acting on this assumption, Kinji decided to recklessly go full force on his offense and attempted to defeat Kashimo within the eight seconds before his unkillable state ceased. This backfired because Kashimo developed a loophole for his attack that Hakari was unaware of, nearly costing the latter his life. [Ch. 188] However, on the verge of death, Hakari was able to remain focused and did the only thing he could in order to survive, using all of his strength to bank on expanding his domain, a gamble that ultimately paid off. [Ch. 188] [Ch. 189]
As Hakari's fight with Kashimo began to draw out, he switched his approach from a straight-on brawl to a more tactical one. He shifted his domain over the ocean before the barrier dispelled and dropped Kashimo into it, correctly assuming that the water would forcibly draw out his electrified cursed energy. [Ch. 189] While fighting in the ocean, Hakari carefully considered his options of either returning to the surface or using his last five seconds of immortality to finish the fight. Kinji recklessly decided to try to finish Kashimo off, only to end up on the receiving end of a steam explosion. [Ch. 189] He survived thanks to quick and clever thinking, using a last-second binding vow to sacrifice his left arm so the rest of him would be unharmed. Hakari knew Kashimo grabbed onto his arm before initiating the explosion in case he survived, which would've made Hakari unable to make the hand signs to cast his domain again. With this information, Hakari wisely decided to sacrifice his arm entirely and used the cursed energy reinforcing it to protect the rest of his body. As displayed at the end of their battle, Hakari's cleverness and forward thinking are what allowed him to best Kashimo in the end. [Ch. 190]

#### Intangible
Immense Luck: While Hakari is an extremely gifted sorcerer, more so than any of his skills, he's extraordinarily lucky. He's as fortunate as he is talented and can comfortably rely on this to win fights. Hakari's domain is based on drawing a jackpot with only a 1/239 chance of doing so, yet he claims to have never needed to spin more than 30 times. [Ch. 183] It only took him two spins to hit the jackpot against Charles [Ch. 183] and he hit it twice in a row on the first try against Kashimo. [Ch. 187] The second time, Hakari was mere moments from death and needed an immediate jackpot in order to heal himself. His good fortune pulled through and even Kashimo was forced to admit that Hakari truly has incredible luck. [Ch. 189]

#### Cursed Energy
Cursed Energy Trait: Hakari's cursed energy apparently has a much rougher texture to it than others. He was told by Gojo that his cursed energy has an "edge", giving his blunt attacks a sharp cutting effect. Regardless of how much or how little force Hakari puts behind a blow, his opponent will be struck with a sharp pain regardless. Yuji compares it to being hit with a serrated bat [Ch. 157, Ep. 53] and Charles likened it to sandpaper. [Ch. 182]
Cursed Energy Reinforcement: Hakari is very adept at reinforcing his physical attacks with cursed energy, allowing him to deliver devastating blows. While being filled with unlimited cursed energy, a single cursed energy-reinforced strike from Hakari could leave dents in large shipping containers and send them flying, either by punching his opponents into them or hitting them himself. [Ch. 186] [Ch. 188] Additionally, Hakari is also proficient in using cursed energy defensively to reinforce his physical durability. When confronted with a steam explosion, Hakari made a spur-of-the-moment binding vow that shifted the cursed energy protecting his left arm to the rest of his body, sacrificing the arm but bolstering the rest of his body to survive the explosion.
