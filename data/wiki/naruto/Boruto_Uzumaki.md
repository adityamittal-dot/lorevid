# Boruto Uzumaki

Source: https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Boruto_Uzumaki (fetched 2026-10-16). Bracketed [Ch. N] / [Ep. N] are the wiki's own citations.

is a missing-nin from Konohagakure's Uzumaki clan and Hyūga clan through his parents. After being marked by Momoshiki Ōtsutsuki, and meeting Kawaki, who shared a similar mark, the boy was adopted into Boruto's family, with hopes of removing the marks. However, Boruto was later forced to defect from the village when he was framed for killing his parents after the world's memories of him were altered. [Boruto Ch. 80] However, despite losing everything, he remains determined to prove his innocence and regain everything taken from him.

### Background
Boruto is the first child of Naruto and Hinata Uzumaki, born four years after the Fourth Shinobi World War. He received a younger sister, Himawari, two years later. As his parents continued to stay close to their childhood friends over the years, Boruto ultimately came to know the various parents' respective kids well.
On the day that Naruto was to be inaugurated as the Seventh Hokage, Himawari wanted to bring her panda toy to the ceremony, but Boruto, fearing that he would end up carrying it, tried to take it away from her. When the toy's head was ripped off in the ensuing tug-of-war, Himawari awakened her Byakugan and attacked Boruto in her anger, despite his vehement apologies. Naruto, sensing her killing intent, arrived and shielded Boruto, and was knocked out in the process due to the attack landing on one of his chakra points. Fearful of what an attack that could knock-out his father would do to him, Boruto tried to run from Himawari, although she easily located him hiding in a closet. From then on, he vowed never to anger her again.
Soon after, Naruto's important new job left little time for him to be with his family, angering Boruto. He would begin pulling various pranks about the village, all for the goal of getting his father's attention.

### Personality
Boruto is loud, energetic, and stubborn not unlike his father; even possessing a verbal tic, , similar to Naruto's "dattebayo" and his paternal grandmother's . But compared to his father, Naruto at his age, Boruto is more refined and crafty, seemingly knowing all the shortcuts, as well as more mature in knowing how the world works. Between the two, he is more sarcastic. Unlike Naruto, Boruto doesn't yell what he feels, which is the biggest difference. Despite his laid-back attitude, he is very diligent. [Boruto Ep. 5] Conversely, Boruto's upbringing has also been different than that of his father's: Boruto's family is always there for him, and he likewise has many friends, and his acquisitions came easily and naturally to him from a combination of prodigious talents and nurturing parents. In complete contrast, Naruto grew up without parents or friends, never having any talent as a shinobi and had to work harder than others to earn respect.
For these reasons, Boruto has a high opinion of himself and freely brags about his abilities. Still, his arrogance has made him put no value in teammates or teamwork, believing he can do anything on his own. He felt in achieving success as the main point of anything, even taking insincere shortcuts to do so, such as utilising a cheat program to win a video game easily, or using the Kote to create a façade of performing an advanced technique to earn his right to be Sasuke's disciple, and later swiftly resorting to the same device to defeat opponents once they seem to have an upper hand. After seeing how selfish and amoral he was acting from resorting to such a tool, he gained a newfound respect for actual hard work. At the same time, he also earned a strong dislike at anyone relying on such advanced tools, believing that one should rely solely on their natural skill. Upon meeting Ao, however, he came to appreciate that tools, no matter how advanced, are not inherently good or bad, but merely a means to a goal that depends on its user. Also, upon seeing how such inventions could be used even small animals, he came to appreciate Katasuke Tōno's work.
Despite Boruto's arrogance, he does not look down on anyone else and is very loyal to his friends. Without hesitation, he had repeatedly come to the aid of Denki Kaminarimon when he was physically harmed. He also avoids confrontation, or name-calling when someone is talking behind his back, completely ignoring the situation altogether unless his friends get hurt, which shows his maturity. [Boruto Ep. 2] Even when a friend was revealed to be an enemy, he remained determined to help bring them back from their dark path. He loves his mother and sister deeply — becoming indignant when either is hurt physically or emotionally. Despite loving his little sister, Boruto is terrified of her and promised himself he would never upset her again. Both Sasuke and Naruto commented that Boruto resembled each other when they were younger, and he had indeed displayed characteristics of both shinobi when they were younger, causing Naruto to state that his son is something entirely different from both of them. For example, he displayed Naruto's mischievous habits to get attention and Sasuke's desire for power even by using shortcuts. Similar to that of their parents, Boruto shares a complicated relationship with Sasuke's daughter, Sarada. The two are often at odds, with the young Uchiha valuing the Hokage post while Boruto was showing utter disdain for it. Despite that, Boruto cares for Sarada and her dream, as witnessed during the Chūnin Exams, when he voiced his desire to win to not diminish Sarada's progress to becoming Hokage. Three years after leaving the village, Boruto is quite happy to reunite with Sarada and even seemed somewhat flustered by her impulsively hugging him. Though he quickly leaves due to still being branded a traitor, Boruto swears to rescue her father and eventually return to her.
Overall, Boruto's unwavering loyalty to his friends have continually proven to be a strong influence to all around him, many even noting that he has become the foundation for his friends as he is determined to keep his friends together. Boruto is also against sacrificing other people to ensure his own survival, as when Kawaki suggested him to implant Kāma onto someone to create his own vessel, Boruto was against the idea, even when Kawaki considered using Code, a fanatical worshipper of Isshiki Ōtsutsuki and a definite enemy to Boruto.
Although Boruto loves his father, Naruto and is overjoyed when they spend time together, their relationship has been strained ever since Naruto became the Seventh Hokage. Because of the job's many responsibilities that prevent Naruto from being around all the time, it has led Boruto feeling neglected and becoming resentful toward the Hokage title, only seeing it as a position that takes his father away from him and declaring it, aware that Naruto grew up as an orphan, as only fit for those with no loved ones. Eventually, after Boruto is exposed to the complications of his father's life, he finally understands and forgives his father's busy schedule and stops trying to take the easy options in life.
While initially only wanting to know about his father's past so as to surpass his father, Boruto becomes genuinely curious about Naruto's history, as he asks his father about his past, particularly about how he has come to become one of the strongest shinobi ever to live, despite being seemingly utterly hopeless in everything else. From his battle against Momoshiki Ōtsutsuki, Boruto ultimately came to see his father in a new light, both admiring his might as a shinobi yet still having such an equally selfless nature to protect the village. Afterward, he and Naruto were able to find common grounds and have a healthy relationship again, with Naruto agreeing to show up more often in person while Boruto stopped demanding his father's time altogether, both openly pushing the other forward in their goals. Despite having let go of his anger towards Naruto and his disdain for the Kage title, even coming to respect his father and his great might as Hokage, Boruto plainly states that he has no intention to become Hokage. He opts to walk his own path instead, becoming a support for the Hokage instead, like how his master is. Boruto also has a remarkable will, able to endure surreal experiences with optimism and actually feel for the people who caused them when he knows there was no true malice behind it, such as his ordeal with Ada and Kawaki rewrote his history in everyone's memories. Momoshiki's attempts to demoralise him over this were also unsuccessful.
Boruto likes burgers where he would rush to get a limited-edition burger, on the last day it would be sold, and would sometimes carry burger discount coupons. He notes that his favourite dish is a yakisoba bun. [Boruto Ep. 4] Boruto also has a love for spicy food, having a greater tolerance for the flavour than his friends, whom usually become sickened from it. He also appears to enjoy drawing a lot, while seemingly oblivious to his poor skill for it. [Boruto Ep. 7] Despite this, Sai noted Boruto had the potential to use Super Beast Scroll due to his passion for drawing. Boruto appears to be influenced by the famous movie character, Kagemasa, as after obtaining his Jōgan, he for a while mimicked the hero's attitude while wearing a replica of the character's visor.
During the three years that Boruto remained away from Konoha, his personality matured drastically; he became calm, nonchalant, focused and decisive, refusing to let anything or anyone rile him up, and rarely ever smiles. Even when he was locked up and under threat of torture and execution, Boruto is still able to maintain his composure. However, despite the hardships he has endured for three years, he still remains loyal to his loved ones, eager to protect them and being quite happy to see Sarada and Himawari again, showing that his kind-heartedness hasn't entirely vanished. He even desires to peacefully reconcile with Kawaki as he still viewed the boy as his brother, though he is more than willing to defend himself should Kawaki attack him.
Having narrowly escaped death at Jura's hands, Boruto grew so desperate to defeat the God Trees, he proposed a deal to Momoshiki. If Momoshiki provided his full support in utilizing the Karma against Jura, he'll willingly hand over his body to him. However, Boruto makes it clear that this agreement will only be honored on the condition they destroy all the God Trees first. Though due to their thoughts melding together, Momoshiki deduces that Boruto is banking on Kawaki killing the both of them first.

Boruto is a fair-skinned boy of lean, well-built frame [Boruto Ch. 68] and short height. He has spiky, blond hair, and blue eyes, which according to Sarada, are brighter than Naruto's. Influenced from his Jinchūriki father, Boruto and two whisker markings on each of his cheeks. Overall, he bears a great resemblance to his father. At age 5, Boruto wore a black tracksuit that had a red fire symbol on the left breast, a symbol that resembled a bolt on the back, and dark pink stripes along the sides of his pants. Early in his days as an Academy student, his outfit is quite similar to his previous attire, with additional dark pink stripes on the jacket. Beneath this jacket — which he leaves unzipped — he wears a white T-shirt and a bolt tied to a string around his neck. He wears a longer jacket by the time he's a genin and a belt in the manga, but his clothing is otherwise the same, except the addition of a forehead protector and a dark pink Uzumaki Clan symbol on the back instead of the bolt.

Boruto also briefly wore his father's original orange jacket, along with Sasuke's scratched forehead protector. After Momoshiki makes contact with his arm, Boruto gains the Kāma, a black diamond-shape seal, on his right palm. [Boruto Ch. 14] While staying in the past, Boruto wore a red vest, grey long sleeve, and long shorts as a disguise. In the anime, at Sasuke's order, Boruto began wearing a fingerless glove on his right hand to conceal the Kāma.
After the defeat of Isshiki Ōtsutsuki, Boruto resumed wearing Sasuke's forehead protector. He would wear his old clothes again when he was an Academy student. When Momoshiki used the Kāma's remaining data to reconstruct Boruto's chest cavity after he was mortally wounded by Kawaki, Boruto gained a large scar resembling a black geometric Kāma pattern on his chest. [Boruto Ch. 67] Boruto's right eye was later slashed by Kawaki, leaving a scar extending diagonally from just above his eyebrow, a small corneal abrasion over the pupil, [Boruto Ch. 78] to his cheek, intersecting his top whisker marking. The attack resulted in Boruto's eye going blind and damaged the right side of Sasuke's forehead protector, leaving a matching cut that crosses the existing cut created by Naruto. [Boruto Ch. 79]
One year later after his departure from Konoha, he became noticeably taller and his hair became long with bangs covering the left side of his face. He began wearing a white button-downed shirt, black pants, black shin-high sandals, a black jacket with a purple ends on the sleeve, and black cloak. His shirt is half buttoned, leaving his chest exposed and the shirt cuffs unbuttoned and folded up. On his pants, he wears two beige belts with six silver studs on the top one while the lower one has silver chain hanging off it. His jacket has lapel flaps and the sleeves are folded up to his elbows. After his master was imprisoned, Boruto began carrying Sasuke's sword.
Two years later, his hair was trimmed, but became unruly. He is also noted to have become very handsome. [Boruto Two Blue Vortex Ch. 34] On his blazer, he has three silver pins on his right lapel in the shape of a hexagon, a rectangle, and the sun respectively, and another pin on his left lapel shaped like the Konoha symbol. He also wears a second necklace with an Uzumaki symbol pendant. His cloak has purple on the inside and has open collars folded downward. On his left arm, he wears a simple beige bracelet and a black, fingerless glove.
After battling the Human God Trees in the Land of Wind, he began wearing a third necklace resembling a Flying Thunder God Kunai and donned a new dress shirt and exchanged his blazer for a black funnel collar zip-up track jacket with front pockets and pink lining and rolled up pink cuffs, a bolt design on the left chest, and his pins arranged to the right of the zipper.
By age 16, he wears an outfit similar to his genin days, but with a beige belt that's flap hangs loosely, black pants reaching above his ankles with red lining on the ends, regular shinobi sandals, and thicker red lining around the ends of the jacket sleeves. He also continues to wear a black glove on his left hand, black cloak, and Uzumaki symbol necklace. He also resumes wearing Sasuke's forehead protector.

### Abilities
Stemming from his Uzumaki-Hyūga heritage, Boruto is regarded as an elite genin who can accomplish anything in short periods of time, [Boruto: Two Blue Vortex Ch. 5] and noted as having the potential to surpass his father. [Boruto Ch. 6] A prodigy who inherited his paternal grandfather's genius and his father's unpredictability, [Boruto Ep. 50] [Boruto Ep. 168] his skills were evaluated as second highest on his first day of Academy classes. [Boruto Ep. 2] By the Genin Exams, despite having often been lazy in his studies and training, he was recognised by the Sixth Hokage as having chūnin-level prowess and being well-above the rest of his generation. [Boruto Ep. 36] He is considered a threat to the Ōtsutsuki Clan, [Boruto Ep. 66] proving crucial in defeating members like Momoshiki and Urashiki. Also, in the anime, his aid was key in defeating the Kara Inner member, Deepa. After Momoshiki's Kāma made Boruto a complete Ōtsutsuki, it greatly enhanced his combat prowess. [Boruto Ch. 68]  A year after leaving Konoha, Boruto mastered every skill and technique Sasuke could pass on to him and advanced beyond Sasuke's ability to train him. [Boruto: Two Blue Vortex Ch. 5] Two years later, with Koji Kashin's guidance and foresight, Boruto's prowess had greatly improved, able to easily overpower the full-powered Code. [Boruto: Two Blue Vortex Ch. 3] He also became a threat to the Human God Trees, [Boruto: Two Blue Vortex Ch. 22] able to defeat members like Hidari, [Boruto: Two Blue Vortex Ch. 12] and could hold his own against their leader, Jura. [Boruto: Two Blue Vortex Ch. 23] Later, after forming an agreement with Momoshiki, Boruto gains access to the entirety of the Ōtsutsuki's powers and becomes strong enough to easily overpower dozens of Human God Trees at once.

#### Chakra and Physical Prowess
From being an Uzumaki, Boruto inherited a powerful life-force. Like his father, he has very large chakra reserves, able to create multiple shadow clones even after most of his chakra was absorbed. [Boruto Ep. 6] In the anime, Boruto's body proved strong enough to handle natural energy and learn senjutsu, having passed the trials of Ryūchi Cave. From his Hyūga heritage, he has the natural potential to manifest the Byakugan. Also from this, his chakra control is very advanced, able to perform and effectively use the Rasengan after a few days of training with a single hand and instinctively improving on it. [Boruto: Two Blue Vortex Ch. 6] He is also able to perform one-handed handsigns. [Boruto: Two Blue Vortex Ch. 6] It is stated, in the anime, that he strongly inherited the power of the Ōtsutsuki. [Boruto Ep. 65] To which, he can perceive and communicate with deceased Ōtsutsuki spirits. [Boruto Ch. 10] After adjusting to his newfound Ōtsutsuki state, Boruto became able to conceal his signature on instinct from others. [Boruto: Two Blue Vortex Ch. 6]
Before entering the Academy, Boruto is noticeably fast and skilled in taijutsu, able to quickly get behind his opponents unnoticed and perform relentless assaults through coordinated strikes from each of his limbs. From his mother's family, he was taught the Gentle Fist. [Boruto Ch. 4] Though due to not originally possessing the Byakugan, and thereby could not target an enemy's tenketsu, he became able to through the manifestation of either his Jōgan or the Kāma's Byakugan. [Boruto Ch. 64] He also possesses considerable strength, as his strikes can upturn the ground [Boruto Ep. 37] plus can pick up and carry a man twice his size. [Boruto Ep. 233] After Boruto became a complete Ōtsutsuki, his base form's physical prowess had noticeably grown, as he could block an attack from a Kāma-enhanced Kawaki, [Boruto Ch. 78] and evade attacks from a Sage Mode-enhanced Mitsuki. [Boruto Ch. 79] Three years later, he becomes fast enough to dodge Jura’s various Tailed Beast Ball-sniper shots. [Boruto: Two Blue Vortex Ch. 23]

#### Kāma
After defeating Momoshiki, Boruto's right hand was forcibly marked with a four-point black diamond seal. Noted to have similar properties to Orochimaru's Juinjutsu, it is a genetic backup of the branding Ōtsutsuki, subconsciously allowing Boruto to access Momoshiki's combat experience and powers. According to Isshiki, Boruto has a rare natural compatibility for the mark, such that it will enable the God Tree to produce the highest quality of Chakra Fruit. This compatibility also allowed Boruto to instinctively develop and synchronise with it at an accelerated rate, progressing tremendously in the short time since obtaining it. Even without proper training, Boruto became able to utilise more of the mark's power with little struggle, something that even Isshiki found surprising.
When accessing the Kāma's power, Boruto becomes able to fight on par with Inners of Kara. When activated, the mark stretches and expands to cover his entire arm and the right side of his face in a series of elegant lines. While doing so, it greatly boosts his physical parameters, lets him absorb virtually any ninjutsu or chakra-based target, and can produce portals to other realms. At first, he could not access the mark willingly and it took a huge toll on his chakra, but was later taught how to be more efficient by Kawaki.
Originally, the Kāma was a threat to Boruto's life: from the moment he was branded with it, his entire genetic data was gradually being rewritten with Momoshiki’s so that he could resurrect in Boruto's body. [Boruto Ch. 46] When drained of enough chakra or knocked out, Boruto's Kāma will fully manifest Momoshiki's consciousness to take control of Boruto's body, gaining access to Boruto's knowledge and abilities in addition to his own. In this state, a flat horn protrudes from the right side of Boruto's forehead, curving up and back slightly. This also causes his right eye to become a Byakugan. Likewise, all previous damage to him is negated while the Kāma is active.
Boruto's contact with Kawaki, who bears Isshiki's Kāma, created a resonance that accelerated the takeover rate of both vessels. After Isshiki's defeat, Boruto was given a special medicine developed by Amado to suppress the Kāma's progressing. It seemingly allowed him to access the Kāma's powers without his mind being taken over by Momoshiki. This however proved only temporary. Following his death in an attempt to stop Momoshiki's control, Boruto was resurrected by Momoshiki using the remaining Kāma data, making Boruto a complete Ōtsutsuki while physically remaining himself. [Boruto Ch. 67] This made Momoshiki no longer able to resurrect through Boruto's body, though their respective thoughts would soon become available to one another against their will [Boruto Ch. 75] and allow Boruto temporary access to Momoshiki's foresight. [Boruto Ch. 75]
After becoming a complete Ōtsutsuki, Boruto believed he could better control Momoshiki's power, as his base form's physical prowess became further enhanced. Though, a year after leaving Konoha, further usage of the Kāma resumed the process of Momoshiki taking over his body, prompting Boruto from using it since. [Boruto Two Blue Vortex Ch. 25] Sometime after, Boruto gains access to the Ōtsutsuki ability to fly. [Boruto: Two Blue Vortex Ch. 6] He later demonstrates the ability to make contact with Momoshiki's spirit. Eventually, in desperation to stop the Human God Trees, Boruto formed an agreement with Momoshiki, gaining full control of the Kāma's power and all of Momoshiki's abilities. [Boruto Two Blue Vortex Ch. 15] [Boruto Two Blue Vortex Ch. 29]

#### Dōjutsu

##### Jōgan
At age 7, Boruto began to subconsciously unlock the Jōgan in his right eye, prompted by a prophetic dream imparted by Toneri Ōtsutsuki and the appearance of Nue. Originally, the eye was only instinctively activated at times of great threats, otherwise remaining dormant. Later however, by age 16, he was able to activate it at will. This dōjutsu grants him the ability to detect things generally unseen by a normal eye. It can perceive the flow of chakra, enabling Boruto to see the visible changes in one's chakra and likewise track a target through its chakra. It has heightened analytical powers, being able to uncover and thoroughly comprehend hidden details to better react to and counter the opponent's next move. It can also determine the chakra pathway system including key points like its tenketsu, and see through the invisible barriers that connect dimensions. It can access Space–Time Ninjutsu, able to generate portals to different dimensions. [Boruto Ep. 14] Uniquely, despite the damage to his right eye, while activating this dōjutsu, his eye apparently becomes fully-restored, and able to see with and still uses its powers.

##### Byakugan
While a direct descendant of the Hyūga Clan through his mother, he was not born with any known traits of the Byakugan. Despite this, he was noted to have the potential to awaken it. Later, from gaining  the Kāma of Momoshiki Ōtsutsuki, Boruto's right eye would eventually manifest Momoshiki's Byakugan. While originally unable to access this power, after convincing Momoshiki to loan Boruto his aid, Boruto became able to access this power at will. During which, Boruto's right eye provides him near 360° field of vision (except for a small blind spot at the upper thoracic vertebrae), x-ray vision, the ability to see through solid surfaces, across great distances, clearly perceiving the chakra pathway system, and can locate the tenketsu's chakra. This gives Boruto enhanced aim and precision on his attacks. Momoshiki's Byakugan also provides Boruto fragmented visions of the future surrounding himself.

#### Intelligence
Despite his carefree nature and preference to a direct approach in battle, Boruto is a very keen-minded individual. Adept at various intricate misdirection and feints, he is noted to excel at outwitting physically superior opponents with a systematic approach, [Boruto Ep. 50] even against famed and veteran jōnin like Ao with decades of experience and powerful cybernetic weapons. [Boruto Ch. 22] He quickly reading the situation and adapting to it, he can exploit flaws and weaknesses in both himself and other targets as key focuses in his plans. He is also effective in employing others' abilities in creating a strategy, making the most out of his allies' skills. Not unlike his father, he shows noticeable ingenuity with his techniques, like the shadow clones, for complex tactics. He is also shown to be able to flawlessly solve advanced problems through his own methods, noted as having a knack for it. In the Academy, Boruto is noticeably ahead of most of his peers, as his personal knowledge made him the only student to pass the written test of the graduation exams without cheating, getting a perfect score.

### New Era: Part I

#### Academy Entrance Arc
In the anime, Boruto came across Denki Kaminarimon who was being bullied by some kids. After rescuing him, they become friends and insist that Denki stands up to his father for being forced to join the Academy against his will. The next day on route to begin his studies at the Academy, Boruto unknowingly awakens his dōjutsu and notices a dark shroud surrounding Denki. As he follows him onto a Thunder Rail carriage, Boruto stops Denki from taking revenge on the bullies from the previous day and divert the carriage from crashing. Boruto and Denki then depart with the bullies and take the carriage to the Academy's entrance ceremony, where they manage to crash it into his father's stone face, leading to him receiving a two-week suspension. For the next two weeks, Boruto is home-schooled by Hinata. After returning to the Academy, he is placed in Shino Aburame's homeroom, and introduces himself to his classmates, to which they begin talking negatively about him for being under the impression Boruto gets special treatment for being the Hokage's son. He soon met Iwabee, who views Boruto negatively. Through the heated encounter, the two decide to fight each-other in a match. Upon barely defeating Iwabee within the rules he set in place, his classmates view Boruto differently than they did initially.
Later, when Boruto's recklessness caused some property damage, Shino decided to have Boruto and other students help repair to the Hokage Rock. The following day, Boruto, along with Inojin and Shikadai found Metal Lee, who was acting unusually aggressive. Boruto's dōjutsu activated, noticing the same dark chakra surrounding Lee that Denki had. With a group effort, they were able to subdue Lee long enough for the dark chakra to leave him. After apologising to Lee for the other day and applauding his remarkable combat skills, the group became much closer. Later, concerned about how only he could see the dark chakra, he asked Hinata more about the Byakugan. Later, while the students began learning about the Summoning Technique, Boruto caused tension between the girls and boys of his class, leading to Shino organising a race to capture a flag between the two groups. In the climax of it, Boruto attempted to use the Summoning Technique, leading to the Ghost being summoned. As it began causing havoc, Konohamaru Sarutobi subdues the beast. Afterwards, the girls win the competition and make peace with the boys.
