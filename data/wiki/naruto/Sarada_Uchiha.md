# Sarada Uchiha

Source: https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sarada_Uchiha (fetched 2026-10-23). Bracketed [Ch. N] / [Ep. N] are the wiki's own citations.

is a kunoichi from Konohagakure's Uchiha Clan. Because she was raised only by her mother without having her father around, Sarada initially struggled to understand who she is or what she's supposed to be. After meeting her father with the help of Naruto Uzumaki, Sarada comes to believe that she is defined by the connections she has with others. From this, she seeks to someday become Hokage so that she can connect with as many people as possible.

### Background
Sarada is the only child of Sasuke and Sakura Uchiha, and the first Uchiha child born since the clan massacre. She was born while her mother was accompanying her father on his travels and was delivered with the help of Sasuke's old teammate, Karin, in one of Orochimaru's hideouts; [Naruto Ch. 700] as such, there is no record of her birth at the Konoha Hospital. [Naruto Ch. 700] As a way to keep a connection with Sasuke, despite being happy for him, Karin kept the umbilical cord between Sakura and Sarada. After that, Sarada's family returned to Konoha to raise her, where her parents helped her learn to walk. [Naruto Ch. 700] During her father's absence from the village while gathering information on Kaguya Ōtsutsuki, she became ill with a high fever during which her mother looked after her. After the fever ended, she began wearing glasses, which unknown to her were a gift from Karin. [Naruto Ch. 700]
Due to the importance of his mission, Sasuke wasn't around much, and it is because of this that Sarada did not know anything about him, including what he physically looked like as an adult. Sakura did her best to comfort Sarada during Sasuke's absence, assuring her that her father loved both of them and that, when he completed his important mission, he'd come home. When this conversation went on for too long, Sakura poked Sarada's forehead and promised to continue it some other time. [Naruto Ch. 700]
As Sarada's parents continued to stay close to their own childhood friends, she ultimately got to know the kids of her parents' respective friends very well, [Boruto Ep. 2] especially growing close to Boruto Uzumaki. [Boruto Ep. 254] Whilst initially shy and unable to fit in with other children of her group much like her mother, Boruto insisted she join them, becoming one of her first friends, "saving her from loneliness". She also formed close female friendships with Chōchō Akimichi and Sumire Kakei, even confessing her doubts about her family to the other girls. In the anime, Sarada also appears to be close to Inojin Yamanaka and Himawari Uzumaki as well, referring to the younger girl as "Hima-chan".
On the day of Naruto Uzumaki's inauguration as the Seventh Hokage, Sarada watched the ceremony beside her mother and Chōchō, and silently wondered why it was taking so long for the Hokage to appear.

### Personality
As a child, Sarada was portrayed to be like her father, after the events of the Uchiha Clan Massacre: unfriendly, unsmiling and sullen, but her personality changed around the time of her Academy graduation. According to Naruto, her personality takes after her mother's, which he bases on the suspicion that Sarada is "scary" when she's angry; [Naruto Ch. 700] she indeed has somewhat of a strong temper, which flares up more often after her appointment to Team Konohamaru. In these and other moments of particularly strong feeling, Sarada often exclaims her mother's characteristic "Shannarō!". According to Chōchō, Sarada is also known for having a very dark side to her when confronting problems. [Boruto Ep. 24] Anger aside, she displays a normal range of emotions, from laughing to crying to blushing in appropriate situations, contrasting with Sasuke's typical stoic reaction to every circumstance. In most situations, Sarada is kind-hearted and brave, polite to others and considerate of her friends and family, wishing well for them and standing up for them when they need her. She doesn't mind saying what she is thinking, whether it is a question she wants the answer to, an opinion she holds firmly, or a frustration she can't let go of. Like Sakura, Sarada is quite well-read: she feels comfortable arguing the differences between a panda and a normal bear, and she is apparently the only participant in the Chūnin Exams to have heard of the book series called Shinobi Strategist Detective Story, much less read all four volumes.
Stemming from her mother's influence, Sarada's wisdom, self-awareness and independence are greater than most of her peers. When Sakura told her Sasuke did have a habit of showing affection that was greater than kissing, Sarada thought Sakura was referring to sexual intercourse. However, she was actually referring to Sasuke's habit of poking her in the forehead. In the anime, Sarada seems to attempt to portray as "normal" and wanting to maintain a certain extent of normality despite her career as a kunoichi, as shown by her habit of cooking for her overworked mother, her refusal of riding on top of a train or attempting to not sprint over buildings, [Boruto Ep. 17] and her genuine fear of horror movies. [Boruto Ep. 139]
The prolonged absences of her father upset Sarada, especially since their clan is so small, and she would periodically ask her mother about him during her childhood. Sakura would do her best to field Sarada's questions about who Sasuke was, what he was doing, and how he felt about Sarada, yet at the same time she would speak cryptically, either giving vague answers or avoiding topics entirely, not wanting to tell her daughter her father's past yet and the details of his S-rank mission. Although she put up with this for many years, Sarada started to feel that Sakura was withholding something from her in the days just before her graduation from the Academy, even extending this assumed dishonesty to all medical-nin since Sakura is one of the best healers. After getting into an argument about what Sakura may be hiding from her, Sarada came to suspect about her own lineage, a suspicion that is briefly, even though wrongly, confirmed to her at first. This initially caused her to angrily react against her parents and briefly consider leaving her home. She reconsidered this choice after talking to Naruto, who reminds her that family is defined by how people feel for each other and not from blood. Because Sarada loves Sakura and has had countless examples that Sakura loves her too, [Naruto Ch. 700] Sarada soon reconciles with her mother, thereafter restoring the good mother-daughter relationship of mutual trust, protection, and honesty the two have.
Sarada's temporary strain with Sakura occurs as a result of her attempt to cope with Sasuke's absence in her life; when she could no longer endure not having her father around, as her mother could tell her almost nothing about him. [Naruto Ch. 700] She decided to personally seek out Sasuke so that she could finally meet him and ask him directly all the things she had wanted to know, such as if he actually cared about her or her mother. Her curiosity grew after Naruto mentioned her father is a great shinobi. The excitement of getting to be in the same room as him caused her Sharingan to awaken. She was accordingly disappointed when, on seeing him, he is apparently cold and indifferent to her, providing her with none of the answers or comfort she craved, and therefore became angry with him, berating him for neglecting his own family. Nevertheless, Sarada eventually saw the emotionless shinobi exterior lifted in his brief moments of kindness to Sakura and herself. Although he is not as forthcoming as Sarada wants him to be, Sasuke is able to convince Sarada that he cares about her by reminding her that she singularly represents his bond with Sakura and his family. Since the incident with Sarada, Sasuke makes a greater effort to spend time with his family and console his child when he must be away, which, although it makes her sad, she now understands. When she does get to see him, Sarada is happy, according to Sakura, even happier than herself, and her family often takes the opportunity to eat and train together. She sometimes likes to playfully tease her parents about their love life, much to her amusement and their chagrin.
Sarada is appreciative of Naruto, for his help in mending her relationship with her parents, finding him to be kind, warm and a cool uncle figure. She is quite interested in his philosophy of the Will of Fire, wherein he tries to form bonds with everyone that he can, particularly villagers of Konoha. Because of Naruto, Sarada decides to make it her dream to become Hokage, wanting a life in which she is surrounded by familiar people instead of the necessary but lonely ninja path her father endures. She is so fond of Naruto that in a conversation with Kawaki, she admits she has wondered on many occasions what her life would be like if she were Naruto's daughter. In the anime, she shows great appreciation for the Hokage Rock and fantasises about having her own likeness carved into it. At the same time, Sarada shares a complicated relationship with Naruto's son, Boruto. Despite he is the first friend she made, which helped her to gain the confidence to befriend reliable peers such as Chōchō, Sumire and Mitsuki, the two ninja are often at odds with one another, arguing over almost everything. She initially felt jealousy towards Boruto for the wonderful father he had and even recriminated him for "not appreciating and reproaching" his father for his difficult work as a Hokage, though she comes to acknowledge her teammate's pain and reactions as valid. Despite their arguments, they are very close friends, deeply caring for one another's well-being and actually supporting their dreams greatly. Sarada even said they have been close since they were little despite their constant fights and openly expressed seeing Boruto as someone very close to her.
Sarada's worry for her family and friends, however, sometimes lets her emotions cloud her better judgment, resulting in her not taking into consideration other people's feelings or reasons. For example, she recklessly confronted Kawaki when he sealed Naruto and attempted to kill Boruto, being an Ōtsutsuki, even when Kawaki had grown powerful enough to the point that no one in Konoha could stop him. When Boruto and Kawaki's positions were switched due to Omnipotence, where she and Sumire were not affected, Sarada felt deep agony about Boruto's situation. So genuine was her regard for him that upon understanding the scale of his dire situation in which his life was at risk, it awakened her Mangekyō Sharingan. This was something so potent that Sasuke, whose own memories were affected, grasped that something important had happened to his beloved child and decided to help the boy who seemingly kill his best friend. Three years later, she futilely tried to appeal for the innocence of Boruto and her father, and stubbornly insisted on helping the two by asking about his location. However, Sumire pointed out that Boruto had his reasons for keeping to himself, the main reason he nearly died to Jura was because he was focused on defending them, that Sarada failed to consider his reasons for keeping away and that she kept disregarding his pain, causing a temporary strain between her and Sumire. Upon facing the sentient God Trees and realising the true power of her Mangekyō Sharingan, Sarada began to reflect on all her goals and the way she went about them, deciding to reject all self-doubt in herself and reach her potential so there would be less obtacles in her way. Following this revelation, Sarada came to realise she had deeper feelings for Boruto. Afterward, she reconciled with Sumire, and the two agree to a friendly rivalry for his affection.

According to Naruto, whereas Sarada's personality takes more after her mother, her appearance takes after her father: she has inherited the dark colour of his eyes and hair, which she keeps shoulder-length and parted to the right; Sarada is stated to have inherited most of her facial traits from her father. She often wears a pair of red framed glasses.
Early in her Academy career, [Naruto Ch. 700] Sarada wears a vermilion jacket, under which there is a cream-coloured vest, under which she has a high-collared white shirt with a red tie. She also wears a red skirt and black open-toed boots. As she nears graduation, Sarada begins wearing a red sleeveless qipao shirt - similar to what Sakura wore in Part II - with the Uchiha crest on the back. She also wears a pair of lilac-coloured shorts, dark purple thigh-length stockings, black shinobi sandals, and long dark purple arm warmers. Once she receives a forehead protector, she wears it around her forehead with her bangs hanging over. In the Boruto: Naruto Next Generations manga, Sarada wears a sleeveless, buttoned dull red dress bound by a yellow tie and a white belt. She wears white bandages underneath. She also wears a black fingerless glove on her right hand, similar to her father's, a black arm warmer on her left arm, and a pair of black ankle-length, open-toed high-heeled sandals with wooden soles.
Three years after Boruto left Konoha, Sarada's hair was trimmed and swiped to the left with wavy bangs. She wears earrings shaped like the Uchiha symbol, a dark choker with a circular pendant, frameless glasses, and pale-pink lipstick. She also wears a black tube top shirt with red lining on the top, black shorts, a red belt, black, high-heel sandals with black leg warmer calf-high socks with red lining on the top, and an oversized black jacket with red interior that she leaves open, with a metal plate engraved with the Konoha emblem attached to the right front panel. She typically wears her jacket off shoulder, exposing her left shoulder. When on missions, Sarada wears a standard Konoha forehead protector with a long red band as a hairband, similar to her mother's style, with the knot tied just behind her left ear and its loose ends hanging down to her shoulder.

### Abilities
Stemming from her special half-Uchiha heritage, Sarada is regarded as an elite genin that excels at everything. [Boruto Ep. 50] Noted by her Academy teachers to have exceptional talent, [Boruto Ep. 95] she is considered more skilled than her father was at her age. [Boruto Ep. 222] She is deemed as a future contender to become Hokage, as her skills were evaluated as the third highest at the start of her Academy class. Even before graduating the Academy, her combat skills were already considerably high, being able to quickly subdue several Mangekyo Sharingan-wielding Shin Uchiha clones. [Naruto Ch. 700] In the anime, she could even compete with a fully fledged Kiri-shinobi like Buntan Kurosuki and even defeat her. Three years after becoming a genin, she becomes powerful enough to be able to hold her own against the sentient God Tree, Hidari. [Boruto: Two Blue Vortex Ch. 10] [Boruto: Two Blue Vortex Ch. 12] Even more, with her Mangekyō Sharingan, she is regarded as crucial in the battle against rest of the Human God Trees, proven by her easily defeating Ryū [Boruto: Two Blue Vortex Ch. 21] and several copies of Mamushi. [Boruto: Two Blue Vortex Ch. 31]

#### Chakra and Physical Prowess
As an Uchiha, Sarada has powerful chakra. She also inherited her mother’s expert chakra control and ability to focus it into enhanced strength. Even before graduating from the Academy, this enhanced strength could allow Sarada to lift and hurl massive structures with ease or release the collected chakra on her strikes after making contact with a target for devastating effect, such as cratering the ground. [Naruto Ch. 700] Though in the anime, this skill was initially underdeveloped, wasting too much chakra and making the end results much weaker. As such, Sakura trained Sarada to better refine it. [Boruto Ep. 171] Eventually, her chakra-enhanced punches became strong enough to slightly crack Deepa's carbon defense, [Boruto Ep. 175] which was previously shown completely withstanding attacks from Mitsuki's Sage Mode [Boruto Ep. 166] and Shinki’s Iron Sand. [Boruto Ep. 169] She is also shown to be very adept in taijutsu, able to maneuver, with great efficiency and strike from various angles. [Boruto Ch. 11] [Boruto Ch. 21] She is also incredibly fast, attacking the Kara Inner, Boro with her Chidori strike at speeds faster than light before he could react in time to avoid it. [Boruto Ch. 42] Three years later, her physical prowess increased to where she could physically overpower the sentient God Tree, Hidari, [Boruto: Two Blue Vortex Ch. 12] and could even dodge his Chidori-sneak attack at point blank range, then immediately counter with her own Chidori strike. [Boruto: Two Blue Vortex Ch. 10]

#### Ninjutsu
Inheriting her father’s natural talent, Sarada is very knowledgeable in ninjutsu, and has been taught many of her parents' respective techniques. Like many Uchiha, Sarada has an affinity for ninja tools and her speciality is shurikenjutsu, [Boruto Ch. 3] gaining the highest marks in class. [Boruto Ep. 4] She is able to throw her weapons quickly and with precision, [Naruto Ch. 700] even deflect a projectile with one of her own launched kunai. [Boruto Ep. 7] She has similar aim while devoid of sight, able to perfectly hit the target mat six times in a row. [Boruto Ch. 13] While originally struggling with the skill in the anime, [Boruto Ep. 152] she gained proficiency in Medical Ninjutsu, [Boruto Ep. 249] able to focus her chakra to restart a person's cardiovascular system. She is also proficient in Cooperation Ninjutsu and Fūinjutsu, able to use both to restrict a person's movements and increase the might by synchronising with fellow users. [Boruto Ep. 37]

##### Nature Transformation
Despite Sarada's age, she is quite the talent for nature transformation, able of use Wind, Fire, Lightning and Yin Release. She has learned several of her father's signature techniques such as the Great Fireball Technique, the Phoenix Sage Fire Technique, as well as the Chidori and Chidori Current. She can also infuse lightning into Fūma Shuriken to stun or knock back an opponent. In the anime, after copying many of Buntan's lightning-based techniques, Sarada could create orbs, streams, and even a protective armour out of lightning. [Boruto Ep. 30] She also later copied the Wind Release: Gale Palm from Mitsuki, which she can use in collaboration with her team-mates to launch her allies for a quick assault. [Boruto Ep. 147]

#### Dōjutsu

##### Sharingan
In the anime, it was stated that Sarada had awakened her Sharingan at an early age due to Sasuke's absence which affected her deeply. [Boruto Ep. 21] Sarada reawakened her single-tomoe Sharingan in each eye at age 11, out of excitement to see her father again after many years. [Naruto Ch. 700] While having just reawakened it, she quickly adjusted to its heightened visual perception, being able to efficiently react to and avoid threats more easily [Naruto Ch. 700] and see Chakra Threads. [Boruto Ep. 60] Using it, she can perform genjutsu proficiently, as well as dispel it. [Boruto Ch. 3] Upon witnessing a technique being performed, she can copy the technique's hand seals to perform it simultaneously with the original user. In the anime, originally having low chakra reserves, she nearly passed out from over-usage of her Sharingan after acquiring it, she later could maintain it for an extended amount of time. In the anime, she awakened the second tomoe in both Sharingan during her second battle against Deepa. By the time Jigen invaded Konoha, she is shown to have awakened a third tomoe in both Sharingan, which when concentrated acts like a microscope, allowing Sarada to clearly identify things as small as viruses. [Boruto Ch. 41]

##### Mangekyō Sharingan
At age 12, Sarada awakened her Mangekyō Sharingan as a result of her deep anguish and worry for Boruto's safety after Ada swapped everyone's memories of him with their memories of Kawaki, who told everyone that Naruto had been killed by Boruto. Its design is eight triangles outwardly pointing in a circle around the pupil, giving a sunburst pattern. Her awakening was extremely rare, if not unique in circumstances as it was out of fear of losing a loved one, rather than the anguish of actually losing them. Both of Sarada's eyes possess Ōhirume, a powerful technique which allows her to summon numerous orbs which she can choose to control the gravitational force around her or other targets and draw them in. [Boruto: Two Blue Vortex Ch. 20] The technique is very versatile, as proper positioning and control of the force and perform feats like levitating the target and redirect them. Offensively, by pulling the target into the orb, the gravitational force can be amplified to the point of implosion.
Despite quickly adjusting to and mastering her Mangekyō Sharingan abilities, like all wielders, each usage of it risks permanent damage to the given eye. By the victory over Mamushi, her sight diminished considerably. This required her a stronger glasses prescription as her previous pair still left her vision greatly blurred.

#### Intelligence
Like both her parents, Sarada is highly intelligent and wise beyond her years. Due to her academic prowess, she was recognized as an honors student who achieved perfect scores in her Academy class. [Boruto Ep. 35] [Boruto Ep. 152] In combat, she is noted by elite strategists like Shikamaru to already possess chūnin-level leadership and battle analysis skills. [Boruto Ep. 112] These leadership skills have led to her being approved as team leader over other chūnin present, [Boruto Ep. 246] or even by her own teammates during their fight against Boro. [Boruto Ch. 41] She is capable of analysing her opponents' techniques to understand their mechanics and purposes, which has been shown numerous times when she determined that Kakō was unable to use ninjutsu for a certain period after using his Dust Release, [Boruto Ep. 87] or when deducing the location of Boro’s regeneration core and determining that her Chidori strike would be strong enough to disable it. [Boruto Ch. 42]

### New Era: Part I

#### Academy Entrance Arc
In the anime, on the first day of the Academy, Sarada attended her class entrance ceremony, and was placed in Shino Aburame's homeroom. After Boruto's suspension ended two weeks later, Chōchō noted that Sarada was especially close to Boruto due to their respective parents, though Sarada voiced her dislike of being considered close friends simply because of their parents. Later as Boruto fought against Iwabee Yuino, she watched the fight in the Training Hall, leading to Anko Mitarashi scolding them for skipping class.
When the class began learning about the Summoning Technique, tension arose between Sarada and Boruto, which lead to the class' two genders bickering each other. Seeing this, Shino organised a race between the boys and girls, which involved reaching a flag onto of the Academy. The challenge quickly got heated, with many of the boys being taken out of the contest until only Boruto, Shikadai, and Inojin were left on their team. Getting desperate, Boruto found a summoning scroll and miraculously summoned a snake-like creature. Going on a rampage, it knocked Chōchō off the roof, leading to Sarada and Boruto rescuing her until Konohamaru Sarutobi arrived and subdued the beast. During the ordeal, Sumire Kakei captured the flag, though the girls chose to make peace with the boys.
When Mitsuki joined her class, many girls found him very handsome, though Sarada sighed in distaste at them for judging someone purely on looks. The next day, Sarada participated in the classes welcoming party for Mitsuki, which became a disaster after Mitsuki disrupted the party with his Wind Release. Afterwards, a school repairman who was possessed went berserk, leading to Chōchō dragging Sarada into the fray. Combating the worker alongside, Iwabee, Shikadai Nara, Boruto and Mitsuki, the students managed to subdue him. The day after Sumire turned down Magire Kakuremino, Sarada and Chōchō protected her when he continued to stalk her, leading to him trapping the pair in a room while he abducted Sumire. After escaping, the group of students tracked Sumire to the Academy roof, during which Sarada blocked Magire's kunai attack on Chōchō. Talking to Magire, Chōchō deescalated the situation which consequently freed Magire from being possessed.
On her way to reporting to her sensei after night training, Sarada bumped into Boruto and began talking about Kagemasa, during which he runs off after someone. Tracking him down, Sarada saved him from a possessed Gongorō Kamakura, and managed to defeat the actor, resulting him being freed from being possessed. With a workplace field trip coming up, Sarada and Chōchō decided to do their workplace experience at a nail salon. After Sumire, Wasabi Izuno and Namida Suzumeno were attack by a possessed villager, Sarada and other students visited the three in hospital, where she escorted Boruto out when he arrived so it would remain quiet. Later, after practising in a three-man team in class, Shino was called aside to discuss the absent Sumire, leaving Sarada in charge. Returning with Sumire, all the students were delighted and embraced her return.

#### Sarada Uchiha Arc
In the anime, after learning how to walk on water, Sarada criticised the Academy boys who were raiding on a Thunder Train. The next day, as Sakura was heading off for a vacation trip, Sarada offered to take a patient's teddy bear to them in place of Sakura. During the trip there, Sarada misplaced the toy, leading her on a journey with Chōchō throughout Konoha to get it back. After recovering it, she gave it back to its owner much to her delight. During the day of the Kage Summit in Konoha, as class was ended early for it, Boruto asked his classmates to join him in pulling a prank. While none of them joined him, Sarada did follow and observe him from afar, keeping herself hidden. Later when she arrived home, she told her mother that boys are stupid, but remarked that Boruto and herself had something in common, like their similar feelings towards their fathers.
As her Academy graduation neared, Sarada became jealous that her classmates had the opportunity to train with their fathers, leading to her asking Sakura about Sasuke. Unable to answer if he wore glasses, Sakura accidentally destroyed their house when Sarada questioned if they were even married, resulting in her fainting. Leaving Sakura in Shizune's care, Sarada discovered a group photo of Taka, leading to her becoming suspicious of the woman with glasses photographed next to Sasuke. Unable to get details revolving around her birth from Shizune, she approached Chōchō, who had doubts about her own parents as well. Sarada decided to seek out Sasuke, leading to her heading to the Hokage Office, during which she overheard Naruto discussing plans to go and meet with Sasuke. Preparing to follow him, Chōchō tagged along, and they noticed Boruto arriving late to deliver a lunch to his father. Sarada offered to take it in his place, in order for her to have a reason to chase after Naruto.
As she and Chōchō hurried to catch up with Naruto, they were confronted by a boy with Sharingan named Shin Uchiha. Sarada was surprised by him, having been informed that she and her father were the only biological Uchiha left, and so Sarada refused Shin's request to go with him. He attacked them, leading to Naruto saving them after having sensed them. Overwhelmed, the boy retreated with aid from a creature. Naruto decided it would be safer for them to accompany him rather than return to the village without an escort. During their trip, Naruto told Sarada his history with Sasuke and that she resembled both of her parents, sharing her father's looks and her mother's personality. Nearing Ridge Tower, Sarada excused herself to the bathroom, when she instead ran towards the rendezvous point, during which she awakened her Sharingan at the thought of meeting Sasuke. Entering the tower, Sasuke drew his sword on her for information, believing she was one of Shin's associates. Calling him "dad", Sasuke realised who Sarada is, just as Naruto and Chōchō arrived. Reprimanding the Kage for bringing his daughter and Chōchō, Sarada defended Naruto, saying that she came without his permission because she wanted to ask him if Sakura was really her mother. Not providing any clarification or explanation for his absence from her life, Sarada ran outside and cried.
While being comforted by Naruto, the pair are attacked by a man also called Shin Uchiha, who was accompanied by the other Shin. Protected by Naruto from Shin's assault, he found himself crippled, leading to Sasuke having to shield Sarada from Shin's blades with his body. On the verge of defeat, Sakura arrived and immediately attacked the elder Shin. With both Shin's defeated, their creature transported Sakura and the pair away. Turning their attention to rescuing Sakura, the fathers decide to go to Orochimaru for intel about Shin. Arriving at the Sannin's residence, Sarada discovered that the two shinobi in her photo resided there. Pulling Suigetsu aside and informing him of her parentage doubts, Suitgetsu took DNA from her and tested it against the DNA of what he believed to be Karin's umbilical cord, which resulted in the DNA test being a perfect match. Distraught, Naruto comforted her, before the two ran outside to depart for Sakura's whereabouts, which were revealed by Orochimaru.
Arriving at Shin's hideout, Sasuke immediately rescued Sakura while crippling Shin, leading to his clone sons finishing him off. While Naruto and Sasuke fought the clones, Sarada attacked the creature that the original Shin was in the process of using to escape before he died. Approached by more clones, Sarada launched herself into the battle and decimated their foothold, repelling them away. After Naruto deescalated the situation and calmed the clones, Sakura told Sarada there was no doubt they were mother and daughter, to which Sarada agreed, and Sasuke stated she is the proof of her parents' connection. After the ordeal, the group enrolled the clones into the Konoha Orphanage before returning to Konoha with Sasuke. After spending some time together, the Uchiha took a family photo, and Sarada along with Sakura later saw Sasuke off as he left the village. In Orochimaru's hideout, Karin revealed that the umbilical cord belonged to Sakura and Sarada. Returning to the Academy, Sarada thanked Boruto, as her act of delivering Naruto's launch to him ultimately led her to decide on becoming Hokage. In the anime, after Sarada learned Boruto's Shadow Clone Technique by copying it with her Sharingan, she began to train her dōjutsu in secret, hoping to master it before revealing it to her peers.

#### Naruto Shinden: Parent and Child Day
During the new Konohagakure holiday, Parent and Child Day, Sarada resigned herself to spend the family-uniting holiday training with Boruto while Sasuke was still away on mission and Sakura had to spend much of her time at the hospital. Later, she found to her joy that Sasuke had indeed returned to the village. Having spent such little time with Sarada, the normally poised man struggled to connect with his daughter, simply trying to get inspiration wherever he could, instead simply embarrassing Sarada. Finally having enough, Sarada stormed off. She was then approached by Boruto. While Sarada feared that her father really didn't know her, Boruto countered that Sarada didn't know much about him either. At Boruto’s suggestion, he insisted to just enjoy the time she has with him. Later, while working on her shuriken skills, Sasuke approached her again, applauding her on the goal of becoming Hokage. He insisted that she would make a better Hokage than he ever could and would support her through her struggles, much to her delight. As the day ended, she and Sasuke returned home where the entire Uchiha family enjoyed a hearty meal together.

#### School Trip Arc
In the anime, Sarada volunteered Boruto to be their class' trip leader for their upcoming trip to the Land of Water. Upon arriving in Kirigakure, the class was escorted around the village by Kagura Karatachi, during which they met the Fifth and Sixth Mizukage. Later, Sarada found Tsurushi Hachiya injured, leading to her treating his injuries and being informed of Boruto was in danger. Finding Boruto defeated at the Kirigakure Academy, she gave him first aid and was informed that Shizuma Hoshigaki was exploiting Kagura.
