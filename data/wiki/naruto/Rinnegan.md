# Rinnegan

Source: https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rinnegan (fetched 2026-10-22). Bracketed [Ch. N] / [Ep. N] are the wiki's own citations.

The  is reputed as the most exalted eye amongst the "Three Great Dōjutsu", [Naruto Ch. 373] the others being the Sharingan and the Byakugan, and the strongest dōjutsu. It is said that in times of disorder, one who wields the Rinnegan is sent down from the heavens to become either a "God of Creation" who will calm the world or a "God of Destruction" who will reduce everything to nothingness. The Rinnegan is characterised by concentric circles covering the eyeball.

### Acquisition
The first person on Earth to wield the Rinnegan was Hagoromo Ōtsutsuki, whose mother possessed the Rinne Sharingan. In the anime, he obtained his Rinnegan at the same time as his Mangekyō Sharingan, which awakened as a result of mortally wounding his brother. [Naruto: Shippūden Ep. 462] Using the power of the Rinnegan, Hagoromo saved the world from the Ten-Tails and created ninshū, the precursor to modern ninjutsu. For his contributions to the world, Hagoromo became revered as the Sage of Six Paths, and both he and his eyes rose to mythological status following his death; [Naruto Ch. 425] even when the Rinnegan was later proven to exist, some dismissed it as a random mutation. [Naruto Ch. 462]
It is possible for a Sharingan wielder to awaken a new Rinnegan by obtaining Hagoromo's chakra, either by combining the chakra of his sons, Indra and Asura, to recreate his own, [Naruto Ch. 671] or by directly receiving it from Hagoromo himself. Black Zetsu spent centuries attempting to manipulate the reincarnations of Indra and Asura into awakening the Rinnegan, but it was not until the time of Madara Uchiha — Indra's reincarnate — that this was successful. [Naruto Ch. 681] Madara implanted the flesh of Hashirama Senju — Asura's reincarnate — into his own body, causing a part of Asura's chakra to combine with Indra's. However, Madara's eyes did not become Rinnegan until decades later, when his natural lifespan was nearing its end; [Naruto Ch. 606] this also restored his right eye's vision, which had been lost from his earlier use of Izanagi. [Naruto Ch. 681] Orochimaru and Kabuto Yakushi later hypothesised that the Sharingan can be evolved into the Rinnegan by combining Uchiha and Senju DNA. To that end, Kabuto successfully recreated the Rinnegan by infusing Hashirama's cells into Madara's reincarnated body, [Naruto Ch. 560] though these fake eyes did not have full access to the original's abilities. [Naruto Ch. 659]
After receiving half of Hagoromo's chakra from his spirit, Sasuke awakened a Rinnegan in his left eye, which contains three tomoe on its two innermost circles for a total of six. In addition to the Rinnegan's power, this eye retains the abilities of Sasuke's left Mangekyō Sharingan, including the ability to cast ocular genjutsu and use Amaterasu. [Naruto Ch. 682] [Naruto Ch. 692] If Sasuke overuses the power of his Rinnegan, his ocular power will temporarily diminish, causing the tomoe in his Rinnegan to vanish until his full power returns. During this time, he is also seemingly unable to activate his right eye's Mangekyō Sharingan or use Susanoo. [Naruto Ch. 700]
Momoshiki Ōtsutsuki had a pair of Rinnegan embedded in the palms of his hands. They were initially red, but after he consumed his guardian, Kinshiki, they became purple (in Boruto: Naruto the Movie) or golden yellow (in Boruto: Naruto Next Generations). This also granted him a third Rinnegan on his forehead, which matched the other two in colour. [Boruto Ch. 8] [Boruto Ep. 64] In the anime, Momoshiki's yellow Rinnegan temporarily turned red when he used their power. [Boruto Ep. 65]
After Code used Claw Marks on a Ten-Tails to create Claw Grimes, at least one of them manifested a single Rinnegan. Upon its manifestation, the Fission being gave off the presence of levelling up, and was accompanied with an increase of strength. [Boruto: Two Blue Vortex Ch. 2] Later, the same Ten-Tails completely separated into several sentient God Trees, with each one possessing a pair of purple Rinnegan. If a God Tree had their body destroyed and transformed into a Thorn Soul Bulb, their Rinnegan are regenerated alongside their entire body using roots of a specific tree. [Boruto: Two Blue Vortex Ch. 13]
In the anime, Urashiki Ōtsutsuki manifested his Rinnegan in the same pair of eyes as his Byakugan, and could switch between the two dōjutsu at will. Urashiki's Rinnegan were able to take on several forms to access different abilities: at times, they were red and contained six tomoe, [Boruto Ep. 62] while at other times, they were blue with a white ripple pattern while using certain techniques. [Boruto Ep. 125] [Boruto Ep. 133] After consuming all of the chakra he had stolen from others, Urashiki removed and devoured both of his Rinnegan, causing him to undergo a transformation. In this form, his eyes were restored, an additional Rinnegan appeared on his forehead, and all of his dōjutsu turned yellow, much like Momoshiki before him. [Boruto Ep. 135]
As with any dōjutsu, the Rinnegan can be transplanted into others. Prior to his death, Madara implanted both of his eyes into the young Nagato without the boy's knowledge, and he was able to make effective use of them throughout his life due to his Senju lineage. [Naruto Ch. 606] After Nagato's death, the Rinnegan were taken by Obito Uchiha, [Naruto Ch. 514] who implanted the left eye into himself and hid the other, knowing he could not handle the power of both eyes at once. [Naruto Ch. 675] Madara later reclaimed both of his Rinnegan after he was revived, [Naruto Ch. 658] [Naruto Ch. 676] and they were last seen deactivated upon his death. [Naruto Ch. 691] He was also able to revert his Rinnegan to his Eternal Mangekyō Sharingan, though only seen when reanimated. [Naruto Ch. 588] [Naruto Shippūden Ep. 339] Sasuke has noted that his Rinnegan would remain active after his death, and that it could be transplanted into and be usable by Kakashi Hatake. [Naruto Ch. 698]

### Abilities
The Rinnegan contains extraordinarily powerful chakra and boasts enormous ocular power, such that an unworthy wielder risks "losing themselves" upon implanting one. Their full power can only be brought out by their original owner, particularly when wielding both eyes, but possession of even a single transplanted Rinnegan can still grant overwhelming power. It is able to see chakra and its flow within the body, [Naruto Ch. 668] as well as otherwise invisible barriers, [Naruto Ch. 419] but cannot see through obstructions such as smoke bombs. [Naruto Ch. 433] Possession of the Rinnegan allows one to easily master any jutsu as well as all five basic nature transformations. [Naruto Ch. 375] The Rinnegan is the only dōjutsu able to fully decipher the Sage of Six Paths' stone tablet. [Naruto Ch. 467]
One who wields the Rinnegan can utilise six abilities collectively known as the Six Paths Technique: controlling attractive and repulsive forces with the Deva Path; mechanically altering one's body with the Asura Path; extracting souls through the Human Path; summoning various creatures through the Animal Path; absorbing chakra through the Preta Path; and access to the King of Hell through the Naraka Path. A seventh ability, called the Outer Path, is said to allow the user to preside over life and death, granting them the ability to revive the dead. [Naruto Ch. 449] The Outer Path also allows the user to transmit their chakra into black receivers, [Naruto Ch. 569] manifest chakra chains to bind the tailed beasts, [Naruto Ch. 567] and create the Six Paths of Pain: six bodies controlled through the use of black receivers, which can each utilise one aspect of the Six Paths Technique. The Rinnegan grants the user a shared field of vision with their Six Paths of Pain, the creatures summoned through the Animal Path, and the King of Hell, all of which possess copies of the Rinnegan in their own eyes. [Naruto Ch. 551] [Naruto Ch. 565]
The Rinnegan is the only means of breaking the seal placed on the Demonic Statue of the Outer Path and summoning it, [Naruto Ch. 606] though it cannot be summoned using a fake reincarnated Rinnegan. The dōjutsu is also used to seal the Ten-Tails inside one's body and become its jinchūriki. When one who wields the power of Rinne approaches the moon, they can awaken the Rinne Sharingan and cast the Infinite Tsukuyomi. [Naruto Ch. 676] In turn, another Rinnegan wielder is able to repel the Infinite Tsukuyomi's light, and this protection can be extended to others by using the Rinnegan in conjunction with Susanoo. [Naruto Ch. 677] In order to end the technique entirely, a Rinnegan wielder must form the rat hand seal together with someone who possesses the chakra of all nine tailed beasts. [Naruto Ch. 692]
Certain Rinnegan abilities have only been demonstrated by single users:
*Sasuke Uchiha's Rinnegan allows him to use Amenotejikara to swap places with a person or object within a certain range of himself, [Naruto Ch. 685] as well as create portals to other dimensions. It also has several sciences to it, and one of them is pattern recognition. It helps him analyse patterns inside codes, and by comparing them to similar patterns, decipher what is written. When put into use, he can take large amounts of information from a small amount of text.
*Madara Uchiha can create corporeal shadows in the world of Limbo, which are invisible to the those lacking the Rinnegan. [Naruto Ch. 674] These shadows can also be sensed with Six Paths Senjutsu [Naruto Ch. 674] and can only be physically interacted with by using Six Paths Chakra.
*Momoshiki Ōtsutsuki is able to absorb any ninjutsu with his right Rinnegan and subsequently release it in an amplified form from his left Rinnegan, as well as transform chakra and living individuals into edibles.
*Urashiki Ōtsutsuki has two different Rinnegan with their own special abilities. With his red tomoe Rinnegan, he could use Yomotsu Hirasaka to travel through dimensions. With his blue Rinnegan, he could use the Origami Replacement Technique to displace himself by disappearing and leaving behind multiple red chakra orizuru, leaving his chakra signature untraceable. He could also transport himself into the past to redo events.
*Jura, as an incarnation of the Ten-Tails and a sentient God Tree, can fire a Tailed Beast Ball from his Rinnegan. [Boruto: Two Blue Vortex Ch. 9]
