# Shepherd Sommers

Source: https://onepiece.fandom.com/wiki/Shepherd_Sommers (fetched 2026-10-22). Bracketed [Ch. N] / [Ep. N] are the wiki's own citations.

Saint Shepherd Sommers is a World Noble and a member of the Knights of God.
Due to his actions and role, he is a major antagonist in the Elbaph Arc.

### Appearance
Sommers is a tall, muscular, older man with wrinkles, a bulbous nose, a somewhat hairy body, orange-blonde long hair held in place by pigtails and a short full beard along with a cross shape below his chin. His left arm has a distinct, elaborate tattoo and he wears a pair of plain gray glasses when off-duty, and switches to a pair of red tinted sunglasses during official missions.
Sommers' Knights of God attire consists of a purple tunic held together by gold strings, a belt and decorated with various medals and a rose pinned on his attire's left breast, leaving the right comparatively bare save for a single medal in parallel to his rose. His tunic is also open at the upper torso, exposing part of his chest, and he wears long white gloves and purple boots, the latter reaching all the way to his thighs. Furthermore, he wears a white armband on his left arm with the Knights of God insignia depicted on it. Sommers completes his uniform with a fancy coat, black on the outside, and red on the inside, sporting a furry collar and epaulets draped over his shoulders. At least four pentagrams decorate his attire at various spots, one above his rose, one above his belt, one below and positioned between his sword and a final one on his pants. His belt buckle is decorated with the Hoof of the Soaring Dragon flocked by a pair of feathered wings. He keeps a sword with a thorn shaped guard at his side, held in place by a shoulder sash-like belt.
When first summoned, Sommers was in a state of undress, wearing a white undershirt and dark purple briefs with pink heart-shaped polka dots, and his military cape was draped over his shoulders.
Sommers has a mechanical heart made of steel with a gauge on it; it has been indicated that this is a byproduct of his immortality. [Ch. 1193]
When young, he had a pair of standard glasses and did not have any facial hair (apart from his cross below his chin). Whenever he transformed his sword into a kanabō, it was colored white instead of black. 14 Years after the God Valley Incident, he had fully grown his beard, and was starting to grow his mustache.

### Personality
Sommers is a sadistic and violent man who actively enjoys watching people suffer, to the point that he goes to great lengths in his missions to inflict cruelty for his own enjoyment; he has no shame about admitting these desires and discussing them casually with his peers. Believing that loving is about "hurting one another", [Ch. 1142, Ep. 1172] Sommers has repeatedly taken advantage of the bonds of love between his victims, particularly that between a parent and child, [Ch. 1143, Ep. 1173] by creating hopeless tragedies in which one party faces certain doom, and the other party can only either watch in despair or harm themselves in a fruitless effort to stop it. Upon actually witnessing such a tragedy, Sommers became so excited that he could barely control himself, laughing to the point of crying. [Ch. 1145, Ep. 1175] [Ch. 1174] As a result, Sommers has regularly targeted children in his missions in order to satisfy his desires, and he enjoys mocking defenseless kids that he fully intended to kill. [Ch. 1160]
Befitting his lack of empathy towards the common people, he was excited at the prospect of taking Shakuyaku, who many considered to be the most beautiful in the world, as a slave, [Ch. 1159] but quickly attacked and attempted to kill her when chaos struck God Valley, stating a woman of worth disgusts him and that he preferred naive women, and decades later, he attempted to kill Nico Robin as if she were any other person, not even showing any interest towards enslaving her. He apparently is excited by emotionally breaking his opponents, as he tried to kill Shakuyaku just to see the pirates' reaction should they find her slaughtered brutally, and in the present day, was delighted at holding the giants of Elbaph hostage using their children. He apparently likes to drag out his opponent's suffering, as he suggested he could get rid of Shakuyaku's optimism if he just smashed her in five blows, seemingly taking pleasure in his victims losing any faith or hope. [Ch. 1161]
Sommers is fully committed to the goals of the World Government and will do whatever is necessary to achieve success. Like the other Celestial Dragons who are aware of his existence, Sommers is very loyal to Nerona Imu, following his will without question and encouraging younger Knight Killingham to make carrying out Imu's will his top priority. [Ch. 1182] He also believes that academic pursuits and historical retention to be completely worthless for warriors, especially those he wishes to have under the government. [Ch. 1147, Ep. 1177] With that said, Sommers does not care for immediately being pressed into action while he is in a state of relaxation, and pushed back somewhat on his summons to Elbaph because he was concerned about the issues plaguing Mary Geoise, though ultimately accepted the mission without much fuss. Additionally, Sommers' dedication to his missions may lapse if he gets a chance to witness a tragedy, even if this would hinder his goals, so long as he can justify his inaction by blaming extenuating circumstances. Unlike his superiors, he does not seem to believe in Nika, dismissing it as a mere fairy tale for children, [Ch. 1175] though he appears to be more sensitive about the Rain God Zaza, admitting that he was unnerved that Killingham had summoned it as a MMA.
Sommers can be dense sometimes, such as not registering that he had been summoned to Elbaph and believing that Gunko and Figarland Shamrock were in fact visiting his mansion instead. This thoughtlessness can extend into combat, such as when Sommers initially treated Robin's attack on him as a romantic advance, as well as when he destroyed the escape ship for his team's hostages while caught up in fending off a MMA. He may be overbearing in casual contact with his colleagues, as he acted offended when Gunko brushed him off and started needling her on her habits, earning a rebuke from Killingham. Sommers has a tendency to be overconfident, having stated multiple times that his team had "already won" on Elbaph as soon as they secured a decisive strategic advantage. This may lead him to express boredom and desire for a challenge; he was interested at facing a Four Emperors crew during the takeover at Elbaph and contemplated taking some of the Straw Hat Pirates hostage after Gunko captured them, though later admitted that it would be unwise to do this. Though Sommers does not shy away from a fight, he is ultimately a cowardly bully. He is arrogant and takes great pleasure in hurting people who cannot fight back, but openly expresses fear and shock in the rare instance when faced with an opponent truly capable of defeating him. [Ch. 1148, Ep. 1178] [Ch. 1193] When confronted by someone who is legitimately capable of ending his life, Sommers reacts with true terror and will attempt to flee. [Ch. 1195]
According to Killingham, Sommers is a gourmet with good taste in food. He also enjoys live musical performances, though by his own admission he is very strict against mistakes and will punish anyone who plays imperfectly. Unlike most World Nobles, Saint Sommers appears to be more informed of the goings on of the "Lower World" and calmer in handling the crisis of the food shortage ravaging the Holy Land.
Like several other characters, Sommers has a unique laughing style: "Gihaha".

### Relationships

#### Knights of God
Sommers is a member of the Knights of God and appears to be amiable and laid-back with his fellow knights, though his casual state of undress greatly embarrassed Gunko so much so that she blushed and had to turn away while he got dressed. Figarland Shamrock appeared unfazed by this however, and instead moved on topic to discuss the mission at hand. Notably, unlike Gunko and Killingham, Sommers does not address his superior Shamrock by rank, instead using his proper name instead, implying a degree of close familiarity between the two.
Gunko's serious attitude provides a contrast to Sommers' boisterousness in collegial interactions, which has caused Sommers to act facetiously offended when Gunko brushes off his declarations and needle her over her interests, such as her propensity to listen to music from the Lower World, which caused him to be berated by Killingham. [Ch. 1144, Ep. 1174]

#### Harald
Sommers is aware of the late King Harald and possibly respected him, as he wondered if Shamrock could have been visiting his grave. However, he does curse him for complicating their plans with his pushes for pacifism, indicating a deeper connection between him and the World Government. [Ch. 1146, Ep. 1176]

#### Scopper Gaban
Sommers seems to have met Scopper Gaban in the past, as Gaban recognized Sommers when they encountered each other on Elbaph, with Sommers acknowledging Gaban's strength, looking panicked and terrified when Gaban charged at him and sliced his hand off before being struck vertically in the chest. [Ch. 1148, Ep. 1178]

#### Shakuyaku
Sommers first laid eyes on Shakky when she was presented as a prize in the Native Hunting Competition on God Valley. Unlike Garling, who intended to win and take her as his wife, Sommers had more cruel intentions in mind involving enslaving her. When the two actually met that day, Sommers immediately found himself annoyed by her personality, and went so far as to strike her for daring to make a quip about the situation on the island. Sommers showed how little he valued her life when he attempted to kill her, solely as a means to make rescue attempts for her for naught. [Ch. 1161]

#### Nerona Imu
Sommers is very loyal and respectful of Nerona Imu, regarding the secret ruler of the World Government as Boss. [Ch. 1150, Ep. 1180] However, this may be simply a facade due to his position, as he was at one point willing to allow Imu to take the blame for his own failure, just to save himself.

### Abilities and Powers
As a World Noble Sommers has the power to do essentially as he pleases and can summon an Admiral to handle any transgressor that may interfere with or threaten him. However, given his status as a member of the Knights of God, Sommers is a powerful fighter and can easily defend himself without the intervention of an Admiral if he ever runs into trouble as shown when he was summoned to the fearsome land of Elbaph. He effortlessly overpowered former Vice Admiral Saul without any damage to himself, and later defeated Nico Robin with relative ease. He however, appears to be still no match against the top fighters of the world, as Scopper Gaban,  Silvers Rayleigh and both Monkey D. Luffy and Loki easily defeated him not to mention Zoro even using him as training dummy to practice Advanced Conqueror's Haki.

#### Devil Fruit
Sommers ate the Iba Iba no Mi, a Paramecia-type Devil Fruit which grants him the ability to create and control thorny vines. Once the vines are created, he can wrap them around himself, or bind them around any target of his choosing, even to multiple individuals at once. He can create enough oversized vines to cover an entire battlefield. He can also create an enormous thorn-covered kanabō many times larger than himself.
If someone attempts to make physical contact with a person wrapped by the vines, they will get hurt by the thorns. Additionally, the thorns can be made invisible, making it easier for someone to fall into the trap and more difficult for them to figure out the nature of the ability.

#### Covenant
Through a covenant with Imu—either the Depths Covenant or the Abyssal Covenant—Sommers was granted an increase in power and several mystical abilities, including the ability to use the Abyss as a means of transport. During the invasion of Elbaph, Imu personally bestowed an Omen to Sommers which the Knight of God was overjoyed to receive as it massively enhanced his physical abilities allowing him to leap great distances,, carry enormous weights such as a pirate ship big enough for giants, and fly with a set of wings. Sommers refers to himself as "Super Sommers" in this state.

##### Immortality
Due to the covenant, Sommers is effectively immortal as he can heal instantaneously from injuries. When Nico Robin used her powers to snap Sommers' spine in half, he effortlessly restored himself as if nothing happened. [Ch. 1148, Ep. 1178] However, when former Roger Pirates member Scopper Gaban severed Sommers' arm with Supreme King Haki, the Noble was shocked to find out it was not growing back. Scopper's attack slowed his regeneration so that despite being decapitated in battle, Sommers still survived and was eventually able to pull himself together and regenerate his arm though he felt great pain. [Ch. 1149, Ep. 1179] Upon being enhanced with the power of the Omen, the regeneration rate seemed to speed up significantly. [Ch. 1194]
Sommers possesses a steel heart that beats even when separated from the rest of his body. When Roronoa Zoro suggested destroying the heart to see if it would prevent Sommers from regenerating, Sommers became visibly frightened at Zoro's words, implying that the steel heart is somewhat connected to his immortality. [Ch. 1179]

#### Haki
Sommers possesses Haki. His arrival on Elbaph was accompanied by a massive burst of Haki which resembled black lightning. His Haki was sensed by Scopper Gaban and described as being abnormal. [Ch. 1145, Ep. 1175] In the anime, Sommers was shown to be able to harden his kanabō and the thorns he creates with Armament Haki. [Ep. 1176]

#### Weapons
Sommers is shown to carry a sword with a thorn-covered guard over the hilt. Using his Devil Fruit power, Sommers can transform his sword into a thorny kanabō many times larger than he is tall which he has used to beat Saul, using the many spikes on it to inflict extra damage.
Through the Omen bestowed on him, Sommers received permission to wield the ancestral sword of the Shepherd Family, the ancient sword Gram.

### History

#### Past
38 years ago, Sommers went to God Valley to participate in the Native Hunting Competition. When the former pirate and Kuja empress Shakuyaku was presented as the grand prize, Sommers declared that he would take her as a slave if he won. The competition then began, and Sommers preyed on a native boy in a tree by placing thorns below him and waiting for him to fall. The boy's father moved to catch him, only for both of them to be impaled by the thorns as Sommers looked on in glee. Not long into the competition, however, the Rocks Pirates and Roger Pirates launched assaults on God Valley, and Sommers mobilized with the Knights of God to protect the rest of the World Nobles. Sommers went to where Shakky was being held and found that she had subdued all the Marines who had tried to evacuate her. Sommers slapped Shakky and she kicked him in the head, but he then seized her leg and manifested a spiked club to strike her. Knowing that she would wind up with Figarland Garling if she remained alive, Sommers decided to kill Shakky and revel in the pirates and Nobles' emotional reactions to finding her dead. However, Silvers Rayleigh then arrived and blasted Sommers into the wall with a single swing of his sword, saving Shakky. As the God Valley Incident reached its climax, Sommers ultimately managed to evacuate the island before it collapsed into the sea. [Ch. 1166]
In the 13 years following the God Valley Incident, Garling mobilized the Knights against the Roger Pirates to retrieve his son Shanks, but the Knights were beaten back every time. Sommers told Shanks of this when he eventually joined the Knights around 15 years ago. [Ch. 1170]
24 years ago, Sommers was in Shangara when he learned that the King of Elbaph, Harald, had joined their ranks as a Devoted Blade. He was surprised and questioned whether the giant could take orders, but Harald assured the Knights that he was at their service. [Ch. 1167]

#### Final Saga

##### Elbaph Arc
Sommers was sitting in his house in his underwear when he received a call summoning him to a mission on Elbaph. When he asked if he would have time to get ready, he was suddenly transported to Elbaph via Abyss alongside Rimoshifu Killingham. While getting dressed, Sommers and Killingham were briefed by Figarland Shamrock and Manmayer Gunko about their task to assist Gunko in kidnapping some Elbaph children as part of the Knights' mission to subjugate the land of giants. Before he departed to Mary Geoise to handle the issues there, Shamrock suggested that the Knights make a game out of kidnapping the children using their powers. The Knights approached the Walrus School, and Killingham used his powers to make a group of nine children fall asleep. Gunko then used an arrow to force the children to sleepwalk to the port, and Sommers placed rings of invisible thorns around each child to prevent anyone from stopping them. The trio watched from a distance as nightmare creatures summoned by Killingham called MMA began rampaging around the school, before sitting down to eat a meal. Sommers expressed glee about the effectiveness of his thorns and believed that they had effectively won already, but Gunko warned him that the Straw Hat Pirates were on the island, and the Knights saw that a giant blaze had erupted in the children's path.
A group of Straw Hats arrived to put out the fire, and Gunko moved to attack them while Killingham went back to the Walrus School to find a Dengo Mushi. Sommers attacked schoolteacher and former Marine Vice Admiral Jaguar D. Saul, and quickly subdued the giant by surrounding his path with thorns. Sommers expressed his ire toward the giants' culture transitioning from warmongering to education and pacifism, and cursed Harald for having prevented the World Government from subjugating Elbaph sooner. Gunko then brought the Straw Hats she captured back to Sommers, and Killingham said he had secured the Dengo Mushi. Sommers spoke into it through his Den Den Mushi call to Killingham, broadcasting his voice across the Sun World. He explained the Knights' intentions and named the hostages, telling Western Village elder Jarul that they would send the children falling to their deaths one by one unless he agreed to have the Walrus School and Owl Library burned down without removing any of their contents.
Nico Robin then ambushed Sommers, using her Devil Fruit powers to strike Sommers in the jaw and break his back. Sommers was able to reform his body quickly without issue due to his immortality, but Robin had a clone of herself tackle Sommers, sending him falling into the Underworld in the hope that she could put him out of range of the children. However, Sommers quickly dispatched the clone with his thorns and launched a vine to tether himself to the Sun World, allowing his powers to remain active while he continued broadcasting. Sommers pulled himself back onto the branch and wrapped Robin in thorns, but before he could strike her, he was suddenly attacked by Scopper Gaban. Gaban severed Sommers' right hand and dealt him a cut to the chest using Supreme King Haki imbuement, preventing Sommers from immediately regenerating. However, Sommers quickly recovered and summoned vines to counter Gaban's follow-up strikes before surrounding himself with spikes on the ground. Despite these measures, Gaban leaped toward Sommers, cut through the vines, and decapitated him at the shoulders, causing Sommers' body parts to fall and be impaled on his own spikes.
Soon after this, Imu decided to personally intervene in the Knights' mission by possessing Gunko. Once Sommers' body was intact again, Imu commanded him to personally accompany the hostage children to the port. Imu's arrival caused the battle to tilt even more heavily in the Knights' favor, and he said they would continue their assault until Elbaph's spirit broke. [Ch. 1151] Sommers remained with the children until the port and escape ship came into view, but right as this happened, a group of the children's relatives and a Nika MMA entered the vicinity. The MMA was defeated and sent flying toward Sommers, who hit it away with a club; however, this unwittingly sent the MMA crashing into the escape ship, destroying it and the Sea Clouds below. [Ch. 1173] Sommers started to panic, as he could not get Gunko to stop the children from walking off the edge while Imu was possessing her. Ultimately, he decided to sit back and watch the children fall to their deaths while reveling in their relatives' anguish. Sommers laughed sadistically as he watched the children's loved ones choose to accompany them off the edge, but was then left utterly shocked as the giants rose back up on the back of Loki, who had transformed into the legendary Nidhöggr. [Ch. 1174]
Loki deposited the children and their relatives at the Western Village, and Sommers moved to kill them with thorn projectiles. However, Zoro cut apart the thorns while Luffy moved to attack Sommers. The giant-sized Emperor quickly struck Sommers with Gomu Gomu no Dawn Thor Rifle, which blew apart the Knight's body and caused the thorns around the children to disappear. Overhearing Ripley refer to Luffy as Nika, Sommers dismissed the figure as a bedtime tale for children before his body parts were smashed into the ground by Loki's foot. Once the battle moved elsewhere, Sommers struggled to reattach his body parts as his heart was left out in the open. [Ch. 1178] Right before Sommers could fully reform his body, he was discovered and seized by Gerd while Goldberg found his heart, and Sommers was left terrified as Zoro mulled over destroying it. However, things took a turn in the battle as Sommers looked on in shock at Imu arriving on Elbaph in person. [Ch. 1179] Imu swiftly moved to the village and freed Sommers by slicing off Gerd's fingers, allowing the Knight to finish reforming and retake his heart. Imu then granted a ring of Omen to Sommers and told him to move quickly, which Sommers did as he leapt high above the village. [Ch. 1180]
Sommers found a new ship and brought it to the port with his bare hands. Killingham called him and confessed that he had mistakenly summoned his own worst fear, Zaza, as a MMA to recapture the children, but Sommers pressed him to use it in their mission and satisfy Imu's commands. While waiting at the port, Sommers was confronted by Zoro, who assured the Knight that Killingham and the children would be prevented from coming because of his crewmate Sanji, which Sommers scoffed at as he and the swordsman began to clash. [Ch. 1187] Sommers soon used his Omen to manifest his family blade, the sword Gram, and use it against Zoro. [Ch. 1189] Sommers was able to catch Zoro in his invisible trap thorns, gloating at his apparent victory. Noticing Zoro apparently ignoring him, Sommers dropped down to finish his enemy off, only for Zoro to easily cut his way out of his thorny prison and stab Sommers right through the chest. Sommers was left terrified, realizing his opponent was far more capable than he initially realized. [Ch. 1193]
Sommers surmised that Zoro was capable of using Supreme King Haki, but incapable of controlling it. He mocked Zoro for it but acknowledging that he had to end Zoro as soon as possible before Zoro could master his Conqueror's Haki. Despite trying to fight back, he still found himself getting diced apart left and right, his thorns struggling to even hit their intended target. This frustrated him as he realized that Zoro was using him as training dummy. In spite of this, Sommers' regeneration ability remained strong, and he was even able to utilize the Omen to counter a Supreme King Haki attack using Thorn Bulent Dragon Fang. Unfortunately, the ship took the brunt of the damage instead forcing Sommers to stop relying on ranged attacks and to instead fight Zoro more directly. As he proclaimed Zoro lacked the qualities of a king, Sommers' right hand was severed, this time not growing back. Sommers was mortified to witness Zoro now fully channeling Supreme King Haki. He promptly ran for his life, fleeing the ship as he begged Zoro not to kill him. Zoro pursued, managing to strike Sommers on one of the nearby Adam Tree branches.
