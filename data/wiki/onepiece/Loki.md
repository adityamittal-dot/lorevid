# Loki

Source: https://onepiece.fandom.com/wiki/Loki (fetched 2026-10-21). Bracketed [Ch. N] / [Ep. N] are the wiki's own citations.

Loki is the prince of Elbaph, the son of King Harald and Queen Estrid, the younger half-brother of Hajrudin, and the foster son of Ida. [Ch. 1137, Ep. 1167]
An outcast since childhood, Loki was tasked by Harald to kill him to spare Elbaph from the World Government's attempt to control the giants. After doing so, Loki refused to clear his name earning him a reputation as the "Shame of Elbaph", and he instead set out to sea to take revenge against the World Government. Loki was later brought back to Elbaph by Shanks and imprisoned for the next six years [Ch. 1136, Ep. 1166] before being freed by Monkey D. Luffy and Hajrudin to assist them against the World Government's latest attempt to annex the land of giants. [Ch. 1171]
A devotee of the legendary pirate Rocks D. Xebec, Loki has long sought the destruction of the current world that the “Sun God” Nika is prophesied to bring, and in the present day has declared himself as the Sun God who will end the world.
First mentioned in the Whole Cake Island Arc, he is a major ally of the Straw Hat Pirates in the Elbaph Arc.

### Appearance
Loki is a very large giant, believed to be the size of Oars, [Ch. 1141, Ep. 1171] with long, lightly-colored magenta hair. The front of his hair is styled into two thick braids, while the back remains messy and unkempt. His well-defined body is relatively lanky with long legs and muscular, red-tattooed arms. He also sports a pointy goatee, a metallic shell over his nose, and sometimes tends to leave his tongue hanging out while speaking. Having inherited Ancient Giant blood from his father, Loki sports a pair of long black horns extending upwards, with the leftmost one tied in place by a wrapping. [Ch. 1143, Ep. 1173] In addition, he has eyes with slit pupils and black sclera, a feature that has not been seen in any other giant. [Ch. 1153]
Like most giants from Elbaph, Loki dresses in Viking-like fashion, including a black, wide-rimmed helmet that has his horns sticking through, as well as a dark ensemble of gloves, pants, boots, a purple fluffy cape, and a belt sporting a crowned Jolly Roger design worn over his abdomen. Loki also has his eyes covered in bandages, a feature that has been present since childhood.

### Personality
Loki presents himself as a jovial, brash, and confident warrior who follows his own path. Fancying himself as a destroyer, [Ch. 1174] Loki has long addressed his problems by using violence; as a young child, he was little more than a common bully who used his superior strength to justify his destructive rampages. [Ch. 1154] As a young adult, Loki still regularly shows impetuous behavior and a lack of regard for his surroundings. Even when fighting to defend Elbaph, Loki does not care to restrain his lightning-based attacks even though they can ignite fires which pose a great danger to the Sun World. [Ch. 1177] Due to his pride in his destructive behavior, Loki does not like to be seen as kind, friendly, or heroic. He supports Elbaph's historical warmongering culture and has consistently opposed his father Harald's attempts to change it, instead taking considerable inspiration from Rocks D. Xebec's unabashed dream of toppling the current world order. [Ch. 1155] In the present day, he has declared the intention of ending the world as the self-proclaimed "Sun God", indicating he is still interested in causing global upheaval and fulfilling Elbaph's supposedly apocalyptic texts regarding Nika.
Considering himself the strongest in all of Elbaph, Loki does not fear anyone and will make clear to those who wrong him that he will pursue revenge, even if he is in a compromising position where such threats may be met with brutal retaliation. [Ch. 1138, Ep. 1168] Even as a young child, he did not fear Rocks and even challenged him to a duel at one point despite having been attacked and wounded by the pirate before. He has always been fiercely independent, with Rocks being the only man he has ever desired to follow. Loki expressed outrage when Luffy suggested that he join his crew. [Ch. 1152] To get what he wants in the event he is unable to attain it himself, Loki has no issue resorting to intimidation and threats. [Ch. 1131, Ep. 1161] Showing weakness to no one, Loki may even bluff threats to get what he wants if he is in no condition to actually fight. Loki may eschew intimidation in favor of attempting to make a deal, claiming he will repay the other party by eliminating any enemy of their choice, showing the vast confidence Loki has in his power. [Ch. 1151] Loki does his best to ensure that he has the upper hand in these dealings at all times, making the other party swear to secrecy and not being quick to fulfill his promises. [Ch. 1145, Ep. 1175] He will not submit even if an enemy threatens one of his companions, as he believes a warrior should not complain about their way of death, and he will simply use the rage caused by their death to further his own aims. He believes in thoroughly destroying his enemies so as to not be on the receiving end of their own vengeance. [Ch. 1134, Ep. 1164]
In casual conversation, Loki generally acts uncouth, not caring to remember strangers' names and wanting to dictate the discussion. He is quite blunt, with no qualms about belittling the weakness of his friends and allies. Should he hear the name of an enemy, he will make no secret of his hatred of them, though he may claim to be kidding if his words trigger a strong response from the other party. Loki talks roughly even to those he cares about, but his coarse words belie a genuine interest in their well-being, which has been noted by both Harald and Mosa. [Ch. 1151] Loki has shown giant supremacy beliefs, which have historically been held by much of Elbaph's population, saying he refuses to take orders from a human; he also echoed Elbaph's beliefs regarding outsiders to denigrate the foreigner Ida and his half-brother Hajrudin. However, Loki is clearly not as small-minded now, in large part because of the love he received from Elbaph's social outcasts; while he initially hurled accusations that Ida was trying to steal his birth mother's status, Loki later became enraged when he learned that Estrid's village had poisoned Ida because of these very same accusations. [Ch. 1156] He does still possess pride in his heritage, though, which was displayed when he expressed resentment at Hajrudin trusting outsiders to defend Elbaph over him.
Much of Loki's violent and antisocial behavior, in fact, stems from and masks a deep longing for familial love, which was denied to him by his biological mother since the day he was born. It was said that Loki gave up on love when he was just a few months old, and he showed himself to be very distrusting of anyone trying to enter his life, such as his father, and his discomfort with the people of Elbaph led to him going to great lengths to escape and find freedom. Before receiving enough inspiration from Rocks to pursue a large-scale destructive goal, Loki despaired so much as a young child as to become suicidal and unsuccessfully attempt to kill himself at least once. However, Loki has since come to recognize the love that members of his family like Harald and Ida have shown to him, and though he does not care to show much affection, he has done his best to return this love through acts of practical service. [Ch. 1167] Many of Loki's recent outbursts and vendettas have been for the sake of his loved ones, a major example being his destruction of Brewers Village after their actions against Ida, which even the pacifist Harald recognized as a pure demonstration of Loki's love. It was in this moment, while blinded with rage, that Loki unabashedly stated his love for someone for the first time, and Loki has been shown to weep openly when he loses people he cares for. [Ch. 1166] [Ch. 1171] It is apparent that Loki has matured enough that he no longer directly harms others for no reason, least of all his family. Though he went through with it out of necessity, even Loki considers patricide to be an unconscionable act. Although he did not consider it worthwhile to make his case before Elbaph regarding his role in Harald's death, Loki possesses strong regret for it and cannot help but interject when directly accused of cold-hearted murder.
Like several other characters, Loki has a unique laughing style: "Dogahaha".

### Relationships

#### Family

##### Harald
Since first meeting his son at a few months old, Harald loved Loki and took pride in his progeny. However, Harald's frequent absences and Loki already forsaking love caused him to resent and distrust his father. At age 15, Loki did not believe his father cared enough about him to return to Elbaph if he was in trouble and was surprised when Harald indeed did so, though he tried to rationalize it as a coincidence. In their limited time together, Harald was shown to react strongly whenever Loki lashed out, though he believed his younger son would grow to help Elbaph and formally requested that Loki and Hajrudin do their best to work together.
Despite their disagreements, on the day Harald died, he put his trust in his son to kill him and assume the throne of Elbaph, while Loki, though loyal enough to obey this command and knowing his father was being corrupted, took no pleasure in having to kill his father. Loki ultimately expressed his love for his father in a resentful outburst about the toll being put on him to commit patricide, and Harald, recognizing the kindness and love behind Loki's coarse words and violent actions, told his son he loved him too, and Loki wept uncontrollably during and immediately after the incident.
Though he has always opposed Harald's pacifistic nature and attempted reforms, Loki knew and understood that his father only had the benefit of Elbaph in mind and was devastated at the ironic tragic end Harald received for it. Going against his father's last request, he maintained silence about the truth to protect Harald's reputation as a great king. Though he allowed the rest of Elbaph to believe he had killed Harald for personal gain, in truth Loki is highly bothered by this fictitious idea and could not help but speak up for his true feelings when Hajrudin directly accused him. During his first direct confrontation with Imu, Loki proudly declared that Harald was his father. [Ch. 1180]

##### Estrid
Being her biological son, in infancy Loki craved his mother Estrid's touch. However, because of his strange-looking face and eyes, Estrid was repulsed enough by her son to immediately reject him and even attempt to kill him. [Ch. 1153] Though this murder attempt failed, Estrid made sure that the guards kept Loki away from her and covered his eyes; this devastated Loki as a result and caused him to give up on love. Though Estrid died when Loki was no more than a year old, he has never forgotten her treatment of him, even remembering how she cast him into the Underworld. Thus, he possesses nothing but absolute hatred toward his birth mother and eventually came to recognize Ida as his real mother instead.

##### Hajrudin
Loki and his older half-brother Hajrudin had a troubled relationship for a long time, with a young Loki taking advantage of his superior size and social status to physically bully the smaller Hajrudin and belittle his illegitimate parentage, while Hajrudin was trying to defend their father's dominion from Loki's violent outbursts. Hajrudin also became enraged when Loki degraded his mother Ida and her outsider status. Although Harald asked his sons to work together to help Elbaph in the future, there is no indication that they ever put aside their differences and collaborated during Harald's lifetime.
Loki did not inform Hajrudin of the true circumstances behind Harald's murder, allowing Hajrudin to believe that his half-brother was a heartless killer along with the rest of Elbaph. In the present day, Hajrudin desperately tried to stop Monkey D. Luffy from freeing Loki, and as he stood by a subdued Loki, Hajrudin became so enraged over Harald's death that he visibly considered killing his half-brother. Hajrudin's accusations caused Loki to defend himself; finally, at Scopper Gaban's suggestion, Loki went ahead and shared the true account. With Gaban's backing, Hajrudin believed Loki's words and apologized for his accusations, asking Loki to fight alongside him for Elbaph's sake.

##### Ida
Despite not being her offspring, Hajrudin's mother Ida always cared for Loki and doted on him whenever she could, being aware of his loneliness and making clear that he was always welcome to live with her and Hajrudin. Initially, Loki resented Ida's attention toward him and lashed out at her, belittling her outsider status and accusing her of trying to usurp his birth mother's status as queen. As time went on, however, Loki started hanging around Ida's bar more often, and his perception of her softened as a result, though he did not readily make this apparent. When Ida fell ill, Loki delivered her a bull to eat, citing that he wanted the bar to be open so he could keep hanging out there; one of Ida's attendants noted this as a display of love from the impudent Loki. Ultimately, Loki made clear his true feelings about Ida upon learning that his birth mother's village had poisoned her; his care for Ida drove him to burn down the village in retaliation, and during his attack he openly declared that Ida was his only mother.

##### Regent
After Estrid's death, her older brother was placed in charge of Loki's care. The regent made no secret of his disdain for Loki, calling him a "horrid thing" and feeding him rotten scraps. Loki has not been shown reacting to the regent's abuse but was seen smirking after it was reported that the regent had notably been found dead in the Underworld.

#### Elbaph
As the prince of Elbaph, Loki was born as a respected individual within the kingdom of giants; his birth was toasted during the Winter Solstice Festival. However, Estrid's hatred for her son coupled with a series of great hardships on the land caused rumors to spread that Loki was "accursed" and that his curse was the reason for these tribulations, making him an outcast despite his royal status. In turn, Loki inflicted wanton destruction on Elbaph's villages as a child whenever Harald was away, causing dread among the people. Loki hated his homeland, viewing its shift toward pacifism as pathetic, even as a child. expressed the desire to set sail and live freely. The residents of Brewers Village, where Loki's biological mother came from, seemed to like him, but only because of the increased social standing they received from Estrid's queenship. They informed Loki that they had poisoned Ida under the impression that he would approve, but this actually enraged Loki and caused him to burn the village down, leading to him being imprisoned.
After killing Harald, Loki had no interest in explaining the ordeal to his countrymen and ruling over them because he knew how they would receive the story, not caring to try and persuade them that he was not a depraved murderer. Indeed, this belief quickly took hold in Elbaph, and as Loki expected, they reconciled this belief with the violent acts (both justified and not) he had previously committed and his accursed reputation to depict him as a villain. The only residents to know the truth of the matter—Jarul, Scopper Gaban, and the surviving castle guards—were instructed by Loki to not divulge the truth, and the guards accompanied him out to sea. When Loki was taken down and brought back to Elbaph, most of the populace wished to have him executed; Hajrudin remarked that the sentiment would likely resurface even after six years if Loki reappeared before his countrymen. It was only in the present day, when Jarul told the populace what really happened, that they learned of Loki's innocence. [Ch. 1172] In spite of Loki's general disdain for his country, he has expressed interest in defending it from the World Government's attack in the present day, viewing it as important for natives like himself to defend their homeland.
Loki has befriended the giant animals residing in the Underworld, and they have been his companions since his childhood. In current times, they have defended the imprisoned Loki from anyone who may threaten him. When Gunko and Shamrock tried using one of them as a hostage, Loki said he would be sad and angry for the beast to be killed but would not give in to the Knights' demands to spare it. While imprisoned, Loki has also recruited humans who have invaded Elbaph as allies, though he has a low view of their strength and considers them little more than cannon fodder, which he has said right in front of them.

##### Jarul
Loki and Jarul were first seen interacting when they were both summoned to Aurust Castle on the day of Harald's death. Loki was rude to the old warrior, joking about him living too long and arguing with him about Harald's reforms; Jarul was appalled at the impetuous violence Loki displayed. However, the two then worked together to stop the corrupted Harald, with Jarul risking his life so Loki could acquire the legendary Devil Fruit before Harald. [Ch. 1169] Afterwards, Jarul reluctantly agreed to Loki's demand that the truth of the affair be kept a secret, but once Loki was brought back to Elbaph, Jarul worked to spare his life by pleading for amnesty from execution. When the Knights of God launched a full-on assault of Elbaph, Jarul took this development to declare Loki's innocence to the rest of the populace.

#### Rocks D. Xebec
Loki has idolized the pirate Rocks D. Xebec since soon after they first met. In their initial meeting, Loki considered Rocks as just another human and was outraged to receive orders from him, resulting in a fight that Loki quickly lost. Afterwards, however, Loki overheard Rocks telling Harald his plan to become the King of the World and was awestruck, believing he had found his calling in life. Loki once stated to Rocks that he believed the pirate was Nika, suggesting he anticipated the pirate triggering the prophesied end of the world. For the next 18 years Loki closely followed Rocks' exploits, and every time the pirate came to Elbaph Loki would plead to join his crew. Rocks always refused and brushed him off, and when Loki challenged him to a fight, Rocks did not hold back and brutalized him, though this did not change Loki's admiration of him. Though he witnessed Rocks eventually end his friendship with Harald, Loki did not give up hope that the pirate would return to Elbaph again, until one day he learned that Rocks had died, which caused him to weep openly.

#### Monkey D. Luffy
When the pirate Monkey D. Luffy ran into him by chance, Loki immediately attempted to enlist him to find the key to his chains. He showed frustration with Luffy when the pirate interjected and became distracted in their conversation and found himself being attacked by Luffy after casually insulting Shanks, who, unbeknownst to him, is a hero of the pirate. Still, Loki took advantage of this relationship by offering to reveal information about Shanks in return for being freed, and despite knowing of Loki's status in Elbaph, Luffy agreed to this deal. Because he was unsure of Loki's true motives, Luffy relied on the belief that he would beat the giant in combat if he threatened his friends.
After Luffy's attempt to free Loki was interrupted by Hajrudin's crew, Luffy attempted to feed Loki but initially overwhelmed him with careless servings of meat and hot soup, angering the giant. Recognizing the tension between Loki and his countrymen, Luffy supported his bid for freedom by inviting the giant to join his crew, which outraged the fiercely independent Loki. While he was ultimately willing to work together with Luffy, Loki remained dismissive of him. [Ch. 1178]

#### Shanks
Shanks, who also fought in Aurust Castle on the day of Harald's death, was the one who captured Loki six years ago for reasons unknown. Loki still holds a grudge towards Shanks up to this day, even projecting his anger towards Shanks' twin brother, Shamrock. Loki has described Shanks as a "coward," although it is unknown if this was entirely meant to rile Luffy up or if he perceived Shanks' methods in capturing him in that way.

#### Charlotte Lola
Loki is said to have fallen in love with Charlotte Lola at first sight. However, Lola did not reciprocate the prince's feelings and ran away in order to find her own true love. Lola's rejection of Loki is notable given she would go on to develop a reputation for proposing to thousands of people, often at first sight. Although it is unknown why Loki fell in love with Lola, it can be assumed that his love for her is at least more than simply based on their physical appearance, as he was unwilling to marry Chiffon in her stead. Given that Loki would not have met Lola until he exiled himself from Elbaph, the true nature and purpose of the proposed wedding are currently uncertain, as is Big Mom's assertion that this marriage would have aligned her with Elbaph.

#### Enemies

##### World Government
Loki has always hated the World Government, particularly for its role in the demise of Rocks. He said he could not forgive Harald for becoming their lapdog, and after the government's corruption forced Loki to slay his father, he decided to enact a campaign of retribution against it despite its massive scale making his quest extremely risky. Loki made himself well-known to the World Government during his time at sea, being regarded as dangerous enough to warrant a bounty of 2,600,000,000. Six years following his captivity, the World Government was interested enough in Loki's power to have Figarland Shamrock and Manmayer Gunko personally recruit him into the Knights of God. Loki bluntly rejected this invitation, as he considered the Celestial Dragons as "garbage", causing an irate Gunko to attack him and his companions, though to no avail when Loki declared he would never stoop from a warrior to the level of "trash" as the Celestial Dragons. Afterwards, Loki was cocky enough to threaten revenge on them, provoking Shamrock himself into action and causing him to inflict severe wounds on Loki using Cerberus. When he is freed, Loki unleashes his wrath upon the Knights of God by smashing the crumbling Shepherd Sommers.

##### Nerona Imu
As the King of the World and secret leader of the World Government who manipulated Harald to enslave Elbaph, Loki regards Imu as his archenemy and wants him dead for his actions in enslaving Harald and making him kill his father.  Loki first learnt about Imu from Shanks. Despite the latter's warnings about Imu's power and influence, Loki still chose to risk taking revenge alone though was never able to successfully find him. They first met when Imu came to Elbaph and the latter admitted he was the one who manipulated Harald to enslave Elbaph. Loki was profoundly furious at how Imu regarded Harald as his servant and Imu coldly disregard coercing his father to an early death by claiming to be the one who was inconvenienced from having to give and lose a Depths Covenant for nothing. When Imu demanded that Loki and Luffy surrender or die, Loki still defied Imu's authority, causing Imu to declare that both Loki and Luffy were to be erased by him.
As he takes great pride in Elbaph's warrior culture, Loki was annoyed at Imu refusing to take their duel seriously and was confused by his ramblings regarding the Void Century. Loki was similarly furious at Imu for using underhanded tactics in order to kidnap the children of Elbaph.

### Abilities and Powers
As prince of Elbaph, he previously held authority and influence over the warriors and citizens of Elbaph, which is said to be the strongest kingdom in the entire world. [Ch. 847, Ep. 812] Following his sentencing as a criminal and subsequent punishment, however, Loki does not appear to hold royal privileges anymore. Still, in spite of his current predicament, Loki has retained influence over a number of wild beasts that he befriended in his childhood, as well as a small group of loyal humans. This allows Loki to still threaten anyone into cooperating, assuming they manage to survive long enough in Elbaph's Underworld to meet him.
The World Government issued a special bounty of 2,600,000,000 on Loki, although the specifics behind why that bounty is considered "special" to begin with are currently unknown. This consideration has led him to being offered a position amongst the Knights of God.
Loki is shown to have strong willpower, having endured massive bursts of Supreme King Haki from both his father and Xebec during one of their duels as a child. [Ch. 1158] Along with Monkey D. Luffy, Loki is immune to Imu's "Domi Reversi".

#### Tactical Skills
Loki is reputedly an extremely cunning and conniving trickster who will say anything in order to accomplish his goals. [Ch. 1141, Ep. 1171] Upon noticing a connection between Luffy and Shanks, Loki provoked Luffy by insulting Shanks and then used his knowledge of Shanks' whereabouts as leverage in a deal with Luffy to secure his freedom. [Ch. 1131, Ep. 1161] After being injured by the Knights of God and partly freed from his chains by Luffy and Zoro, Loki demonstrated acting skills by downplaying the severity of his condition and putting on a bluff of being prepared to fight and lay waste to Elbaph if he was not freed from his last shackle. [Ch. 1142, Ep. 1172] [Ch. 1143, Ep. 1173]

#### Physical Abilities
At roughly the same size as the ancient giant Oars, Loki is large and especially strong even among the giants, being noted to have required all of Elbaph's finest warriors to keep him in check even while imprisoned. He considers himself to be the strongest warrior in all of Elbaph, and many giants believe that Loki has enough power to destroy the world should he ever be freed once again.He is shown to be capable of fighting toe-to-toe against someone as extremely powerful as Nerona Imu in a fight.
Even as a newborn, Loki already possessed monstrous strength and durability, having survived a fall from Elbaph's Sun World to its Underworld (something that can kill a normal adult giant), decapitating a large bear, and then climbing back up the Treasure Tree Adam on his own. He later attempted to commit suicide by throwing himself into the Underworld but failed, receiving only scratches as a result. At age 10, he was able to single-handedly kill many more beasts in Elbaph's Underworld, as well as control the rest into doing his bidding. [Ch. 1142, Ep. 1172] During his rampages as a young child, he was capable of terrorizing adult giants and easily overwhelmed his half-brother Hajrudin, whom he was bigger than despite being 18 years younger.
Loki has impressive reaction speed and is capable of dodging an incoming Gear 4 strike at point-blank range while chained up.
Loki's endurance in particular appears to be phenomenal, as he has been chained in seastone for nearly six years, yet has not succumbed to the freezing climate of Elbaph's Underworld (in fact he seems to be completely unbothered by it) and even managed to almost escape at one point. Even after being beaten up repeatedly by Gunko using her Devil Fruit powers and stabbed through the chest by multiple gigantic swords, Loki could stand up immediately after being partly freed, lift his hammer, and put on a bluff that he was able to fight before succumbing to his injuries. [Ch. 1141, Ep. 1171] Despite Seastone weakening him as a Devil Fruit user, he has said that having only one leg trapped in it would do little to contain his power and promptly demonstrated this by displaying enough might to swing his massive warhammer without issue. After he collapsed, Gerd said it would likely take months for Loki's wounds to fully heal, but Loki managed to recover and enter the fray no more than a few minutes after receiving proper treatment and a hearty meal, showing a remarkable recovery rate. During a fight with Imu, he has shown to be able to withstand two direct Omen attacks from Imu and still standing and fight. It took Imu to send Omen directly to already existing wound to put Loki down, yet he still remain conscious. Even after that, Loki returned to the fight against Imu just moments later after receiving first aid by Gerd and Chopper and still capable of performing powerful attacks.

#### Devil Fruit
Loki ate the Ryu Ryu no Mi, Model: Nidhöggr, a Mythical Zoan-type Devil Fruit whose power is considered to be legendary by the citizens of Elbaph. While transformed, Loki becomes the largest dragon in the world, and due to his Ancient Giant heritage providing great size to begin with, the creature that he becomes is proportionally even more colossal in size, allowing several giants to fit comfortably on his enormous back and causing him to barely fit in the halls of Aurust Castle, a place designed for giants to live in. He gained claws and phenomenal strength that enabled him to dominate Harald in battle with little trouble, while Shanks and Gaban, with the Aurust Castle giant guards' assistance, could only stall him. [Ch. 1170] He becomes capable of breathing powerful blasts of lightning, and his transformed state also has wings that allow him to fly. [Ch. 1171]

#### Haki

##### Supreme King Haki
Loki is one of the few people in the world able to use Supreme King Haki and has enough mastery over it to infuse it in his attacks, something a "handful" can do. Against the immortal body of the Depths Covenant-bearing Harald, Loki's initial attacks managed to knock him back to his senses while slowing his regeneration. Ultimately, Loki managed to kill Harald with a direct blow from a Supreme King-infused Ragnir, which is currently the only time an outside party has been shown to fully override this immortality. [Ch. 1170] Despite this, Loki was unaware of his infusion capabilities and the role they played in Harald's death until Scopper Gaban mentioned it as the key to harming other immortal Knights of God. [Ch. 1152] In his Mythical Zoan-transformed dragon state, he can even infuse his Supreme King Haki into his lightning-based attacks. [Ch. 1175]

##### Observation Haki
Loki can use Observation Haki, allowing him to sense the arrival of Nerona Imu on Elbaph. [Ch. 1179]

#### Weapons
Loki's primary weapon is a large warhammer named Ragnir, which has acquired the power of a Mythical Zoan-type Devil Fruit, the Risu Risu no Mi, Model: Ratatoskr, allowing it to become a gigantic ice squirrel. When Loki swings his hammer, he can call down gargantuan bolts of lightning from the clouds to strike whatever he has hit, or he can freeze them solid by encasing them in ice. [Ch. 1171]
In the past, he wielded a similar, albeit much more ornate, warhammer, [Ch. 1152] which shattered during his duel with Ragnir. [Ch. 1170] In his childhood, Loki wielded a large, spiked club. [Ch. 1143, Ep. 1173]

### History

#### Past

##### The Cursed Prince and Rocks the Pirate
Loki was born 63 years ago, not long before the Winter Solstice Festival, in Aurust Castle. Before his birth, his father, Harald, was believed to have prophesied that he would be killed by his son. Loki's mother, Estrid, was immediately repulsed upon seeing his face and did not give the infant Loki the touch that he craved but rather threw him into the Underworld in an attempt to kill him. However, Loki survived the fall and promptly started climbing the Treasure Tree Adam back to the Sun World. Afterwards, Estrid fell ill, and Loki attempted to crawl into her bedroom, but she had her guards keep him away and ordered them to cover his eyes. Estrid then died, and Loki met Harald for the first time following her funeral. However, Loki had already abandoned hope for love at only a few months old. Because Harald was always away at sea, he left Loki in the care of Estrid's older brother, who hated and abused him. However, Loki soon managed to kill Estrid's lucky horse, Asla, and by the time he was 15, the regent was found dead in the Underworld.
As he grew out of infancy, Loki started going on destructive rampages around Elbaph whenever Harald was away. 48 years ago, he and his animal companions attacked a village that Harald had recently rebuilt, and his older half-brother, Hajrudin, rose to stop him. Loki responded by striking Hajrudin with a club, belittling his brother's illegitimate parentage as well as Harald's ideals of pacifism. In truth, however, Loki rampaged in a fruitless attempt at replacing the love he was never given. Soon after this, he attempted suicide by throwing himself into the Underworld but proved too strong to die. Right after landing, Loki encountered a group of human pirates led by Rocks D. Xebec.
Rocks had come looking for Harald, and Loki revealed that his father was away. Rocks told the boy to have a message sent to Harald that he was gravely wounded, but Loki refused. Rocks then responded by actually injuring Loki with a sword strike, ensuring the message would be sent. Harald made his way back to Elbaph to check on Loki, who was privately surprised that his father had done this. He then overheard Harald and Rocks' conversation in which Rocks revealed his dream of world conquest, a goal that inspired Loki and gave him a new outlook on life. Over the next four years, Loki continued getting into trouble; one time, the royal guards had to save him from drowning after he tried setting out to sea. Hajrudin's mother, Ida, fawned over Loki and invited him to live with her and Hajrudin in the village, but he refused and insulted her, saying he wanted to live freely like Rocks.
After this, Loki begged to join Rocks every time he came to Elbaph to recruit Harald to his crew, but Rocks refused every time. When Rocks was away, Loki typically hung around Ida's bar. Loki even challenged Rocks at one point after being refused, causing the pirate to brutalize him in response. Another time, Loki told Rocks that he believed the pirate to be Nika, and Rocks revealed to him that he was seeking Harald in order to lead the legendary Galleila band. 40 years ago, Loki watched as Harald got into a fight with Rocks, severing the two men's friendship. For the next two years, he continued following Rocks' exploits through the newspaper while waiting for the pirate to return to Elbaph, which he never did. [Ch. 1158] 38 years ago, Loki read the news about Rocks' demise on God Valley and wept openly.

##### The Death of Harald
Soon after this, Harald gathered Loki and Hajrudin, expressing his desire that they work together to support Elbaph in his absence. 23 years later, when Ida fell ill, Loki delivered her a bull to eat so he could hang out at her bar again. However, while in Estrid's hometown of Brewers Village, Loki learned that the residents there had poisoned Ida to prevent her from ever becoming queen. Enraged, Loki attacked and burned down the village as he declared that Ida was the only mother he had. Loki was imprisoned as a result of this, and he silently mourned Ida when she died. [Ch. 1168]
Fourteen years ago, Loki was summoned to Aurust Castle by Harald. At the castle entrance he met with Jarul, and the two briefly argued about Harald's pacifistic reforms before Loki impetuously kicked down the door to the throne room, where he and Jarul were stunned to see Harald being impaled by several guards. However, Harald regenerated from these wounds before retaliating against the guards and then clashing with his son, who had no idea what was going on. Loki and Jarul then managed to pin Harald to the ground, and he confessed that the World Government had tricked him and was on the verge of using him as a puppet to enslave Elbaph's population as soldiers. Harald then asked Jarul to feed Elbaph's treasured Devil Fruit to Loki and asked Loki to kill him and take the throne. But while Loki was heading toward the treasure, Harald lost his mind again and slew many of the guards as he pursued Loki with the intent of obtaining the fruit first.
Harald was delayed long enough for Loki to reach the treasure room, where he saw a chest and the hammer Ragnir. To his shock, Ragnir started moving to attack him on its own, and Loki tried avoiding it but could not manage to get around it to the chest. As Ragnir started spinning towards him, Loki met it head-on with his own Supreme King Haki-infused hammer, causing a great collision that shattered Loki's hammer. Ragnir bounced around on the ground before transforming into a beaten-up squirrel. Defeated by Loki, Ragnir guided him to the chest and watched as he ate the Devil Fruit, the Ryu Ryu no Mi, Model: Nidhöggr.
Loki then transformed into the Nidhöggr, taking up much of the space in the castle. He headed toward Harald, and managed to easily overpower and heavily wound his father in his Beast Form. As Loki returned to his giant form and readied Ragnir to strike, Harald managed to regain his mind and expressed pride in his son, even thanking him for showing his love for Ida by his actions against Brewers Village. Loki expressed resentment toward his father for being forced to kill him, and Harald told his son he loved him. Loki then struck Harald in the chest with a Supreme King-infused Ragnir, successfully killing Harald despite his immortality, and wept as he did so.
Following this, Loki sat weeping over his father's body as he remarked bitterly on the fate Harald sustained for loving his country. Later, Loki informed Jarul, Scopper Gaban, and Shanks that he would take the surviving guards and head out to sea, forgoing his father's wish for him to lead Elbaph because he knew he could not tell the country what really happened, and he forbade anyone else from revealing the truth. Instead, he wished to use his powers to avenge his father and the soldiers he had been forced to slay. In spite of Shanks' attempts to convince him otherwise, Loki remained sure of himself in his task, even upon learning of Rocks' failure to defeat the World Government as well. [Ch. 1189]
Loki went out to sea, allowing Elbaph to believe that he had killed his father for personal gain. In the following eight years, he attained significant infamy by sinking Marine and World Government ships using the same tactics used by Harald against pirates, enough to earn one of the world's highest bounties. At one point, he encountered Charlotte Lola, the daughter of Big Mom of the Four Emperors, and immediately professed love for her. Big Mom arranged for them to be married, but Lola ran away, and Loki was not fooled when Big Mom attempted to use Lola's twin sister, Chiffon, in her place, resulting in no marriage taking place.
Six years before the present day, Loki was stopped by Shanks and brought back to Elbaph, where the kingdom's warriors subdued him and sentenced him to crucifixion. With the aid of Jarul pleading for clemency, Loki was instead imprisoned in the Underworld, bound to the Adam Tree with Seastone chains. In spite of his solitude, Loki was able to rally the beasts of the Underworld around him, as well as humans who were enemies of Elbaph. Additionally, he possessed a Snow Den Den Mushi that allowed him to communicate with a mysterious figure named Mosa, whom he had never actually met in person.

#### Final Saga

##### Elbaph Arc
Loki was approached by Monkey D. Luffy, and they shared their names and aspirations. Revealing the nature of his imprisonment to Luffy, Loki offered to eliminate any rival crew of Luffy's choosing if the pirate acquired the key to his chains but brought out his underlings to try and intimidate Luffy. When one of Loki's human underlings mentioned Shanks and Luffy recognized the name, Loki responded by insulting Shanks, causing Luffy to move to hit Loki with a powerful punch, which he dodged. When Luffy asked why Shanks had been here, Loki replied that he would not share that answer for nothing. Luffy agreed to go and get the key, with Loki promising to share what he knew about Shanks, including the pirate's current location; he told Luffy not to divulge their meeting to anyone. [Ch. 1132, Ep. 1162]
Loki then conversed with Mosa, who informed him of a traumatic event that they had recently gone through. Soon after this, Loki was approached by the Knights of God Figarland Shamrock and Manmayer Gunko, who offered an invitation to join the Knights. He immediately rejected their offer and told a nearby wolf to attack them but watched as Gunko strangled it. Shamrock wondered if Loki would join in exchange for its life, but Loki refused. Gunko then started attacking Loki, conjuring arrows to strike and impale him while subduing more of his beast companions, but Loki did not waver. Shamrock ultimately elected to move on, but as he and Gunko departed, Loki noted that Shamrock looked identical to Shanks. Shamrock revealed that Shanks was his estranged twin brother, and Loki remarked that the Knights would feel his wrath after he was freed. In response to this threat, Shamrock had his sword Cerberus transform into a three-headed canine and lunge toward Loki, impaling him in the chest with a blade protruding from each mouth as Shamrock and Gunko flew off.
Later, Luffy and his crewmate Roronoa Zoro came to Loki with the key, only to find him incapacitated from his heavy wounds. As Luffy started unlocking the chains, the New Giant Warrior Pirates and Sanji fell into the Underworld. Hajrudin warned Luffy against freeing Loki, but now only chained by the left ankle, Loki stood up and moved to grab Ragnir. Bluffing that he was still at full strength, Loki struck the Adam Tree with Ragnir, summoning a lightning bolt that ignited areas in the Sun World. Loki boasted that he would keep inflicting this damage until he was freed, and Luffy moved to attack him, but Loki's bluff then ran out as he collapsed from his wounds.
Gerd quickly tended to Loki's injuries, but Hajrudin contemplated killing his brother for murdering their father. Regaining consciousness, Loki told his brother to consider their surroundings and asked if Hajrudin truly believed that he killed their father with murder in his heart. While eating food prepared by the pirates, Loki refused to tell Luffy what he knew about Shanks until he was fully freed and acknowledged that he idolized a pirate much like Luffy did with Shanks. Loki then rested after eating his fill of food. [Ch. 1146, Ep. 1176] Later, Scopper Gaban fell down into the Underworld, and Loki was left with Gerd and Goldberg while the others went to find him. Mosa then called and informed Loki that they would not be able to call him again for a while, thanking him for the companionship he had provided as Loki expressed worry at his friend's situation.
The rest of the group returned with Tony Tony Chopper and a wounded Gaban in tow, and Chopper told them how Elbaph was under attack by the Knights of God and big monsters. Right then, a powerful burst of Supreme King Haki erupted from the Sun World and was felt all the way down in the Underworld, with Loki saying the others would surely need his help now. As the pirates started heading toward the Sun World, Loki, having come to understand Gaban's words about dealing with the Knights and their immortality, pointed out that he had the experience to assist in this battle and told Hajrudin to free him. Hajrudin refused despite Loki's protests, saying he could not trust Loki after he killed their father. Luffy then intervened by offering for Loki to join his crew, which the giant refused in outrage. However, Gaban advocated for Loki based on what he saw on the day of Harald's death and suggested that Loki should tell his brother what really happened. Despite his trauma from that day, Loki reluctantly went forward with sharing his account of the events.
Once Loki gave his account, Hajrudin accepted that he was absolved from blame and unlocked his remaining chain, apologizing for doubting Loki's love for their father. Right then, a Draugr MMA dropped down into the Underworld, and Loki offered to warm up against it while the others took the lift. Free for the first time in six years, Loki leaped into the air with Luffy on his shoulder and struck the Draugr with Ragnir, encasing it in ice. Afterward, Loki transformed into his beast form, while Luffy entered Gear 5, with the pair and Ragnir beginning their ascent to the Sun World. On the way, due to Sommers' meddling, the children who had been taken hostage were instead doomed to fall, with Luffy managing to cushion their impact as they fortunately landed on Loki's back. Annoyed at accidentally helping others, Loki demanded they get off his back at the first possible chance. Moments later, upon his and Luffy's arrival in the Sun World, snow and lightning began to rain from the sky, with the entire island shaking from the overwhelming power of their presence. [Ch. 1174]
Once they arrived in the Western Village, Loki had all of those on his back disembark, despite the dangers currently present. One of said dangers was Sommers, who was intent on just killing the parents and their children for his own sick pleasure, rather than completing the mission. However, an attack by Luffy left him nearly incapacitated, with Loki dealing the finishing blow by simply crushing the Knight under one of his massive claws. Afterward, he, Luffy, and Ragnir left to hunt down the remaining MMAs in the Sun World. Using Thorheim, he blew up a group of them. [Ch. 1175]
The trio continued fighting across the Sun World until they ran into Luffy's crew. Luffy was happy to see his crewmate's shock at Loki's power, but the situation came to a grinding halt when Nami called the trio out for their sloppiness. As it turned out, Loki's lightning breath had been contributing to the fires in the Sun World, which Nami found particularly outrageous since she was trying to extinguish them. Although Loki did not care about her thoughts, Luffy did, causing him to lash out at Loki for getting him in trouble. Loki responded with a blast of lightning and a claim of just wishing to crush threats to Elbaph but became confused when his attack had no effect on Luffy's rubbery body. Before the two could argue further, both took notice of the source of the strange Haki from earlier, now just near them. Loki confirmed his suspicions upon their arrival, pointing out the Imu-possessed Gunko as the source of it. Loki agreed with Luffy's plan to fight them as Imu ordered the pair to either submit or die.
Loki attempted to blast Imu with his lighting breath, only to miss as Imu flew out of view. Before Luffy could assist, Imu projected himself from Gunko's body, taking a more esoteric form. While mentioning his desire to erase the pair like he did with Rocks in the past, Imu attempted to use Domi Reversi, stabbing Loki and Luffy with tendrils. Although Imu attempted to turn the two against one another, the transformation failed, with both easily freeing themselves. Loki was annoyed but also intrigued by Imu's mention of his idol. Realizing his beast form was simply too large for such an opponent, Loki returned to normal as Luffy unleashed Gomu Gomu no Dawn Gatling, disorienting Imu. Using Ragnir, Loki leaped in and struck Imu using Niflheim, freezing him in a block of ice. Afterward, Loki resigned himself to rest after having to finish the fight so quickly and rejecting an exhausted Luffy's request for food.
In the end, however, Loki decided he was hungry as well and made his way to the Walrus School with Ragnir and Luffy. He was at first confused that the whole place was frozen but was corrected by a nearby Lilith, who informed him that its thermal energy had simply been absorbed. Fittingly enough, the school's lunch supplies also had their thermal energy removed, resulting in frozen meals. Loki passed Luffy and Ragnir their share and insisted Luffy take care of himself first over his wounded crewmates. Although Luffy was concerned that Gunko may have been killed by Ragnir's ability, Loki reassured him that she was simply flash-frozen, would not die, and could only be unfrozen by Ragnir. Unfortunately, Loki was unaware of Imu's esoteric nature, which allowed him to simply teleport back to Mary Geoise while leaving Gunko in the ice. This error in judgment proved to be a critical mistake, as Imu returned to Elbaph in-person, causing a massive magic circle to appear over Aurust Castle, alongside a massive burst of Supreme King Haki, which Loki realized was the same as the one from before.
Realizing that this powerful Haki was now heading directly toward himself and his allies, Loki hastily heated up the food in the school's kitchen, accidentally causing it all to become burnt. Loki ignored Luffy's protests as he scarfed down as much as possible, ordering Luffy to stock up on as much energy as he could for the incoming threat. Moments later, Imu touched down nearby, freeing his subordinate Rimoshifu Killingham and easily defeating Sanji. Loki stepped out to confront him, demanding to know why he had come to Elbaph. Imu revealed his connection to Harald, revealing himself to be responsible for how he acted as he did 14 years prior, and that he was there to find out who had killed his powerful subordinate. Loki declared that he was the one who killed Harald, insulting Imu for daring to think he would not be present for such a confrontation. [Ch. 1180]
Loki proceeded to blame Imu for Harald's early death, receiving an unsympathetic reply about how it was actually Imu who suffered the most from his loss. Enraged by the audacity, Loki attempted to smash Imu using Ragnir, only for Imu to counter using an Omen. Loki confusedly demanded to know how Imu's power worked, only for things to worsen as Imu somehow utilized gigantification to grow to a giant size as well. While lecturing him on how all beings possessed the Omen, Imu utilized his better agility to get above Loki and use Nemesis, stabbing him through the shoulder. Imu continued lecturing as Loki struggled to fight back, eventually hitting the prince with an explosive Omen and asking if he would like to be granted the power of God. However, Loki remained standing and swung Ragnir at Imu, which the latter dodged to then appear behind his foe. Loki rejected Imu's offer, chalking it up to a deal with the devil. While Imu recalled a previous meeting with Joy Boy, Loki took advantage and struck him with Ragnir, sending him flying into Elbaph's Adam Tree. Quickly transforming back into his Nidhöggr form, Loki fired a blast of lightning using Thorheim, although Imu simply blocked it. [Ch. 1181]
Although his enhanced size granted him a significant firepower advantage, Loki became annoyed once more at his inability to easily track Imu. Fortunately, Imu made no attempt to flee and even provoked Ragnir to join the fight. Using an ice cloud created by Ragnir, Loki fired lightning into it, creating powerful projectiles. Imu countered using the Omen, using projectiles of his own. The resulting clash covered the battlefield in explosions. [Ch. 1182] The two eventually stopped clashing, prompting Imu to insult Ragnir further for the latter's insistence on seeking revenge against the former. Loki found himself confused by Imu's ramblings, instead being more concerned with his enemy's unending resilience. Upon Imu's summoning of a blade using the Omen, Loki called down lightning from the sky, resulting in a clash. [Ch. 1183] In the momentary downtime from their stalemate, Loki insulted Imu for the government's apparent cowardly decision to take Elbaph's children hostage. Imu was surprised at Loki's ignorance, informing him that the giants had been subdued 800 years prior using the exact same method. While Imu declared that his victory was assured, Loki attempted to go on the offensive again, only for Luffy to interrupt, punching Imu in the face while ordering him to leave Elbaph. [Ch. 1186]
Unfortunately, Imu recovered rather quickly, sending a pair of Omen projectiles toward his enemies. Meanwhile, Loki complimented Luffy on the successful hit but saved face by claiming he had done most of the damage already. Upon being struck by the tiny Omens, Loki was left confused, especially as they began to spread around his wound from being stabbed earlier. Moments later, Imu activated the second part of his ability, taking Loki down with a massive sword made of the Omen. Loki was left severely wounded, collapsing near the other Straw Hats and dropping Ragnir. [Ch. 1187] During Luffy's subsequent fight with Imu, Luffy stated his intention to avenge the former's death, much to Loki's annoyance. Loki announced that he was still alive, prompting Imu to send a flurry of Omen projectiles to finish him off. Loki ordered Ragnir to flee, but fortunately, Jinbe jumped in, stopping them before they could hit. Although still annoyed, Loki allowed Jinbe to remain as his protector while Luffy continued his battle with Imu. [Ch. 1188]
Loki listened in on the Straw Hats' conversation about Luffy's fight, becoming annoyed when Jinbe mentioned how he had been defeated. Moments later, he was suddenly rammed by Hajrudin, Gerd, and Chopper in a svarr, having been sent by Jarul to assist. Gerd was shocked by the severity of Loki's wounds, annoying him further. When asked by Hajrudin about his knowledge of Imu, Loki recalled Shanks' warning to him years prior about Imu's extensive range of powers. Loki cursed himself for failing to even come close when he had sought to do so and considered Imu's current presence his only chance to get revenge for Harald's death. Although considering Loki's reasoning rather simple, Hajrudin agreed and stated his intention to try his hand as well.
Loki eventually rose to help as well, arriving in time to find Hajrudin already defending a wounded Gaban and Luffy from a further-transformed Imu's wrath. Working as a trio, the three of them combined their might to unleash San Sho: Gokoku against Imu. [Ch. 1191] The attack proved effective, allowing Loki to pursue and follow up with a series of strikes with Ragnir, attempting to crush Imu. Imu was able to dodge, but Luffy chased as well, putting their enemy into the defensive. Although Imu was able to eventually block and knock Luffy away, Loki learned from his previous mistakes, preparing a blast of lightning when his enemy attempted to escape from his range. As he unleashed the blast, Luffy jumped in front, riding it up to Imu's position. Loki's assistance proved vital, as the lightning helped overwhelm Imu long enough for Luffy to use Gomu Gomu no Dawn Thor Bullet against him. [Ch. 1192] Luffy initially led the way to beat Imu before he could heal, but his body had finally given out from all the damage, causing him to instead collapse. Loki proceeded forward anyway, ordering Luffy to rest up while he finished the job, as he transformed into his giant-beast form. [Ch. 1193]
