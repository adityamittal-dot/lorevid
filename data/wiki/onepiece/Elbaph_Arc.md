# Elbaph Arc

Source: https://onepiece.fandom.com/wiki/Elbaph_Arc (fetched 2026-10-17). Bracketed [Ch. N] / [Ep. N] are the wiki's own citations.

The Elbaph Arc is the 33rd story arc of the manga and anime One Piece, the second story arc of the Final Saga, and the tenth story arc of the second half of the series.
The Straw Hat Pirates, joined by Lilith, Kuma, Bonney and the Giant Warrior Pirates, travel to Elbaph, where Robin reunites with Jaguar D. Saul and Luffy meets Elbaph's imprisoned and disgraced prince Loki. The history of Elbaph's previous king Harald and his ties to Rocks D. Xebec, including the truth of the God Valley Incident, are revealed. Meanwhile, as the world reels from Vegapunk's shocking announcement, the Red Hair Pirates, Cross Guild and the Blackbeard Pirates actively search for the One Piece while the Knights of God begin to mobilize, with their eyes set on annexing Elbaph for the World Government.

### Summary

#### Hunters and Hunted: Misadventure in a Land of Gods
While sailing to Elbaph, Oimo and Kashii explained to the Straw Hat Pirates that they had reached Little Garden and found Dorry and Brogy dueling with their fists. Since this left them at a stalemate, the two giants had since put their duel on hold until they returned to Elbaph and got new weapons. The Straw Hats and giants frolicked together as they sailed, while Jewelry Bonney finally got to see the sea horizon with her father Kuma.
On Gartel Island, Bartolomeo was subdued by the Red Hair Pirates after burning their Jolly Roger a month prior. Hongo gave Bartolomeo a vial of poison and ordered him to have Luffy drink it. Bartolomeo refused and drank the poison himself, only to discover that it was harmless. The Barto Club was allowed to escape with their lives, and they made haste in setting sail. Shanks admitted appreciating seeing other pirates care for Luffy this much, but Yasopp then declared that the Barto Club still needed to be punished for their actions as he shot down the Going Luffy-senpai with a single bazooka blast. The Barto Club sank there, leaving their statuses unknown.
On Hachinosu, Blackbeard reprimanded Avalo Pizarro for allowing Koby and Gecko Moria to escape. However, he was pleased to hear Kuzan's report that they had captured Garp alive, and morale was high on the island overall as Blackbeard's followers anticipated their admiral's next move. Blackbeard contacted Laffitte, who reported from the Umit Shipping headquarters that the Revolutionary Army was successfully carrying out a blockade on all food being imported into Mary Geoise. Meanwhile, Catarina Devon and Van Augur arrived at Hachinosu with Caribou onboard.
Two days later, the Thousand Sunny and Great Eirik were separated, with Luffy, Zoro, Nami, Usopp, and Chopper being on the Sunny. [Ch. 1126, Ep. 1156] After a considerable search, the remaining Straw Hats eventually decided to call it off and wait for their crewmates in Elbaph. They then read the newspaper and learned that Dorry and Brogy's bounties had been reissued after a century, being 1,800,000,000 each now. Additionally, the reports on the Egghead Incident attributed Vegapunk's murder to Luffy. Robin took notice of a picture of Luffy in his Gear 5 form, in which he was holding up his left arm which bore an X mark. Brogy noted that the young warrior Hajrudin was now following Luffy; Hajrudin had intended to go to Egghead as well, but had to stay because of trouble on Elbaph. Said trouble came from the "Accursed Prince" Loki, whom the giants stated had killed his father King Harald in order to obtain a legendary Devil Fruit. All of Elbaph's warriors had been needed just to subdue and imprison him, and one giant pirate believed that the world would be brought to ruin the next time Loki was unleashed. [Ch. 1130, Ep. 1160] As they drew near to Elbaph, Brook cut Robin's hair, giving her the bangs she had back when she had met Saul as a child. [Ch. 1131, Ep. 1161]
Meanwhile, Nami awoke inside a fortress made of giant building blocks wearing a different outfit. She was soon accosted by multiple giant-sized animals, and saw Usopp in the jaws of a giant cat named Iscat. A giant porcupine attacked Nami, and she electrified it using Zeus. The porcupine fell onto Iscat and shocked it, causing it to free Usopp. However, Iscat was only angered by the attack, and it chased Nami and Usopp before hitting them out of the castle. Iscat followed the duo down and transformed into a lion, but was then struck down by simultaneous attacks from Luffy, Zoro, and Sanji, who had already caused a stir in the land after killing and eating the "Ear God". [Ch. 1127, Ep. 1157] Left at a loss as to where they were and how their clothes had been changed, the Straw Hats decided to search the area to find answers. They came across a human riding a grasshopper, who called the place the "Land of Gods". He revealed that the human residents all served giant animal deities, causing the Straw Hats to realize that they had attacked and killed some of these "gods" already. The primary ruler of the land was the "Sun God", but the man warned that no one had ever entered this deity's temple and come back alive. [Ch. 1128, Ep. 1157]
Naturally, Luffy immediately began running to the temple, but unexpectedly ran into a large wall of glass, shattering it. This allowed the Straw Hats to see that there was a giant-sized room outside of this land, where they reunited with Chopper. They came to realize that the Land of Gods was actually a diorama created by a giant, and were confronted by more giant-sized animals. Not long after, the Sun God entered this room to find the Straw Hats fighting his pets. The pirates ran back into the Land of Gods, where they encountered Iscat, and they used force to make the cat carry them to the other side as the Sun God pursued them. [Ch. 1128, Ep. 1158] In truth, the Sun God was Road of the New Giant Warrior Pirates, who had desired to make the Straw Hats his playthings after finding them incapacitated by the mists surrounding Elbaph. He told the crew that they could not escape from this room easily, and upon shattering the mirrors on the other side, Usopp found a wall of solid stone blocking the way. However, Luffy broke through the stone with Gomu Gomu no Kong Gun while Nami used Zeus to strike Road with a massive thunderbolt, allowing the Straw Hats to make it out with ease. [Ch. 1129, Ep. 1159]
The crew found themselves outside a castle atop a mountain range, with a large rope bridge connecting their location to a nearby tree which dwarfed the mountains. They hid as Gerd and Goldberg headed down to the castle to confront Road over his capture of the Straw Hats, then headed up the bridge. However, Luffy decided to drop down to the mainland below, promising to catch back up with everyone. Down on the ground, Luffy came across Loki chained to the tree, and asked who he was and where they were. Loki revealed that they were on Elbaph, and introduced himself as the Sun God who would bring the world to an end. The tree he was chained to was none other than Treasure Tree Adam, and he had been bound by these Seastone chains in the Underworld the last six years. Loki requested that Luffy find the key to his chains, offering to eliminate a rival pirate crew of his choosing in return. Loki then attempted to intimidate Luffy by bringing out his giant beast companions, and a group of humans who had aligned with Loki against Elbaph appeared too. They wondered if they could rob Luffy, remarking that they had not been so fortunate against Loki's last visitor, Shanks. Luffy perked up upon hearing Shanks' name, and when he asked if Loki knew him, Loki decided to refer to Shanks as a coward. This immediately angered Luffy, who entered Gear 4 and threw a punch at Loki's head which the giant barely managed to dodge. Attempting to calm the situation, Loki claimed he was only joking, and Luffy asked why Shanks had visited here and where he was currently. Loki responded that he would not share this information for free.
The Straw Hats on the bridge saw Road running toward them, followed by Gerd and Goldberg carrying the Sunny. Luffy pulled himself up to the two giants, and Gerd explained what Road had done. Goldberg asked Luffy what he had done in the Underworld, but Luffy could not say as Loki had made him swear to secrecy. Road reached the top of the bridge at the Sun World to recapture the Straw Hats, only to be pinned by a falling branch trap set by Zoro and Sanji. Gerd and Goldberg then arrived with Luffy and struck Road in the head for his transgression. They revealed to the Straw Hats that they were Hajrudin's friends, and told them to head straight through the forest until they found Island Clouds which would take them to the village. Down in the Underworld, Hajrudin and Stansen hunted a giant moose to serve as food for the Straw Hats' welcome. As the Great Eirik approached land, a young giant boy named Colon called out to them to ask if they would be stopping at Mato's pub on the coast. Upon learning that they were heading straight up to the Sun World, Mato gave Colon a basket with his mother and father's favorite foods. The Giant Warrior Pirates used a stone called a Painter to generate a rainbow, which they proceeded to sail straight up in order to dock directly at the Western Village; Hajrudin and Stansen followed behind. The Straw Hats all reunited as they converged on the village, and they were awed at the beauty of the Sun World. The explorer Louis Arnote had written about the richness of Elbaph, but his writings also contained a warning for visitors to not overstay their welcome on the island. [Ch. 1132, Ep. 1162]

#### Welcome to Giant Country: Old Friends and New Paths
The Great Eirik docked on the island clouds by the village, while Luffy's group made landfall on a ship called a Svarr that could sail through the air with island clouds attached to its oars. Most of the Straw Hats were eager to partake in the welcoming banquet immediately, but quickly shifted priorities once Robin reminded Luffy that she wanted to reunite with Saul. Gerd took the Straw Hats to the Warrior's Spring below the Owl Library where Saul worked. When they arrived, Ange rushed in and said that Saul had fallen and could not get up, but as she saw her old friend on the shore, Robin knew that he was just recreating the scene of their first meeting. Saul surveyed how Robin had grown, and revealed that the flames from the Buster Call had melted away the ice that had encased him, expressing regret at what had happened to Robin in the 22 years that followed. However, Robin only wanted to focus on the good of her survival, and she and Saul tearfully embraced while confessing how much they had missed each other. [Ch. 1133, Ep. 1163] Following this reunion, Luffy offered Saul his thanks for helping Robin, and Robin told Saul that she had indeed found true friends like he had once assured.
Lilith then introduced herself to Saul, and revealed to everyone that she intended to set up a laboratory space on Elbaph where she could bring out a perfect clone of Vegapunk that was successfully brought from Egghead. Ange showed the Straw Hats the Walrus School which Saul had founded, and began a tour while Saul took Robin, Chopper, Lilith, and Bonney to the library. Some of the giant children started chasing the Straw Hats while acting like traditional fearsome giants, but they were then stopped and chastised by other students who opposed the rough language being used. The Straw Hats were shocked to find that the children had no intention of being ferocious warriors like previous generations of Elbaph giants, and the biology teacher Ripley explained that this shift in culture had been enacted by King Harald, who valued trade and diplomacy over warmongering. [Ch. 1134, Ep. 1164] Luffy and Usopp then got into trouble for scaring the kids by roughhousing them too much, but Colon then arrived and showed willingness to fight with the pirates for fun, as he did not go to school and showed more of an inclination toward Elbaph's old ways. Ironically, he was Ripley's son, and he told the Straw Hats that his father was a human. Inside the library, Robin got to see all the books that had been salvaged from Ohara, which the owl librarian Biblo could turn giant-sized with the Iku Iku no Mi. Luffy's group swung by the library, and Robin and Chopper told them to go on ahead to the banquet. Franky decided to split off as well in order to examine the Adam Tree, and Gerd gave Robin, Franky, Brook, and Jinbe Elbaph-style clothing that she had designed herself.
The Straw Hats, sans Chopper, Robin, and Franky, made it back to the Western Village, and the giants offered a toast to their friends as the banquet commenced. The crew soon met the 408-year-old village elder Jarul, who had a giant sword lodged in his helmet. [Ch. 1135, Ep. 1165] Road was forced to apologize to the Straw Hats, but admitted that being the Sun God is something that all Elbaph residents dream of. This caused an argument to break out, as the giants did not agree on the Sun God's purpose. Jarul told the crew that one of Elbaph's ancient texts, called the Harley, spoke of the world being destroyed twice before in the past, with the Sun God Nika being closely tied to both instances. However, the inaccessibility of the text has resulted in interpretations differing about whether Nika saved the world from the destruction or if he was the one to cause it. Jarul noted that Loki could be considered to be trying to emulate this figure, and when Luffy asked about the prince, the giants universally condemned him, saying he had tormented Elbaph since his birth up to murdering his father Harald. Following this event, Loki began rampaging at sea before being caught by Shanks six years ago. [Ch. 1136, Ep. 1166]
Ripley accompanied Franky in his tour of the Adam Tree, and they came across a mural which Ripley revealed had been dated back to the Void Century. The contents of the mural aligned closely with the contents of the Harley, which Robin got the chance to read in the Owl Library. She read of three different "worlds" spanning the course of history, with Saul claiming that the Third World referred to the present day. Though open to interpretation, Robin's reading of the Third World told of the Sun God guiding the world to its end. [Ch. 1138, Ep. 1168]
Down in the Underworld, Loki conversed with a friend named Mosa via Den Den Mushi; they discussed a traumatic experience that Mosa had recently gone through. Meanwhile, two hooded members of the Knights of God, one male and one female, suddenly teleported into Aurust Castle. The Knights confronted the two giants standing guard and demanded to know Loki's location. When the giants refused, the woman began firing projectiles at one of them, causing him to keel over in pain. The duo ultimately acquired Loki's location and headed down to the Underworld, where they invited him to join the Knights. Loki refused, and his insolent attitude displeased the woman, Manmayer Gunko. Loki's wolf companion confronted the Knights, and Gunko used her Aro Aro no Mi ability to conjure an arrow to strangle it. The man asked if Loki would change his mind in exchange for his companion's life, but Loki again refused.
Gunko began pummeling Loki with her arrows while dispatching more of his companions, but Loki's answer did not change. Finally, Gunko's companion Figarland Shamrock told her to stand down, saying they should pivot to their next goal: bringing the warriors of Elbaph under the World Government's command. [Ch. 1137, Ep. 1167] However, Loki then made note of Shamrock's resemblance to Shanks. Shamrock admitted that they were twin brothers and had been together in Mary Geoise once, but Shanks appeared to prefer the Lower World. Loki declared that he would take revenge on both Shamrock and his brother, drawing a swift response from the Knight. To not waste time, Shamrock had his sword Cerberus transform into a three-headed dog. Blades emerged from each of the dog's mouths, and the three heads jettisoned themselves toward Loki and gruesomely impaled him as Shamrock and Gunko flew off. Shamrock said they would need to target Elbaph's children first, and ordered Gunko to call an additional Knight to the island.
Later, Zoro tracked Luffy to the outskirts of town, realizing his captain was looking to help Loki. Despite Zoro's misgivings, Luffy wanted the information about Shanks and assured that he would beat up Loki if he was lying. Nami and Road then joined the duo to help guide them to Aurust Castle in order for Nami to take treasure from it. Upon reaching the castle, they found that someone had broken out of it, and inside they discovered the massive destruction left by Loki and Harald's battle 14 years ago. Road revealed that Hajrudin was also Harald's son and Loki's half-brother, but was not accepted as a royal because his mother was a foreigner. Although Elbaph traditionally looked down on foreign blood, Road came to admire Hajrudin because the latter accepted him despite being a social outcast, with Hajrudin's goal being to unite all of the giants. Luffy's group then found the guards that Shamrock and Gunko had accosted, and they thought that it was Shanks who attacked them, though Luffy refused to believe this.
The group's search took them to the throne room, which contained a magic circle on the floor that Luffy vaguely remembered seeing on Egghead. A voice then called out to Luffy, and the group saw a human man sitting on the throne. The man introduced himself as Ya-san, the husband of Ripley and father of Colon. Luffy admitted his goal of freeing Loki, and disregarded Ya's warnings about the prince's power. Ya told Road to push the throne, which revealed a hidden passageway to a treasure chamber where Loki's key was kept. However, Ya said Luffy would have to go through him to get the key, revealing himself to be Gol D. Roger's left-hand man Scopper Gaban; during this time, the other Straw Hats learned at the banquet that Gaban was residing on Elbaph. [Ch. 1139, Ep. 1169] Gaban showed his sharpness in combat as he outmaneuvered Luffy and struck him with the Haki-imbued key; he then pulled out his axes and unleashed a whirlwind attack which Zoro rose to meet. The clash tore apart one of the castle towers, and Gaban was impressed at Zoro's devotion. As Luffy entered Gear 5 to fight alongside Zoro, Gaban suddenly yielded and allowed the Straw Hats to take the key. He told them to come see him again if Elbaph was still intact following Loki's release. [Ch. 1140, Ep. 1170]

#### Fears Come to Life: The Knights of God Invade
Shamrock and Gunko flew up to the Sun World, where Gunko manifested an Abyss circle which summoned two Knights, Shepherd Sommers and Rimoshifu Killingham, from Mary Geoise. Shamrock questioned why both of them were summoned, but they revealed that he was being called back to the Holy Land due to the dire situation it was in. Sommers and Killingham contemplated how they would manage to kidnap the giant children without being able to send them through the Abyss, and Shamrock suggested making a game out of it.
The Straw Hats and Road headed out on the Svarr, taking the injured guards with them. Nami returned to the village banquet, while Road pulled Gerd aside to tend to the guards. He struggled to hide his reason for being at the castle, and Gerd quickly noticed his nervousness and forced the truth out of him. Luffy and Zoro reached Loki in the Underworld and found him grievously wounded; Zoro proposed leaving one of his chains locked until they could talk to him. Right then, the New Giant Warrior Pirates and Sanji came crashing down on a Svarr, whose cloud support dissolved in the Underworld's altitude, as they were desperately attempting to prevent Loki's release. Luffy cushioned their landing with Gomu Gomu no Fusen, and Hajrudin pleaded for him to not trust Loki. However, Loki was then able to stand up, only one ankle still bound, and began to grab his hammer Ragnir. [Ch. 1141, Ep. 1171]
Loki threatened to bring Elbaph to ruin unless he was fully released, and swung Ragnir into the Adam Tree, summoning a lightning bolt which ignited fires in the Sun World. [Ch. 1142, Ep. 1172] Luffy moved to attack Loki in response, and the giant then collapsed as the pirates realized he had attempted to bluff his way to freedom. Luffy asked Hajrudin to save Loki's life, and Gerd gave him medical treatment. As he recalled Loki's horrible behavior to his mother in their youth and his killing of their father, Hajrudin quickly grew enraged and moved to kill his half-brother. Loki invited Hajrudin to give in to his petty anger but also implored his brother to consider whether he truly believed Loki had killed Harald with murder in his heart. [Ch. 1143, Ep. 1173]
At the same time, the Walrus School came under attack by a massive Jörmungandr, but Principal Kiba and Saul quickly beat it down. However, they discovered gym teacher Wolf laying bloodied on the ground, and Wolf warned them not to touch the children. Some children then reported that a group of children led by Ylva were starting to wander off while asleep. As the staff tried to figure out what was happening, they saw more monsters in the distance. Ange then went up to Kiba and Saul and revealed that she recognized these monsters, as they looked exactly like her students' drawings of their worst fears. Saul raced to try and stop the children, and tried grabbing Ylva despite Wolf's warning; as a result, he was pierced by an invisible ring of thorns surrounding her. These thorns had been generated by Sommers' Iba Iba no Mi, and Killingham had put the children to sleep and manifested their nightmares into MMA with his Ryu Ryu no Mi, Model: Kirin, while Gunko used her arrows to lead the children down Branch Route 8 to an escape ship. While their powers were in action, the three Knights were able to relax and enjoy a meal. However, the children were headed into an inferno that Loki's lightning strike had caused, and a Fire-Fighting Svarr moving to put it out ended up being struck down by a MMA.
Ripley took Franky to an ancient facility, which she revealed was over 3000 years old; Lilith and Bonney decided to set up their base of operations there. Right then, the group was attacked by a lightning MMA. The villagers raced to take on the MMA, and one adult prevented Colon from joining the fight. Nami informed Jarul that two human intruders had been spotted in Aurust Castle, and she, Usopp, Brook, and Jinbe decided to go look for them while the giants handled the beasts. They encountered Colon packing a Svarr, and he invited them to ride alongside him into battle as the Colon Pirates. Following her meal, Gunko put on headphones to listen to her favorite song, "New World". Saul attempted to use shields to stop the children from moving, but found that the thorns hurt the children if they bumped into each other. He then attempted to hold them back with rope, but a Fenrir MMA arrived to attack him, and Robin suggested searching for the source of these powers. The villagers began to be overwhelmed by a ghost and Nidhöggr MMA, but Dorry and Brogy then arrived on a Svarr alongside Gaban and took them down with Hakoku. [Ch. 1144, Ep. 1174]
The firefighters got into position to douse the flames, but could not reduce them as the children drew near to the fire. However, Jinbe was able to put out the blaze using Fish-Man Jujutsu, allowing the children to continue walking safely. The Colon Pirates' celebration was short-lived, though, as Gunko appeared aboard their Svarr. [Ch. 1145, Ep. 1175] Brook recognized her as one of the Knights of God, and Gunko proclaimed that they had come to subjugate Elbaph in anticipation of a Great War foretold by history that would end the world as they knew it; it was believed that the allegiance of the giants would play a critical role in the war's outcome. Usopp fired a Skull Explosion which blew apart Gunko's body above the waist, but she immediately regenerated from this without issue. Gunko used her arrows to throw off the Straw Hats' attacks and hit them off the Svarr, while strangling Colon with another arrow. At the same time, Killingham subdued Kiba and Ange while Sommers brutalized Saul with his thorns as Robin and Chopper hid in his hair. Sommers derided Elbaph's newfound focus on pacifism and education, and cursed Harald for thwarting an earlier effort to turn the giants into the World Government's mercenaries. [Ch. 1146, Ep. 1176]
Gunko tied up Nami, Usopp, Brook, and Jinbe on the ground near Saul and Sommers, and told Brook that she would take him as a slave, but Brook refused even if the alternative was death. Killingham connected the Knights' Den Den Mushi to a Dengo Mushi, allowing Sommers to broadcast himself across Elbaph as the Giant Warrior Pirates' Svarr was overwhelmed by MMA and crashed. Addressing Jarul, Sommers revealed the names of the ten child hostages, which now included Colon. [Ch. 1147, Ep. 1177] He ordered the giants to burn down the Walrus School and Owl Library, threatening to have each child walk off the branch one by one if they failed to decide within 30 seconds, starting with Ronja. Gaban began heading to the branch, along with Dorry, Brogy, and several other giants. Robin moved to attack Sommers, using her sprouted limbs to break his spine, but the immortal Knight quickly restored himself. Robin then sent a clone of herself to force Sommers off the edge in an attempt to put him out of range of the children, but Sommers impaled the clone and managed to stick around as he sprouted a vine to hold onto the branch. Robin moved to free her crewmates in Sommers' absence, but he quickly pulled himself back up and wrapped thorns around her. As Sommers' first ultimatum was about to expire, Ronja said she would go forward with her sacrifice to save the library and school, but Jarul then ordered for Ange to burn them down.
As Sommers prepared to attack Robin, his hand suddenly went flying off, and he was surprised when it did not immediately regenerate. Gaban then charged at Sommers, who was horrified to see the legendary pirate again as Gaban drew blood with a Supreme King Haki-imbued cut to the Knight's chest. [Ch. 1148, Ep. 1178] The Straw Hats were in awe to see Gaban's attacks being effective against a Knight, and Gaban easily bypassed Sommers' thorny vines as he decapitated the Knight at the shoulders. Gaban then ran off to save Colon, and Chopper emerged from Saul's hair to follow him. Ange obeyed Jarul's order to set fire to the school, while Killingham shot a flaming arrow at the library. Gunko brought Ronja back from the edge as Gaban arrived. However, Colon immediately called out to his father, causing Gunko to move to target him. Gaban urged his son to be strong while he moved to save the children and the library, but upon foreseeing that Gunko would inflict a fatal blow on him, he decided to surrender instead. Gunko then impaled Gaban with multiple arrows and sent him falling down into the Underworld, but as Colon called out to his father, she suddenly had memories come to her. She recalled calling out for her own father in the same way, as well as interacting with Brook in the distant past. She started heading toward Brook, but was then possessed by Imu. Declaring that Mary Geoise was a "sea of fire", Imu declared that they needed to hasten the takeover of Elbaph as Gunko's body gained demonic features. [Ch. 1149, Ep. 1179]
As the children's relatives drew near to them, Imu subdued them and the children with powerful Supreme King Haki before summoning weapons from the Abyss to cause further damage. Imu instructed Killingham to continue producing and reviving MMA, and tasked Sommers with leading the children to the escape ship. Imu turned attention to Dorry and Brogy, telling them to take the throne of Elbaph by killing Jarul. Brogy grabbed Gunko's body, but Imu summoned a shotgun and blasted off his arm, saying that it would be natural for him to receive great power and become a demon. Imu then invoked the Demon Covenant and trapped Brogy in an Abyss, before flipping him around in a technique called Domi Reversi. From this Abyss, Brogy emerged as a demonic and monstrous entity who was now fighting alongside Imu against the other giants. He was soon joined by Dorry, and the warriors who found themselves in between the two captains ended up being transformed as well, resulting in just about all of the Giant Warrior Pirates becoming bloodthirsty demons. They marched toward Jarul and the Western Village, flanked by an increasing number of MMA. [Ch. 1150, Ep. 1180]

#### Loki's Lament: The Accursed Prince and the Era of Rocks
In the Underworld, Zoro, Sanji, and Goldberg hunted down a Groggysaurus to eat. Loki told Hajrudin that it was the Knights of God who had inflicted his wounds. Luffy fed Loki some meat and soup and asked for his information on Shanks, but Loki refused to honor the deal because he had not yet been freed. He noted Luffy's attachment to Shanks and admitted that he once idolized a pirate in the same way, recalling how he tried to join the Rocks Pirates as a child.
Chopper ended up following Gaban off the branch, and he used Guard Point to cushion their fall. The Straw Hats noticed the fall and went to check on it along with Hajrudin, Stansen, and Road. Loki then received a call from Mosa, who assured him they were fine but that they would likely not be able to call him for a while. This sent Loki into a panic as he desperately tried to get more information, but Mosa hung up. Luffy's group then returned with Gaban and Chopper in tow, with Gaban needing urgent medical attention. Chopper revealed to the group that some bad guys had taken children hostage, but the pirates immediately realized the threat once they sensed Imu's Haki unleashed. [Ch. 1151] Hajrudin immediately summoned his crew to the lift, leaving Gerd and Chopper to tend to Gaban and Loki. Gaban told Luffy to be mindful of how he used his Supreme King Haki and that the Knights of God were not immortal; this caused Loki to realize the power he possessed. Gaban noted that Zoro also possessed this Haki, which Zoro himself was not aware of. Loki derided Hajrudin for relying on the Straw Hats over him to save Elbaph, but Hajrudin responded that he could not trust his half-brother. Luffy offered to help the situation by inviting Loki to join his crew, which outraged the giant. However, Gaban then advised Hajrudin to free Loki, revealing that he was at Aurust Castle on the day of Harald's death and suggesting that Loki tell the full story. Hajrudin gave Loki three minutes to do so, and Loki was not thrilled to have to recall that "horrible day."
Fourteen years ago, Loki and Jarul had been summoned to the castle, where they found Harald being impaled by his men. Meanwhile, Shanks was speaking with Gaban at his house; when Gaban brought up Harald, Shanks revealed that the two of them had met in Mary Geoise and there was something he wished to discuss with the giant. On that day, Elbaph lost their great king Harald, but he was not always known as the wise ruler he would become. Having ascended to the throne at age 45 (15 in human years), over a century ago Harald was a cruel and violent young man who regularly preyed on human settlements. [Ch. 1152] One day, he attacked the Bunt Kingdom to rescue a giantess named Ida who was in a cage as part of a sideshow attraction. However, Ida slapped Harald for doing this, as she was happily employed by the humans running the attraction; she challenged his notions of giant supremacy and ordered him to take her back. As Ida began helping the Bunt residents restore their town, Harald's conscience was pricked, and he had his crew go back and help her. Following this, Ida accompanied Harald on his ship, and they got to know each other. Once he returned to Elbaph, Harald declared that he would embark on diplomatic missions to perform peaceful cultural exchanges. Ida guided him in these efforts, and 81 years ago, she gave birth to their son Hajrudin.
However, the kingdom elders forbade Harald from marrying Ida due to her foreign origin and instead presented him with a pureblooded woman from Brewers Village named Estrid. 18 years later, while Harald was away on his diplomatic missions, Estrid gave birth to their son Loki.
