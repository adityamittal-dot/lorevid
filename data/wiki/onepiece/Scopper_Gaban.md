# Scopper Gaban

Source: https://onepiece.fandom.com/wiki/Scopper_Gaban (fetched 2026-10-18). Bracketed [Ch. N] / [Ep. N] are the wiki's own citations.

Scopper Gaban, also known as the "Mountain-Eater", is a legendary retired pirate and navigator of the former Roger Pirates. His fame is said to rival that of his crewmates Gol D. Roger and Silvers Rayleigh, being known as the "Left Hand of the Pirate King". After the disbandment of the Roger Pirates and the execution of Roger, Gaban has come to live out his retirement on Elbaph, alongside his partner Ripley and their son Colon, being known to the giant locals as Ya-san.
First introduced in Buggy's flashback sequence in the Orange Town Arc and later seen in Kouzuki Oden's flashbacks during his time with the Roger Pirates, Gaban made his present-day debut in the series through the "From the Decks of the World" cover story, before eventually playing an active role in the main storyline during the Elbaph Arc where he acts as an ally of the Straw Hat Pirates.

### Appearance
Gaban is a man with wild white hair tied in a bushy ponytail and a wrinkled face as a result of his advanced age. He sports a long beard that is braided at the chin, a flame-shaped scar over his right eye, and a pair of round, black sunglasses. His outfit consists of a red Hawaiian shirt with yellow and orange flower patterns, brown shorts, and black boots, which are complemented by a Kasa (hat), and a purple and black-striped coat whenever he is on the move. In his battle against Nerona Imu, Gaban's left arm was sliced off. [Ch. 1190]
In the past, Gaban was a tall, well-built man with long black hair, with two thin strands of hair falling over his face (which he no longer has in the present). 27 years ago, he wore a long-sleeved blue shirt with white stripes on each sleeve, which was only held closed at the middle with one button. In the anime, he was initially depicted in this outfit with lighter-colored hair, silver sunglasses, and a purple shirt with a yellow stripe on the collar and down each arm. [Ep. 505] 26 years ago, he initially wore a yellow t-shirt with two orange blotch-like patterns on the right side, blue pants, and a purple sash. He then wore a purple shirt, orange pants, and a pale-yellow sash.
Gaban's flame-shaped scar has been depicted inconsistently in flashbacks: He was first shown with the scar 20 years ago, [Ch. 1149, Ep. 1179] but later without the scar 14 years ago. [Ch. 1152] It is currently unclear which depiction is accurate.

### Personality
Gaban can be very quick to get to the point, as he proposed stopping the rampaging Kouzuki Oden early on before the samurai could cause more damage to the crew. [Ch. 966, Ep. 965] He was also the first to voice the crew's dissatisfaction towards Oden after the latter joined the Roger Pirates. Gaban does not seem to care for small talk, cutting Neptune off when he started talking about Shyarly's popularity across Fish-Man Island. [Ch. 967, Ep. 967] Gaban has also wandered the world from time to time, being a navigator, and went to see Crocus at Twin Cape at one point.
He is proud of his strength, as he considered himself the second strongest of the Roger Pirates after his captain; even when critically wounded and in the midst of a chaotic siege, when the New Giant Warrior Pirates considered him the third strongest under Rayleigh, he struggled to correct them as the second strongest. Gaban is condescending about the defeat, chiding Luffy for being saved by "an old man like him" and questioning the consequences of his death; he takes the position of Pirate King very seriously and does not consider Luffy worthy of the title, and discarding it outside the fight area.
In his older years, Gaban has shown an amorous side, declaring that "love is free" and introducing himself as the "Preacher of Love" to the Straw Hat Pirates. [Ch. 1139, Ep. 1169] In his youth, Gaban was one of those who were quick to fall victim to Shakky's charms, like his late captain. He is also highly mischievous, showing great excitement at other people causing trouble and insisting that secrets should be spread instead of being kept. Despite his playful nature, he can adopt an intimidating expression and attack. [Ch. 1148, Ep. 1178]
Gaban is family-oriented, as he considered the Roger Pirates apprentices to be their sons and also cherished Ripley and Colon. He also encouraged Loki to confide the truth of Harald's death to Hajrudin; despite being half brothers and having various issues between them, they are still family by blood.
Gaban acknowledges Haki as a power that requires being understood and tamed and criticizes Roronoa Zoro for not taking notice of possessing Supreme King Haki, believing that someone who serves one of the Four Emperors should be more meticulous. [Ch. 1152]

### Relationships

#### Roger Pirates
Gaban's relationship with most of the Roger Pirates is unknown. However, it is stated that he was very close with his captain, Gol D. Roger, as well as his crewmate Silvers Rayleigh, as Rayleigh is always stated to be the Right Hand of the Pirate King, and Gaban is stated to be the Left Hand of the Pirate King. Gaban has been shown to keep in touch with some members of the crew from time to time despite the crew's disbandment, as he is seen visiting Crocus at the Twin Cape and is on good terms with Shanks.

##### Gol D. Roger
As a longtime member of the Roger Pirates, Gaban understands Roger better than most and knows it is pointless to convince him to run from an enemy. He would often act as the voice of reason for the more impulsive and headstrong Roger, who would argue back by provoking Gaban.
Gaban seems to hold his captain to a high standard, even trying to stop him from bowing his head to Whitebeard so he would not look weak in front of his rival. [Ch. 966, Ep. 966] In fact, Gaban earned his epithet after protecting his captain during a massive pirate attack as a testament to his loyalty.

##### Shanks
Due to his crew having found Shanks as a baby, Gaban greatly cares for Shanks like a son. The two are close to the point that Gaban has physically consoled Shanks when the latter felt shame around the events of his birth and voiced his belief that everyone, especially Shanks, had a purpose in life. Even decades after the disbandment of the Roger Pirates, Gaban and Shanks are still close, as Shanks visited Gaban on Elbaph fourteen years ago to reminisce on old times. In the present, he even tried to persuade Gaban into putting his faith into Luffy, though Gaban was initially reserved about meeting Luffy.

##### Crocus
Even decades after the disbandment of the Roger Pirates, Gaban and Crocus seem to be on friendly terms, as Gaban went all the way to the Twin Cape just to visit Crocus.

##### Silvers Rayleigh
While it's currently unknown how close the two actually are in the present, Gaban and Rayleigh are shown in the past to be on friendly terms. However, even in current times, Gaban insists that he is the second strongest of the crew when others believe him to be the third strongest (behind Rayleigh and Roger).

#### Family

##### Ripley
Ripley is Gaban's common-law wife. Although the two have been together for at least 20 years, Gaban refuses to formally marry her because he ages so much faster than her and their son. [Ch. 1135, Ep. 1165]

##### Colon
Colon is Gaban's son with Ripley. Gaban has remarked that even as a baby, Colon was too big for him to hold in his arms, yet he was thankful for his birth, which he considered one of the greatest moments in his life.
Colon seems proud of his father, as he eagerly told others about Gaban's strength and past as a pirate. Gaban, in turn, loves his son; he expressed pride when he learned that Colon tried to save his kidnapped friends and immediately ran to his son's rescue when he learned Colon was captured as well. [Ch. 1147, Ep. 1177] Gaban cares deeply for Colon's safety and will hesitate in battle if his son's life is held in immediate danger.

#### Other

#### Elbaph
After settling down in Elbaph, Gaban is on friendly terms with the Elbaph giants, many including the New Giant Warrior Pirates, who respected him for his strength as a legendary pirate. Gaban had tried to save the children of the Walrus School, including his son, when they were taken hostage, only failing when Gunko threatened to kill Colon first. Gaban is also on good terms with the elder Jarul and the deceased King Harald. He is also one of the few who knew the truth behind Harald's death, as he was present when Harald was corrupted by a Depths Convenant and started massacring his soldiers in Aurust Castle. When the Knights of God invaded Elbaph, Gaban told Hajrudin to release Loki to enlist his help in defeating them as he divulged the truth of Loki's reason for committing patricide.

##### Monkey D. Luffy
Like Rayleigh, Gaban too reminisced about Roger when meeting Monkey D. Luffy, whose carefree attitude towards going into danger was a character trait of his late captain. [Ch. 1139, Ep. 1169] Gaban did recognize his late captain's signature Straw Hat and teased Luffy about safeguarding it only for Shanks, knowing badmouthing the latter would upset Luffy. Gaban later suggested Luffy pay him a visit as soon as he would leave Elbaph, though Luffy dismissed the idea. Gaban was also seemingly aware of Luffy's awakening as "Nika" and was thrilled to see him enter his Gear 5 form.
After Gaban rescued Luffy from Imu, he expressed his disappointment in Luffy for being unable to live up to his claim to become the Pirate King and told him that as long as Luffy did not take his dream seriously, he would never be worthy of the title. Despite having apparently lost his faith in Luffy, Gaban admits to Imu that he still believes that Luffy is the one who his crew is "waiting for," and the fact that Imu took such great lengths in trying to kill Luffy is enough of a reason to believe in him. Because of this, Gaban willingly clashes with Imu to try saving Luffy, which costs him his left arm, and after that, urges him to leave Elbaph to save himself. [Ch. 1191]

### Abilities and Powers
Considered the "Left Hand of the Pirate King", Gaban is a living legend and one of the strongest pirates in history. In his prime he was said to be comparable to his late captain Roger and the former first mate Rayleigh. According to Hajrudin, he was known as one of Roger's "wings" alongside Rayleigh, as well as the third strongest member overall (a detail he vehemently disagrees with, considering himself the second strongest instead).
In terms of authority, he was shown leading the charge alongside Rayleigh during the crew's last battle with the Whitebeard Pirates. [Ch. 966, Ep. 966] In his younger days, Gaban fought alongside his crew in battles against powerful adversaries like the Marines, Whitebeard Pirates, and Rocks Pirates, making him a true veteran and one of the most experienced pirates in the world. While it is unknown how much Gaban's power has declined from age, he still remains an immensely powerful warrior capable of forcing an Emperor of the Sea like Luffy to use his Gear 5. Another display of Gaban's power is that he was able to swiftly defeat Knight of God Shepherd Sommers without injury. [Ch. 1149, Ep. 1179]
Perhaps the greatest demonstration of Gaban's power was his ability to hold his own against the World Government's secret ruler, Nerona Imu, and inflict injuries upon them in single combat. Imu himself acknowledged Gaban's strength, remarking that it had been a mistake to allow Roger's crew to survive God Valley, admitting he had not expected them to become such a major threat to him and the World Government. It is worth noting that Gaban was wounded from a previous attack by the Knight of God Manmayer Gunko.
In the anime, Gaban was shown to be strong enough to clash on even terms with soon-to-be crewmate Kouzuki Oden, one of the strongest-ever samurai from Wano. [Ep. 966]
Having arrived at Laugh Tale along with the rest of the Roger Pirates, Gaban also knows the truth about the Void Century. However, as stated by Rayleigh, it is likely that he and the rest of the crew could have interpreted that information differently by lacking the archaeological knowledge of scholars of Ohara's caliber. [Ch. 507, Ep. 400]

#### Navigational Skills
As the navigator of the former Roger Pirates, Gaban is a veteran sailor with great knowledge of the sea and how to best traverse it. His crew was the first group known to have fully circumnavigated the world, and Gaban was one of the biggest contributors to that feat thanks to his cartography, having been in charge of drawing the crew's final route to Laugh Tale. [Ch. 967]
In the present day, Gaban still knows how to navigate through the treacherous waters of the Grand Line, being able to cross the ocean all the way to Reverse Mountain in order to reunite himself with Crocus.

#### Physical Abilities
In his youth, Gaban was one of the strongest members of the Roger Pirates, being on par with Rayleigh.
Gaban's strength is still monstrous in old age, being immensely fast and agile enough to keep up with the much younger Luffy while also being extremely powerful, having enough power to destroy a whole castle tower (built to accommodate Giants) in a single move. By his own admission, he considers himself to be rusty in combat despite being touted by others as one of the strongest fighters on Elbaph.

#### Axemanship
During his career as a pirate, Gaban favored a pair of single-edged axes as his weapons of choice, which he was extremely skilled and powerful at wielding. One of his legendary feats was defeating hundreds of enemy pirates by himself with his two axes to protect Roger as he was recovering from his injuries. By the end of that fight, a whole mountain range had been wiped clean of vegetation, earning Gaban the nickname "Mountain Eater".
Even in old age, Gaban still showed formidable axemanship, as he, alongside Shanks, could intercept a rampaging Harald, an Ancient Giant who got enhanced by a Depths Covenant, and could effortlessly throw a gigantic axe around simply as a manner of alerting others of his presence. [Ch. 1139, Ep. 1169] He still wields his dual axes to this day, taking them out when getting serious. From the sheer damage he caused to the Aurust Castle, it is safe to assume that age has not significantly weakened Gaban's axemanship. He can unleash flying slash attacks faster than his enemies can react to.

##### Techniques
*Yasotakeru: Gaban swings both of his axes, creating a whirlwind of slashes. This was first used against Monkey D. Luffy, who immediately sensed the danger it posed. The technique was powerful enough to destroy one of Aurust Castle's giant-sized towers. Its name is based on the same named person from Nihon Shoki and Kojiki. Takeru is an archaic term referring to the leader of a fearless tribe, and eighty is a symbolic number representing "a large number."
**Yachitakeru: A variant of Yasotakeru where Gaban first infuses his axes with Supreme King Haki before unleashing a barrage of slashes. This was first used against Imu, being powerful enough to put the latter on the defensive and destroy a whole mountain-sized branch of the Treasure Tree Adam.
*Futenraku: Gaban infuses his axe with Supreme King Haki and delivers a powerful horizontal slice. This was first used against Shepherd Sommers, bisecting the Knight of God and his weapon in a single swing. The name of the technique may be a pun on Etenraku, a Japanese gagaku melody and dance. Tenraku (天楽), in Buddhism, refers to the divine symphony played by celestial beings in celestial realms. In the VIZ translation, this is called Axe Symphony.
*Kamakiri Buppo: Torobai: A style of axemanship that mimics the attack patterns of a mantis. Gaban swings both of his axes vertically in a circular arc, delivering consecutive strikes at his opponent. This was first used against Imu, who managed to block the attack by spinning his weapon. The name of this technique is likely a pun on tōrōbai, the Japanese name of Chimonanthus praecox (specifically the cultivar C. praecox 'Grandiflorus'). In the VIZ translation, this is called Mantis Axe Style Mantis Plum.
*Kamudo: Gaban infuses his axes with powerful Supreme King Haki and swings them at his target, launching bolts of black lightning with enough destructive power to easily cut the enormous branches of Treasure Tree Adam. The name of this technique is similar to Roger's signature technique, "Kamusari". In the VIZ translation, this is called Gate of God.

#### Haki
Gaban is one of the very few people in the world who can use all three types of Haki.

##### Supreme King Haki
Gaban possesses immense mastery in the usage of Supreme King Haki, a rare form of Haki that only one in several million people in the world can use. [Ch. 519, Ep. 413] He is one of the handful capable of using its advanced application of infusing it into his axes, dramatically increasing the power of his attacks, as indicated by the streaks of black lightning emitted from his axes.
By infusing Supreme King Haki into his attacks, Gaban is capable of inhibiting the Knight of God's immortality and temporarily inflicting serious injuries on them, as evidenced when Sommers' arm took an extended amount of time to regenerate after Gaban severed it. [Ch. 1152]

##### Armament Haki
Gaban is able to use Armament Haki. He can imbue Haki into pre-existing weapons like his axes, or ordinary objects, allowing him to use them as an improvised weapon.

##### Observation Haki
Gaban is a master of Observation Haki. He was able to sense, from a great distance, the abnormal Haki that accompanied the arrival of the Knights of God on Elbaph, with many other giants and pirates on the island still being unaware of it. [Ch. 1139, Ep. 1169] He could later tell there are three of them on Elbaph, despite their effort to hide their presence. [Ch. 1145, Ep. 1175] He is also one of the very few individuals capable of using its advanced form of "Future Vision", which showed his son Colon getting killed by Gunko and him not making it in time to save him. [Ch. 1149, Ep. 1179] Years before, he could also sense Imu's possessing Harald from a far distance.

### History

#### Past
Around 44 years ago, Scopper Gaban was already a member of the Roger Pirates and assisted in an attack on the Kuja Pirates, with the goal of having Shakuyaku join them. Unfortunately, they were all far too lovestruck by her to actually fight, leading to her easily subduing Gaban, Gol D. Roger, and the rest of the crew with ease. While the Kuja attempted to make off with their treasure, Gaban spotted Monkey D. Garp's ship in the distance, forcing the Kuja to flee from the incoming threat. While they did so, Roger rallied his men in preparation for a fight. [Ch. 1156]
42 years ago, following the announcement that Shakuyaku retired from piracy and made residence on Hachinosu, Gaban met with Silvers Rayleigh and pondered what he and Roger discussed in his quarters, as he noted Roger was sulking following the interaction. Though their spirits were raised once Roger declared that they would sail to find her. Come 40 years ago, they did as Gaban and the rest of the crew drank with the Rocks Pirates. [Ch. 1158]
38 years ago, after learning that Shakky was kidnapped as a slave on God Valley, Gaban and the crew sailed to the island to rescue her and were present in what would be known as the God Valley Incident. Alongside his captain Roger and Rayleigh, he was ready to engage in combat. [Ch. 1096, Ep. 1130] [Ch. 1159] After Rayleigh successfully saved Shakky, Gaban and the rest of the crew went to collect a large pile of treasure and Devil Fruits, leading to him briefly clashing with Ganzui. [Ch. 1162] Roger then gave Gaban the order to supply backup to the rest of the crew and make off with the rest of the treasure. However, Gaban felt his axes nearly being pulled away from him by John's magnet powers, attempting to warn Roger, though he pushed forward. [Ch. 1163] As Gaban made his way to the Oro Jackson, he was surprised by Gloriosa's presence as she requested a ride on their ship. Shortly after, he and Rayleigh rushed back inland to assist Roger and Garp. [Ch. 1165]
By the time they arrived, the fight was already over, with all 3 of the combatants downed. While the Knights of God finished off Rocks, Rayleigh and Gaban made off with Roger and Garp, successfully rescuing the pair. After dropping off Garp with the Marines and escaping the collapsing island, the Roger Pirates successfully got away with all of their objectives on God Valley. Sometime later, Roger became well enough to finally open the treasure they secured. However, to everyone's shock, a baby Shanks was present in one of the treasure chests. [Ch. 1166]
Picking up Shanks was not without consequence. The Knights of God attempted multiple times to reclaim him from the Roger Pirates; however, Gaban was able to assist in thwarting them each time. It was during one of these confrontations where he met Shepherd Sommers. [Ch. 1170]
At some point when Roger needed to recover at a hospital in the mountains, he and Gaban were surrounded by enemy pirates. Gaban proceeded to defeat them all with his two axes and cut down every tree on the mountain, which earned him the epithet "Mountain-Eater." Over 27 years ago, Gaban and the Roger Pirates took part in a battle against an unnamed enemy ship, enthusiastically fighting off the enemies.
Gaban was present with the rest of the Roger Pirates in the sea known as Edd War, where they were confronted by Shiki, the captain of the Golden Lion Pirates. Shiki demanded to form an alliance with the Roger Pirates in hopes of taking over the world, and if they did not agree, Shiki would destroy them with his armada. While this event occurred, Gaban tried his best to calm down Buggy, who was hoping to surrender to Shiki, but in the end, Roger refused the alliance, thus resulting in an epic battle where the entire crew, including Gaban, fought it out. Luckily, fate was on the Roger Pirates' side, as during the battle, a massive storm formed and sank half of Shiki's armada, resulting in the Roger Pirates' victory. This event was forever known as the Battle of Edd War. At some point, Gaban and the rest of the crew travelled to Little Garden and met Dorry and Brogy. [Ch. 1152]
Around 26 years ago, the Roger Pirates were causing a ruckus on an island. After defeating a group of Marines, they learned that the Whitebeard Pirates had docked on the other side of the island. [Ch. 965, Ep. 965] The Roger Pirates were then confronted by Kouzuki Oden. Gaban and Silvers Rayleigh prepared to fight him, but Gol D. Roger charged in and blew Oden away with a single slash. The Roger Pirates clashed with the Whitebeard Pirates for three days and three nights before the skirmish turned into a gift exchange. The Roger Pirates were later joined by Oden and his family as well as Inuarashi and Nekomamushi. At some point, the Roger Pirates visited Skypiea. [Ch. 966, Ep. 965]
After leaving Skypiea, the crew's voyage took them to Water 7, Sabaody Archipelago, and Fish-Man Island. Sometime later, they left Toki, Momonosuke, Hiyori, Inuarashi, and Nekomamushi in Wano Country. Sometime after the visit to Zou, the Roger Pirates finally reached Laugh Tale. When the crew discovered Joy Boy's treasure, they responded with a burst of laughter. [Ch. 967, Ep. 967]
Sometime after Roger became Pirate King, Roger decided to disband the crew. Roger was the first to leave, and the crew tearfully parted ways with him. They later returned Oden to Wano Country, and they tearfully bid farewell to him as well. [Ch. 968, Ep. 969]
Roughly twenty years ago, Gaban started living in Elbaph, where he met Ripley and was present for Saul's plans of opening a school for the country. [Ch. 1167] Eventually, he had a child named Colon with her, although the two never married. Fifteen years ago, Gaban, Ripley, and Colon had gotten word that Hajrudin's mother, Ida, had passed away at her tavern. [Ch. 1168]
A year later, Gaban was visited at his home by Shanks. The pair relaxed in the nearby hot spring, reminiscing about the past and how much had changed since Roger's death ten years prior. The two were interrupted by Ripley, who had made a fresh tart. Ripley asked Shanks for some gossip about Gaban's past, but Gaban would have none of it and threatened Shanks from speaking further. Afterward, Gaban revealed that Monkey D. Garp told him Roger had fathered a son, much to Shanks' surprise. Although Shanks voiced his high expectations for the boy's future potential, Gaban reminded him that becoming the pirate king was not guaranteed by blood. [Ch. 1169]
Eventually, the discussion shifted to Elbaph's current king, Harald. Gaban was surprised to learn that Shanks had already met him in the past, back at Mary Geoise. However, he was confused by Shanks' wording, specifically his mention that Harald had to be warned before it was too late. As it turned out, Shanks and Harald had both entered into Covenants with the Knights of God. Both bore the Shallows Covenant, but Shanks left before he could be promoted. Shanks had intended to warn Harald about entering into a deeper pact, as he believed the World Government would use it as a way to take control of him. Unfortunately, Shanks' fears came true at that very moment, as the pair sensed a huge amount of Haki coming from Aurust Castle. The pair quickly made their way over, managing to momentarily stop a rampaging Harald before he could catch his son Loki. They quickly came to the discovery that his mind was no longer his own, and that he was acting on the orders of Imu.
To prevent Harald from reaching Loki, Gaban and Shanks continued playing defense, holding Harald's attention. Gaban remarked that their attacks were slowly wearing him down, and Harald would soon fall if they kept dealing damage using their Supreme King Haki. As they dodged Harald's furious attacks, Shanks expressed his surprise at Gaban's knowledge of immortality, leading Gaban to reveal that he had fought the Knights of God in the past to protect Shanks. Gaban remarked on the strangeness of such a group existing in Mary Geoise, as most World Nobles were fairly weak and lazy individuals. Shanks agreed, noting that his life was significantly more exciting, which Gaban chalked up to fate. As they avoided another attack from Harald, Shanks declared his intention to save Harald, but Gaban reminded him of the hopelessness of such a plan and added that Loki was their best bet. The situation worsened as Harald began using Supreme King Haki of his own, preventing Gaban and Shanks from ending it. Fortunately, Loki managed to consume Elbaph's sacred Devil Fruit and equipped himself with its guardian, Ragnir. Using his newfound power, Loki was able to defeat his father and restore his mind long enough for a few final words. As Gaban and Shanks rested near Jarul, Loki dealt a powerful strike to his father, blowing his body apart.
In the aftermath, Gaban walked with Shanks, Jarul, and the surviving guards to survey the damage to the castle. Gaban voiced his relief at Harald's defeat and death, as well as his shock at Loki's newfound power, which allowed him to take on Harald alone while they had struggled so much. He questioned Jarul on his knowledge of Elbaph's sacred Devil Fruit; however, the elderly giant revealed that he had none other than what they had just witnessed. While they continued, Gaban noticed Shanks suddenly and furiously grasp his Shallows Covenant, forcing Gaban to stop and console him. Gaban embraced Shanks, telling him that he had no reason to be ashamed of his birth or his failure to save Harald, as neither was his fault. Over Shanks' arguing, Gaban declared that Shanks had a purpose to his life and that he would have to wait just a little longer before he found it. Afterward, the two proceeded back to the throne room, where Loki revealed his intentions to leave Elbaph and keep what had happened that day a secret, despite the final request of Harald. Loki left with the remaining guards over Jarul's warnings, with Gaban and Shanks glaring at him in silence as he did so. [Ch. 1171] Afterward, Gaban, Jarul, and Shanks made their way to exit the castle. As they did so, Gaban silently listened while Jarul described the legend behind Loki's Devil Fruit, as well as the power of Ragnir, its guardian. [Ch. 1175]

##### Elbaph Arc
Sometime after the Straw Hat Pirates' arrival, Gaban proceeded across Elbaph, [Ch. 1132, Ep. 1162] to Aurust Castle, with the intent of investigating a "strange" Haki he had sensed there. [Ch. 1138, Ep. 1168]
Upon entering the castle, Gaban spotted the Straw Hats, who had been brought there by Road with the objective of finding the keys to Loki's restraints. Due to their spotting of an Abyss (which Nami had stated to have been used to summon demons), the Straw Hats' guide was naturally terrified when Gaban's voice sounded out through the abandoned castle. Road attempted to flee in fear; however, Gaban flung an axe at the door, causing the giant to fall back and remain. When Gaban was spotted and gave a proper introduction, Road recognized him as "Ya-san", and all previous fears were dispelled. Road introduced Gaban as Ripley's lover and the father of Colon, to their surprise. Gaban naturally followed along, proclaiming himself to be the "preacher of love." Upon being told about the Straw Hats' objective to free Loki, Gaban was surprised and concerned but, most importantly, excited at the prospect of troublemaking. However, before he could even finish issuing his warning about freeing Loki, Luffy and Zoro got bored and attempted to leave. In order to keep them interested, Gaban had Road reveal the hidden chamber with Loki's key in it. Before the Straw Hats could take the key, Gaban grabbed it and challenged Luffy to a fight, with the key being the prize.
Although Luffy begged for Gaban to simply hand the key over, Gaban responded by taunting Luffy into trying to take it by force. Luffy tried multiple times to grab it, but Gaban was too quick, easily avoiding each one. When Luffy finally went for an attack, Gaban coated the key in haki and used it as a weapon, forcing Luffy to dodge. He then proceeded to taunt Luffy further by accusing Shanks of being a cheapskate for "loaning" Luffy his straw hat rather than giving it to him. This led to Luffy becoming outraged, although Gaban easily put him down once more. Gaban then proceeded to equip a pair of axes before unleashing a devastating attack he termed Yasotakeru, forcing Zoro to intervene on Luffy's behalf. As Luffy entered Gear 5 and Zoro readied his swords, Gaban peacefully surrendered, causing the pair to lose their tempers at Gaban's apparent joke of a fight. After placing the key in Luffy's possession, Gaban took his leave and requested that the Straw Hats find him before they left Elbaph, should they survive freeing Loki. [Ch. 1140, Ep. 1170]
Sometime later, during an attack on the Western Village by various MMA, Gaban made his appearance on the Giant Warrior Pirates ship, the Great Eirik, where he was greeted warmly. [Ch. 1144, Ep. 1174] Gaban fought the numerous MMA alongside the Giant Warrior Pirates, easily taking down a cartoonish version of Loki on his own. After getting back onto the ship, Gaban searched the surrounding area, attempting to visually locate the Knights of God he had felt prior, as well as the new ones. Gaban grimly mentioned that if even Elbaph was no longer safe, then the end of the current world was not far. [Ch. 1145, Ep. 1175] Unfortunately, the Giant Warrior Pirates eventually found themselves overwhelmed with MMA, especially after MMA versions of their wives and mothers appeared. As a result, the svarr crashed into the forest below. By happenstance, Gaban's wife Ripley and a few of her allies were running by, allowing for a short reunion. Simultaneously, an island-wide announcement was being given by Shepherd Sommers, who gave the giants an ultimatum of joining the World Government or having their children (including Colon) killed. Gaban refused to let harm befall his son and, at the request of his wife, broke off from the group to save him.
Due to his great speed, Gaban was able to arrive at the branch just in time to witness Sommers about to unleash a finishing blow on Nico Robin. Using an air slash, Gaban sliced one of Sommers' hands clean off, confusing the Knight of God at his inability to regenerate it. Gaban then confronted Sommers personally, causing him to panic as Gaban unleashed a direct slash across his chest. Unfortunately, Sommers was able to continue fighting on and blocked another one of Gaban's strikes using a thorn barrier. After a short scuffle, Sommers attempted to unleash a finishing attack; however, Gaban was able to dodge and decapitate the Knight of God in a swift slash. Having no time to save the Straw Hats, Gaban quickly proceeded over to Gunko's location, where he found Colon alongside the rest of the giant children. To his horror, Colon called out to him as his dad, giving Gunko a critical familial connection to take advantage of, which she did. Gaban was forced to watch as Gunko held a lance to Colon's neck, giving him little choice on how to proceed. Using Haki, Gaban only foresaw Colon's death if he were to try to stop Gunko. With no other options, Gaban held up his hands in surrender, giving Gunko the chance to impale him with arrows and knock him over the branch. As he fell, he quietly apologized to Colon for his failure. [Ch. 1149, Ep. 1179]
Fortunately, Chopper had been hot on Gaban's trail and quickly jumped off the branch after him. As the pair continued to plummet, Chopper managed to get a bit lower before using Guard Point to soften their landing. Conveniently, the pair happened to land right near the rest of Chopper's crewmates, as well as the New Giant Warrior Pirates, who had been keeping watch over an injured Loki. The Straw Hats were shocked to learn of Gaban's true identity, although Gaban was displeased to hear that he was thought of as the third strongest in Roger's crew. Unable to let such a comment slide, Gaban used the last of his strength to set the record straight, weakly indicating that he was in fact the second strongest, above Rayleigh. Moments later, as Chopper revealed the grim situation above, Imu's Haki appeared, shocking everyone at its absurd power. [Ch. 1151]
With little time to spare, the New Giant Warrior Pirates prepped a nearby lift in order for a quick ascension to the Sun World. Before Luffy could leave, Gaban waved him down before informing him that his Supreme King Haki could be used to defeat the supposedly-immortal Knights of God. Gaban then revealed that he sensed another Supreme King Haki user in Luffy's crew, that being Zoro, who was surprised to learn of his own power. Gaban was appalled that Zoro was unaware of his own strength and reminded him that as Luffy's second, he should always be ready to have his captain's back. Meanwhile, Hajrudin and Loki argued over whether or not to take the latter, resulting in Hajrudin eventually deciding not to take Loki to the upcoming battle. Gaban called out to him, before ordering him to release the former Prince. Hajrudin was in disbelief at first but became more skeptical as Gaban revealed that he had sensed a strange Haki fourteen years prior and believed the official story to be lacking details. With little other option, Loki begrudgingly began to give his side of the story. [Ch. 1152]
As it turned out, Harald had actually been controlled by another into going on a rampage, resulting in Loki being forced to strike down his father. Loki made no attempt to correct any rumors around the truth and instead simply accepted any negative perceptions of himself so as not to ruin Harald's good name. With Hajrudin left in stunned silence, Gaban confirmed Loki's account of the events. Hajrudin came to the conclusion that the World Government had been the true enemy all along, with Gaban adding that the new Knights of God were a mysterious bunch that had deeper ties to Harald and offering to give a more in-depth explanation. However, Hajrudin was satisfied by what he had already been told and released Loki from his final shackle, declaring that they would stand together to save Elbaph from the government's meddling. Sure enough, one of the Knights of God's MMA suddenly fell from the Sun World, to the shock of all. While Loki and Luffy moved to deal with it, and the New Giant Warrior Pirates proceeded back up to the Sun World, Gaban requested a lift in order to get back to his son. Sanji obliged and carried Gaban and Chopper back up using his Sky Walk.
While the lift rose to the Sun World, Gaban took the time to lay out their game plan. He revealed that the Knights of God had taken ten children hostage, with the goal of sending them back to Mary Geoise, and were using their Devil Fruit powers to ensure their task was complete. Although weary at the strength of their opponents and their Immortality, Gaban's allies were confident that by targeting the Knights of God, they would win the day. However, upon arriving in the Sun World, the situation was already more grim than they had anticipated. On top of being on fire, the forest before them was completely overrun by swathes of MMA, and the Great Eirik was already downed. To make matters worse, Kashii appeared to warn the group that Dorry and Brogy had been turned into Demons who were deadset on killing Jarul. In addition, the children were still marching toward their destination, despite the parents' attempts to stop them. With Road taking the lead, the groups split up to accomplish their objectives. [Ch. 1172]
Gaban and Chopper remained with Kashii, safely watching from a distance as Loki and Luffy easily took down Sommers and rescued the children, as well as their guardians. Gaban recalled his conversation with Jarul on the subject, realizing just how powerful Loki had become with his ability. Unfortunately, their placement from the battlefield was not far enough, as a pair of demons from the Giant Warrior Pirates approached with intent to either convert or kill them. To make matters worse, Kashii found himself in a dilemma, as he had trouble fighting against his own crewmates, even if they were transformed. Rather than forcing the still-wounded Gaban to play defense, Chopper ate a Rumble Ball and transformed into his Monster Point form, striking one of the demons before it could do any real damage. Strangely enough, Chopper's attack somehow knocked the demonic essence itself from the giant, reverting him back to normal, although the other still remained transformed. [Ch. 1177]
Gaban remained behind while Chopper traveled to the Western Village to help with the remaining demons. The continued failures of his forces inspired Imu to personally take a trip to Elbaph, with his arrival causing a massive magic circle to appear above Aurust Castle. Gaban was unnerved with just how powerful the Haki emitted from Imu was, remarking that it felt even stronger than what he felt on God Valley. [Ch. 1180] After swiftly defeating the present forces, Imu and Sommers left to regroup and correct their plan. This led to Zaza, a MMA manifested from Killingham's own fears, invading and flooding the village in one final gambit to steal the children. With the real enemy locked in a battle with Luffy after defeating Loki, Gaban quickly made his way to the fray. Moments before Imu could finish off a wounded Luffy, Gaban lept in the way, blocking Imu's slash. [Ch. 1189]
Imu attempted to strike Gaban himself, only for the latter to dodge and deliver an insult in response. Noticing Luffy still unable to move, Gaban put his fight on pause to berate the young Emperor, reminding him that failure would doom Elbaph, and the Pirate King's position was not for the weak. Afterward, he tossed Luffy away, before returning to go on the offensive against Imu. During their duel, Gaban questioned why Imu would be so devoted to ending Luffy's life, revealing his knowledge of Joy Boy's message on Laugh Tale. Imu was unnerved by Gaban's awareness, demanding to know why one of Roger's old crewmates would bother defending Luffy in such a way. Gaban informed him that Imu's own insistence on killing him proved that Luffy was the man he and everyone else had been waiting for, something Imu agreed to upon realization. When Imu attempted to finish Luffy off again, Gaban was forced to defend him using Kamudo, cutting huge parts of the Adam Tree down even while struggling from his elderly state. Imu managed to block, reminding Gaban that as old as he was, he was still once a member of Roger's crew. Following another clash, with Gaban using his Yachitakeru against Imu's Honebami, Gaban was left incapacitated, his left arm amputated.
Luffy soon after approached Gaban, apologizing for having to be saved, but Gaban responded by revealing he had simply been protecting Elbaph. Upon being asked what he thought Imu really was, Gaban stated that he was unsure, but believed Imu was a god. Moments later, Imu flew back up to their position, now transformed further, and surprised to see Gaban had survived with only the loss of one limb. Recognizing how grim the situation had become, Gaban ordered Luffy to flee Elbaph while he remained to keep Imu at bay. Luffy refused. As Imu unleashed an attack to finish the job, Hajrudin jumped in to intercept, punching Imu away using Gungnir. Gaban was relieved to have assistance, but remained committed to his goal of helping Luffy escape, asking Hajrudin to assist with it. Gaban became annoyed with Luffy's persistance, pointing out the severity of his wounds, and refused to believe the logic of simply healing them away on the spot. After being defended by Hajrudin from another attack, the group was joined by Loki, ready to assist. To Gaban's great confusion, Luffy finally managed to heal himself, with the trio soon after unleashing San Sho: Gokoku at Imu.
Gaban stood aside with Hajrudin as Luffy and Loki pursued their enemy, keeping the pressure up. Even with their knowledge of Imu's abilities, however, they still found themselves struggling to hurt him, or even break through his defenses. Gaban became worried upon witnessing Luffy be knocked out the air again, and attempted to order Hajrudin to rush in and flee with Luffy. To Gaban's bewilderment, Luffy's crewmate Usopp appeared, calling out Gaban for his continued cynicism and refusal to accept that Luffy simply wanted to save the same giants that saved him and his crew in the past. Gaban responded by pointing out how important Luffy was to the future of the world, but Usopp rejected this belief, declaring that Gaban was enforcing his own beliefs onto Luffy's fate, and that his captain was free to choose his own destiny. Gaban was left stunned by Usopp's words, wondering where he had been the whole time. [Ch. 1192]
