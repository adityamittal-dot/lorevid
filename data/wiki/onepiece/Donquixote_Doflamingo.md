# Donquixote Doflamingo

Source: https://onepiece.fandom.com/wiki/Donquixote_Doflamingo (fetched 2026-10-21). Bracketed [Ch. N] / [Ep. N] are the wiki's own citations.

Donquixote Doflamingo, nicknamed "Heavenly Yaksha", is the captain of the Donquixote Pirates. [Ch. 692, Ep. 618] Before his imprisonment, [Ch. 801, Ep. 746] he was a member of the Seven Warlords of the Sea with a frozen bounty of 340,000,000, [Ch. 234, Ep. 151] as well as the most influential underworld broker under the codename "Joker". He is also a former World Noble, descended from the Donquixote Family.
He is the first son of the late Donquixote Homing [Ch. 763, Ep. 702] and the older brother of the late Rosinante, [Ch. 749, Ep. 686] both of whom he killed out of feeling betrayed by them. [Ch. 747, Ep. 683] Doflamingo became the King of Dressrosa after taking the throne from Riku Doldo III eight years before the start of the series. [Ch. 721, Ep. 651] He ruled the country tyrannically until he was stripped of his positions as a Warlord of the Sea and king after he was defeated by Monkey D. Luffy near the end of the Dressrosa Arc. After Doflamingo was arrested by Admiral Fujitora along with most of his crew, [Ch. 792, Ep. 735] he was locked up at Impel Down and placed in Level 6. [Ch. 906, Ep. 885] Vegapunk later cloned Doflamingo as one of the Seraphim to replace the Warlords. [Ch. 1086, Ep. 1120]
Doflamingo is first introduced during the Skypiea Saga, having a minor role during its epilogue as Bellamy's superior. He is also a major antagonist in the Marineford Arc where he took part in the Summit War of Marineford and sided with the Marines, along with most of the other Warlords of the Sea, and the main antagonist of the Dressrosa Arc and the Dressrosa Saga as a whole, being the impetus for the entirety of the saga's events.

### Appearance
Doflamingo is a very tall (standing at 10' and, as a comparison, taller than Kuzan as shown in their confrontation), very lean and muscular man with a six-pack, fair skin tone (tan skin in the anime), and blond hair that is cut incredibly short, resembling a pixie cut. He generally dresses in flamboyant clothes. About his animal theme, the flamingo, his clothes are brightly colored, with his large, light pink feather coat being the most distinct of all. Doflamingo sports a pair of curved, thin white sunglasses, which appear to serve as his eyesight with orange lenses (red in the anime). He usually walks with an odd, bow-legged waddle, which is also a reference to his animal theme.
In the manga, he wears a white shirt with red stripes (early open, revealing his chest), a green belt, and orange pants with white stripes. He also wears pointed black dress shoes. Originally in the anime, Doflamingo had a red shirt with black stripes, a purple belt, and black pants with red stripes. However, when he reappeared during the events of Sabaody, his color scheme was switched to its manga depiction, except for his glasses. In the manga, he has orange-tinted glasses, while in the anime (pre-timeskip) he has purple-tinted ones. He is also seen often, if not always, smiling with his long tongue hanging out. [Ep. 151] [Ep. 398]
Two years after the war, Doflamingo's outfit is somewhat less elaborate, wearing a long-sleeved plain, open shirt that has small, dark Jolly Rogers as cufflinks, an orange belt with green ropes, and pink striped pants with white patterns. However, he still wears his trademark sunglasses, which have changed from purple to red lenses and dress shoes. Post-timeskip, he drapes his feathered coat on his shoulders instead of leaving his arms in the sleeves, similar to many high-ranking Marines and pirates alike. He also now wears a golden hoop earring on each ear. After his defeat by Luffy, Doflamingo wore a horizontally striped prisoner outfit.
Even as a child, Doflamingo spent most of the time wearing sunglasses, though he had not yet acquired his trademark ones. Before leaving Mary Geoise, Doflamingo wore spiral sunglasses with dark-brown lenses along with the typical garb of the Celestial Dragons, and his hair was fashioned in the same style as his adult self, but it also had a Noble Opener typical of standard Celestial Dragons. After the family left Mary Geoise, his hair was changed to a neat bowl cut, and his wardrobe consisted of simple fine clothes: a low-neck button-up shirt with black stripes and a frilled cravat. [Ch. 760, Ep. 699] However, due to the harsh life on the run from angry citizens, Doflamingo's appearance started to turn more disheveled; in a depiction, his shirt and hair grew messier and unkempt, going barefoot, sitting on a tattered chair near palm trees, and smoking a cigarette.
At age 10, after gaining loyalty from his four closest childhood friends, he changed out of his tattered noble clothes for a simpler, yet cleaner, black shirt and white shorts. [Ch. 782, Ep. 723] From this point onward, Doflamingo began to look more threatening. He eventually took on the appearance of a mob boss, wearing a black coat like a cloak over a pale lavender shirt with an unbuttoned collar and blue-colored shorts. His hair took on a spiky appearance, and he replaced his ordinary sunglasses with the trademark pair of spiral-shaped red lenses that he has now. [Ep. 724]
At age 17, his hair was longer than it is currently, he had two earrings on his left ear, and he sported a pair of goggles that he wore on his forehead. In addition, he also wore sunglasses similar to the ones he wears today, but they had a different temple design and his signature feathered mantle. [Ch. 0, Ep. 0]
At age 25, when Law first came to him requesting membership in the crew, Doflamingo seemed to have done away with the goggles, but otherwise seemed the same in appearance; [Ch. 752, Ep. 690] he wore a black dress shirt with a red tie and white pants with a bright red design, with his signature mantle over it, [Ch. 762, Ep. 701] At age 28, during his trip to Minion Island, he briefly wore a blood-red double-breasted suit with yellow-gold buttons, with matching blood-red dress pants and shoes, a pair of (dark green in the anime, black in the manga) gloves, and changed his sunglasses to the ones he wears currently. [Ch. 766, Ep. 705]
By the time he was 31, he had his hair cut down to the current length. Currently, after his defeat at Dressrosa, Doflamingo now has Seastone shackles on his arms, legs, and entire chest while chained to the floor of Tsuru's ship, he is also wearing a standard Impel Down prison uniform, with a few white bandages on his forehead as well. He was also given a new set of sunglasses. [Ch. 801, Ep. 746]
