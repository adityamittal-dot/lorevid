# Joy Boy

Source: https://onepiece.fandom.com/wiki/Joy_Boy (fetched 2026-10-21). Bracketed [Ch. N] / [Ep. N] are the wiki's own citations.

Joy Boy was a man from the Great Kingdom born 900 years ago at the start of the Void Century, as well as the first pirate in history. An extremely relevant figure in the Void Century, Joy Boy was a chief opponent of the alliance that would eventually become the World Government in the Great War. He played an important role in the history of Fish-Man Island and left behind a vast treasure on the last island of the Grand Line, thus becoming a legendary figure in the world.
Joy Boy's Devil Fruit allowed him to transform into the legendary "Sun God" Nika, causing him to become a key part of the figure's mythos. Following his death, this Devil Fruit eluded capture for eight centuries until being consumed by Monkey D. Luffy. When Luffy awakened the fruit 12 years later, Zunesha proclaimed that Joy Boy had returned. [Ch. 1044, Ep. 1071] In this form, Luffy has been given the "Joy Boy" moniker by Emet and Nerona Imu, who both personally knew the original man. [Ch. 1122, Ep. 1153] [Ch. 1125, Ep. 1155]
He was first mentioned on the Poneglyph read by Nico Robin while at the Sea Forest.

### Appearance
Joy Boy has been noted to strike a strong resemblance to Monkey D. Luffy by Emet. He has only been shown as a silhouette so far, during which he appeared to be wearing a wide-brimmed hat resembling a straw hat, capri pants, and a cape. He also had somewhat messy hair, with several ends jutting out. [Ch. 1181]

### Personality
Although his personality as a whole is unknown, Joy Boy had foresight and cared about his friends, as he left a concentration of his Supreme King Haki to Emet in order to help him in the future after his own death.

### Abilities and Powers
Although the full extent of his powers and abilities are largely unknown, Joy Boy and his allies were considered so powerful that their might was the sole reason the First Twenty could be convinced to band together to defeat them. According to Rocks D. Xebec, Joy Boy is one of the two beings that Nerona Imu genuinely fears, the other being the pirate of myth who lived at the same time as them, Davy D. Jones. [Ch. 1164] Imu called Monkey D. Luffy, one of the Four Emperors in the current age, as being weak compared to Joy Boy. [Ch. 1188]

#### Voice of All Things
Joy Boy possessed the ability to hear the Voice of All Things. He was able to communicate with the robot Emet.

#### Devil Fruit
Joy Boy ate the Hito Hito no Mi, Model: Nika, a Mythical Zoan-type Devil Fruit that allowed him to become an incarnation of the ever-playful sun god, Nika. It gave him a body with the properties of rubber, giving him the freedom to fight however he wanted. Joy Boy appears to have "awakened" his ability while maintaining his personality, as indicated by the ribbon of flaming smoke around his shoulders and torso when he is transformed.

#### Haki
Joy Boy was one of the very few people in the world who wielded Supreme King Haki. The power of his Haki is monstrous, as the giants Dorry and Brogy questioned if his Supreme King Haki was more powerful than that of Shanks', though they noted they sensed no hostility from it. Imu felt the powerful burst of Haki on Egghead all the way from Mary Geoise, immediately recognized it as Joy Boy's Haki, and was visibly panting after it. [Ch. 1122, Ep. 1153]
Most notably, he was capable of using the advanced application of this type of Haki, able to "tie" his Haki into a knot, allowing it to be preserved for centuries and released in a powerful burst once the knot is undone. This burst was of such intensity that it knocked every Marine near the island of Egghead unconscious, including all of the Vice Admirals. [Ch. 1123, Ep. 1154] It also seemed to act as a counter to the esoteric powers of the Five Elders, cancelling their transformations and sending three of them who had been summoned to the island back to Mary Geoise.

### History

#### Biography
Joy Boy was born in the Great Kingdom 900 years ago at the beginning of the Void Century. At some point, he set out to the seas and became the first person ever to be called a pirate.
During his life, he became a comrade of Zunesha. [Ch. 1040, Ep. 1067] At some point, he would meet with Emet, informing the Iron Giant that the time for something was at hand. He informed the machine that he intended to seal some of his Haki within it for use at a later point if it were ever in mortal danger. It was during this time where he also met Nerona Imu. Although the two appeared to be on good terms, for unknown reasons, Imu developed a serious contempt and fear for Joy Boy by the modern day. [Ch. 1181]
More than 800 years ago, during the Void Century, the residents of Fish-Man Island made a promise with Joy Boy. He attempted to raise Noah with Poseidon's aid but broke his promise to Fish-Man Island due to an unknown reason. [Ch. 649, Ep. 569]
Joy Boy was eventually defeated by the Twenty Kingdoms, which marked the end of the Great Kingdom and thus the Void Century. At some point, Joy Boy attempted to preserve the three Ancient Weapons for future generations to inherit, for unknown reasons. [Ch. 1116, Ep. 1149]
At a different point, Joy Boy came across an island, which would later be named Laugh Tale by Gol D. Roger, and left behind a treasure that would become known to the world as the One Piece. [Ch. 967, Ep. 968]

#### Legacy
Joy Boy left messages in the form of Poneglyphs across the world. One of those messages was to Fish-Man Island and to Poseidon, apologizing for breaking his promise and promising that someone would fulfill it in the future. He also left Poneglyphs describing the whereabouts of the Ancient Weapons. [Ch. 967, Ep. 967]
38 years ago, during the God Valley Incident, when Rocks D. Xebec was being transformed by Imu, he questioned the shadow ruler whether he feared his ancestor Davy D. Jones or Joy Boy more, proving that Xebec somehow learned about Joy Boy's existence even before his own rival, Roger, and his crew were able to when they reached the last island in the Grand Line.
Over 20 years before the present, when the Roger Pirates conquered the Grand Line by reaching the then-unnamed island, they read the message and learned the true history of the world but decided to leave it as is, realizing they were too early. [Ch. 972, Ep. 974] [Ch. 507, Ep. 400] Roger found the story hilarious and declared that he wished he and Joy Boy could have lived during the same period upon learning the true history and subsequently named the island Laugh Tale.
Fish-Man Island's half of the promise was almost broken during the events of the Fish-Man Island Arc, when Monkey D. Luffy was asked to protect the island no matter what the cost, even if it meant that Noah had to be destroyed in the process. Before Luffy could destroy the ship completely, Noah's fall was stopped by a group of Sea Kings that were summoned unknowingly by Shirahoshi, thus saving Fish-Man Island and maintaining the nation's promise to Joy Boy. [Ch. 647, Ep. 567] However, the ship was still heavily damaged. The Sea Kings then took the ship to the Sea Forest, where they hoped it could be fixed before the promised time arrives. [Ch. 648, Ep. 568] When Dr. Vegapunk broadcasted his dying message during the Egghead Incident, he revealed to the world what he knew about Joy Boy, including that he was from the Great Kingdom and his status as the original pirate.
Joy Boy's long-sealed Supreme King Haki was eventually released by Emet to save the life of Monkey D. Luffy from the Five Elders on Egghead. [Ch. 1122, Ep. 1153]

##### Second Coming
According to King Neptune, there is a legend in the royal family regarding the person who will fulfill Joy Boy's promise.
Amatsuki Toki used the abilities of her Devil Fruit to travel to Wano in the future in search of the day of Joy Boy's return. [Ch. 964, Ep. 963] [Ch. 973, Ep. 969]
During the Roger Pirates' journey, after Kouzuki Oden joined, they traveled to Fish-Man Island, and on their way, both Roger and Oden heard the Sea Kings talk about the birth of someone who will lead them in 25 years along with the new Poseidon. [Ch. 968, Ep. 969] After the crew's discovery on Laugh Tale, Oden returned to his home in Wano Country, which also had a connection to Joy Boy, and made plans to open up the enclosed land in preparation for Joy Boy's return. [Ch. 968, Ep. 969] However, his efforts were foiled when the shogunate was usurped by Kurozumi Orochi, aided by the pirate Kaidou, culminating in Oden's death during the Legendary Hour; thus, he left the task to his retainers. Sometime after Oden's death, Yamato, Kaidou's child and an admirer of Oden, learned the truth from Oden's journal and worked to fulfill his dream. [Ch. 1024, Ep. 1048] [Ch. 984, Ep. 992]
At an unknown point in time, Kaidou also learned about Joy Boy and, for an unknown reason, began searching for the next person to inherit the name. Kaidou also came to believe that Joy Boy would be someone who would come and defeat him in battle someday. King, a member of the Lunarian race, seemingly believed that Kaidou himself could have become Joy Boy and changed the world, although he eventually came to dismiss the figure as a mere legend. [Ch. 1036, Ep. 1062]
After Monkey D. Luffy was knocked out by Kaidou on the Skull Dome rooftop during the Raid on Onigashima, in his unconscious state, his heartbeat gained the qualities of what Zunesha claimed to be the "Drums of Liberation", causing the ancient pachyderm to declare that Joy Boy had returned. [Ch. 1043, Ep. 1070] Luffy soon regained consciousness with a wild grin as the awakening of his Devil Fruit activated. He then transformed into his Gear 5 form for the first time and challenged Kaidou again. Upon his defeat at Luffy's hand, Kaidou recalled his belief that Joy Boy would be the one to defeat him, implying that in his final moments, he recognized that Luffy was the same Joy Boy he had waited for after all. [Ch. 1049, Ep. 1076]
After the battle, Luffy asked Kouzuki Momonosuke not to name him as the one who defeated Kaidou, [Ch. 1053, Ep. 1080] so the shogun used the name "Joy Boy" instead in the heroic recounting of the raid's events, thus making Joy Boy a legendary figure in Wano. [Ch. 1052, Ep. 1079]
