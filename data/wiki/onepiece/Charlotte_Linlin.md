# Charlotte Linlin

Source: https://onepiece.fandom.com/wiki/Charlotte_Linlin (fetched 2026-10-20). Bracketed [Ch. N] / [Ep. N] are the wiki's own citations.

Charlotte Linlin, [Ch. 829, Ep. 789] better known as "Big Mom", [Ch. 610, Ep. 529] is the captain of the Big Mom Pirates and was formerly one of the Four Emperors ruling over the New World, as the only female member of the group. She is the queen of the archipelago nation Totto Land, a place she seeks to form into a utopia where all the world's races can live together in peace without discrimination or segregation. [Ch. 827, Ep. 785] She is also the matriarch of the Charlotte Family, whose children make up the ministers of her nation and the core infrastructure of her crew. [Ch. 828, Ep. 787]
Big Mom wields the Soru Soru no Mi, which allows her to steal the souls of others and implant them in inanimate objects, resulting in the unique race known as homies, who happily serve her. Big Mom possesses three special homies made from her own soul: the bicorne Napoleon, the sun Prometheus, and the thunder cloud Hera. Hera's position was formerly occupied by another thunder cloud, Zeus, but Big Mom grew tired of Zeus' continued failures and created Hera to replace him. [Ch. 1011, Ep. 1028]
In her childhood, Linlin was raised on Elbaph in the Sheep's House under Carmel after being abandoned by her family. [Ch. 866, Ep. 836] After accidentally slaying Jorul, a hero of Elbaph, during one of her hunger pangs, Linlin was relocated to another island. During Linlin's birthday party, as she gorged on sweets, Carmel and her fellow orphans mysteriously disappeared and Linlin inherited Carmel's Devil Fruit. [Ch. 867, Ep. 837] Now alone, she was discovered by Streusen, and the pair co-founded a pirate crew. [Ch. 868, Ep. 838] As a young adult, Big Mom joined the legendary Rocks Pirates, where she formed a bond with apprentice Kaidou. Some time after the God Valley Incident where the Rocks Pirates fell, she formed her own crew once again, the Big Mom Pirates.
Big Mom developed an enmity with Monkey D. Luffy and the Straw Hat Pirates, who challenged her ownership of Fish-Man Island and then invaded Whole Cake Island, thwarting her plans to steal technology from the advanced Germa Kingdom during an arranged wedding between Sanji and her daughter, Charlotte Pudding. [Ch. 812, Ep. 763] This grudge brought her to Wano Country in pursuit of Luffy, where she formed an alliance with Kaidou and the Beasts Pirates with the intention of conquering the world and claiming the One Piece. [Ch. 954, Ep. 955] However, this never came to fruition as their alliance would go on to be defeated by the Ninja-Pirate-Mink-Samurai Alliance that same night. [Ch. 1040, Ep. 1067] Following her defeat to Eustass Kid and Trafalgar Law, the World Economy News Paper released a new listing of the Four Emperors, with Big Mom no longer being included among them anymore. [Ch. 1053, Ep. 1080]
As a young child at the beginning of her piracy career, Big Mom was assigned a 50,000,000 bounty which was quickly escalated to 500,000,000. By the time of the Raid on Onigashima, at the peak of her piracy career, she held a bounty of 4,388,000,000 which was the second-highest bounty of any living pirate.
Big Mom was the last of the Four Emperors to be named; her epithet was first revealed after the Summit War of Marineford by Eustass Kid and her real name was revealed by Pappag on Fish-Man Island. Since the timeskip, she is a minor antagonist during the Fish-Man Island Arc, the main antagonist of the Whole Cake Island Arc and the Whole Cake Island Saga as a whole, the secondary antagonist of the second half of the Wano Country Arc and one of the two secondary antagonists of Wano Country Saga as a whole.

### Appearance
Big Mom is a huge, obese woman with a large chin and a large, hooked nose. She has an enormous physique, standing at 880 centimeters, or just shy of 29 feet tall, which is the largest known height for any human. She is taller than the extremely large Whitebeard and was able to hold the 277&nbsp;cm tall Brook in her hand. Even as a five-year-old child, Linlin was already massive to the point she was mistaken for a giant and was as tall as giant children. However, as an adult, she is a little over three meters short of the minimum giant height of 12 meters. She has moderately round, orange eyes and prominent eyelashes and wears thick purple eyeshadow. She has a very wide mouth with full lips sporting red lipstick, large, round teeth, and a long, thick tongue that often sticks out. She also has long, curly, and wild pink hair that falls halfway down her back, [Ch. 829, Ep. 789] as well as a long beak-like nose and plump, round cheeks. She also sports a tattoo on her left shoulder and arm, consisting of a large, standard-shaped red heart framed by thin lines curled at their bottom ends, which all tops a far smaller heart.
When she was five years old, Linlin towered over normal humans and was about the same size as a giant child. She had a much rounder face and had freckles on her cheeks; her hair was kept in two buns on both sides of her head. Her nose was more triangular compared to present-day where it now protrudes outward in a rounded, beak-like shape. She wore a polka-dotted dress very similar to the one she currently wears, but with this one reaching her neck and having a bow at the top. [Ch. 866, Ep. 836] At the start of her pirating career, Linlin's hair grew longer, and she donned a dark sunhat. [Ch. 868, Ep. 838]
At 24 years old, Linlin looked drastically different compared to both her early childhood days and her later years. She was remarkably slimmer and had a visible neck, chin, and jawline, and her features were significantly smaller and more proportionate to her face. Her hair was long, yet considerably less wild than in the present day, and she had a large strand of hair falling to the left side of her face. She wore a dark fedora with a polka dot pattern, a bandana, a dark polka-dotted top with a captain's coat draped over her shoulders, a striped dress, and a pair of shorts.
In Oda's depiction of her at this time, she wore a dark fedora with a polka dot pattern, a bandana, and a dark, polka-dotted top with a captain's coat draped over her shoulders. She had a similar appearance during her time in the Rocks Pirates at age 30.
At 48 years old, her neck was considerably larger and more muscular than it was at 28, and she had a much larger, more pronounced jaw. By this time, she had already begun wearing Napoleon and her polka-dotted bandana.
Five years ago, at age 63, her physique was very similar to how it is currently at age 68, and she wore a purple and pink vertically striped dress. [Ch. 850, Ep. 816]
During her first appearance, Big Mom was shown to often drool large quantities of saliva when talking. [Ch. 827, Ep. 786] When in her regular fits of rage from not having a certain food at her direct disposal or simply when extremely furious, her pupils take on a spiral appearance. [Ch. 829, Ep. 789] [Ch. 863, Ep. 833]
Big Mom wears a large pink bicorne hat bearing a Jolly Roger on the front. She has worn two versions: the first design consisted of a standard skull with crossbones behind it, as well as large flame-like patterns to the sides, [Ch. 818, Ep. 769] whereas the current version, named Napoleon, has two crossed sabers instead of crossbones, possesses smaller lateral flame patterns, and is imbued with a fragment of her soul, causing its skull to change expressions and talk to Big Mom; [Ch. 829, Ep. 788] [Ch. 846, Ep. 811] on its top, the hat is edged with a thick yellow streak; it has a strip snap-fastened to it, [Ch. 829, Ep. 789] and beneath it is a light, polka-dotted bandana fastened around Big Mom's head. Big Mom also dons a pink dress with red polka dots and white ruffles on its neckline and hemline, [Ch. 651, Ep. 571] with a wide, rippling white cape attached to its back that falls to the ground. [Ch. 827, Ep. 786] The outfit is completed with dark blue high heels, one turquoise pearl wristband on either wrist, and golden, jeweled rings on all her fingers. [Ch. 829, Ep. 788]
Hours after her craving fit for wedding cake started, Big Mom was noted to have gotten slimmer and slimmer [Ch. 888, Ep. 862] [Ch. 889, Ep. 863] up to the point where she has become emaciated. [Ch. 894, Ep. 868] She went back to her usual appearance after eating Sanji's wedding cake. [Ch. 900, Ep. 875]
As O-Lin during the Wano Country Arc, she wears her hair in a shimada. [Ch. 934, Ep. 929] During the Fire Festival, she changed into a black furisode kimono with a white minimalist floral pattern over a light pink robe, tied with a yellow polka-dotted red sash tied in a bow. She also wears a more elaborate shimada. [Ch. 982, Ep. 989]
