# Ancient Weapons

Source: https://onepiece.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_Weapons (fetched 2026-10-21). Bracketed [Ch. N] / [Ep. N] are the wiki's own citations.

The Ancient Weapons are three weapons capable of mass destruction, each taking a different form. They are known as Pluton, Poseidon, and Uranus, each named after a deity for their godly powers. [Ch. 650, Ep. 570] The weapons are not limited only to inanimate objects, but consist of living beings as well.
The Ancient Weapons were used during the initial Great War during the Void Century and are majorly responsible for the rise of global sea levels that shaped much of the current Blue Planet geography. [Ch. 1115, Ep. 1149] The ancient pirate Joy Boy had a role in their preservation so future generations could still access and use the weapons, but his reasons for doing so are still a complete mystery. [Ch. 1116, Ep. 1149]
The Ancient Weapons are the argument used by the World Government to forbid research of the Void Century, since they fear that knowledge of the weapons could result in a global war. However, this has not stopped certain individuals, such as the former Warlord Crocodile, the government agent Spandam, and most notably the former members of the Four Emperors, Charlotte Linlin and Kaidou, from attempting to gain one or even all of the weapons for themselves. Even without knowledge of the missing years, their power has been known to entice casual observers (as was the case with Vander Decken IX's interest in Shirahoshi, otherwise known as the Ancient Weapon Poseidon).

### Pluton
While Pluton has yet to appear in the story, it has been confirmed to be an ancient battleship capable of massive damage and destruction. It was built on the island of Water 7 sometime during the Void Century, [Ch. 344, Ep. 243] though the precise reason for its construction remains unknown. It currently rests within a secret cavern at the foot of the submerged Mt. Fuji in old Wano Country, below the cavern where Wano's Road Poneglyph is currently hidden. Since the Arabasta Poneglyph contains the location of Pluton, the battleship must have been brought to Wano shortly after its construction. [Ch. 1053, Ep. 1080] In order for Pluton to be released, the mountainous walls surrounding Wano would have to be brought down; this is the true meaning of "opening Wano's borders". [Ch. 1055, Ep. 1082]
The shipwrights that built Pluton kept the blueprints to the ship as a safety measure; in case Pluton were to ever fall into the wrong hands, another one could be built to fight the existing one. The blueprints were passed down from master to apprentice shipwrights for generations, and ten years prior to the main storyline were held by the fish-man carpenter, Tom, of Tom's Workers. At this point, Spandam of CP5 sought him out in order to acquire the blueprints so that they could use Pluton to put an end to the Great Pirate Era (in reality, it was to overthrow the World Government). Tom denied the request, and shortly afterwards handed them down to his apprentice Iceburg, who then entrusted them to Tom's other apprentice Cutty Flam, otherwise known as "Franky", five years later in order to better hide them.
When Franky was captured by the World Government, he kept the blueprints hidden within his own cyborg body until he understood that Nico Robin, the only living person who could read the Poneglyphs and subsequently revive the Ancient Weapons, had no intention of doing so or even planned on using that power to destroy the world; thus, he subsequently burned the blueprints to prevent them from falling into Spandam's hands. [Ch. 399, Ep. 284]

### Poseidon
Despite their status as a "weapon", Poseidon was not an object, but in fact a special mermaid that had the power to talk to Sea Kings, enormous sea creatures capable of mass destruction. Despite having the ability to talk to sea creatures, no other merfolk are able to communicate with Sea Kings. Poseidon is usually born a princess of Ryugu Kingdom once every several centuries.
The previous known holder of Poseidon's power was a mermaid princess who lived during the Void Century. Eight hundred years later, Princess Shirahoshi also developed this power, and as stated in a conversation between Neptune and Robin, she has now inherited the title "Poseidon". [Ch. 649, Ep. 569]
Poseidon was first mentioned when Nico Robin read the Poneglyph on Skypiea. However, details of the weapon were not delved into until much later, when she reached Fish-Man Island. Legend states that the Mermaid Princess with the power to communicate with the Sea Kings will meet someone who will help guide her power.

### Uranus
Nothing is currently known about Uranus, except its name. Given the nature of Pluton and Poseidon, Uranus could easily be an animate lifeform or an inanimate object.
