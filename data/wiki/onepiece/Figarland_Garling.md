# Figarland Garling

Source: https://onepiece.fandom.com/wiki/Figarland_Garling (fetched 2026-10-25). Bracketed [Ch. N] / [Ep. N] are the wiki's own citations.

Saint Figarland Garling [Ch. 1125, Ep. 1155] is one of the Five Elders who publicly head the World Government, holding the title Warrior God of Science and Defense; prior to this, he held the position of Supreme Commander of the Knights of God. A World Noble who hails from the Figarland Family, Garling is the biological father of Knights commander Shamrock, [Ch. 1137] as well as the pirate Shanks of the Four Emperors. [Ch. 1138]
Garling became the "champion" of God Valley during the battle that took place there 38 years ago. After the Egghead Incident, Garling was named an Elder to succeed Jaygarcia Saturn.
Due to his role, Garling serves as one of the main antagonists of the Final Saga along with the other Elders.

### Appearance
Garling is a tall, well-built man of advanced age, with a visibly wrinkled face and an aquiline nose. His pale-blond hair is long and very distinctively styled into a pair of unbent, side-facing spikes left and right on his head, alongside one extremely tall, forward-bent spike on top. Garling's facial hair consists of a mustache with a large beard linked to his sideburns (also forming spikes), which culminates in a large forward spike below his chin and makes it so that his hair-and-beard combo resembles a front-facing crescent moon; when seen from the front, his facial hair more so resembles a four-pointed star. He has red eyes too.
As a Knight of God, Garling wore what resembled a variant of the typical World Noble suit, red in color with a thick, standing collar, which was mostly hidden underneath an ankle-length, hooded black coat adorned with golden knobs (bearing the World Government's five-circle insignia) on the upper arms; unlike other Celestial Dragons, however, he did not wear gloves with his outfit. Garling's footwear consisted of black dress boots, and his eyes were covered by red circle-frame sunglasses. Upon being appointed as one of the Five Elders, Garling changed his wardrobe into a full dark-colored suit paired with a simple light-colored dress shirt and tie, plus a light red-colored, collared cape draped over his shoulders. He also took off his shades upon greeting his fellow Elders in the Room of Authority, but continued to carry his saber in his left hand.
Thirty-eight years ago, a much younger Garling had a similar appearance to now, his blonde head hair already matching its present-day size and shape (with two strands loose on either side of his face), though his facial hair was still absent beyond minor chin stubble and his facial appearance greatly resembles his sons, Shanks and Shamrock. Garling was apparently considered quite attractive given that he had female fans swooning over him. Like other Knights of God, he eschewed wearing a bubble around his head. Similar to his current cloak, Garling wore a red cape with a white fur trim particularly thick around the collar, and below a three-button shirt of gold color: high-collared, adorned on the left by two disc brooches of lighter gold color (blue in the middle) with the government symbol, and cut off above his abdomen like a half-shirt, transitioning into a light-purple lower half that went past his waistline, was thickly and vertically striated in red and white next to each front hem, and held closed by a brown belt with two gold buckles (red in the middle, the government symbol on each). In addition, Garling donned brown gloves and plate-armor components, namely two dark-gray vambraces and pauldrons (the latter made of a circular cop and one lame each). Garling's lower wear consisted of white pants with dark-gray greaves and dark-brown, ankle-high boots. [Ch. 1095, Ep. 1129]
Twenty-four years ago, Garling's face started to grow wrinkles, growing a mustache and his beard started getting longer. He wore a long, black, double-breasted trench coat with a furred collar cape with matching gloves and boots, all of matching colors.

### Personality
Garling is an equanimous and confident man. While not as self-indulgent, cowardly, or ignorant of the world as lesser World Nobles, he is just as cruel and depraved. He has regularly been shown touting the superiority of Celestial Dragons over the rest of the world's population, viewing commoners as "trash" and not even considering them to be people at all. He does not understand why anyone would desire to live in the Lower World and expressed pity toward his son Shanks for having been "deprived" in this way. [Ch. 1171] He judges harshly against Celestial Dragons who defend commoners, viewing them as just as, if not more, worthless and going so far as to execute Donquixote Mjosgard for this supposed grave crime.
Garling has desired common women, but only as trophies to improve his status or bear him children; typical for a male Celestial Dragon, he has craved women solely because of their beauty and will dispose of past lovers with no remorse or emotion whatsoever. [Ch. 1159] Saint Maffey expressed surprise upon learning that Garling had fathered sons, as she did not consider him the type to have children. However, Garling since demonstrated a considerable amount of interest in his progeny to be willing to commit significant time and manpower toward pursuing Shanks in his youth. Garling seems to treat subordinates poorly, as a person who called Maffey on God Valley expressed fear that Garling would kill them for losing track of his infant sons; [Ch. 1161] in the present day, when York complained about the lack of food in Mary Geoise, Garling cast the fault on her due to her previous association with Vegapunk.
Garling is extremely loyal to the World Government and its aims. Upon learning of the Davy Family's existence on God Valley and knowing of their marked status, he took quick action to arrange their slaughter in the Native Hunting Competition. When discovering new locations whose resources may benefit the World Government, such as God Valley, he has sought to diplomatically extend an invitation to affiliation, but should it be refused, he has no issue with taking what he and the government want by any means desired. [Ch. 1158] When the king of God Valley protested prior to the start of the native hunt, Garling immediately slaughtered him even though this was a penalty in the competition. As an Elder, Garling has demonstrated a practical mentality and inclination toward exacting necessary military force to deal with unrest, even if such actions might be considered undignified. Though shown to favor taking direct action, Garling also believes in fate, having credited it with his discovery of the Davy Family and also openly considering its role in the global upheaval unfolding since the last Levely. [Ch. 1179]
Garling has always been greatly self-assured, having no reservations about immediately dealing with the other Elders as equals upon his promotion. A younger Garling showed himself to be very confident in his abilities, claiming that his incurrence of a 10,000-point penalty during the aforesaid hunting contest (for starting early) was a good handicap to make things competitive. When the Rocks and Roger Pirates made landfall on God Valley, Garling was unperturbed and ready to take on the pirates despite both crews being the strongest of their time, highlighting his confident disposition; [Ch. 1096, Ep. 1130] as a result, he cursed in frustration when Rocks bested him. [Ch. 1162]

### Relationships

#### Family

##### Shamrock and Shanks
Because they are his sons, Garling wants Shamrock and Shanks to take their birthright as World Nobles. Although he was unsuccessful in retrieving Shanks from God Valley, Garling cared enough about him to commandeer missions to take him back from the Roger Pirates, and was persistent in attempting this despite the Knights of God being repelled every time. Garling pitied Shanks for having to spend nearly a quarter of a century living in the Lower World, and gladly accepted him into the fold when he finally arrived at Mary Geoise 15 years ago. However, Shanks' desire to reclaim his birth status was really an act, and he in fact has been shown to despise his father's elitism and disregard for the rest of the world. It is not known how Garling has viewed Shanks since he deserted Mary Geoise and gained infamy as a pirate. In contrast, Shamrock has been a trusted subordinate to his father as the Commander of the Knights of God.

##### Magnolia
Despite being a common woman from the West Blue, Magnolia became Garling's lover during his trip to God Valley 40 years ago. Garling did not disclose his position or status, and Magnolia grew attached to him, believing he would one day return and live with her and their sons on God Valley. However, Garling did not care for Magnolia at all and viewed her as disposable, expressing with no remorse during their reunion that not only would her home be destroyed soon, but that he intended to take Shakuyaku as his wife. Not interested in hearing any more from her, Garling inflicted a fatal blow on Magnolia; she did, though, survive long enough to attempt to get their sons far away from him. [Ch. 1160]

#### World Government

##### Nerona Imu
Garling has shown absolute loyalty to the King of the World, Nerona Imu. As a Knight of God, and being aware of the Davy Family's marked status, he wasted no time in reporting his discovery of the family on God Valley because he knew the king would be very interested to hear it. Garling was shown questioning Imu's elevation of the giant outsider Harald into the Knights, but as an Elder 24 years later, Garling rebuked his contemporaries when they expressed hesitation at Imu's intention to head to the Lower World, saying that they had no right to stop him.

##### Five Elders
As a Knight, Garling showed the due respect to the Five Elders, meeting with them on their time to discuss important information he had. Once Imu appointed him to the Elders as Saturn's successor, Garling made his way to the Room of Authority of his own accord and immediately began addressing the other Elders as equals. Since they were unaware at first that Garling had been promoted, the other Elders were outraged by his complete lack of deference until he revealed that he had taken on Saturn's title. Since then, Garling has taken his role seriously and has not been reserved about expressing his views on how to address pressing global affairs.

### Abilities and Powers
As Supreme Commander of the Knights of God, Garling held authority over the rest of the group and could pass judgment upon his fellow World Nobles for behavior deemed intolerable, making him one of the most eminent Celestial Dragons. [Ch. 1054, Ep. 1081] With his ascension to one of the Five Elders, Garling has become one of the five "Highest Authorities in the World" who wield unrivaled influence over world politics and government operations, second only to Imu's. Among other things, Garling's Elder status grants him the second-highest command (below Jewelry Bonney's) over all Kuma-model Pacifistas as well as the highest command over the Seraphim; [Ch. 1106] [Ch. 1069, Ep. 1100] additionally, the Vegapunk York, with her considerable scientific prowess, has been assigned as Garling's subordinate. If, as the Warrior God of Science and Defense, Garling himself has any scientific expertise like his predecessor Saturn is unclear. So far, he handles the defense portion of his title while delegating the science portion to York.
Little is currently known about Garling's combat prowess. However, given his leadership of the Knights of God whom Monkey D. Dragon, head of the Revolutionary Army, is wary of, [Ch. 1083, Ep. 1117] Garling being deemed a worthy candidate to be one of the Five Elders (all of whom have proven exceedingly powerful), as well as the great power held by both of Garling's sons, it can be assumed that Garling himself is quite a powerful individual.
Garling is said to have been a "champion" of God Valley. On said island 38 years ago, he was the World Nobles' favorite to win their Native Hunting Competition in which, despite starting with a negative 10,000 points, Garling successfully took the lead prior to the contest's interruption. [Ch. 1096, Ep. 1130] When both the Rocks and Roger Pirates (two of the strongest pirate crews of that era) arrived on God Valley, Garling was ready to take on the enemy with his fellow Knights of God; [Ch. 1096, Ep. 1130] nonetheless, Garling seemed to stand little chance against Rocks D. Xebec, finding himself quickly overpowered and grievously injured by the legendary pirate captain. [Ch. 1162] Apart from this and killing Rocks when he was defeated by Roger and Garp and his survival, what involvement Garling ultimately had in the God Valley Incident is still unknown.
During the Native Hunting Competition, Garling was seen riding an aviator cap-wearing flightless bird, similar to a Super Spot-Billed Duck.

#### Covenant
Through a covenant with Imu—either the Depths or Abyssal Covenant—Garling was granted an increase in power and several supernatural abilities.

##### Immortality
Through the covenant with Imu, Garling possesses immortality, manifested in the ability to regenerate from all injuries he sustains, allowing him to shrug off even fatal injuries like a massive cut in his head; however, damage from attacks infused with powerful Haki take longer to heal, as seen with the slashing damage Garling received from Rocks on God Valley. [Ch. 1162]

#### Weapons
Garling's trademark weapon is a large, double-edged straight sword with a circular, golden knuckle-bow guard, a hilt wrapped in purple leather and/or wire, a buttoned pommel also in gold, and a solid-black sheath accented with two golden bands near the guard. Garling already possessed this blade 38 years ago on God Valley, but his skill with the weapon remains unknown, as he has only been seen using it to slay Magnolia, God Valley's king, slaves, and an incapacitated Rocks D. Xebec.

### History

#### Past
40 years ago, while Garling was busy with something, he happened to sail to God Valley, where he met and impregnated Magnolia. Learning the island had a unique culture, Garling discovered that it was rich in resources, and he initially recommended that God Valley join the World Government, although the suggestion was rejected by the local population, as they couldn't reach an agreement. While staying on the island, he also discovered the existence of the Davy Family residing there. Returning back to Mary Geoise, Garling promptly met with the Five Elders to reveal his findings. Garling made it clear that their findings would be especially pleasing to Nerona Imu and urged them to select God Valley as the next location of the Native Hunting Competition.
39 years ago, Garling became a father to his twin sons Shamrock and Shanks. 38 years ago, Garling's request for the location of the next hunting competition came to pass. Returning to God Valley, Garling met with Magnolia, who introduced Garling to his sons, which he found adorable. Suggesting he can live with the three of them on the island, Garling revealed that God Valley would not exist for much longer and that he had already worked out a deal to secure an actual bride, Shakuyaku. Before Magnolia could properly react, Garling stabbed her and then issued an order for the Marines to move in and secure the island.
At the beginning of the attack, he executed the former king of the island for protesting the game and having his own countrymen culled for sport. This action led to Garling receiving a 10,000-point demerit for killing before the timer started, but Garling did not care and saw it as a suitable "handicap" to improve competition. Not long after, when Shakky was being presented as the prize of the tournament, Garling bickered with Shepherd Sommers over who would secure her as their bride. When the Davy Family members were presented, Saturn commended him on his find, but Garling simply chalked it up to fate. [Ch. 1160] Following an announcement about how many points the different hunting targets ("Rabbits") awarded and the prizes awaiting the winners, Garling was quick to enter the contest and amass 100,000 points by going for the high-value Rabbits. Once both the Rocks and Roger Pirates had made landfall on God Valley in a surprise assault, an irritated Garling issued the order to pull back the Marines and have them guard the Celestial Dragons instead, noting that the Knights of God were to handle the pirates instead.
Garling eventually came to blows with Rocks D. Xebec himself, having discovered him and various members of the Davy Family trying to escape. Although Garling was unable to stop Rocks from getting hold of his wife and son, he or his ally was somehow able to convert the various Davy Family members into demons. While pursuing Rocks and his family, Garling noted how Rocks' swordsmanship was quite powerful, making their regenerative abilities take significantly longer than usual. His fellow Knight of God reminded him that killing off Rocks would be worth it to please Imu, to which Garling agreed. Rocks and his family eventually split, allowing Garling's group to finally catch up. Although confident in their abilities, Garling found that even alone, Rocks was a very capable fighter. His overconfidence quickly resulted in he and his allies being defeated by the latter. [Ch. 1162]
Following an appearance by Imu, which led to Rocks being converted into a demon and, subsequently, his defeat by Garp and Roger, Garling decided to make his move. Reared by two of his fellow Knights of God, Garling approached the downed Rocks and delivered the killing blow. Following that, the island collapsed in on itself, forcing Garling to evacuate. [Ch. 1166] As a result of his actions, he was eventually declared a "champion" of God Valley. Due to Dragon's involvement, his son Shanks ended up in a treasure chest that was taken by the Roger Pirates and was thus raised by them as an apprentice until Roger's execution.
24 years ago, Garling met King Harald in Mary Geoise after the latter was granted the position of "Devoted Blade", making him an assistant of the Knights of God. Although skeptical of Imu's plans regarding Harald, Garling relented and granted his new subordinate a long-range Den Den Mushi with which to contact him, should he be needed.
15 years ago, Garling was ecstatic to finally meet his other son returning to Mary Geoise, believing he had suffered a lifetime of hardship in the lower world. He declared that Shanks could have anything he desired and to forget about his life on the surface, as well as any relationships he had with the surface folk. [Ch. 1171] Following Fisher Tiger's Assault on Mary Geoise, Garling introduced Shanks to Harald, voicing his high expectations for him.

#### Final Saga

##### Egghead Arc
Following the most recent Levely, Garling acted as a judge against Saint Donquixote Mjosgard, who was accused of abetting the escape of the pirates suspected of Saint Rosward Charlos' attempted murder. Garling chose to execute Mjosgard for protecting "trash" and was subsequently seen lounging in a chair at the execution site in Mary Geoise, with cheering Celestial Dragons around.
Later, Garling calmly listened to Vegapunk's pre-recorded worldwide message as the scientist spoke about the One Piece. [Ch. 1121, Ep. 1153] Following the events of the Egghead Incident, Garling received "orders" to become the new Warrior God of Science and Defense among the Five Elders (unbeknownst to the other Elders), effectively displacing Jaygarcia Saturn in his duties. After arriving in the Room of Authority, Garling proclaimed that York was now under his purview before alerting his fellow Elders to prepare themselves for an imminent wave of chaos and uncertainty regarding the future of the world.

##### Elbaph Arc
During the events of the World Government's assault on Elbaph, Imu was forced to return by Loki and Luffy's actions. Deciding that he could no longer rely on possession or the Knights of God to complete the takeover, Imu informed the Elders that he would personally be attending to the matter. While the other Elders were horrified, Garling concurred, declaring that they could no longer stand idly by while the government was assaulted from all sides. He was interrupted by his new employee, York, who demanded food, despite the shortage in Mary Geoise. Garling scolded her for dragging her feet, reminding her that their current problems could be resolved if she would simply produce energy with the Mother Flame. When York reminded him of the deal she made, Garling shut her down by mentioning that the original Vegapunk was partly responsible for the situation and that her hunger would serve as punishment. Garling continued the meeting by mentioning his belief that Nika's resurgence in the form of Luffy was the catalyst for all the recent disasters in the world. Having heard enough, Imu decided that the Holy Land would be left in their hands during his absence, to which Garling reminded his fellow Elders that they had no authority to stop their ruler.

### Early Concepts
Garling's original appearance varied a bit from his finished one. He was always envisioned as an elderly man upon introduction, but his hair and beard frequently changed. At one time, both were much softer in appearance, making him look like a regular World Noble. A design closer to his final one featured a similar hairstyle, but much less curved and significantly more pointed.
