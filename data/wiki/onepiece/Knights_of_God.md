# Knights of God

Source: https://onepiece.fandom.com/wiki/Knights_of_God (fetched 2026-10-21). Bracketed [Ch. N] / [Ep. N] are the wiki's own citations.

The Knights of God [Ch. 1160] are an elite squad of warriors from the Holy Land Mary Geoise, with authority to oversee the World Nobles. [Ch. 1054, Ep. 1081] They operated under the command of their Supreme Commander, Saint Figarland Garling, but Garling's command over the Knights in any capacity is now unknown following his promotion to one of the Five Elders. [Ch. 1125, Ep. 1155]
Due to their actions and role, they are the secondary antagonists of the Elbaph Arc and a major antagonist group of the Final Saga.

### Overview

#### Organization
The Knights of God are a group of combatants led by a Supreme Commander, with a Commander below that. [Ch. 1137, Ep. 1167] When invited to join their ranks, Loki suspected that such an invitation came with the status of World Noble, but no definitive answer was given; [Ch. 1136, Ep. 1166] notably, however, Harald was not granted that status when he became a full member. Gunko specifically referred to the status achieved through joining as "divine", indicating some supposed elevation upon joining. The Knights are headquartered at a temple called Shangara inside Mary Geoise.
Given the Five Elders' statement that only 13 people possess the Depths and Abyssal Covenants at one time, and given that the Elders also possess Covenant-related abilities, this would suggest that there are no more than eight full-fledged Knights of God at any given time. [Ch. 1167]
The Knights of God employ lower-ranking subordinates similar to squires known as Devoted Blades of God. [Ch. 1160] [Ch. 1167] Devoted Blades may be granted knighthood after sufficiently proving themselves on enough missions, but the timeframe depends on the person. Shanks of the Figarland Family was on track to be promoted after only a year in the government's service, while it took the outsider Harald ten years to be promoted, and it would have been longer had Shanks, the first choice, not disappeared. It appears that most Devoted Blades are not aware of Imu's existence until they are knighted (Gunko being an exception due to Imu being present during the invasion of Esperia and interest in unknown abilities she may have [Ch. 1155] [Ch. 1186]), but they come to meet the ruler at the Empty Throne for the knighthood ceremony, where Imu invokes the Depths Covenant on their body. [Ch. 1168]

#### Duties and Practices
The Knights of God are an elite combat force that carries the highest law enforcement authority in the world, though most enforcement is left to the Marines and the Knights are only called upon in select instances. They most commonly exercise their jurisdiction over the Holy Land Mary Geoise; in particular, they alone possess the authority to intervene in disputes between World Nobles. The Supreme Commander is known to serve as a mediator and judge, being capable of sentencing another World Noble to death if they are found guilty of an offense.
The Knights of God will actively mobilize if Mary Geoise faces a significant enough outside threat, being a supplement to the more typical Marine protection. Monkey D. Dragon and Emporio Ivankov have speculated that the Knights of God may be mobilized in response to the Revolutionary Army's harmful exploits against the World Government since their attack on the Levely and that the Knights may specifically target Sabo due to the latter being a new symbol of hope for uprising nations. This, according to Dragon, could mark the beginning of the real battle awaiting the Revolutionaries. The Knights of God also perform sporadic missions in the outside world at the behest of Imu. They were called upon to annex Elbaph, one of the world's most powerful independent nations, into the World Government by force and initially undertook this mission without outside reinforcement, and in the past, were also called upon to retrieve Princess Shuri from the Esperia Kingdom. The Knights' and Devoted Blades' everyday activities are not heavily regimented, and they may freely travel to the Lower World of their own accord. However, they are sworn to total secrecy about their activities in Mary Geoise.
Aside from their jurisdiction over Mary Geoise, the Knights do not possess any more influence over political affairs than the other World Nobles. The Elders stated that Commander Shamrock should not get involved in politics because of his standing, especially during the Levely. However, the Knights may request an audience with the Elders to provide information and suggestions regarding the World Government's plans and activities.
The Knights generally do not adhere to the standardized attire of other World Nobles, such as the thick white suit with knobs, resin bubbles, or glass helmets that isolate air supply from non-Nobles, nor the "Noble Opener" hairstyle. Instead, they wear ornate black military uniforms during active duty, and have been shown donning metal armor during the Native Hunting Competition, [Ch. 1083, Ep. 1117] Even when off-duty, they do not dress the same way as their lower-ranked compatriots, as Sommers was in a state of undress while relaxing in his residence. [Ch. 1140, Ep. 1170] Garling, while still the Supreme Commander, wore a black variant of the World Nobles' robes, while performing the execution of Saint Donquixote Mjosgard. While travelling, members have worn full cloaks with hoods to obscure their appearances. [Ch. 1134, Ep. 1164] When Harald served as a Devoted Blade of God and eventually coronated into full knighthood, he retained his own outfit as an Elbaph warrior and king instead.
Although they do not engage in many of the slovenly behaviors that typical World Nobles partake in, this does not preclude the Knights of God from exhibiting the same haughty and selfish attitudes and inflicting harm and suffering on commoners as they see fit. Indeed, much of their role as arbitrators in the Holy Land is to enforce the established social hierarchy, and so they will take action against Celestial Dragons found to be acting in the interests of commoners. They consider themselves gods, like all World Nobles do, and Gunko has said that a commoner receiving an invitation to join their ranks is nothing less than a divine miracle. They also take part in the Native Hunting Competitions, with their increased combat power making them heavy favorites to win. Knights have been shown to covet commoners for forced marriage like typical World Nobles, as well as brutally murder commoners for no reason other than convenience and personal desire. [Ch. 1159]

### Abilities and Powers
As World Nobles, they possess immense authority and share the same privileges as their lesser brethren. However, they can fully defend themselves without relying on an Admiral whenever disturbed. They are also apparently more well-informed than a usual World Noble, with Gunko having knowledge about the resurgence of the Great War and Sommers instantly knowing Jaguar D. Saul's status as a former Vice Admiral and his survival in the Ohara Incident at first glance (by comparison, Saint Rosward Shalria does not even know what the Void Century is despite it being common knowledge amongst the World Nobles).
Their position gives them even greater authority than the average Celestial Dragon. If any World Nobles are in danger while the Knights of God are present, then the Knights will work to protect said nobles from harm. However, should a World Noble act in a way that defies proper Celestial Dragon conduct, the Knights of God can and will punish them accordingly and even execute them if they do something truly out of line, such as defending those who attack another World Noble. The only people who exceed their jurisdiction are the Five Elders and Nerona Imu, and it appears that the Knights are top contenders to be promoted to Elder should a spot open - with the Supreme Commander being a clear choice.
Alongside the Five Elders, the Knights of God appear to be the only World Nobles with combat capabilities, but they have shown themselves to be one of the top fighting forces in the world; based on Dragon's comments, it is apparent that they are the most powerful and dangerous combat group in the World Government's service. Due to their Depths Covenant, they possess superhuman strength, and the degree of their power is evident by the fact that only three members were commissioned to subjugate Elbaph despite the near-unparalleled might of the kingdom's giant population, and they quickly managed to get the upper hand with a combination of fighting prowess and strategy even before Imu joined the invasion. They were significant players in the God Valley Incident as well, as they did battle against the fearsome Rocks Pirates. Notably, though, top combatant Garling did suffer defeat to Rocks D. Xebec in this conflict and only attained the title of "champion" by finishing Rocks off after he was defeated and incapacitated later on. [Ch. 1162] In addition, the Knights of God suffered multiple defeats against the Roger Pirates in the years following the God Valley Incident. [Ch. 1170]
Several of the Knights possess powerful Devil Fruit abilities, which they have shown great versatility with as well as awakening, and they also possess various weapons, which they have consistently shown immense skill in using.
It appears that Supreme King Haki is not a common ability among the Knights of God, as Scopper Gaban, who fought against the Knights multiple times, had never fought one who could use this type of Haki before fighting Harald. Harald is currently the only confirmed Supreme King Haki user to serve as a Knight of God.

#### Covenant
All Knights of God and Devoted Blades of God are marked with a Covenant as a contract of their service to Imu. Once a member is promoted to full knighthood, they are upgraded from the Shallows to the Depths Covenant by Imu and are granted esoteric abilities that they share with the Five Elders. These abilities include an increase of strength to the level of superhuman and use of the Abyss, which allows them to travel great distances across the world or summon their fellow Knights to their position, as well as immortality that prevents them from being killed. They can easily regenerate from the majority of attacks, with only a select few immensely powerful individuals showing the capability to bypass this.
However, these abilities and the Covenant come at the cost of being totally subservient to Imu, which allows the ruler to overrule a Knight's personal will and even possess their body outright. While Devoted Blades can elude Imu's grasp if they are away from Mary Geoise, the Depths Covenant results in the bearer being at Imu's mercy no matter where in the world they are. [Ch. 1169]

### History

#### Past
When the Knights of God were formed is unknown, but they existed at least 62 years ago as Manmayer Gurou was already a member. [Ch. 1186] 40 years ago, the Knight Figarland Garling personally met with the Five Elders to discuss intel on God Valley. [Ch. 1158] 38 years ago, they participated in the scheduled Native Hunting Competition, whose location of God Valley had been recommended by Garling. The competition devolved into chaos when the Rocks Pirates and Roger Pirates arrived on the island, and the Knights of God entered the fray of combat. Garling took on Rocks D. Xebec and was defeated, but later killed the pirate after he had been defeated by Gol D. Roger and Monkey D. Garp, causing him to be renowned as the "champion" of God Valley. Garling had also fathered twin sons with a woman from the island, and commissioned Satchels Maffey to acquire them during the slaughter. Maffey managed to obtain Shamrock, but overlooked Shanks, and so Shamrock eventually became a Knight of God alongside his father.
Some time after the God Valley Incident, the Knights of God became aware that Shanks was onboard the Roger Pirates' ship. They attempted multiple times to steal the boy back, but were thwarted each time.
Soon after God Valley, the giant king Harald entered a pact of servitude toward the World Government in the hope of obtaining affiliation for the long-independent nation of Elbaph. After 18 years of service, Harald was granted membership in the Devoted Blades of God 20 years before the present. Five years later, Shanks returned to Mary Geoise and joined the Devoted Blades as well, but worked against the government during Fisher Tiger's raid and then suddenly left a year later. Because of Shanks' desertion, Harald was promoted to the Knights of God and received the Depths Covenant from Imu. After placing an Abyss in his castle in Elbaph, Harald was commanded by Imu to mobilize his subjects into a fighting force serving the World Government. Harald objected, as he had entered into this service to promote a culture of peace on Elbaph, but Imu used the Covenant to assert control over him. However, Harald would somehow be killed soon afterward, with his son Loki being attributed as the killer.

#### Whole Cake Island Saga

##### Levely Arc
During the Levely, the commander of the Knights of God, Figarland Shamrock, arrived in Mary Geoise under disguise for a meeting with the Five Elders. After arriving at Pangaea Castle's Room of Authority, Shamrock informed them that he needed to discuss a "certain pirate".

#### Wano Country Saga

##### Wano Country Arc
The Knights of God were involved in the aftermath of the Revolutionary Army's assault on Mary Geoise, taking over the investigation into the assassination attempt on Saint Charlos and the involvement of Mjosgard in the purported suspects' escape.

#### Final Saga

##### Egghead Arc
Garling personally executed Mjosgard in the Domain of the Gods for the latter's treason. Following the death of Jaygarcia Saturn, Garling was promoted to the position of Warrior God of Science and Defense within the Five Elders, leaving his status as a member of Knights of God unknown.

##### Elbaph Arc
The Knights of God were commissioned to subjugate and annex the land of giants, Elbaph, in order to ensure the giants would fight on the World Government's side when the Great War revived. Commander Figarland Shamrock and Manmayer Gunko arrived on Elbaph via the Abyss for the purpose of recruiting the imprisoned Loki to their ranks, though he refused to join. Gunko attempted a more forceful manner of recruiting, beating Loki into submission, but Loki's answer did not change. Moving on to taking over Elbaph, Shamrock called for another Knight to be summoned as reinforcement, [Ch. 1137, Ep. 1167] but two of them, Shepherd Sommers and Rimoshifu Killingham, were summoned instead because Shamrock was being called back to Mary Geoise.
Gunko, Sommers, and Killingham utilized their Devil Fruits to kidnap a group of schoolchildren and lead them to a boat to be taken hostage, with Killingham creating MMA dream monsters to occupy the adults in the village. They eventually encountered resistance from the villagers and some of the Straw Hat Pirates, but overwhelmed them fairly easily. The Knights used the schoolchildren to coerce Elbaph's elder Jarul into allowing the Owl Library to be burned down, signifying a turning away from Harald's efforts toward peace and education. [Ch. 1146] Scopper Gaban then arrived and overpowered Sommers, [Ch. 1148] but Gunko managed to best him. [Ch. 1149]
Right then, Imu decided to possess Gunko in order to hasten the takeover, as Mary Geoise was in a dire situation. [Ch. 1149] Imu quickly transformed many of the Giant Warrior Pirates into demons, turning them against Elbaph's population alongside Killingham's strengthened MMA. [Ch. 1150] However, the children's relatives destroyed the escape ship, and the children were rescued by Monkey D. Luffy and Loki, the latter having been freed. [Ch. 1174] The arrival of Loki, the Straw Hats' Monster Trio, and the New Giant Warrior Pirates led to Sommers and Killingham being overpowered while the demons and MMA were quelled, and Loki used Ragnir to encase the possessed Gunko in ice. [Ch. 1178] With no other recourse, Imu came to Elbaph in person and used his Omen to give Sommers and Killingham a boost in power. [Ch. 1180]
