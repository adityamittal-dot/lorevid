# Rocks D. Xebec

Source: https://onepiece.fandom.com/wiki/Rocks_D._Xebec (fetched 2026-10-25). Bracketed [Ch. N] / [Ep. N] are the wiki's own citations.

Rocks D. Xebec, [Ch. 1154] born Davy D. Xebec, was the captain of the legendary Rocks Pirates. Born on God Valley, he was a member of the Davy Family and the father of Marshall D. Teach, one of the current Four Emperors. His goal in life was to become King of the World, [Ch. 1155] a title he believed was held by his ancestor, the legendary pirate Davy D. Jones. [Ch. 1164]
Rocks was a dominant figure four decades ago, [Ch. 907, Ep. 887] during which he had assembled one of the strongest and most infamous pirate crews in history, many of whom went on to be highly infamous pirates themselves. He had a rivalry with Gol D. Roger years before Roger reached the heights of his own power. He was also a close friend of Elbaph's most recent king, Harald, and was idolized by Harald's son Loki.
His dominance over the seas came to an end during the God Valley Incident, during which he was corrupted by Nerona Imu into a demon and forced to battle Roger and Monkey D. Garp against his will [Ch. 1164] before ultimately being killed by Figarland Garling while in a weakened state. [Ch. 1166] His defeat, which was publicly attributed solely to Garp, is what earned Garp the epithet of "Hero of the Marines."
Due to his actions and his relationship with several major characters, he is a prominent character in the flashback events of the Elbaph Arc.

### Appearance
Rocks was a tall muscular man, but more normally proportioned in comparison to some of his huge (albeit not giant-sized) crewmates. He had blue and white hair in unruly spikes, with a thin strand falling over the right side of his face and a purple lightning bolt-shaped tattoo running alongside his left eye. Overall, Rocks' facial features greatly resembled those of his son, Marshall D. Teach, including a bulbous nose, a prominent-lipped mouth, and dark stubble that grows into a beard at the chin, but with a full set of teeth unlike Teach's. He had slanted eyes and a thick neck. Although sporting a large physique like Teach, Rocks had more visible definition and development to his muscles instead of his son's more barrel-like physique. Rocks' main outfit consisted of a black feathery coat on his back, red wrist cuffs, black pants with a gold chain on the right side, and black boots. He kept his chest bare, wore a red, fur-trimmed belt around his abdomen, and had gold earrings.
Upon becoming a demon, Rocks became much bigger, towering over his subordinates Newgate and Linlin, with protruding fangs and flaming eyes, though he lacked the horns prevalent in other demons. His coat appeared to flow into large bat-like wings, with the shape of a flame over his shoulders.

### Personality
Rocks was a man of extreme ambition who dreamed of becoming the "King of the World." His ambition and greed were unchecked and endless, often making him brutal and destructive, not caring who he attacked or the destruction or chaos caused as long as he could satisfy his personal desires. Despite his violent and selfish nature, he was consistent and honest about adhering to his desires, operating under his own personal code of honor and dignity. During his reign, he led his crew in spreading chaos and destruction across the seas. According to Whitebeard, Rocks was careless and easily distracted in most of his efforts, commonly losing sight of the tasks at hand and going off on his own, either out of sheer amusement or boredom. [Ch. 1096, Ep. 1130] According to Rocks himself, he was not the type of person who would take "no" as an answer.
Rocks often resorted to violence to get his own way and to ensure that, due to their fear, fewer people would refuse him. This extended even to children, as can be seen in his treatment of the young giant Loki. When Rocks and his subordinates first arrived on Elbaph, Rocks did not hesitate to violently injure Loki just to give Harald a reason to immediately return to Elbaph; even when Loki still wished to join Rocks' crew, Rocks refused to take Loki out to sea with him, not out of concern for the child's safety but because he did not want to be bothered with a child on his ship. When Loki challenged him to a duel, he savagely defeated the boy and left a blade impaled into him. Despite his habit of turning to violence, Rocks was also capable of reasoning, as seen when he asked Harald to talk in private when the giant came to attack Rocks and his crew, despite his own crew's willingness to attack Harald. [Ch. 1159]
Rocks' lack of self-restraint in his ambition led him to believe that no one can rule the world forever. Capricious and anarchistic, he seemed to let the whims of fate direct his path, not caring to listen to orders or to make plans beyond his own desire to do as he liked. While reckless, he was also curious, and his curiosity led him to dabble in subjects that the World Government had deemed forbidden, even to the point of infiltrating Mary Geoise, learning of the existence of Imu, and confronting him in the Room of Flowers. During said confrontation, Rocks claimed to be a "worshiper of Davy Jones", his legendary ancestor. [Ch. 1155] As a self-proclaimed worshiper of the legendary pirate, Rocks repeatedly engaged and partook in the pirate tradition of Davy Back Fights, using said fights to attack and recruit numerous pirates into his crew in a manner similar to how the pirate Foxy would in the future. Unlike Foxy, however, Rocks seemingly prioritized quality over quantity when it came to his use of the Davy Back Fight, as he took his time accumulating numerous high-profile and powerful pirates, scoundrels, and outlaws before ultimately formally creating his infamous pirate crew. [Ch. 1155]
While appearing callous and cruel on the surface, Rocks was also honorable and deeply cared for those he genuinely respected, a stark contrast from his son Teach. This can be seen when Kaidou suggested forcibly subduing Rocks' friend Harald and his fellow giants of Elbaph, and Rocks' response was to angrily kick and threaten Kaidou. [Ch. 1156] His loyalty to those he considered friends could also be seen when he demanded Imu not to harm Harald and his people, even though he disagreed with Harald's mission of making Elbaph a member of the World Government. When Imu brainwashed and transformed Rocks into a demon, he resisted desperately and even begged Garp and Roger to kill him, preferring to die rather than to submit, showing his unyielding nature and pride. Right before his death, Rocks internally thanked them for stopping his rampage and begged them to get out of God Valley alive, a sign of respect for his enemies and rivals. Rocks was also a loving father and husband, sending his wife and child back to his hometown of God Valley to protect them from his extended criminal record and ties to the Davy Family. Rocks was willing to put his family's well-being above his own, even openly declaring he was content to die if his wife and son managed to survive. Despite his generally serious demeanor, Rocks could display humor as well, being one of the many men who were lovestruck by Shakuyaku after she opened a bar on Hachinosu.
Overall, his character traits resembled those of other inheritors of the Will of D. Like Teach, Rocks appeared to deeply believe in the workings of fate, which also contributed to his apparent acceptance of an eventual death. However, unlike his son, Rocks was an utterly fearless individual who never backed down from a fight, embracing the thrill of battle wholeheartedly regardless of the opponent, and Rocks was a far more honorable man than his son. In this regard, he much more resembled the likes of Monkey D. Luffy and his contemporary rival Gol D. Roger.
Like many other characters, Rocks had a unique laugh: "Vohahahahahaha!". [Ch. 1155]

### Relationships

#### Family

##### Davy D. Jones
Commonly known as the mythical pirate "Davy Jones", he is the ancestor of the Davy Family that is highly persecuted by the World Government and, more specifically, the World Nobles. Despite being the reason his family is ruthlessly hunted by the government, Rocks held nothing but the upmost respect and admiration for his ancestor. He went as far as to present himself as a worshipper of Davy Jones and would constantly partake in the pirate games dedicated to his distant ancestor, the Davy Back Fight, in order to build his crew.
Despite his admiration for his ancestor, Rocks was smart enough to know that being part of his bloodline would make him a top-priority target for death by the highest echelons of the World Government and thus changed his real name in order to ensure nobody would learn of his connection to the legendary figure. However, Rocks shows absolutely no shame in being Davy Jones' descendant and instead takes it in great stride. The only people whom Rocks trusted enough to tell the truth about his lineage were his wife, Eris, and King Harald of Elbaph.
During his interaction with Imu on God Valley, Rocks claimed that Davy D. Jones was the previous king of the world and asked Imu whether he feared him or Joy Boy more, much to Imu's anger, as he claimed Jones was never a king for even a moment and that both he and Joy Boy would never return as long as Xebec and his family were extinguished. This belief that Jones once ruled the world seems to be Rocks' motivation for wanting to rule the world himself, as despite being backed into a corner by Imu's demonic powers, he still mocked him by claiming he feared the name of "Davy" and that eventually, someone would inherit Davy D. Jones' will and fulfill a certain promise—which is the reason why Imu both despises Jones on the same level as Joy Boy and seeks to end his bloodline to ensure the promise will never be fulfilled.

##### Eris
At some point, Rocks would meet a woman named Eris, who would eventually become his wife. However, this was kept secret from everyone, even from his own crew. Despite this, Rocks seemed very happy with his marriage to Eris despite his piracy career forcing him to be away from her for so long. As Shakuyaku noticed how content Rocks was following the birth of his son, she wondered if he had found someone else, as he no longer seemed to obsess over her like almost every other man in her presence.
During the God Valley Incident, Rocks prioritized finding Eris and their child so that the two of them could escape the World Nobles' grasp, with him even ignoring Shakky's attempted enslavement, which was the main cause of the Roger Pirates and most of his own crew's reason for coming to God Valley. In his talk with Harald before the incident, he confessed that as long as Eris and their son could make it out alive, he considered everything else, his own life included, to be a secondary concern, showcasing his profound love and faith for his wife.
While demonized, Xebec believed he had killed his wife at Imu's order (unaware that she and Teach had been saved by Kuma), so he begged Roger and Garp to kill him as he no longer wished to live.

##### Marshall D. Teach
Rocks was shown to be a surprisingly good father to his son, as despite his natural viciousness to almost everyone he meets and lack of comradery between him and his crew, he was shown to be very content following the birth of his son when he and the Rocks Pirates were visiting Shakky's bar on Hachinosu, with Buckingham Stussy realizing his pleasant attitude was the same as that of a new father, while Shakky and the others all believed it to be because he simply found a woman.
Knowing full well that the World Government would target his son for death due to being a member of Davy D. Jones' bloodline, Rocks immediately instructed his wife, Eris, to take their son to his homeland of God Valley to hide there with the rest of the Davy Family. Upon learning that the government chose God Valley to be the next unaffiliated country for their Native Hunting Competition, he was quick to put the pieces together that their real reason was in order to exterminate the Davy bloodline. This caused him to drop everything to go there and ensure his son's safety, with his crew luckily also wanting to go because of learning that Shakky had been kidnapped as the event's major prize, as he casually told Harald that as long as he got his son and wife out alive, nothing else (including his life) mattered, showing that Rocks valued Teach over even his goal of becoming the King of the World.
In his demonized state, Xebec believed he had killed Teach by Imu's order (unaware that he and his mother were saved by Kuma) and desired that Roger and Garp kill him while crying over the belief that he had murdered his son.

##### Other Relatives
While his relationship with the rest of his family was not known, it was evidently clear that Xebec cared for them. When his father and grandmother, whom Xebec affectionately referred them "pop" and "granny" were turned into demons by Imu and made them try to kill both Eris and Teach, Xebec was furious at Imu for his action and that he had to reluctantly kill them to stop their rampage.

#### Allies

##### Crew
Despite being the captain of the crew, and even if they recognized his strength, in general, Rocks was not respected at all among his subordinates. While they did go along with his plans and followed his orders in battle, they made it plenty clear that they were only out for themselves and only aligned with Rocks for their own benefit. Buckingham Stussy explained that the only reason they were with Rocks was solely because their plans could bring them great fortune and fame.
Charlotte Linlin considered him to be untrustworthy and "good-for-nothing." [Ch. 1041, Ep. 1068] Shiki laughed at the notion of caring about Rocks' life, stating to Harald that it was completely irrelevant to him, or any other member of the crew, whether Rocks lived or died. Whitebeard was disgusted by his cruelty towards even children, mentioning having bad experiences with him and his crew. [Ch. 964, Ep. 963] Kaidou respected his strength, recognizing Rocks as one of the few capable of challenging him in combat, but in general, they had a hostile relationship. [Ch. 1001, Ep. 1016] Rocks himself did not seem to care much for his crew, as the members were constantly arguing and killing each other, and he also did not hesitate to attack or threaten any of his crew members if they did or said something that angered him and openly declared that they could leave if they did not agree with his plan. [Ch. 1156]
Overall, Rocks' relationship with his crew closely resembled his son's relationship with his own crew: pirates who had no true comradery and were simply united by their alignment of interests.

#### Enemies

##### World Government
Since Rocks had the ambition to become the "King of the World", he was naturally opposed to the World Government as the current globe-ruling regime, including the Celestial Dragons, who were the focus of his attacks during the God Valley Incident.
Rocks' actions were destructive and anarchic enough that the government considered him akin to a terrorist, meaning many of his feats were not properly recorded or were simply erased from history, causing Rocks to eventually fade from public consciousness, save the memory of some veteran Marines and the former members of his crew.

##### Nerona Imu
Most notably among his enemies is the secret ruler of the World Government, Nerona Imu. Rocks accomplished a feat that had not been seen before or since: invading Imu's inner sanctum, the Room of Flowers. Rocks kidnapped five kings attending the Levely that year and attempted to use them as a bargaining chip, but Imu simply did not care and paid him no mind. While there, Rocks cautiously realized there was nothing he could do but menacingly declared that he would one day return, likely to dethrone Imu once and for all, though this would not be fulfilled following the fall of the Rocks Pirates and Rocks' death at God Valley.
While Imu initially ignored the pirate, upon learning that Rocks was a member of the Davy Family, descendants of Davy D. Jones, and members of the family were hiding in secret on God Valley, Imu arrived at God Valley during the eponymous incident to ensure Rocks and his clan were completely erased and subsequently converted Rocks into a demon and ordered him to kill his own family, which led to Garp and Roger teaming up to stop his rampage.

##### Garp and Roger
Rocks appeared to be familiar enough with Vice Admiral Monkey D. Garp to know the latter's rebellious dislike toward the World Government, claiming he would've been better off being a pirate and showing respect for his abilities. At the same time, Rocks had a strong rivalry with Gol D. Roger of the Roger Pirates while on the seas. During the God Valley Incident, Roger and Garp joined forces to stop Rocks after he was transformed into a demon by Imu. That said, Garp is not fond of recounting this story despite its role in solidifying his reputation as a hero, since it involved multiple details being fabricated, him allying with a pirate, and the fact that he only received said reputation because the government suppressed the fact of Roger's involvement.
After Garp and Roger broke the curse, Rocks felt nothing but gratitude for his rivals, and even in his final moments, under the threat of the approaching Knights of God: Figarland Garling, Shepherd Sommers, and Satchels Maffey, he was more worried about them than himself. His final thoughts were thanking them for stopping his rampage.

#### Others

##### Harald
Rocks met Harald when the giant king snuck into the Levely 56 years ago. After this meeting of chance, Rocks developed an interest in Harald and repeatedly tried to recruit him, though he was refused every time on the grounds that it would sour whatever friendly relationship Elbaph had secured with the other countries should its king choose to ally himself with a known criminal. Rocks was confident that he could get Harald to join his crew and desired for him to eat Elbaph's legendary Devil Fruit, seeing him and Elbaph as a whole as the key to taking down the Holy Land, showing he had great respect for the king's abilities in combat and the strength of the giants.
Interestingly, when Kaidou suggested Rocks forcibly submit Harald to his rule, Rocks angrily attacked him for it, claiming that he considered Harald a friend and always went back to Elbaph to drink and try to invite him into his crew, no matter how many times Harald turned down his offer. [Ch. 1156] After Harald accepted the Five Elders' offer to kill him in exchange for Elbaph joining the World Government, this led to another fight between the two, where Rocks tried to convince Harald that the World Government was only using him, but his attempts fell on deaf ears. Ultimately, Rocks could not bring himself to kill Harald, but he ended his friendship with him and announced he was done trying to recruit the giant king. [Ch. 1158]
Rocks and Harald would meet for the last time near God Valley, under the monitoring of the World Government. In their last meeting, Rocks trusted Harald enough to tell him his real name and his lineage, something he had not even shared with anyone in his own crew, and asked Harald to lend him a hand saving his family. Being understanding of Rocks' position and desire to save his family at God Valley, Harald refused to help the Rocks Pirates directly but still allowed them to sail away unobstructed. When Imu stated his intention to subjugate Elbaph during the clash on God Valley, Rocks angrily demanded Imu to leave Harald and his nation alone, showing respect and understanding of Harald's desires for his homeland. Rocks' death devastated Harald, who tearfully regretted that he abandoned his friend in his time of need for the sake of his mission of bringing peace to his homeland.

##### Loki
Rocks met Loki when the giant prince was only 15 years old. At first, Loki underestimated Rocks and picked a fight with him only to be seriously injured. After Rocks proclaimed his ambition toward Harald to entice him to join his crew, Loki found his sense of purpose through his charisma and deeply idolized Rocks ever since. Despite Loki idolizing him and requesting to join his crew, Rocks rejected him and savagely beat Loki with a bladed weapon when the prince demanded a fight. [Ch. 1145, Ep. 1175] Loki repeatedly asked Rocks to allow him to join his crew, and Rocks continuously denied him for not wanting a child on his crew and was bemused that the person he did want would not join him but his son was the opposite. Rocks would sometimes speak to Loki more casually, denying to the boy that he was Nika, who Loki said would destroy the world, as Rocks had no such intention, and declared his intentions to one day team up with his father to rally the Galleila giants and take over the world. [Ch. 1166]
In spite of all the cruel and abusive treatment Loki occasionally received from Rocks, he was devastated upon learning of his death and still idolizes and admires him in the present, lamenting that he was simply "born in the wrong era."

##### Joy Boy
During his fight against Imu on God Valley, Rocks revealed that he knew of Joy Boy's existence alongside Davy D. Jones when he asked Imu which of the two they feared more, much to Imu's anger at the mention of the names of both legendary pirates who lived during the Void Century. It is unknown how Rocks learned of Joy Boy's existence without having gone to Laugh Tale and over a decade before his own rival Roger was able to learn such when he and his crew traveled there, but this shows that Rocks views Joy Boy on the same level of respect as his own ancestor as a personal and powerful enemy of Imu.

### Abilities and Powers
Rocks was one of the most powerful pirates in history, and was considered the most influential pirate of his time, referred to as the "Era of Rocks". [Ch. 1157] He was regarded as the first and most formidable rival of the Pirate King, Gol D. Roger—surpassing even the challenge later posed by his own subordinate, Edward Newgate, who came to be renowned as the "Strongest Man in the World". [Ch. 957, Ep. 958] Rocks' defeat was also what caused Garp to become a legend and to be known as the "Hero of the Marines", showing how much of a threat the World Government considered him to be. Kaidou, who by the present has come to be called the world's "Strongest Creature", included Xebec when he listed a very small number of notable figures that were worthy to stand against him in battle, alongside Kouzuki Oden, Shanks, Newgate, and Roger. [Ch. 1001, Ep. 1016]
Rocks led a crew composed of exceptionally strong pirates, many of whom had clashing personalities and little camaraderie. As a group, they regularly thwarted and overwhelmed pursuing Marine battleships even when outnumbered, razed several civilizations, and even orchestrated the downfall of an unknown kingdom. After the crew's dissolution, several of its members rose to great infamy; "Whitebeard" Edward Newgate, "Big Mom" Charlotte Linlin, and Kaidou came to constitute three of the original Four Emperors, widely regarded as the strongest pirates in the world during the Great Pirate Era. Another prominent member, the "Golden Lion" Shiki, would become a rival to Roger and Whitebeard after Rocks' death, while Captain John would also go on to be famous for his legendary treasure. Rocks was evidently adept in the Davy Back Fight, which he utilized to form his crew, and was not indicated to have lost any of the challenges he posed. He conquered the lawless island of Hachinosu, bringing all the criminals inside it under his control and ruling over the island until his death. [Ch. 1155]
Rocks' bold actions led to him becoming involved with World Government taboos like few others have throughout history; in total, 25 of his crimes were completely shrouded from public knowledge. He was considered so dangerous by the World Government that they attempted to utilize leverage with Elbaph to get its king, Harald, to take care of him. [Ch. 1157] When this did not work, Imu directly intervened during the God Valley Incident to personally confront the pirate. Rocks appeared to have considerable knowledge of historical groups and objects, such as Galleila, which he plotted to find and use to aid in his goals. [Ch. 1156] Although he normally took a fearless and aggressive approach toward combating even his strongest foes, Rocks did show himself to be capable of strategy and forethought, even in the heat of battle as shown when he told Harald during their first clash to strike their surroundings to enable a mutual escape. [Ch. 1155]
Rocks possessed exceptional willpower, which was best shown when he resisted Imu's Domi Reversi technique to keep from being transformed into a demon long enough to exchange a few more taunts with the ruler. Even after he was transformed, Rocks could manifest his true will through the Voice of All Things which allowed his true self to communicate with Roger and Garp; he was also able to shed tears to communicate his sorrow.

#### Physical Abilities
Rocks possessed an immense level of superhuman strength, which factored heavily in his swordsmanship style. In combat, Rocks was regularly seen inflicting massive damage to large ships (including Marine battleships and the giant Harald's flagship) with ease, and was said to have even destroyed one of the Gates of Justice. Early in his pirating career, he accomplished the extremely rare feat of defeating a Marine Admiral, dealing them a fatal wound in the process. He also clashed with Harald, a powerful Elbaph warrior whose ancient giant heritage made him much bigger and stronger than an average giant, multiple times without issue, and even managed to overpower Harald in some of their duels. Notably, he regularly met Harald's strikes in midair, demonstrating sufficient strength to counter him with no leverage whatsoever.
During the God Valley Incident, he bested Figarland Garling, a tremendously powerful member of the Knights of God who would go on to become its Supreme Commander and eventually one of the Five Elders, in direct combat. [Ch. 1162] He was also shown physically overpowering a young Kaidou, whose physical might would become nearly unparalleled later in time, as an act of discipline. Rocks possessed tremendous jumping power, which he regularly employed to clash with Harald in midair; on God Valley, he leaped up to Imu's manifested head dozens of meters above the ground in order to slice it. [Ch. 1163]

#### Swordsmanship
Rocks was a tremendously powerful swordsman whose strikes were famous for blasting away whatever he struck as though they were hit by a gigantic hammer or cannonball. He had no issue clashing swords with Harald, whose own sword dwarfed his entire body. With a swing of his sword, he could blast a hole straight through Marine battleships, [Ch. 1157] swiftly injure Harald's giant son Loki, and easily cleave through one of Saturn's gyuki legs. [Ch. 1163] When battling Figarland Garling on God Valley, Rocks launched a flying slash which ripped through the ground of the island all the way to the ocean, creating a small chasm. Notably, he seemingly performed many of these feats without using Armament Haki, as he was rarely shown hardening his sword with it.

#### Haki

##### Supreme King Haki
Rocks was one of the few people in the world known to possess Supreme King Haki. Clashes between him and fellow Supreme King wielder Harald regularly caused black lightning to erupt; their first clash on Mary Geoise was said to have knocked out everyone in a five-kilometer radius. Rocks was also among the "handful" capable of infusing this Haki, drastically increasing his attack power and causing a black lightning-like energy to flow from his sword. By infusing Supreme King Haki into his attacks, Rocks was capable of inhibiting the Knights of God's immortality and temporarily inflicting serious injuries on them; and though Garling, the Knight that Rocks felled in battle, managed to survive, he noted that it would take a while even for him to fully recover from the pirate's attack.
He used a unique application of infusion on his demonized body, using it defensively similar to Armament Haki to counter and cancel out Roger's and Garp's attacks, which were imbued with Armament and Supreme King Haki, making a battle of attrition almost impossible. In this state, his clash with Roger and Garp created a massive shockwave that split the heavens. [Ch. 1165] However, their final attack, which was imbued with the "critical mass" of their Supreme King Haki, broke through his defenses, landing critical blows on him. [Ch. 1165]

##### Armament Haki
Rocks could use Armament Haki. As a demon, he imbued his sword with Armament Haki, turning his blade black while facing Roger and Garp. [Ch. 1165]

#### Weapons
Rocks' main weapon was a large, single-edged saber named Eclipse. Coupled with Rocks' Haki and strength, it was powerful and durable enough to hold up against Rocks' many mighty opponents and targets. Its status after his death is unknown.
He was also versatile with other weapons, such as an axe and firearms, which he used to win Davy Back Fights. He was also seen holding a pistol when he invaded the Levely and confronted Imu. [Ch. 1155]

#### Demonic Powers
Following his conversion into a demon by Imu, Rocks' already prodigious physical strength was amplified so that he could unleash devastatingly powerful attacks, which he used to overpower Linlin and Kaidou with one blow each. His clash with Whitebeard ended up destroying a substantial portion of God Valley, toppling one of the two mountains in the center of the island. The transformation also made him many times larger than before so that he stood at equal stature with Whitebeard and towered over both Roger and Garp.
As a demon, Rocks also gained an immortal body, allowing him to regenerate from potentially lethal wounds inflicted on him by Roger and Garp, combined with him using Supreme King Haki "infusion" defensively to counter their attacks. It took a combined attack imbued with the "critical mass" of Supreme King Haki from Roger and Garp to overcome demon Rocks once and for all and undo the transformation. [Ch. 1166]

### History

#### Biography

##### Rise to Infamy
Xebec was born on God Valley in the West Blue to the Davy Family 81 years ago, descending from the legendary pirate Davy D. Jones. Because of the family's persecution by the World Government, Xebec took the surname "Rocks" to hide his true heritage.
56 years before the present day, Rocks invaded Mary Geoise during the Levely and defeated a Marine Admiral with a deadly blow; he then took five kings hostage and infiltrated the Room of Flowers, where he personally encountered the World Government's highest authority and king of the world, Nerona Imu. Imu was not swayed by the capture of the kings, and knowing he could do nothing more against the ruler, Rocks departed, but not without warning Imu that he would return one day; this echoed a promise made by Davy Jones long ago. While running away from Pangaea Castle, Rocks then encountered the king of Elbaph, Harald, who attacked him, resulting in a powerful Supreme King Haki clash. The two men exchanged names, and Rocks advised the giant to aim his next strike at the town to aid in their escape. After this, Rocks pointed Harald in the direction of the New World and voiced his desire to meet again. [Ch. 1155]
These exploits instantly launched Rocks' name into worldwide infamy, and sometime later, he attacked and hijacked a ship carrying the Heavenly Tribute before destroying one of the Gates of Justice. Focusing mainly on the World Government in his piracy, he only went after other pirate crews in order to take desired subordinates using the Davy Back Fight. [Ch. 1155] Eight years after his attack on the Levely, Rocks and his crew arrived on Elbaph. They soon encountered Harald's young son Loki, who revealed his father was away on an expedition. Rocks ordered Loki to relay a message that he had been hurt in order to bring Harald back, but Loki refused to take orders from him, causing Rocks to actually strike and injure the child. [Ch. 1155] This indeed hastened Harald's return, and Rocks reunited with his old acquaintance in Aurust Castle in order to invite him to join his crew. Harald refused, as he wished to align with the World Government to achieve diplomacy with the rest of the world. Rocks revealed his plot to take over the lawless island of Hachinosu and bring all of its criminals under his control, forming a crew that could topple the World Government and make him the King of the World. [Ch. 1155]

##### Rocks' Era and Pursuit of Power
Harald continued to refuse and ignore Rocks' repeated requests for the next four years; Loki would always ask to join Rocks but was likewise rebuffed. Rocks and his crew then successfully took over Hachinosu, resulting in the official formation of the Rocks Pirates as several more powerful pirates joined them. After settling into their new base, Rocks proclaimed his next goals: finding two Devil Fruits (one of which was on Elbaph, with Rocks wishing for Harald to eat it) and finding the missing band of giants called Galleila. Kaidou questioned why Rocks did not simply forcibly coerce Harald and Elbaph into his service, but Rocks attacked his new subordinate in anger for suggesting this. Although there was little camaraderie among his band, Rocks rallied them together with the promise of showing them the peak of the world. [Ch. 1156]
Some time later, the Rocks Pirates invaded and pillaged the Foot Colony of the International Philanthropy Federation, which had enriched themselves on the money that they had supposedly collected for charity. They emerged from the destroyed temple to find themselves surrounded by Marine battleships, but Rocks simply responded by attacking, and the crew ultimately made their escape. Within two years of their founding, the Rocks Pirates had sunk 76 ships in 16 raids, left 14 ports, five towns, and a kingdom in ruins, and committed unprecedented violations of World Law that led to 25 violations being covered up entirely, leading to this time period becoming known as the "Era of Rocks."
Rocks returned to Elbaph to yet again convince Harald to join him after learning that the giant had clashed with government forces, but Harald still rebuffed him. [Ch. 1157] 42 years ago, the legendary captain of the Kuja Pirates, Shakuyaku, quit piracy and came to Hachinosu to run a bar; lovestruck along the rest of his male crew members, Rocks eagerly assisted in helping her build this establishment. [Ch. 1157] Two years later, however, Rocks had secretly married a woman named Eris and fathered a son named Teach; he sent them to God Valley to be kept safe by his clan, [Ch. 1159] but Shakky noticed his evident change in demeanor. Rocks' rival, Gol D. Roger, then arrived at the bar, and Rocks confronted him but was reminded that fighting between customers was not allowed on the island; however, the two did engage in combat out at sea.
During Rocks' next visit to Elbaph, he was attacked by Harald, who had been tasked to kill Rocks in order to gain affiliation with the World Government. Rocks tried to reason with the king, claiming that the government only wanted Elbaph for its military might and would enslave its citizens as soldiers. However, Harald still believed that despite the government's flaws, there was no better way to mend Elbaph's relations with the world. Their battle ended in a draw, but with their friendship ended, Rocks had given up on recruiting Harald.

##### God Valley Incident and Downfall
39 years ago, Rocks and his forces discovered that Shakky had been kidnapped by an unknown perpetrator who also managed to kill Don Marlon in the process. [Ch. 1159] A year later, they learned that she was being held by the World Government for the World Nobles' Native Hunting Competition, which was being held on God Valley. Rocks ordered his crew to follow him to the island; unbeknownst to them, his main goal was to protect Eris and Teach. As the Rocks Pirates sailed there, they were confronted at sea by Harald, but Rocks asked to speak with him in private. Rocks revealed his real name and the dilemma with Eris and Teach before once again asking the giant to help him, but Harald again refused, causing the two to fight once again. Rocks inflicted a powerful blow on Harald's ship, and he and his crew managed to get away and continue to God Valley undeterred.
The Rocks Pirates successfully made landfall on God Valley and disrupted the World Nobles' massacre, but they were soon joined by the Roger Pirates, and the Marines led by Monkey D. Garp as well as the Knights of God quickly rose up against the pirates. [Ch. 1160] Rocks went off on his own to look for Eris and Teach and found them as he entered into combat with Figarland Garling and another Knight. However, Garling and his companion were able to transform Rocks' father and grandmother into demons, forcing Rocks to flee with his wife and child in tow. However, Eris convinced Rocks to let her go off alone with Teach, saying they would both work to survive and reunite at the Lulusia Kingdom. Rocks then stood to confront Garling and ultimately overpowered the Knight; however, he then watched as Imu possessed Jaygarcia Saturn nearby.
Rocks went up to confront Imu, knowing the ruler was after him, and affirmed his intent to keep Davy Jones' promise as he severed one of Saturn's gyuki legs in their ensuing duel. Roger, Garp, Edward Newgate, Charlotte Linlin, and Kaidou then attacked Imu alongside Rocks, but the ruler was unharmed and spotted Eris and Teach running elsewhere on the island. Rocks jumped up and cut the head of Imu's shadowy form into several pieces, but Imu manifested two tendrils and impaled Rocks before enacting Domi Reversi to transform him into a demon. [Ch. 1163] Rocks was briefly able to resist the transformation and taunted Imu, saying that Davy's will would be passed down to fulfill his promise and asking if Imu was more afraid of Davy Jones or Joy Boy. Imu brushed this off and revealed his intention of turning the giants of Elbaph to the government's will, which enraged Rocks, but the demon transformation was then completed as Rocks became enslaved to Imu's will. Rocks attacked and overpowered Kaidou and Linlin before clashing with Newgate, causing one of God Valley's mountains to collapse. He then pursued Eris and Teach to the coast, but Bartholomew Kuma arrived and transported them off the island right as Rocks and Marine cannonballs were about to strike. Thinking they had been killed, Imu ordered Rocks to wipe out everyone that was still alive on the island.
After overwhelming some pirates, Rocks was confronted by Roger and Garp; at that moment, Rocks called out telepathically with his real mind, urging the two men to kill him, which they could hear. Roger and Garp proceeded to clash with Rocks, with all three of them imbuing Supreme King Haki, and their clash split the heavens. Teaming up, Roger and Garp battled Rocks for hours but made little progress due to Rocks' demonic strength and Supreme King protective coating. Recognizing that Rocks was truly sorrowful to be going through this, Roger and Garp combined a critical mass of their Supreme King Haki to launch at Rocks simultaneously, which successfully reverted him to normal. As he lay on the ground, Rocks began to regain his mind as he saw Garling, Shepherd Sommers, and Satchels Maffey approaching him. In his final moments, Rocks internally hoped that Roger and Garp would be able to escape and thanked them for stopping him; he was then impaled by Garling's sword and killed. Soon afterward, the entirety of God Valley collapsed into the ocean. [Ch. 1166]

#### Legacy
Rocks' exploits and achievements in history have been considerably diminished in the decades since his death, with many younger Marines being unaware of who he is. According to Sengoku, this lack of knowledge about him is mainly due to the World Government's intervention, as his path resulted in him dabbling in subjects they forbid, with the most notable being him having learned about Nerona Imu, the true secret ruler of the world, prior to his rise into piracy, a thing that only the Five Elders and Knights of God have knowledge of. Additionally, due to the overall lack of camaraderie within the Rocks Pirates, most of Rocks' past subordinates are not inclined to talk about their time sailing with the crew. The World Government and press gave Garp all of the credit for Rocks' defeat, leading to him receiving the title "Hero of the Marines." [Ch. 1166] Garp's alliance with Roger was covered up, along with Imu's involvement. There has been no indication that the world at large was aware of the Knights of God's presence during the incident; Garling did attain the distinction of "champion" of God Valley, [Ch. 1086, Ep. 1120] though it is unconfirmed if his killing Rocks played a role in receiving the title.
Many of Rocks' subordinates would go on to become extremely notable pirates, particularly Edward Newgate, Charlotte Linlin, and Kaidou, who became three of the Four Emperors ruling over the New World. Shiki infamously clashed with the Roger Pirates multiple times and became the first documented inmate to escape Impel Down, [Ch. 530, Ep. 425] while Captain John gathered a fabled treasure that has been sought for decades. [Ch. 233, Ep. 145] A full 38 years after Rocks' death, Kaidou and Linlin decided to put aside their enmity with each other and form a temporary alliance to conquer the world. [Ch. 954, Ep. 955] This development, though ultimately short-lived, caused the old guard of the Marines to recall the Era of Rocks and necessitated instruction of the younger sailors about his actions.
Rocks' dream of taking over the world would be inherited by his son Marshall D. Teach. Teach would name his flagship the "Saber of Xebec", directly kill and usurp Whitebeard's position as Emperor, [Ch. 650, Ep. 570] and reclaim Rocks' old territory on Hachinosu from his former subordinate Ochoku. [Ch. 1059, Ep. 1088] Rocks, being Teach's father, is seemingly known only to Imu and the Five Elders as well as the Ten Titanic Captains of the Blackbeard Pirates, as not even Fleet Admiral Sengoku knew when the name was nominated to be a Warlord.
