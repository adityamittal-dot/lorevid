## Takeaways
1. Medieval Europe and violent "raider/soldier" framings are heavily saturated in this niche right now; an under-told, non-violent profession in a different region reads as fresher.
2. The proven structural pattern is a single person's single role, followed start to finish — keep the "whole life arc," don't turn it into a fact dump.
3. Delivery tone (whispered, unhurried) is as important to the audience's expectation as subject matter — the script and voice choice must both stay gentle throughout.
4. A vivid "smuggled origin" or craft-secret style fact makes a strong cold-open hook even for a calm video.
5. Nothing in this month's documentary news cycle is relevant to an evergreen history piece, which fits the RESERVE brief (avoid anything time-bound).
