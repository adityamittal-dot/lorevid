**Chosen:** Your Life as an Aztec Merchant in Tenochtitlan

**Angle:** Not a warrior or a sacrifice victim — a pochtecatl, one of the Aztec empire's professional
long-distance trading merchants. The video follows one trading life from apprentice porter to master
trader: the pre-dawn departure from Tenochtitlan in plain clothes to hide your wealth, weeks on foot
through hostile borderlands carrying cacao beans, cotton cloaks and quetzal feathers, the danger of being
robbed or captured for lacking an escort, the secret intelligence-gathering role pochteca played for the
emperor, and the disguised return home at night to a private feast among your own trading guild — ending
on the quiet, comfortable weight of a life spent walking the empire's roads and always, eventually, coming
home. Evergreen: no current-events hook, purely period daily life, works as well in five years as today.
