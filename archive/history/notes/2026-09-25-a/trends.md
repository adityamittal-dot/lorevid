## Findings
- The "Your Life as a ___" format is an established sub-genre inside the "boring/sleep history" niche, run as long-form (often 1-2 hour) compilations by channels like Boring History For Sleep — e.g. "Your Life as The REAL Ragnar Lothbrok and more" and "Your Life as a Viking Shieldmaiden and more" — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=exgMcwNaz3E and https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y9Q2JpEv4w4
- Titles are almost always second-person, role-first, no punctuation beyond a comma/colon: "Your Life as a Geisha", "Your Life as Miyamoto Musashi" — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ndPvh4DLWzU and https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3sEyhYXUIWE
- Dedicated bedtime-history channels (History to Sleep, Bedtime History, Just Sleep) all promise "slow-paced, soft-spoken", "no loud sounds, just consistent quiet narration" — the calm, unhurried narration is the product, not just a bonus — https://www.youtube.com/@HistorytoSleep-d6j
- 404media reported that AI-narrated "boring history for sleep" videos are now flooding YouTube and are frequently generic/low-effort, which means specificity (named places, real prices, real sensory detail) is what will differentiate a video rather than blend in — https://www.404media.co/ai-generated-boring-history-videos-are-flooding-youtube-and-drowning-out-real-history/
- Within the existing "Your Life as an Aztec ___" and "Your Life as a Viking ___" catalogue, the roles already claimed are dramatic/violent ones: Aztec Sacrifice Priestess, Aztec Jaguar Warrior, Aztec Concubine, Viking Captive, Viking Shieldmaiden, Viking Berserker — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tb7D7X52Aow, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=emKcDb1gt9E, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IoJZ-mLtnQk, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HLIuWiEVgxo
- No existing "Your Life as an Aztec Merchant" (or pochteca-themed) video turned up in search; instead the pochteca material lives on museum/academic pages (Cambridge, Australian Museum, Britannica, Mexico Unexplained, aztecsandtenochtitlan.com), which means the role is dramatically rich (night travel in disguise, spies, danger on the road, no pack animals or wheels) but is not yet a saturated "Your Life as" title — https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/everyday-life-in-the-aztec-world/merchant/FD80A5E407DFEB532219756016CA3269, https://mexicounexplained.com/pochteca-aztec-traders-aztec-spies/, https://britannica.com/topic/pochteca
- Documentaries broadly are moving toward genre-blending and one-off deep-dive films rather than sprawling docu-series, and non-Western narratives/production are getting more attention this year, which supports choosing an under-covered non-Western role over another Rome/Viking retread — https://www.tasteray.com/articles/history-documentary-movies, https://www.accio.com/business/popular-documentaries-trend-2026
- General-audience "what life was like in ___" explainer videos (Ancient Alexandria, Ancient Egypt, Ancient cities) confirm daily-life/sensory framing ("what was daily life like") consistently outperforms battle/political framing as a hook for this kind of calm content — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L1BPjHILuM8, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Hk6Kh_IdgK4
- Medieval daily-life topics are heavily saturated in the "boring history for sleep" catalogue already ("Why You Wouldn't Last a Day in Medieval Times", "What Medieval JUNK FOOD Was Like", "Forgotten Medieval SURVIVAL Techniques"), so a plain medieval-peasant script risks feeling like a retread unless given a very specific angle — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9jnekLeHz3c, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=51rnRbpsZ-c, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FC5XAHlqmLc

## Takeaways
1. Specificity beats genre-generic AI slop right now — lean hard on named places, real prices/wages, and concrete sensory detail from research.md rather than vague "life was hard" language.
2. Favor a role nobody in the existing "Your Life as a ___" catalogue has claimed yet (merchant/trader over yet another warrior or captive) — it reads as fresher and avoids echoing a competitor's exact title.
3. Keep the second-person, role-first title convention with no gimmicky punctuation; 2-4 highlighted thumbnail words should promise curiosity (a rule, a risk, a secret), not violence, matching the calm-bedtime brand.
4. A trade/travel narrative (danger on the road, disguise, homecoming) gives natural rising-and-falling tension without needing battle or gore, which fits the safety rules and the calm bedtime tone better than a soldier or sacrifice-adjacent role.
5. Favor an under-covered civilization/role (per WRITER.md's guidance to mix in Aztec, Chola, Maratha, Ottoman, etc.) over a third Viking or another medieval-peasant script this month.
