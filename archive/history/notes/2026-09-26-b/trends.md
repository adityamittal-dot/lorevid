## Takeaways
1. Calm, unhurried, sensory narration beats comedic or fast-cut styles for this audience — protect the slow bedtime pace above all.
2. A well-known "instant recognition" era (Rome, Vikings, medieval Europe) is heavily saturated right now; an under-covered empire read as fresher and is explicitly encouraged by our own topic-scoring criteria.
3. First-person/second-person immersive framing (the viewer *is* the character) is trending in 2026 documentary formats generally, validating the channel's whole premise.
4. Even niche channels sustain an audience with modest, consistent uploads and a distinctive emotional hook — the title/thumbnail promise still has to be sharp and honest.
5. Reliable encyclopedic sources exist for less-common empires, so freshness doesn't have to cost factual depth.
