# Critique — Your Life as an Ottoman Janissary

## Bedtime viewer
- The opening hook lands well ("By supper you will not be your mother's son anymore") — vivid, a real stake, no slow intro.
- Chapter 4 (the siege) is the one place a sleepy viewer could get jolted rather than soothed: as drafted, five scenes in a row carry `tense` or `urgent` tags ("At night the mehter band strikes up..." through "By afternoon the ancient walls have fallen...") with almost no calm breathing room between them. That is too much unbroken intensity for this audience.
- Chapter 2's farm years read a little like a list of facts ("You learn to drive oxen, mend fences...") rather than a lived-in sensory moment — mild retention risk before the story reaches Istanbul.
- The ending is genuinely gentle and warm, which is right for this audience, and the small-carved-comb detail is a nice quiet anchor — but it only appears at the very start and the very end, so the emotional throughline goes quiet in the middle of the video exactly when the viewer most needs a small reassuring touchstone.
- I do feel like "I am there" for the barracks, the cauldron, the cap — the sensory specificity (felt cap, iron cauldron, drums) is doing real work.

## Historian
- "You are eight, barefoot..." — research.md's devshirme entries (facts 1-2) never give an exact recruitment age; stating "eight" as fact is an invented specific the file doesn't support.
- "Mehmed, your young sultan, has brought cannon so large that four hundred men are needed to haul just one." — no fact in research.md gives a headcount for hauling the siege cannon; this specific number is invented and must go.
- "For fifty days the guns batter Constantinople's walls" — research.md's fact 17 mentions the siege and the cannon but cites no duration; "fifty days" is an invented specific.
- "You are twenty eight years old" at the fall of Constantinople — this number is only as solid as the invented recruitment age it was built on; once that goes, this must go too, or it becomes an unsupported claim standing alone.
- Everything else traces cleanly to research.md: the çorbacı/kazan naming (facts 8-9), the börk and Hacı Bektaş Veli (facts 12-13), the marriage and trade ban (fact 16), the akçe pay structure (fact 14), the bastinado (fact 15), the baklava procession (fact 11), the mehter band at the walls (fact 18), and the matchlock-and-cannon combination at the 1453 siege (fact 17) are all well grounded.

## YouTube strategist
- First thirty seconds: strong — clear stake, no "in this video," no throat-clearing.
- Retention dip risk: the farm-years stretch in Chapter 2 (see Bedtime viewer note) and the fact that Chapter 2's closing line ("...something in you starts to answer to a new name") is soft mood rather than a real open loop — it doesn't tease anything specific about the initiation coming in Chapter 3.
- Chapter 3's closing line ("...you no longer remember missing anyone, only that you once did") has the same problem: purely backward-looking, no forward pull into the march to Constantinople.
- Repetition check: "brothers of the spoon" (Chapter 3) and the closing line's "spoon by spoon, year by year" (Chapter 5) reuse the exact same word twice as the big final callback — it reads more like reaching for the same line twice than an intentional echo.
- No AI-sounding stock phrases present ("tapestry," "testament," "delve," "little did you know," "in a world where") — checked the whole draft, clean.
- Narrator voice `am_onyx` and mostly calm/warm tones read well aloud for a soldier's-life story; the only pacing problem read aloud is the tense/urgent cluster in Chapter 4 already flagged above.
- Title/thumbnail promise: not yet written at draft stage — flagging that the eventual title must not overclaim battle/gore given how gently this story is actually told.

## Must fix
1. Cut the invented recruitment age in "You are eight, barefoot in the mud of a Balkan mountain village..." — remove the specific number since research.md gives no exact age for devshirme recruits.
2. Cut "four hundred men are needed to haul just one" cannon — not supported anywhere in research.md; replace with a non-numeric sensory description grounded in fact 17's "huge siege cannon."
3. Cut "For fifty days the guns batter Constantinople's walls" — no duration is sourced in research.md; rephrase without a specific number of days.
4. Recompute or remove "You are twenty eight years old" at the siege, since it was built on the now-removed recruitment age — replace with an age-neutral line ("You are a grown man now...").
5. Break up the five-scene tense/urgent run in Chapter 4 ("At night the mehter band strikes up..." through "By afternoon the ancient walls have fallen...") by inserting at least one calm sensory beat mid-sequence (e.g. the camp's waiting rhythm, or cleaning the matchlock by firelight) so the siege doesn't read as one unbroken jolt.
6. Rewrite Chapter 2's closing line ("...something in you starts to answer to a new name") into a real open loop that specifically teases the initiation into a full company.
7. Rewrite Chapter 3's closing line ("...you no longer remember missing anyone, only that you once did") to also pull forward into the coming campaign, not just look backward.
8. Vary the Chapter 5 callback so it does not reuse "spoon" a third time identically to Chapter 3's "brothers of the spoon" — find a different word for the final echo (e.g. built around the cauldron instead of the spoon).
9. Tighten Chapter 2's farm-life scenes ("You learn to drive oxen, mend fences...") so they read as one or two lived sensory moments rather than a list of skills learned.
10. Thread the small-carved-comb/river motif through the middle of the story too, not only the opening and closing chapters, so the emotional anchor doesn't go quiet for the whole middle of the video.
