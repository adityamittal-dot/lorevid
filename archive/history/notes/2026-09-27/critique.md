# Critique — Your Life as an Egyptian Pyramid Builder

## Round 1

### Bedtime viewer
- The hook is strong and stakes-first, but "does not let you see her face while she does it" underplays the emotional beat — it tells us she's hiding something rather than letting us feel it.
- Chapter Three leans expository in places rather than sensory: "A man named Merer keeps a written count of every trip his boats make, and you wonder what it feels like to be trusted with numbers" reads like a fact dropped in, not something the viewer is experiencing through their own body.
- The simile "the way ___" recurs three times ("the way a field path packs hard," "the way grain tax is owed," "the way men say goodbye who expect") — read aloud back to back across the script, this starts to feel like a verbal tic rather than natural voice.
- Chapter Four's "sealed inside forever... makes your chest go tight" and Chapter Five's closing "your name still waits in the dark, patient, unseen, and entirely yours" cover very similar emotional ground twice; the ending would land harder if it didn't repeat a beat we already felt.
- The injury scene is handled soberly and doesn't jolt the calm, but it would benefit from one more slow, textural beat immediately after (sound of the gallery, weight of the night) before cutting to morning, so the viewer's pulse settles before the scene change.

### Historian
- "nine days of work, one day that belongs to your own aching legs and nothing else" — research.md only supports a ten-day week showing up in Merer's shipping log (fact 25: "two or three round-trips every ten-day week"); it does not confirm a specific nine-on/one-off rest allocation for laborers. This line states more certainty than the sources give.
- "Too much water and the sand turns to mud that swallows the sledge whole" — overstates fact 22, which says excess water makes sand clump and pile up in front of the sledge, not that it swallows the load. The mechanism should match the source.
- "boats you saw at the quarry landing, wide-bellied vessels bringing pale limestone up the flooded river" is vague where it could be specific and better grounded: fact 25 names the Tura quarry and the Giza destination directly, and tying the line explicitly to Tura would strengthen accuracy rather than leaving it generic.

### YouTube strategist
- "Second sunrise" (Ch.1), "second wage" (Ch.2), and "second load" (Ch.3) form an accidental repeating tic rather than a deliberate motif — keep the sunrise bookend as the one intentional callback and reword the other two.
- Chapter Two ends on a calm, settled beat ("writes it down as if it matters") with no forward pull into the physical work of Chapter Three — add a soft open-loop line so the chapter break earns a "watch on."
- The two natural tense beats (the crushing injury in Ch.3, the heaviest hauling week in Ch.4) sit one chapter apart, which is fine, but this needs to be locked in explicitly once tones are assigned so no two tense/urgent scenes land back to back.
- No AI-sounding stock phrases found (no "tapestry," "testament," "delve," "little did you know," "in a world where") — clean on that front.

## Must fix
1. Rewrite "does not let you see her face while she does it" (Ch.1) into a concrete sensory beat instead of a stated absence.
2. Rewrite the expository Merer line in Ch.3 ("you wonder what it feels like to be trusted with numbers") to something the viewer feels in their own body, not a reflection on someone else's job.
3. Remove or vary at least two of the three "the way ___" similes so the device doesn't repeat more than once.
4. Cut the redundancy between the Ch.4 "chest go tight" beat and the Ch.5 closing line about the hidden name — keep the idea once, in the ending, and let Ch.4 land on something else.
5. Add one slow textural beat after the injury scene (Ch.3) before cutting to the next morning, so tension settles before the scene change.
6. Soften "nine days of work, one day that belongs to your own aching legs" to match what research.md actually supports (the ten-day rhythm from Merer's log), without asserting an exact, unsupported rest-day count.
7. Fix the wet-sand physics line so the failure mode matches fact 22 (sand clumps and piles up in front of the sledge) rather than "swallows the sledge whole."
8. Make the boat/limestone line in Ch.3 specific to the Tura quarry and Giza, per fact 25, instead of a generic "wide-bellied vessels."
9. Remove the accidental "second ___" repetition in Ch.2 and Ch.3 (second wage / second load), keeping only the Ch.1 "second sunrise" bookend.
10. Add a soft open-loop line at the end of Chapter Two pulling into the physical labor of Chapter Three.
