**Chosen:** Your Life as a Royal Elephant Keeper in Mughal India (reworded from the `topics/long.txt` line "Your Life as a Mughal Royal Elephant Keeper" for a clearer, more evocative title; same topic, same queue item)

**Angle:** A boy who inherits his father's post as a mahout in Akbar's imperial stables follows one elephant from her arrival as a nervous, half-wild calf through a whole working life together — the daily washing and feeding rituals, a terrifying first taste of musth, a procession before the emperor, the sobering duties elephants were put to at court, and finally old age, when keeper and elephant are both grey and slow together. It trades soldierly danger for a quieter, animal-centred emotional throughline that still carries real imperial spectacle.
