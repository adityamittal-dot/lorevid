## Takeaways
1. Avoid the blacksmith topic this round — it is the most crowded angle in current search results ("why it sucked", "harsh reality" titles repeated across channels); falconer is fresher for the same "medieval craft/labour" itch.
2. Lean into the falconer's unusual closeness to the monarch (a servant who sits near the throne) as the emotional spine — status, trust, and the risk of losing a prized bird are higher stakes than pure daily-routine description.
3. Use the training process (glove, hood, lure, patient conditioning of a wild hawk) as the sensory/tactile core of the middle chapters — it maps naturally to slow, ASMR-adjacent bedtime narration.
4. Keep the title blunt and concrete like the top-performing videos found ("Why It Sucked...", "The Harsh Reality...") rather than vague or poetic.
5. No sudden shocks — the format's audience wants a steady emotional build with a soft, resolved ending, so any danger (a lost hawk, a jealous rival, a hunting accident) should resolve calmly, not violently.
